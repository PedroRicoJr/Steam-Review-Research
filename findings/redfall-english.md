| | |
|---|---|

| Thumbs up in the sample | **493 of 1,014 — 48.6%**, ±2.52 points |
| Thumbs up on Steam for this group | **38.5%** |

**How to read the words below.** A **bullet** is one thing one person said. A **tag** is where that
bullet was filed. Tags have three levels: a **division** (who at a studio owns it — game design, art,
engineering), a **subject** (the thing being talked about), and a **mode** (what actually happened).
`unknown` means the person raised a subject and never said what about it.

## 0. ⚠️ Read this before any number below

**The sample says 48.6% up. Steam says 38.5%. That 10-point gap is not sampling error** — the
interval is ±2.52 points and cannot stretch that far.

**It is the shape of the pull, and it is the method working as designed.** SAMPLING-RULES reads every
month at a similar depth so that a game's whole life is visible. Redfall's negatives are not spread
across its life. **They are stacked in one month:**

| | Reviews on Steam | Reviews read | Depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2023-05, the launch month** | **1,986** (65% of the group) | 404 | **20.3%** |
| Every other month (39 of them) | 1,085 | 611 | **56.3%** |

The launch month is two thirds of everything ever written about this game in English, and it is the
month read least deeply. **An even read across months therefore under-weights the launch and
over-weights the long tail.**

**Weighting each month back to its true Steam volume (Rule 6) gives 42.9%.** That is 4.4 points from
Steam's own figure, and the remainder is the same effect one level down: **within** May 2023 the
sampler spread across weekly windows, and the very first days — the worst days — are still read
thinner than the rest of the month.

**So use the numbers this way:**

- **The direction of every finding below is sound.** It rests on 1,014 reviews and a third of the
  population.
- **Any single headline percentage is not Steam's percentage** and should never be quoted as one.
- **Where a number matters, the weighted figure is given beside the raw one.**

**Three of the five are about what to pay and what other people think.** The first fact about the

- **The game recovered and the score did not.** May–Aug 2023 runs at **34.7%** up; September 2023

  reading. **Steam shows one number for both.**
- **124 reviewers argue the game is judged worse than it is. Nine argue the bad reputation is
  accurate.** That is not a close argument.
- **The game will not start for a growing share of buyers.** One in ten of every 2026 review read
  says so. Section 6 is that finding.

## 3. ⭐ The finding: two games are being reviewed under one score

**Every month, split at the patch that moved the score:**

| Window | Reviews read | % up | 95% interval |
|---|---|---|---|
| **May – Aug 2023** | 470 | **34.7%** | 30.4 – 39.0 |
| **Sept 2023 – Aug 2026** | 544 | **60.7%** | 56.6 – 64.8 |

**The intervals do not touch, and have not touched across nine rounds of reading.** This is the most
stable finding in the Redfall corpus.

**The same tags, split the same way** — as a share of the reviews in each half:

| Tag | Launch half | After | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| `publishing.price.too-high-for-what-it-is` | **21.3%** | 8.1% | ⬇ fell by two thirds |
| `game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour` | **17.0%** | 8.5% | ⬇ halved |
| `production.launch-state.shipped-broken` | **14.7%** | 5.5% | ⬇ |
| `engineering.performance.unstable-framerate` | **8.5%** | 1.7% | ⬇ **nearly gone** |
| `publishing.sale-dependency.buy-on-sale-only` | 8.7% | **24.1%** | ⬆ **tripled** |
| `live-ops.abandonment.the-owner-pulled-the-plug` | 0.2% | **10.8%** | ⬆ |
| `engineering.stability.crashes-repeatedly` | 1.7% | **5.5%** | ⬆ **tripled** |
| `engineering.access.plays-offline` | **0.0%** | 3.9% | ⬆ did not exist before |
| `engineering.servers.cannot-connect` | 0.6% | **2.8%** | ⬆ |

**Read the two columns as two different games.**

**The launch game** was too expensive, ran badly, and had stupid enemies. **Every one of those
complaints is now a third to a fifth of what it was.**

**The current game** is cheap, is recommended on sale, has no owner, **and crashes more than the
launch version did.** Frame rate was fixed; stability was not.

⚠️ **`judged-unfairly` is the exception that proves the split is real.** It runs at **13.0% before**
and **11.6% after** — flat. **People were already arguing the pile-on was unfair while the pile-on
was happening.** It is not a late revision of history.

## 5. ⭐ The most-upvoted complaint in the corpus is about a free copy

`marketing.discovery.came-free-with-hardware` — Redfall was bundled with graphics cards — appears on
only **50 bullets**, but those bullets carry **3,935 helpful votes: 79 votes per bullet, the highest
rate of any tag in the group.**

**The single most-upvoted review in the entire Redfall corpus** (1,070 helpful, thumbs down, one hour
played) carries exactly three tags: `shipped-broken`, `too-high-for-what-it-is`, and
`came-free-with-hardware`.

**Tags by the helpful votes they carry, not by how often they are said:**

| Tag | Votes | Bullets | Votes per bullet |
|---|---|---|---|
| `publishing.price.too-high-for-what-it-is` | 6,107 | 144 | 42 |
| `production.launch-state.shipped-broken` | 4,289 | 99 | 43 |
| **`marketing.discovery.came-free-with-hardware`** | **3,935** | **50** | **79** |
| `game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour` | 3,448 | 126 | 27 |
| `publishing.sale-dependency.buy-on-sale-only` | 2,559 | 172 | **15** |

⚠️ **Compare the top and bottom rows.** The advice to buy on sale is said **most often** (172) and

**Steam's helpful vote rewards the launch-week verdict and ignores the later advice** — one more
reason the store's single number stays at 38.5% while the reviews underneath it move.

## 6. ⭐ The game does not start, and there is nobody left to fix it

`engineering.servers.cannot-connect` holds **18 reviews**. They fall into two groups:

| Period | Reviews | Zero hours played | Helpful votes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 launch | 3 | 0 | 56 |
| **After the studio closed (Nov 2024 →)** | **13** | **7** | **593** |

**Seven people wrote a review of a game they were never able to launch.**

> *"Don't buy, game just is stuck on a connecting to server page."* — **39 helpful, 0 hours**
> *"offline mode doesn't exist? it won't let me start the game just gets stuck trying to connect to
> non existent servers"* — **54 helpful, 0 hours**
> *"It still tries to connect to a server that isn't there anymore and doesn't boot up cuz of it…
> **Was refused a refund and the game is still for sale despite being unplayable.**"* — **77 helpful**

**The mechanism, from the reviews themselves:** the first launch overlays a server-connection dialog
on top of the licence agreement. **The agreement must be accepted to connect, and the connection
window sits on top of it.** The offline mode that would bypass the whole thing is behind the same
screen for anyone who did not enable it before the servers went quiet.

**And the buyers are doing the support work.**
`community.player-conduct.players-teach-each-other-the-fix` (**8 bullets**) is people publishing the
workaround inside their reviews:

> *"**DON'T use the mouse is the key, use the keyboard and hit enter.**"*
> *"press the space bar multiple times while pressing cancel and voilà — you're in."*
> *"if you **spam enter**, you can get it to work. if you do not do this, it will never connect."*

**The same pattern shows on the crashes.** `engineering.stability.one-setting-causes-the-crashes` was
built during this run because two reviewers found the single option behind them and published it —
*"switching off DLSS cured ALL the crashing"*, *"lower your shadow quality and turn on FXA."*

⚠️ **This is the practical finding for a studio.** An always-online check outlived the company that
answered it. **It does not stop the game selling** — `store-page-hides-a-dealbreaker` holds 8 bullets
and two reviewers ask outright for the game to be delisted — **it just turns every new buyer into a
zero-hour thumbs down.** 101 reviews in this corpus (10.0%) have zero hours; **89 of them are
negative.**

## 7. What this corpus cannot tell you

1. ⚠️ **It cannot give you Steam's 38.5%.** See section 0. The even-month read is a deliberate choice
   that makes the game's whole life visible and its launch month under-weighted.
2. **It cannot tell you how many people never wrote a review.** Redfall shipped into Game Pass, and
   `came-free-with-hardware` shows a large bundled audience. **Reviewers are the ones who cared
   enough to type.**
3. **It cannot separate "the game improved" from "the audience changed."** Both happen after October
   2023: patches landed, and the people arriving afterwards paid a tenth of the price and had read
   the reviews first. **`low-expectations-made-it-better` is in the tree for exactly this reason.**
   Nothing in review text splits the two cleanly.
4. **English only.** 3,071 of the game's 4,716 reviews are English. The other 1,645 are unread.
5. **175 reviews (17.3%) carry more than one date** — an original and an edit. Every one is filed
   under its **creation** month, on standing instruction. **A review written at launch and rewritten
   in 2026 counts as a launch review here.** This is an open question with Rico and is not settled.
6. **Steam's language tag is the reviewer's client language, not the language they wrote in.** At
   least one review in this group is written in Portuguese. It was read and tagged on what it says.
