## 1. The short version

**Players come for their friends: "much better with friends" is the top specific praise (103), and 20
more praise the 8-player lobby itself.**

**The thumbs down are about the numbers: a rival does it better (19 of 149 thumbs down), badly scaled
difficulty (19), too little money and too few good drops (13), and damage-sponge enemies (12).**

**Solo play is the second fight: 27 reviews say it is punishing or unplayable alone, 10 that it works;
reviewers report boss health the same for 1 player or 8.**

**There is not enough yet: 31 say too little content, 12 that it runs out fast, 11 that nothing is left
after a win; 48 want more because they like it.**

**The thumb dipped to 74% in March-April and came back to about 80%; complaints fell from 85-92 to 56
per 100 after an August update that reviewers say widened the builds.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**A co-op game sold for any group size has to be tuned for every group size — and its rewards must not
be split between players who need different things.** LORT is loved as something to play with friends
(103 notes, 20 more for the 8-player lobby), but 27 reviews say it is punishing alone, 7 that a small
group faces numbers set for a full one, and reviewers report a boss with the same health for 1 player or
8. Three reviewers found co-op could make each player weaker: each character grows from one of three
stats, most camps give one, so a mixed group shares the camps it needs ("we're practically playing
solo", 218819580). Add stacking death penalties (8) and a host whose crash ends everyone's run, and the
group that the game is for is also the group it punishes. The complaints fell after reviewers say the
easy setting and an August update arrived, but the cross-group tuning is the lesson.
