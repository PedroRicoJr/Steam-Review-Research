# ZCREW — English

**A census: all 77 English reviews read. No margin of error.** Every English review on the game was
read, so there is no sample and no margin. Steam's own English count is 38 up and 39 down; so is ours.
Figures are as of 2026-09-10; the tables in section 2 were re-run on 2026-10-03.

**A top-down twin-stick co-op shooter against zombies from Snail Games, in early access from 2020-12
and released 2022-09.** Missions with objectives end in a timed run to an evacuation helicopter;
classes with their own skill trees. Paid; one reviewer recommends it "at $5" (the full price is not on
this page). The engine, the hosting model and the player count are not recorded on this page. Steam,
all languages: 363 reviews, 191 up, 52.6% — English is 21%. ⚠️ **The first grid measured only 42
reviews for this game** — it started at the 1.0 date and missed the 35 early-access reviews. The start
month was corrected on 2026-09-10 and the group re-pulled; every figure here is from the corrected 77.
It was read as the second of the ten-game block. **It shares co-op, PvE, missions that end in getting
out by helicopter (close to extraction), and classes with Dominion; it is top-down, not third person,
and zombies, not sci-fi.**

## 1. In plain words

**What decides how players feel is whether the game works.** 11 times a reviewer said a bug stopped
them playing — a mission goal stuck at 8 of 10, a helicopter that will not come, enemies that stop
appearing — and 10 of those 11 voted thumbs down.

**The only real case for buying it is that it is cheap.** 11 times a reviewer said the price is fair,
and all 11 recommend it. Recommenders say it is not great, but it costs about five dollars.

**The menus are the next biggest complaint.** 7 times a reviewer said the menus and screens are hard
to find your way around. 4 said a game pad only half works, so you have to play with a mouse.

**Players who left complain about bugs; players who stayed liked the classes and the updates.** 3
said each class plays its own way, and 4 praised steady updates — all of them in early access.

**It changed from "an example to all early access games" to "abandoned".** In 2021, 4 praised steady
updates and 3 said early access grew into what it promised. After full release, the reviews are bug
reports, and some call it forsaken.

**A reply from the studio became a scorecard.** The studio answered one review in November 2021
promising full controller support; the reviewer edited the review three times over the next two and
a half years to say it never came.

**The surprise: the end-of-mission run to the helicopter is a complaint in its own right.** 3 times a
reviewer said the timer to reach the helicopter fails the mission or should be optional — beside the
bug where the helicopter does not respond at all.

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **A bug that stops a mission is the one thing a small game is not forgiven for.**
   Evidence: 11 times it was said, 10 thumbs down; stuck goals, an evacuation helicopter that will not
   respond, enemies that stop spawning. Compare Zombie Girl, where 12 of 14 "too short" comments still
   recommend.
   Dominion could: give every objective and every extraction a fail-safe (a timer or a host command
   that forces the next step), log stuck states, and test extraction under host lag and client
   disconnects on a listen server.
   Strength: medium for this game alone; the contrast with forgiven "too short" makes it stronger.
2. **The extraction moment must always work.**
   Evidence: the evacuation helicopter that does not respond is named among the 11 game-stopping bugs.
   Dominion could: make extraction its most tested system, with a backup path if the main trigger
   fails.
   Strength: weak by count, high stakes for an extraction game.
3. **Full release raises the bar; bugs after 1.0 are judged harder.**
   Evidence: praise for updates is all 2021; game-stopping bugs are 7 of the 18 reviews after 2023;
   *"no excuse for this after 4 years"* (`158283309`).
   Dominion could: do not call it 1.0 until missions finish reliably.
   Strength: medium.
4. **Anything the studio promises in a reply becomes a scorecard.**
   Evidence: one review edited three times over two and a half years, counting the missing controller
   support.
   Dominion could: only promise in public what is scheduled; follow up on the same thread when it ships
   or is dropped.
   Strength: weak (one review), clear.
5. **Do not tie a character's look or gender to the class.**
   Evidence: said in ZCREW and one other game, 4 times across the two.
   Dominion could: let any player pick any look for any role.
   Strength: weak to medium.
6. **Expect comparison to the big co-op name, and to lose on "soul".**
   Evidence: 10 of 77 reviews name another game, 5 name Helldivers; 4 say it is worse.
   Dominion could: give the game its own personality, voice lines and moments so it is not "the same
   but with the charm sucked out".
   Strength: weak to medium.
7. **Make achievements track reliably.**
   Evidence: once here, 3 times across three games.
   Strength: weak.
