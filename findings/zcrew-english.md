## 1. ⭐ This game's negative reviews are about whether it runs, not how it plays

| Mode | Bullets | Thumbs up | Thumbs down |
|---|---|---|---|
| `engineering.bugs.breaks-play` | **11** | 1 | **10** |
| `production.craftsmanship.needs-more-work` | 6 | 0 | **6** |
| `engineering.stability.crashes-repeatedly` | 3 | 0 | 3 |
| `engineering.performance.unstable-framerate` | 3 | 1 | 2 |

**`engineering` is 15.0% of this game's bullets against 7.3% for the eight co-op games and 5.4% for
Immortal: Unchained** — the highest share of any game read. The complaints are specific and
repeated: a mission objective that sticks at 8 of 10, an evacuation helicopter that will not respond,
enemies that stop spawning, triggers that never fire.

> *"after the tutorial mission, you cannot progress any further due to: crashes, game not spawning
> any enemies, game not giving you any missions... there is no excuse for this after 4 years"*
> (`158283309`, 2024)

🔑 **Ten of the eleven `breaks-play` bullets are thumbs down. In Zombie Girl, twelve of fourteen
"too short" bullets were thumbs up.** A player forgives a small game for being small. **Nobody
forgives it for not working.**

## 2. "Cheap" is the whole case for it — again

`publishing.price.fair` is **11 bullets, every one thumbs up**, tied for the largest mode in the game.

> *"At $20 or more, I'd be giving it a negative review. But at $5? Yes, I can absolutely recommend
> it."* (`122276388`)
> *"its cheaper than the £7 they charge at the cinema for popcorn"* (`197864285`)

**Two block games in, the recommender's argument is the same sentence both times: it is not good,
it is cheap.** Zombie Girl's recommenders wrote 1.32 complaints each; ZCREW's write 1.16.

## 3. The early access arc, dated

| Period | Reviews | % up | What they say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020-12 launch into early access | 9 | 56% | *"an interesting idea and a good foundation, BUT... so barren"* |
| 2021 | 20 | 45% | `update-cadence.steady-stream` ×4, `early-access.grew-into-its-promise` ×3 — *"an example to All Early Access games"* (`87208986`) |
| 2022 (1.0 in September) | 30 | 53% | *"probably not totally finished despite it not saying early access on steam"* (`122526677`) |
| 2023–2026 | 18 | 44% | `breaks-play` ×7, `abandonment` ×3 — *"another forsaken child of the game industry"* (`230608709`) |

🔑 **The praise for the studio's update cadence is all from 2021. The bug reports are all from after
1.0.** `production.early-access.grew-into-its-promise` (3) and `.never-grew-into-its-promise` (1) both
appear — **the same game, judged a year apart.**

**The developer answered reviews.** `96754160` quotes the studio's November 2021 reply promising
*"full, intuitive controller support"*, then adds edits in September 2022, January 2024 and May 2024
saying it never came. **That is the third sighting of `replied-to-my-review` and the clearest case:
a reply on the store page is a promise the review then keeps score against.**

## 4. The comparison is always Helldivers

`explained-by-naming-other-games` 6 and `beaten-by-a-competitor` 4 — **ten of 77 reviews name
another game, and five of those name Helldivers.**

> *"Zcrew is trying to be some kind of Helldivers mixed with zombies. The problem is it never even
> comes close"* (`108187929`)
> *"It's a lot like a favorite game of mine called helldivers, but with all the soul/charm sucked out"*
> (`122276388`)

One names Kill Squad and calls ZCREW a reskin of it (`118117616`). One says *"nothing does what it
does"* (`122526677`). **As in Immortal: Unchained, the same feature reads as unique to one player and
derivative to the next.**
