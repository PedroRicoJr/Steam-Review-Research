## 1. The short version

**A game people love for what it is and complain about for how little of it there is.**

- **The most-said specific thing is the war cry** — *"For the Emperor"*, 310 bullets, 21.9 per 100
  read. One review in five is the slogan and nothing else.
- **The number one complaint is *too little content*, 84 bullets — and 66 of them come from people
  who recommend the game.** The median reviewer who says it has 17 hours.
- **The praise is the power fantasy** — `makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong`, 64, built for this game.
  Its inverse is 13, and 12 of those are thumbs-down.
- **The thumb never moved**: 88.5% in the launch month, 87.8% in 2026. What moved is the complaint
  list — crashes to zero, *buy it on sale* from 0.5 to 5.1 per 100.
- **The most-helpful review is a 632-vote thumbs-down about one faction** — the Chaos missions are
  a difficulty tier above the rest *"not by design but through clutter, misleading cues, flinching
  and stun-lock."*

## 5. ⭐ The finding: a flat thumb over a moving complaint list

**Praise and complaint per 100 readers, by period:**

| Period | Reviews | Thumbs up | Praise/100 | Complaint/100 | Mean hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024-09 (launch month) | 591 | 88.5% | 106.8 | **55.2** | 29 |
| 2024-10 to 12 | 330 | 87.0% | 122.1 | 61.8 | 55 |
| 2025-01 to 06 | 146 | 87.7% | 122.6 | 60.3 | 63 |
| 2025-07 to 12 | 155 | 85.2% | 113.5 | 61.3 | 47 |
| 2026-01 to 09 | 196 | 87.8% | **127.6** | **70.9** | 74 |

**The thumb sits inside three points for two years while both lines rise.** The 2026 reviewer says
more, good and bad, and lands in the same place. Under the flat line the complaints change subject:
`crashes-repeatedly` 3.2 → 0.0 per 100, `frequent-disconnects` peaks at 3.4 in the April 2025 update
and goes to 0.0, `buy-on-sale-only` 0.5 → 5.1, `grindy` 0.7 → 2.6. **The engineering complaints were
fixed and the price complaint replaced them.**

**The power fantasy is the split.** 64 bullets say the game makes you feel superhumanly strong — 63
of them from recommenders. 13 say it never makes you feel as strong as the fiction says — 12 from
non-recommenders. **A game built on a fantasy is reviewed on whether the fantasy held**, and the
mechanism that breaks it is always the same: a stun or a swarm that takes control away.

**The Chaos problem is one review and four modes.** `217297755` (632 helpful) says the Chaos missions
are a tier harder than the Tyranid ones through clutter, cues and stun-lock, so players avoid them and
the map pool halves. The sample files the same complaint on `one-part-is-far-harder-than-the-rest`
(4), `threats-unclear` (5), `stuns-take-control-away` (9) and `ranged-enemies-hit-you-from-anywhere-while-you-are-swarmed`
(5). No patch notes were pulled; of the 169 reviews from 2026-02 on, one mentions Chaos.

## 6. The open method questions this game raised

**None is answered here. All are Rico's.**

1. **Flattening.** 155 reviews (10.9%) carry a later edit and are counted on the created date. In
   this game the edits run both ways — *"got my cape"* and *"just awesome nowadays"* next to withdrawn
   launch complaints — so the direction of the error is unknown. Open since Helldivers 2.
2. **The friendly-fire subject merge.** One review names the absence of friendly fire as a plus
   (`220778360`, *"Helldivers without accidentally shooting your friends"*); left on
   `explained-by-naming-other-games` until the merge is decided.
3. **The missing `game-design.loot` subject (gap 331)** does not arise here — this game has no loot.
4. **The publisher-communication split** does not arise in the sample — reviewers name Saber, never
   Focus.
5. **The `accessibility.memory-and-attention` subject** Rico cleared on 2026-09-10 is at two sightings
   from this game (story lost during combat `189003013`, cluttered-screen tracking `196658368`); the
   third builds it.

## 7. What this page cannot tell you

1. **What the other languages think.** About 100,000 reviews unread; none pulled.
2. **Anything about a single month after 2024-12.** 21 of 25 months are under 40 reviews; the
   five-period table is the honest resolution.
3. **Whether the studio did anything.** No patch notes were pulled. The reviews name the ultrawide
   patch, the April 2025 *Trygon* update and its 2025-04-24 fix, the weapon and parry changes, and the
   2026 Siege mode; none is confirmed from the studio's side.
4. **Whether the Chaos problem was fixed or abandoned.** One Chaos mention in 169 reviews from 2026-02
   on is consistent with both; the tree records what the player felt, not the root cause.
5. **Whether the PvP is dead.** `a-mode-falls-flat` (15) is almost always the PvP, and one 2026 review
   says *"PvP dead"* (`224995971`); the sample cannot count a population.
