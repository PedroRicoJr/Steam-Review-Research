## 1. The short version

- **95.7% thumbs up, 38.9 complaints and 140.2 praise per 100 reviews; praise to complaint 3.6 : 1.**
  Between Gunfire Reborn (3.9 : 1) and Escape from Duckov (3.3 : 1) on the same count (`cross-game.md`
  section 23).
- **The camp is what is praised most.** `cheesy-on-purpose` 169 (9.6 per 100), used by no other game;
  `memorable-lines` 134 (7.6 per 100), **more than ten times any other game in the corpus.**
- **It is judged as a sequel, first to 4.1, then beside Helldivers 2 and EDF 6.**
  `successor-framing-accepted` 133; 125 reviews name 4.1, 36 name Helldivers (none thumbs down), 33
  name EDF 6.
- **The top complaint is the price, not the game.** `buy-on-sale-only` 60, `too-high-for-what-it-is`
  20; `looks-dated` 44 is next.
- **A host-run online co-op game with a list of online frictions.** Separate solo and online progress
  (7), gear caps online (4), no joining a mission in progress (4), uneven scaling by head count (2).
- **463 of the 684 complaint bullets (67.7%) come from reviews that recommend the game.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding: a game loved for being itself, criticised for its price and its online rules

**The praise is about identity**: the camp (169), the lines players quote (134), the classes (71), the
scale and the destruction (22 and 44). **Most of the complaints sit around the game**: the price (60 and
20), dated looks (44), and the sequel measured against 4.1 (16, 12 and 16). **The design complaints
that do sit inside the game are about how it is played online and how loot is collected.** Progress is
separate solo and online, gear is capped online, drops are picked up by hand. None is large alone. They
are the costs of a co-op game hosted by one player. They are also the part of this game closest to
Dominion's own design (`earth-defense-force-5-english.md` section 4).
