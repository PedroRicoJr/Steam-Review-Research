# The roguelike block — ten small games, read whole

**Ten failed or forgotten third-person games with 100 or more Steam reviews, every English review
read. 751 of 751. No margin of error on any of them.** Rico cleared the block on 2026-09-10 and it
was read the same day. Per-game pages exist for the eight games with more than forty reviews; the two
near-empty groups — VOIDCRISIS (17) and Town Of The Dead Life (1) — are reported only here.

**The block was chosen to answer one question:** can the tree say *why* a game with a hundred
reviews failed, when the answer is usually "nobody showed up" and not "the combat was bad"?

## 1. ⭐ The tree held, and the block proved it a different way than Immortal: Unchained did

**Ten games, 327 distinct modes, and four of them are used by the block and no other game.** Two of
the four were built during the block on one-game evidence (`the-content-is-visibly-censored`,
`the-checkpoint-is-single-use`); the other two existed already and happened to fire only here.

**Eight modes were built during the block. Six of them came from claims that were already in the
corpus, one sighting per game, sitting on catch-alls:**

| Mode | Sightings when built | Games | Where they had been parked |
|---|---|---|---|
| `build-and-customisation.the-class-decides-your-gender` | 4 | 2 | `cannot-change-how-you-look` |
| `review.discloses-a-free-copy-from-the-studio` | 3 | 3 | **never captured at all** |
| `achievements.do-not-track-what-you-actually-did` | 3 | 3 | `engineering.bugs.buggy`, `.harmless-and-funny` |
| `storefront.played-it-for-the-platform-reward` | 2 | 2 | `trading-cards.unknown` |
| `game-feel.combat.the-scenery-swallows-your-shots` | 6 | 3 | `breaks-play`, `shots-go-where-they-want` |
| `review.used-a-cheat-to-get-through-it` | 2 | 2 | `difficulty-tuning.too-hard` (gap 316) |

🔑 **Immortal: Unchained tested the tree by bringing a genre it had never seen. The block tested it
by bringing volume at the small end.** A claim that is one bullet in two thousand never tips in a
big sample; the same claim in a 77-review game read whole is the second sighting. **The small
censuses did not find new kinds of complaint. They supplied the second sighting for complaints the
big samples could not tip.**

## 2. ⭐ What the block is about: price, amount, and who made it

| Claim | Reviews | Share of 751 |
|---|---|---|
| `review.positive.unknown` — a thumbs up that says nothing | **82** | **10.9%** |
| `explained-by-naming-other-games` | 53 | 7.1% |
| **Price or amount** — `price.fair` 38, `too-high-for-what-it-is` 26, `buy-on-sale-only` 25, `content-amount.too-little` 32 | **118** | **15.7%** |
| `review.grades-it-against-the-studios-size` | 24 | 3.2% |
| `developer-communication.listens-and-acts` | 23 | 3.1% |

**One review in six is about what the game costs or how much of it there is, and one in nine says
nothing at all.** The block's recommenders write the same sentence in every game: *it is short, it is
janky, it is cheap, I liked it.* Seven FULL METAL SCHOOLGIRL reviewers name the bundle price they
paid; Banzai Escape's five "too short" bullets are all thumbs up; Zombie Girl's fourteen `price.fair`
bullets are all thumbs up.

🔑 **The ruler is the studio.** 24 reviews say outright they are grading against a one- or two-person
team, and another 12 are addressed to the studio rather than to a buyer. **In the nine big games,
`grades-it-against-the-studios-size` fired on 0.7% of reviews. In the block it fires on 3.2%.**

## 3. ⭐ The arc: praised for listening in year one, called abandoned by year three

| Claim | Block reviews | Years |
|---|---|---|
| `developer-communication.listens-and-acts` | 23 | **2018–2023 — every one in the game's launch window** |
| `live-ops.abandonment.updates-stopped` | 16 | **2021–2026 — every one a year or more after launch** |
| `live-ops.abandonment.still-supported` | 3 | Alien Dawn ×2, Zombie Girl ×1 |

| Game | "the devs listen" | "the devs left" | Still supported |
|---|---|---|---|
| ArcRunner | 10 | 9 | — |
| Zombie Girl | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| ZCREW | 1 | 2 | — |
| Die After Sunset | 1 | 1 | — |
| VOIDCRISIS | — | 2 (*"not even three months in early access and they abandon ship"*) | — |
| **Alien Dawn** | **7** | **0** | **2** |

> *"the devs really listen to the community, insane the speed they're putting patches out"* — ArcRunner,
> May 2023. *"absolute GEM that the devs seemed to have abandoned"* — the same review, edited January
> 2024.

🔑 **Five of the nine real block games turned from "the developers listen" to "the developers left"
inside two years, and the review that praised the responsiveness is often the one that later records
the abandonment.** A small studio's launch-window responsiveness is the most-praised thing about it
and the least predictive. **Alien Dawn is the exception and its reviews say so in 2025.**

⚠️ The ten-year Banzai Escape is the other exception, in the other direction: never praised for
updates, never called abandoned, and the same ammo-economy complaint in every year from 2016 to 2026.

## 4. Two games whose review pool is a fight about fan service, not about play

|---|---|---|
| The argument | the store's *Nudity / NSFW* tags lied | the black shadow under the skirt is censorship |
| Reviews making the claim | 12 (9 thumbs down) | 12 (9 thumbs down) |
| Reviews rejecting it | 8 (8 thumbs up) | 11 (11 thumbs up) |
| Share of the game's helpful votes on the claim | — | **52%** |

**Nineteen of Zombie Girl's 89 reviews and 22 of FULL METAL SCHOOLGIRL's 116 are this argument, and in
both games the two sides split the thumb almost perfectly.** Strip them out and the rest are 77.1%
and 72.3% positive, with little to fight about. **The tree records both positions and cannot settle either.**

## 5. What the block never touched — and this time it is not the genre

**24 subjects the other nine games use and the block never did.** The top five:

| Subject | Bullets in the other nine | Bullets in the block |
|---|---|---|
| `community.culture` | **1,325** | 0 |
| `community.player-conduct` | 315 | 0 |
| `marketing.discovery` | 235 | 0 |
| `community.social-features` | 181 | 0 |
| `game-design.game-feel.reward-moment` | 82 | 0 |

🔑 **Immortal: Unchained never touched `community` because it was single-player. Four of these ten
games are co-op - ZCREW, ArcRunner, Alien Dawn, VOIDCRISIS - and they never touched `community.culture` either.** A game with 80 players has no
shared ritual, no in-joke, no conduct to complain about. **Culture needs a population. The division is
empty in the block not because the tree cannot describe these games but because there was nobody
there to describe.** That is the answer to the question the block was chosen for: **the tree can say
a game failed for lack of players, by saying nothing in the places where players would be.**

| 320 | meta-progression makes the early floors trivial and nothing lets you skip them | 2, one game | expect it in another roguelite |

| 322 | the AI teammates cost in-game currency each run | 3, one game | one game |

**Closed:** gap 316 (`review.used-a-cheat-to-get-through-it`) on its second sighting.

**Three questions raised by this block, all answered by Rico on 2026-09-10:**

1. **The `accessibility` division had no subject for memory or mental load.** Rico cleared it and it
   was built the same day: `accessibility.memory-and-attention`, one negative mode
   (`.relies-on-remembering-with-no-text`, two sightings, two games) and `.unknown`. The positive side
   is gap 323 on one sighting.
2. **The tree cannot say "these four complaints are one defect"** — Banzai Escape's mobile port sits
   under four subjects. **Rico's ruling: that is as designed.** The tree records what the player felt;
   working out that four feelings share one cause is the developer's job, done in house. A findings page
   may point it out, as the Banzai Escape page does; the tags do not.
3. **`developer-communication` is the only `community` subject that survives a game with no players.**
   **Rico's ruling: it stays where it is.** The studio is talking to the community; that is a community
   matter whether or not anyone else is in the room.

## Confidence and limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Margin of error | **None on any game.** 751 of 751, checked against Steam's own English totals. Two games (Banzai Escape, SCP: Abhorrent) have gained one review each since the pull. |
| The grid defect | Three games were first measured from their 1.0 date and missed early-access reviews — ZCREW 35, Die After Sunset 40, VOIDCRISIS 11. Fixed and re-pulled before any of the three was read. The nine finished games were checked the same way and are whole. |
| Other languages | 0 of 1,849 non-English reviews across the ten. Zombie Girl is the outlier: English is 9% of its audience and 22 points more positive than the rest. |
| Reviews not in English, labelled `english` by Steam | 9 across the block, all read from content |
| Invalid tags | **0** · Unfitted | **0** · Direction audit | 0 disagreements across 32,000+ bullets |
