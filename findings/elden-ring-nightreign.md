## 1. The short version

**It is judged as a co-op game first.** "Much better with friends" is the top named praise (125
bullets, 9.6 per 100 reviews), and players stay a long time: the median review shows 43 hours.

**The complaints are about the link between players, not the fighting.** No way to talk to the team
leads (36), then the missing two-player mode at launch (26, gone once the studio added it on
2025-07-31), connection drops (22) and bad strangers (21).

**The fighting is what they praise.** Hard in a good way (61), the pull of one more run (60), the
bosses (47) and characters that each play their own way (38).

**It carries Elden Ring's weight both ways.** 28 call it reused Elden Ring content — the highest rate
in the corpus — and 16 say it assumes you played Elden Ring; 16 others say it is made for those fans
and better for it.

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**In a co-op game built on runs, the connection between players is the product.** The fighting draws
praise; the complaints gather around the link between players. Players could not talk (36; 2.8 per
100, second only to Aliens: Fireteam Elite in the corpus), could not play as a pair until patch 1.02
(26 asks in two months, none after), lost 30-45 minute runs to drops (22 drops, 8 runs lost, penalties
for crashes and for leaving after strangers quit), and could not join friends on other platforms (15,
the highest rate in the corpus). Our reading for Dominion: build voice chat, every group size from one
to four, rejoin after a drop and fair leave rules in from the start. They are core features, not
something to add later.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- **±3.18% on whole-sample shares**; periods ±3.4 to ±10.1. Most complaint counts are under 30.
- **The launch month is read at 0.38% and weighted ×264** (the game came out on the 29th; Rico's ruling
  of 2026-09-04 reads short launch months as pulled). The two launch months are 65.4% of all reviews.
- **63% of reviews are 15 words or fewer**; a quarter of bullets name nothing.
- **Not checked:** the leave-penalty rules (reviewers disagree), "about 5% new content", solo boss health,
  a forum report of save corruption, cheaters on VPNs.
- **English only.** About 94,700 reviews in other languages; none pulled.
