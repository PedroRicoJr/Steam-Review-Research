## 1. The short version

**It is judged against the trilogy before anything else: 213 notes say it falls short of the earlier
games, 187 that it was judged unfairly, 55 that it lives up to them.**

**The combat is what people keep: 127 notes praise it, and 75 the freedom to mix powers and builds.**

**The writing is what people leave over: of 461 thumbs down, the most common named reasons are falling
short of the trilogy (88), flat characters and dialogue (81), the EA app (51) and a thin story (45);
79 notes say the choices change nothing.**

**A game bought on sale: 110 notes say buy it only on sale, against 13 that call the price fair.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**Great moment-to-moment combat does not save a game whose fans expected something else, and a co-op
mode bolted onto a story game is barely noticed.** Andromeda's combat is praised by people who dislike
everything around it, yet the thumb falls from 82% at the Steam launch to about two in three from 2023,
and reviewers keep scoring it against three older games. Its four-player wave mode, the part closest to
Dominion, is mentioned in 56 of 1,585 reviews, and by 2024-2025 some say they find nobody to play with.
What players name instead is what Dominion can avoid: errands that pad the hours, travel scenes that
cannot be skipped, a launcher between them and the game, and faces that looked wrong on day one.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- 1,585 of 11,214 reviews, ±2.51%; periods ±4.8% to ±13.1%; 2020-06 is read at ×13.
- The notes per review changed during the read (about 3.0, 2.3, then 3.5 by batch), so per-100 changes
  across periods are not read as changes in the players.
- EA's launcher change, the last patch date, peer-to-peer hosting and the multiplayer's population are
  the reviewers' account, not checked.
- 508 of 1,585 reviews are 15 words or fewer.
