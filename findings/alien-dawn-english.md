# Alien Dawn — English

**A census: all 74 English reviews read. No margin of error.** Every English review on the game was
read, so there is no sample and no margin. Steam's own English count is 47 up and 27 down; so is ours.
Figures are as of 2026-09-10; the tables in section 2 were re-run on 2026-10-03.

**A two-person VR-and-desktop co-op open-world survival shooter, in early access since 2021-10 at
about $15, still in early access.** Crash-land, loot farmhouses, avoid the mothership, reach the
helicopter. VR players and flat-screen players share a server. One reviewer describes a change of
engine; the engines are not named on this page, nor the hosting model or the flat-screen camera (one
reviewer lists ten-player multiplayer). Steam, all languages: 100 reviews, 64 up, 64.0% — English is
74%. It was read as the seventh of the ten-game block. **It is fairly close to Dominion in shape:
co-op, sci-fi (aliens), PvE, a session that ends by reaching a helicopter to leave (close to
extraction).** It differs in being open world, survival, VR-first and not built on runs.

## 1. In plain words

**What decides how players feel is the two developers.** 21 of the 74 reviews say something about
them, and 20 of those 21 are warm. 7 say the developers listen and act, and 6 say they bought the
game to fund the studio.

**It is the one game in the block that players did not call abandoned.** Two reviews say it is still
supported — one in 2022 and one edited in 2025 — and none says the studio left.

**VR is the fault line.** 10 times a reviewer said the VR handling of guns, holsters and menus does
not respond well, and 8 times that the game did not work on their VR setup — 7 of those 8 voted thumbs
down.

**Playing VR and flat-screen players together is loved.** 5 times a reviewer praised it, all thumbs
up, and 6 said it is much better with friends.

**But finding other players is hard.** 5 times a reviewer said nobody is playing, so there are no
groups to join.

**It got less liked over time.** 80.0% of the 2021 reviews are thumbs up, 57.9% in 2022 and 45.5% of
the 11 reviews since.

**The surprise: praise and complaint are almost even, and the praise is for the people, not the game.**
That makes the game look better than larger games on paper; it is a smaller game with a closer studio.

🔑 **Twenty-one of the 74 reviews say something about the two developers, and twenty of the

and the review pool says so.** `findings/cross-game.md` section 15: *"Alien Dawn is the exception, and
its 2025 edits say so."*

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **A small studio that talks to players, by name, earns praise that outweighs rough edges.**
   Evidence: 21 of 74 reviews mention the two developers, 20 warmly; 7 say they listen and act; 3 name
   them; 6 bought to fund them.
   Dominion could: be present in a Discord, invite unhappy players to talk, play with the community,
   and let players know who the team is.
   Strength: medium.
2. **Keeping updates going is the difference from the rest of the block.**
   Evidence: 0 abandonment comments here against 9 in ArcRunner; "still supported" in 2022 and 2025.
   Dominion could: plan a long, steady update rhythm rather than a launch burst.
   Strength: medium (read with the block).
3. **Letting players on different setups play together is loved; a broken setup is fatal.**
   Evidence: crossplay of VR and normal screens praised 5 times (all thumbs up); setup failures 8
   times (7 thumbs down).
   Dominion could: if it lets mixed inputs or platforms play together, test every combination; a
   player whose setup does not launch writes a thumbs down.
   Strength: medium.
4. **Open spaces need landmarks.**
   Evidence: 4 times it was said; the same complaint about empty spaces came up for ArcRunner's arenas
   and Zombie Girl's floors.
   Dominion could: give every arena and extraction map tall, unique landmarks to find your way and
   call out positions to teammates.
   Strength: weak to medium (small counts, three games).
5. **Players will pay to support a studio they trust, but say so in the thumb.**
   Evidence: 6 bought to support; one gave a thumbs down only because there is no middle option.
   Dominion could: be honest about early access state on the store page so supporters are not
   reviewing a promise.
   Strength: weak.
