> *"invite those who have negative experiences to the discord to chat about where the problems lie"*
> (`101695010`)

> review"* (`101584057`)

> thumbs down — *"only because Steam has no sideways thumbs"*)

🔑 **Twenty-one of the 74 reviews say something about the two developers, and twenty of the
twenty-one are warm** - the one exception reports a support request that went unanswered. Six say outright that they bought the game to fund it. **No other game in the corpus has the
studio relationship as its largest praise cluster** — the co-op games praised the loop, Immortal:
Unchained praised the levels, this one praises the people.

**And it is the one block game that was not abandoned.** `abandonment.still-supported` fires in
2022 (*"Devs are NOT abandoning this game"*) and again in a 2025 edit (*"the new update I played for the
first time tonight was proof the devs have been doing their jobs"*). Zombie Girl, ZCREW and ArcRunner
each turned from *"the devs listen"* to *"the devs left"* inside two years. **This one did not turn,
and the review pool says so.**

**Seven of the eight platform failures are thumbs down; all five crossplay bullets are thumbs up.**
The same feature — a VR game that desktop players can join — is the reason to buy and the reason it
does not work.

> *"The fact that you can play with friends that don't have VR is amazing."* (`101852717`)
> *"Wouldn't buy in its current state if you intend on playing VR."* (`103293267`)

## 3. The world is the design complaint, and it is the same one ArcRunner got

`game-design.level-design.no-memorable-moments` — 4 bullets. *"there are no unique landmarks that
are important in open world games for pathfinding... Everything is designed on a flat axis plane"*
(`104979629`). ArcRunner's reviewers said the same of its arenas; Zombie Girl's said it of its
procedural floors. **Three block games, one complaint: the space has nothing in it to remember.**

---

## 4. What the tree learned

**67 distinct modes, 0 unique, 0 built.** A VR game — the first in the corpus — needed nothing new:
its VR complaints landed on `controls.unresponsive`, `platform-support.broken-on-my-platform` and
`vision.causes-motion-sickness`, all of which existed. `crossplay-and-platform-mix.works-well` had
never been used for VR-versus-flat before, and fit without a word changed.

**The praise-to-complaint ratio of 1.03 is real, unlike SCP: Abhorrent's.** The praise is unironic
and it is mostly about the developers. **A reader using the ratio as a quality signal would rank this
above Back 4 Blood (0.86). It is not a better game; it is a smaller game with a closer studio.**
