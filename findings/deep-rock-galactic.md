## 1. The short version

**Almost nobody dislikes this game, and most of what they praise is not the game.** 97.1% thumbs up:
61 thumbs down in 2,130 reviews. Good remarks outnumber bad ones 7 to 1 (3,744 against 515).

**The top four praises are all about the people.** The catchphrase, playing with friends, the name
players give themselves, and how kind strangers are. **The first thing about the game itself comes
fifth.**

**The whole complaint list is small enough to read.** The single biggest complaint — it gets
repetitive — is **29 remarks in 2,133 reviews**, 1.4 per 100 readers. The top complaints are about
running out of the game, mostly when you have nobody to play with.

**14.4% of remarks raise a subject and never say what about it.**

**The scale gap is the point.** The top praise has **610 bullets**; the top complaint has **29**.
That is **21 to 1**, on the single most-said thing in each direction.

**`shared-ritual` alone is 13.5% of everything written about this game.** The catchphrase has more

**Rows 6 and 19 are the two nobody would design on purpose.**
`useless-actions-players-love` counts players praising the emotes, the beer, the barrel, the dance —
**actions with no mechanical effect at all, 111 bullets.** `fine-with-strangers` counts people saying
matchmaking with strangers is *good* — **41 bullets against 4 saying it is bad, 10 to 1.** Helldivers
runs 14 to 9. Back 4 Blood runs 8 to 30, the other way round. **Only this game's players like the
people the game gives them.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding: the complaints are what the praise costs

**Put the two lists side by side and four pairs sit on the same axis, pointing opposite ways.**

| The complaint | Count | The praise it is the cost of | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| `content-variety.repetitive` | 29 | `procedurally-varied` + `varied-runs` | **115** |
| `solo-viability.punishing-solo` | 27 | `solo-viability.works-solo` | **79** |
| `playing-with-friends.needs-a-group` | 22 | `much-better-with-friends` | **215** |
| `progression.unlock-pace.grindy` | 15 | `satisfying-progression` | **40** |

**Each pair is one design decision, seen from both ends.** The game is built to be played with a
group, on procedurally built maps, with a long unlock road. **The people who like that say so 449
times. The people who do not say so 93 times.**

**This is what a healthy criticism profile looks like:** the complaints name the deliberate
trade-offs, not defects. **Nothing in Deep Rock's top 25 complaints is an accident.**

In plain words: the game asks you to play with a group, on maps that repeat in shape, with a long road
of unlocks. Most players love exactly that. The few who do not are the ones who play alone or run out
of new things — and they say so far less often than the fans say the opposite.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

1. **What four of the five languages think.** 112,943 reviews on Steam, unread.
2. **Whether the 2025–26 rise is real or a sampling wobble.** 443 reviews across two years, against
   a group of 215,564. The margin of error is not counted on this page.
3. **Why complaint bottomed out in 2023.** The patch record is in the repo; nothing here links a
   patch to a count. The patch record reaches back only to 2024-03.
4. **What the reviewers actually wrote.** These are tags. Pulling the raw text behind the top
   entries is a separate pass — see plan §7, "The two-stage read".
5. **Claims not checked:** anything a reviewer says about prices, events or the studio.
6. **Small mismatches:** thumbs up is 2,072 of 2,133 on one count and 2,069 of 2,130 on the other (both
   97.1%); the complaint list above leaves out the 17 wordless thumbs-down that the English page lists
   fifth.
