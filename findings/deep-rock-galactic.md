⚠️ **This page reads one language.** Four groups covering **112,943 reviews on Steam** — Russian
(65,935), Simplified Chinese (36,562), LatAm Spanish (2,138), European Spanish (8,308) — were pulled
and never read. **Nothing here speaks for them.** Because the sampling rate is the same for every
row on this page, the ranked order is the raw order — no weighting was needed.

---

## 1. The short version

**Almost nobody dislikes this game, and most of what they praise is not the game.**

- **97.1% thumbs up.** 61 thumbs down in 2,130 tagged reviews.
- **3,744 positive bullets against 515 negative — 7 to 1.**
- **The top four praises are all `community`.** The catchphrase, playing with friends, the identity
  players adopt, and how strangers behave. **The first thing about the game itself is fifth.**
- **The whole complaint list is small enough to read.** The single biggest complaint has **29
  bullets in 2,133 reviews** — 1.4 per 100 readers.
- **14.4% of bullets are `unknown`** — a subject raised and never finished.

**The scale gap is the point.** The top praise has **610 bullets**; the top complaint has **29**.
That is **21 to 1**, on the single most-said thing in each direction.

**`shared-ritual` alone is 13.5% of everything written about this game.** The catchphrase has more
bullets than combat (42), the whole `art` division (133), price (65), progression (267) and
difficulty (92) **added together — 610 against 599.**

**Rows 6 and 19 are the two nobody would design on purpose.**
`useless-actions-players-love` counts players praising the emotes, the beer, the barrel, the dance —
**actions with no mechanical effect at all, 111 bullets.** `fine-with-strangers` counts people saying
matchmaking with strangers is *good* — **41 bullets against 4 saying it is bad, 10 to 1.** Helldivers
runs 14 to 9. Back 4 Blood runs 8 to 30, the other way round. **Only this game's players like the
people the game gives them.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding: the complaints are what the praise costs

**Put the two lists side by side and four pairs sit on the same axis, pointing opposite ways.**

| The complaint | Count | The praise it is the cost of | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| `content-variety.repetitive` | 29 | `procedurally-varied` + `varied-runs` | **115** |
| `solo-viability.punishing-solo` | 27 | `solo-viability.works-solo` | **79** |
| `playing-with-friends.needs-a-group` | 22 | `much-better-with-friends` | **215** |
| `progression.unlock-pace.grindy` | 15 | `satisfying-progression` | **40** |

**Each pair is one design decision, seen from both ends.** The game is built to be played with a
group, on procedurally built maps, with a long unlock road. **The people who like that say so 449
times. The people who do not say so 93 times.**

**This is what a healthy criticism profile looks like:** the complaints name the deliberate
trade-offs, not defects. **Nothing in Deep Rock's top 25 complaints is an accident.**

## 6. Year by year

| Year | Reviews | Thumbs down | Bullets/review | Praise per 100 | Complaint per 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2018 | 146 | 4.1% | 2.51 | 174.7 | **55.5** |
| 2019 | 197 | 4.6% | **3.03** | **234.0** | 43.7 |
| 2020 | 258 | 1.9% | 2.49 | 210.5 | 26.4 |
| 2021 | 252 | 2.4% | 2.08 | 176.2 | 21.4 |
| 2022 | 284 | 2.5% | 1.97 | 168.7 | 19.7 |
| 2023 | 290 | **1.7%** | 1.72 | 153.8 | **10.3** |
| 2024 | 260 | 2.7% | 1.65 | 148.5 | 11.9 |
| 2025 | 266 | 3.4% | 1.94 | 164.7 | 22.9 |
| 2026 | 177 | 4.0% | 2.12 | 165.0 | 27.1 |

**Complaint falls 81% from 2018 to 2023** — 55.5 per 100 down to 10.3 — and then **rises again, to
27.1 in 2026.** The 2018 figure is an Early Access game; the 2023 figure is five years of shipping.

**The 2025–26 rise is the one to watch.** Thumbs down is still under 5%, but complaint per reader has
more than doubled from its floor. **This corpus does not say why**, and the last two years are the
thinnest part of the sample.
