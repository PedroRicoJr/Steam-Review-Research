# Escape from Duckov — English

**A sample: 1,166 of 11,004 English reviews read, ±2.83% on any whole-sample share.** The pull
(2026-09-26) found 11,004 English reviews across 12 months; Steam's own English count on 2026-09-27
was 11,007. Figures are as of 2026-09-27.

**A single-player, top-down PvE extraction shooter, on Steam since 2025-10** — a comic take on Escape
from Tarkov with ducks. You go out on a run, gather loot and must get out alive; if you die you lose your
gear, with one chance to go back and pick it up. Areas the reviews name include Farm Town and the Labs
(late game). Difficulty can be set in detail, including whether you lose all your gear on death. There
is a character creator and a Steam Workshop for mods; there is no co-op except through mods. Paid game.
The reviews name the studio as Team Soda and the publisher as Bilibili (no store data was pulled to
confirm it); the pages do not name the engine. 104,492 reviews in all languages on 2026-09-27, 84%
positive, *Very Positive*. **It is A1 on `planning/action-roguelike-list.md`, the closest match to
Dominion on either list, picked by the active loop under Rico's standing order of 2026-09-25.** It has
runs that start and end, loot you can lose, and extraction — but alone and against the game, not against
players. It does not share co-op, the camera (top-down, not third person), hosting (there is nothing to
host), or the sci-fi arena side.

## 1. In plain words

**Players judge it against Tarkov, and it wins.** **135 of 1,160 reviews name Tarkov**, and only 6 of
the 135 are thumbs down. Saying it beats its rivals was said **71** times (the third most common thing
said), and explaining it by naming other games **69** more. *"Very fun game that takes out the worst part
of Tarkov, which is the cheaters"* (216039510, 100% completed).

**Taking the other players out is the feature, not a gap.** "No fighting other players is a plus" was
said **51 times, every one under a thumbs up**; **16** people who normally avoid this kind of game say it
won them over, all thumbs up. *"i have never played an extraction shooter, mainly because i just dont
enjoy PVP."* (211985235, 100 hours).

**The duck is read as a joke that turns out to be a real game.** That was said **39 times, all thumbs
up**, and of no other game when it was read. *"Don't let the silly presentation fool you into thinking
this is a joke game."* (212777542). A curator's review with **127 found it helpful** ends *"Just
because it’s with ducks, don’t expect it to be any more forgiving!"* (214029794).

**The asks are co-op and a controller.** A missing mode is the top complaint, **39** times; 36 of the 39
are under a thumbs up, 6 mention fighting other players, and the rest ask for co-op. No controller
support is **33, and 12 of those 33 are thumbs down** — the one gap that costs votes. *"not having
official controller support at this point is criminal"* (228527236).

**Most complaints come from people who still like it.** **255 of the 469 complaints (54.4%) come from
reviews that recommend the game**, and every complaint is small: the top one is 3.4 per 100 reviews.
The 105 thumbs-down reviews show a median of 9 hours played, against 19 for all reviews.

**The one sharp drop is not about the game.** The thumb falls from **96.7% in 2025-11 to 74.4% in
2025-12**, when a fight over a workshop mod broke (section 3.2). **13 of December's 34 thumbs down are
about it.**

**Where the game itself loses people is the end.** The late game turning into a grind was said 14 times,
and the part you need never dropping 23 times (in 21 reviews); both peak after the launch months
(section 3.1).

**The shape is the same in almost every one: the early and middle game praised, then the last area
(the Labs, or Farm Town before them) where keys and rare parts must be found by chance.** *"once you get
to Jlabs, it becomes considerably less fun and more of a grind"* (212074993, 98 hours, up). *"Early to
mid game is fantastic!"* — then the end game (212801415, 94 hours, down). *"Duckov absolutely tanks the
endgame"* (217761449, 84 hours, down, 12 found it helpful). A 100%-completion review at 171 hours calls
some things *"a MASSIVE grind for little reward"* (212775495).

**The complaint is about chance, not length.** The single-player setting makes the random drop harder to
accept: nobody else can trade it to you, and the only fix is another run of the same route. The part you
need never dropping is **2.0 per 100 here, the highest in the corpus** (Remnant II 1.7, Warframe 1.2,
Risk of Rain 2 0.3; `cross-game.md` section 21). Round 491 built three modes from one end-game review
alone (212801415): a single chance to recover your gear, an area that needs gear it never mentions, and
enemies whose ammo is too random to prepare for.

**The corpse run is praised and complained about.** The one-grave rule is five complaints; the quick
recovery that keeps the flow is praised 8 times and stakes worth the risk 33. Players like that dying
costs something and resent losing everything twice.

**Checked against a primary source:** nothing; no patch notes were pulled.

## 3.2 ⭐ The mod dispute: a month of protest votes

**28 reviews take part in the workshop dispute; 17 of them are thumbs down, and 13 of those 17 were
written in 2025-12.** The reviews say a popular workshop mod hid code that closed the game for players
on its author's blacklist, and that the studio asked the author to remove it rather than punishing
them. **This page records the claims, not a verified incident.**

| Mode | | Bullets |
|---|---|---|
| `community.moderation.went-easy-on-a-harmful-mod-author` | − | 16 |
| `community.user-created-content.mods-said-to-carry-malware` | − | 8 |
| `review.thumb-is-a-protest-vote` | − | 7 |
| `review.the-controversy-did-not-change-my-verdict` | ~ | 8 |
| `review.calls-the-negative-reviews-a-review-bomb` | ~ | 2 |

**10 of the 28 defend the game or tell readers to ignore the dispute** — mostly players who never used
mods: *"I don't know what the downvotes are about. I play the vanilla game, and I enjoy it."* (215970764,
40 found it helpful). The dispute review with the most hours is a protest at 233 hours: *"the publisher
allowing a malicious mod to remain hosted on Steam despite multiple reports is unacceptable."*
(213339351, 17 found it helpful).

**The same statement was pasted at least four times** (213987337, 217133568, 226071171, 231744444),
from 2025-12 to 2026-07. A second, smaller dispute — a collaboration with Tarkov's makers — brought 2
thumbs down on 2026-01-31.

**For the tree this is a new kind of complaint**: not the mod, but how the studio handled the mod's
author. It is the sixth most common complaint and existed only in this game when it was read. **The
monthly thumb needs the round notes beside it**; a dip is not a verdict on the game until the bullets say
so (`cross-game.md` section 21).

**Checked against a primary source:** nothing. Everything about the mod, the studio's handling and the
collaboration comes from the reviews.

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play.** **It is single-player**, with no co-op except through mods, so nothing is
  said about co-op play itself. **Alone works, but co-op is the wish**: it works alone 15 and no
  fighting other players is a plus 51, against a missing mode 39. The co-op request comes from people who
  like the game: 36 of the 39 are thumbs up. *"Now if only it had co-op it would be perfect."*
  (212024443). One review wants a companion in the solo game. What solo costs: with nobody to trade with,
  a missing rare part can only be found by running the same route again. Hosting, joining, dropping out,
  scaling, bots, friendly fire, matchmaking, servers, lag: **nothing said** — the game has none.
- **Combat and feel.** Combat hits hard 27 (3.1 per 100 at launch, 0.8 later). Enemy ammo is too random
  to prepare for, from one end-game review (212801415). Single bullets on the stamina running out too
  fast and the scenery swallowing shots (tag grep).
- **Movement and controls.** **Controllers are the one technical gap people leave over.** 12 of the 33
  reviews asking for controller support are thumbs down; 9 of the 33 mention the Steam Deck. A workshop
  mod adds it, and some reviews point to it. Controls responsive and clear 4; single bullets on no
  rebinding, an awkward default layout, one button doing too much, and a dodge roll that locks you in.
  Slow movement 4. Camera and sight: the narrow view is a handicap 6; the game draws only what your
  character can see, and 4 complain that this hides enemies (2 call the system clever); no choice of
  view 4; the camera works well 3, gets in the way 3.
- **Enemies, bosses and difficulty.** **Difficulty is a dial, and the dial is praised.** Satisfyingly hard
  49, well graded 29, difficulty can be tuned in detail 6, a game to unwind to 48. Too easy and too hard
  are 2 each. The reviews describe choosing between a relaxed loot run and a punishing one, and some turn
  full gear loss on or off to suit themselves (212777542). One review (214751423) complains the dial is
  not fine enough: full gear loss comes only with higher enemy damage. One part is far harder than the rest
  5; each death makes the next try harder (a stronger enemy appears) 5; an area needs gear it never tells
  you about (212801415). The bosses are a highlight for 10 (tag grep); one thumbs-down review says it
  stutters in every Farm Town boss fight (232131285). Enemy behaviour: poor 4, good 3, always knows where
  you are 2.
- **Progression, loot and randomness.** Satisfying progress 61; deep and varied builds 19; the character
  creator is fun 16. **Loss is liked when it can be recovered.** *"Loot runs are stressful in a good way,
  deaths hurt, and progression feels earned."* (215425450). Stakes worth the risk 33; quick recovery keeps
  the flow 8. Against: one chance to recover your gear 5; dying costs too much 7; each death makes the
  next try harder 5. The recovery run is part of the tension; losing it is where the complaints start. The
  end game rests on chance (section 3.1): the part you need may never drop 23, the late game turns into a
  grind 14, grindy 13. Real-world timers hold progress back 5. Quests force a set weapon or method 9.
  Inventory is a chore 18; missing conveniences 9; the game hides information you need 7.
- **Runs, content and replay.** Keeps pulling you back 98; plenty of content 48 (strongest at launch);
  good in short sittings 18; a game to relax to 48; "I want more because it is good" 19. Repetitive 8; a
  warning that it takes over your life 7. Mods extend the game 36, but 6 say the studio leans on mods to
  fill gaps, and 6 that most mods are in another language. Run length is not counted on this page.
- **Money and price.** The price is fair 68 (5.9 per 100), falling from 8.2 per 100 at launch to 2.4 in
  2026-04 to 09. Too high for what it is 4 (tag grep). A collaboration with Tarkov's makers drew 2 thumbs
  down on 2026-01-31 (the reviewers say). **Nothing said** about DLC, a shop or a season pass.
- **Tech.** Engineering is 2.4% of everything said (53 bullets). Runs well 7, bugs rare and minor 6,
  against stutter 4 — few but severe (232131285). From the tag grep: runs well on my platform 8, broken on
  my platform 3, runs poorly on my platform 2, crashes repeatedly 2, plays offline 2. One reviewer says
  their offline play is not counted in their Steam hours. The engine is not named.
- **The studio and the community.** Updates and live service: 6 bullets in all (steady stream 3, a patch
  made it better 2, fixes within hours 1) — the game is under a year old. The mod dispute (section 3.2)
  is the community story: 28 reviews. **The joke carries the marketing**: looks like a joke, plays like a
  real game 39, the satire lands 31, the character creator 16. Only one review (217753377) says the duck
  theme wore thin. Music fits the game 13.
- **What players asked for.** Co-op — most of the 39 missing-mode requests (6 of the 39 mention fighting
  other players); controller support 33 (9 mention the Steam Deck); better inventory management 18;
  basic conveniences 9; show the information needed 7; less chance in the end game (the part you need
  never drops 23, late-game grind 14); more than one chance to get gear back 5; less harsh restarts 7;
  quests that let you play your own way 9; a wider view 6, and the line-of-sight view to show more 4; a
  finer difficulty dial, with gear loss separate from enemy damage, 1 (214751423); mods in English 6;
  firmer action against harmful mod authors 16; more content, 19 times as praise ("I want more"); a
  companion in the solo game 1.

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

This is the nearest game in the queue so far — solo PvE extraction with runs that end. The lines that
carry over: **taking PvP out is praised as a feature by 51 reviewers and wins over people who avoid the
genre; a difficulty dial lets one game be both relaxing and hard; the corpse run is liked until it
becomes one-try-only; the end game must not rest on a random drop; and co-op is the first thing fans ask
for.** The lessons below expand on each.

1. **PvE-only extraction is a selling point, not a gap - say so loudly.** Evidence: no PvP praised as a
   feature 51 times, all thumbs up (no other game reaches 1 per 100); 16 people who avoid the genre were
   won over, all thumbs up; *"i have never played an extraction shooter, mainly because i just dont enjoy
   PVP."* (211985235). Dominion could: put "no PvP, no cheaters" in its pitch, and aim at players who like
   extraction tension but not other players. Strength: **strong**.
2. **Co-op is the first thing fans of PvE extraction want - Dominion's core is the answer.** Evidence: the
   top complaint, 39 times, 36 of them thumbs up, most asking for co-op; in ARC Raiders, 42 of 55
   missing-mode requests ask for PvE (cross-game). Dominion could: treat four-player co-op extraction as
   the headline, and still keep solo working well, since 15 praise this game as a solo game. Strength:
   **strong**.
3. **A difficulty dial lets one game be both relaxing and hard.** Evidence: hard in a good way 49, a game
   to relax to 48, well-spaced levels 29, tuned in detail 6; too easy and too hard only 2 each; some
   players turn full gear loss on or off (212777542). But one player wanted gear loss separate from enemy
   damage (214751423). Dominion could: offer separate settings for enemy strength and for how much you
   lose on death, chosen by the host for the group. Strength: **strong**.
4. **Losing gear is liked when you can get it back; losing it twice is not.** Evidence: stakes worth the
   risk 33; quick recovery keeps the flow 8; *"Loot runs are stressful in a good way, deaths hurt, and
   progression feels earned."* (215425450). Against: only one chance to recover gear 5, dying costs too
   much 7 (rising late to 2.4 per 100), each death makes the next try harder 5. Dominion could: give a
   recovery run, and think about letting teammates recover a fallen player's gear, so the loss is shared
   and not total. Strength: **medium**.
5. **The end game must not rest on a random drop.** Evidence: the part you need may never drop 23 times in
   21 reviews (the highest rate in the corpus), rising to 7.1 per 100 in 2026-01 to 03; late-game grind
   14; grindy 13; *"Duckov absolutely tanks the endgame"* (217761449, 12 helpful). The complaint is about
   chance, not length; solo play makes it worse because nobody can trade the part. Dominion could: add a
   sure path to key items (crafting from parts, a bad-luck counter, or trading between teammates in a
   run). Strength: **medium to strong**.
6. **Ship controller support at launch.** Evidence: 33 times, and 12 of the 33 are thumbs down - the one
   tech gap people leave over; 9 mention the Steam Deck. Dominion could: support controllers and the Steam
   Deck from day one, with full rebinding. Strength: **strong**.
7. **Inventory and menus must not be a chore.** Evidence: inventory is a chore 18; missing basic
   conveniences 9; the game hides information you need 7. Dominion could: sort, stack and auto-move loot
   with one button, and show item values and needs clearly at the extraction screen. Strength: **medium**.
8. **Tell players what gear an area needs, and avoid one sudden spike.** Evidence: one part far harder
   than the rest 5; an area that needs gear it never mentions (212801415). Dominion could: show threat and
   gear advice before a run starts. Strength: **weak**.
9. **Do not force a set weapon in quests.** Evidence: quests force how you play 9. Dominion could: let
   goals be met in more than one way. Strength: **weak**.
10. **Real-world timers annoy.** Evidence: 5 times. Dominion could: avoid crafting timers. Strength:
    **weak**.
11. **An outside fight can move the thumb more than the game does - have a mod and moderation policy
    first.** Evidence: thumbs up fell from 96.7% to 74.4% in 2025-12 over a mod dispute; 28 reviews took
    part, 17 thumbs down; the complaint was how the studio handled the mod author (16 times), plus claims
    of harmful code in mods (8). Mods were also praised (36 times). Dominion could: if it opens mod
    support, publish clear rules first, act fast and visibly on harmful mods, and say what was done.
    Strength: **medium**.
12. **A clear "better than X" story sells a game.** Evidence: beats its rivals 71 (highest in the corpus);
    135 reviews name Tarkov, 6 thumbs down; *"takes out the worst part of Tarkov, which is the cheaters"*
    (216039510). Dominion could: name what it fixes in the games players already know. Strength:
    **medium**.
13. **A light look can hide a serious game - and that surprise is praised.** Evidence: looks like a joke,
    plays like a real game 39, all thumbs up; the satire lands 31; only one review found the theme wore
    thin. Dominion could: if it uses humour, back it with real depth, and show the depth in trailers.
    Strength: **medium**.
14. **A fun character creator is worth having.** Evidence: 16 times, said of no other game. Dominion
    could: give players a way to make their character their own (looks, colours, a silly option).
    Strength: **weak to medium**.
15. **Runs that fit a short sitting are valued.** Evidence: good in short sittings 18; a game to relax to
    48. Dominion could: keep a standard run short enough for one evening slot, with a clear way out.
    Strength: **medium**.
16. **Watch the camera's view range.** Evidence: the narrow view is a handicap 6; the line-of-sight view
    hides enemies 4. Dominion could: offer a field-of-view setting and make enemies easy to read at the
    edge of the screen. Strength: **weak** (and from a top-down game).
17. **Boss fights must run smoothly.** Evidence: stutter only 4, but one review says every Farm Town boss
    fight stutters (232131285); runs well 7. Dominion could: test frame rate on boss and arena peaks, on
    the host machine too. Strength: **weak**.
18. **Launch praise for "plenty of content" fades.** Evidence: 6.5 per 100 at launch, 1.6 to 2.4 later;
    fair price fell from 8.2 to 2.4 per 100 over the same months. Dominion could: plan content updates for
    the months after launch. Strength: **weak** (late periods are small).

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **The sample is 10.6% of the population.** Whole-sample shares carry ±2.83%. The launch months (294
  and 483 kept) are solid; the three later periods (123 to 133 reviews) carry roughly ±8.5 to ±8.8 each
  and are direction only. 2026-01 to 2026-09 are read at 20 to 60 reviews a month.
- **The launch month is the heaviest weight** (×13.8). Weighted shares lean on it.
- **62 reviews (5.3%) were edited after creation and are counted on the created date.** The flattening
  rule is open with Rico.
- **No patch notes were pulled.** Update names and dates come from the reviews; so does everything said
  about the mod dispute, the collaboration, the studio and the publisher.
- **Hours are the hours shown on the review.** One reviewer says their offline play is not counted. The
  thumbs-down median was first given as 9; the summary-file grep in section 8 gives 8.
- **Reviews written in Chinese were read in translation**; one short slang phrase (211853631) was left
  untranslated rather than guessed.
- **The game has no co-op**, so the page says nothing about co-op play itself, only the wish for it.
- **The game is under a year old**; nothing here speaks to a long live service.
- **About 93,000 reviews in other languages** (much of it Chinese) were not pulled.
- **Slurs, jabs and personal details:** none are repeated on this page.
