# Escape from Duckov — English

**A sample: 1,166 of 11,004 English reviews read, ±2.83% on any whole-sample share.** The pull
(2026-09-26) found 11,004 English reviews across 12 months; Steam's own English count on 2026-09-27
was 11,007. Figures are as of 2026-09-27.

**A single-player, top-down PvE extraction shooter, on Steam since 2025-10** — a comic take on Escape
from Tarkov with ducks. The reviews name the studio as Team Soda and the publisher as Bilibili (no store
data was pulled to confirm it). 104,492 reviews in all languages on 2026-09-27, 84% positive, *Very
Positive*. **It is A1 on `planning/action-roguelike-list.md`, the closest match to Dominion on either
list, picked by the active loop under Rico's standing order of 2026-09-25.** It has runs that start and
end, loot you can lose, and extraction — but alone and against the game, not against players.

## 1. The short version

**Players judge it against Tarkov, and it wins.** **135 of 1,160 reviews name Tarkov**, and only 6 of
the 135 are thumbs down. `beats-its-rivals` is **71** (the third most common tag) and
`explained-by-naming-other-games` **69**. *"Very fun game that takes out the worst part of Tarkov, which
is the cheaters"* (216039510, 100% completed).

**Taking PvP out is the feature, not a gap.** `no-pvp-is-a-feature` is **51 bullets, every one under a
thumbs up**; `won-over-someone-who-avoids-the-genre` is **16, all thumbs up**. *"i have never played an
extraction shooter, mainly because i just dont enjoy PVP."* (211985235, 100 hours).

**The duck is read as a joke that turns out to be a real game.** `looks-like-a-joke-plays-like-a-real-game`
is **39, all thumbs up**, and it exists only in this game. *"Don't let the silly presentation fool you
into thinking this is a joke game."* (212777542). A curator's review with **127 found it helpful** ends
*"Just because it’s with ducks, don’t expect it to be any more forgiving!"* (214029794).

**The asks are co-op and a controller.** `expected-mode-missing` is **39** (the top complaint; 36 of the
39 under a thumbs up, 6 mention PvP, the rest ask for co-op). `missing-expected-bindings` — no
controller support — is **33, and 12 of those 33 are thumbs down.** *"not having official controller
support at this point is criminal"* (228527236).

**The one sharp drop is not about the game.** The thumb falls from **96.7% in 2025-11 to 74.4% in
2025-12**, when a workshop mod dispute broke (section 4). **13 of December's 34 thumbs down are about
it.**

**Where the game itself loses people is the end.** `the-late-game-turns-into-a-grind` is 14,
`the-thing-you-need-may-never-roll` 23 (21 reviews), and both peak after the launch months (section 3).

## 3. ⭐ The end game: early and middle praised, the last area where it sours

**Five modes describe the same stretch.**

| Mode | | Bullets | Reviews | Thumbs down |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `unlock-pace.the-late-game-turns-into-a-grind` | − | 14 | 14 | 4 |
| `unlock-pace.grindy` | − | 13 | 13 | 6 |
| `randomness.the-thing-you-need-may-never-roll` | − | 23 | 21 | 4 |
| `difficulty-tuning.one-part-is-far-harder-than-the-rest` | − | 5 | — | — |
| `punishment-model.one-chance-to-recover-your-gear` | − | 5 | 5 | 2 |

**The shape is the same in almost every one: the early and middle game praised, then the last area
(the Labs, or Farm Town before them) where keys and rare parts must be found by chance.** *"once you get
to Jlabs, it becomes considerably less fun and more of a grind"* (212074993, 98 hours, up). *"Early to
mid game is fantastic!"* — then the end game (212801415, 94 hours, down). *"Duckov absolutely tanks the
endgame"* (217761449, 84 hours, down, 12 found it helpful). A 100%-completion review at 171 hours calls
some things *"a MASSIVE grind for little reward"* (212775495).

**The complaint is about chance, not length.** The single-player setting makes the random drop harder to
accept: nobody else can trade it to you, and the only fix is another run of the same route. Round 491
built three modes from one end-game review alone (212801415): a single chance to recover your gear,
an area that needs gear it never mentions, and enemies whose ammo is too random to prepare for.

**The corpse run is praised and complained about.** The one-grave rule is five complaints; the
`quick-recovery-keeps-flow` side is 8 and `stakes-worth-the-risk` 33. Players like that dying costs
something and resent losing everything twice.

## 4. ⭐ The mod dispute: a month of protest votes

**28 reviews take part in the workshop dispute; 17 of them are thumbs down, and 13 of those 17 were
written in 2025-12.** The reviews say a popular workshop mod hid code that closed the game for players
on its author's blacklist, and that the studio asked the author to remove it rather than punishing
them. **This page records the claims, not a verified incident.**

| Mode | | Bullets |
|---|---|---|
| `community.moderation.went-easy-on-a-harmful-mod-author` | − | 16 |
| `community.user-created-content.mods-said-to-carry-malware` | − | 8 |
| `review.thumb-is-a-protest-vote` | − | 7 |
| `review.the-controversy-did-not-change-my-verdict` | ~ | 8 |
| `review.calls-the-negative-reviews-a-review-bomb` | ~ | 2 |

**10 of the 28 defend the game or tell readers to ignore the dispute** — mostly players who never used
mods: *"I don't know what the downvotes are about. I play the vanilla game, and I enjoy it."* (215970764,
40 found it helpful). The dispute review with the most hours is a protest at 233 hours: *"the publisher
allowing a malicious mod to remain hosted on Steam despite multiple reports is unacceptable."*
(213339351, 17 found it helpful).

**The same statement was pasted at least four times** (213987337, 217133568, 226071171, 231744444),
from 2025-12 to 2026-07. A second, smaller dispute — a collaboration with Tarkov's makers — brought 2
thumbs down on 2026-01-31.

**For the tree this is a new kind of complaint**: not the mod, but how the studio handled the mod's
author. It is the sixth most common complaint and exists only in this game.

## 5. What the sample says about the design itself

**Alone works, but co-op is the wish.** `works-solo` 15 and `no-pvp-is-a-feature` 51 against
`expected-mode-missing` 39. The co-op request comes from people who like the game: 36 of the 39 are
thumbs up. *"Now if only it had co-op it would be perfect."* (212024443).

**Difficulty is a dial, and the dial is praised.** `satisfyingly-hard` 49, `well-graded` 29,
`difficulty-can-be-tuned-in-detail` 6, `a-game-you-can-unwind-to` 48. `too-easy` and `too-hard` are 2
each. The reviews describe choosing between a relaxed loot run and a punishing one, and some turn full gear loss on or off to suit
themselves (212777542). One review (214751423) complains the dial is not fine
enough: full gear loss comes only with higher enemy damage.

**Loss is liked when it can be recovered.** *"Loot runs are stressful in a good way, deaths hurt, and
progression feels earned."* (215425450). The recovery run is part of the tension; losing it is where
the complaints start.

**Controllers are the one technical gap people leave over.** 12 of the 33 reviews asking for controller
support are thumbs down; 9 of the 33 mention the Steam Deck. A workshop mod adds it, and some reviews point to
it.

**The joke carries the marketing.** `looks-like-a-joke-plays-like-a-real-game` 39, `satire-lands` 31,
`the-character-creator-is-fun` 16 (only in this game). Only one review (217753377) says the duck theme
wore thin.

**For Dominion:** this is the nearest game in the queue so far — solo PvE extraction with runs that end.
The lines that carry over: **taking PvP out is praised as a feature by 51 reviewers and wins over
people who avoid the genre; a difficulty dial lets one game be both relaxing and hard; the corpse run
is liked until it becomes one-try-only; the end game must not rest on a random drop; and co-op is the
first thing fans ask for.**

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **The sample is 10.6% of the population.** Whole-sample shares carry ±2.83%. The launch months (294
  and 483 kept) are solid; the three later periods (123 to 133 reviews) carry roughly ±8.5 to ±8.8 each
  and are direction only.
- **The launch month is the heaviest weight** (×13.8). Weighted shares lean on it.
- **62 reviews (5.3%) were edited after creation and are counted on the created date.** The flattening
  rule is open with Rico.
- **No patch notes were pulled.** Update names and dates come from the reviews; so does everything said
  about the mod dispute and the collaboration.
- **Hours are the hours shown on the review.** One reviewer says their offline play is not counted.
- **Reviews written in Chinese were read in translation**; one short slang phrase (211853631) was left
  untranslated rather than guessed.
