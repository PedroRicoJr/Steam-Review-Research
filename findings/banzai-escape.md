## 1. The short version

**One rule decides how players feel: you have to buy your ammunition**, and running out fails the
mission — 17 times, 11 of them thumbs down, in every year from 2016 to 2021 and again in 2024 and 2026.
**The other half of the complaints come from it being a phone game on PC**: controls that do not
respond (9), deaths that feel unfair (8), no weapon switch (5). **Its fans call it small, funny and
hard**, and 5 want a sequel. **All 5 who say it is too short still recommend it.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**One economy rule can outlast everything else said about a game.** Making players buy their ammo,
and fail when it runs out, is 17 of the game's 105 complaints and was raised in every period from 2016
to 2026. A reviewer says the maker promised in 2018 to fix it in a sequel; this game never changed it.
For a game built on runs, basic tools that run out should never send players back to grind old content.
