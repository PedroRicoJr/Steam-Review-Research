## 1. The short version

**The people who left mostly could not run it: of 259 thumbs down, 66 say it will not start, 40 that EA
says they don't own it, 31 that it needs EA's launcher at all - only 14 blame the ending.**

**The co-op wave mode became the reason to buy it: 127 notes single it out and 70 say it is still alive,
rising to 24 and 16 per 100 reviews in late 2025-2026.**

**The ending splits players (78 against, 44 for), and the story and characters carry the single player.**

**A launcher change in 2023 sank the thumb to 46% for the rest of that year; by late 2025-2026 it was
88%.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**A co-op wave mode with characters that feel truly different can outlive the game it was bolted onto -
and the thing that kills it is not the mode but the account and launcher around it.** The multiplayer in
Mass Effect 3 is described in 2026 as full four-player lobbies in seconds, friendly strangers, and builds
still being discovered after a decade; mentions of it grow every period until it is the main reason
reviewers recommend the game. Meanwhile the most common reasons for a thumbs down have nothing to do with
play: the game will not start, EA's system says the owner doesn't own it, or it needs EA's launcher at
all. The same mode is also hurt by rewards that vanish when a teammate leaves, lag tied to distance from
the host, and loot-box unlocks players cheat past.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- 1,057 of 1,757 reviews, ±2.65%; periods ±6.0% to ±10.0%.
- The multiplayer's numbers (waves, credits, hours, peer-to-peer hosting) and the 2023 licence break are
  the reviewers' account, not checked.
- 450 of 1,057 reviews are 15 words or fewer; many reviewers played on other platforms first, so the
  hours shown understate their play.
