## 1. The short version

**The most-recommended game in the queue, by people who mostly say nothing — and the one game whose
complaint line was falling for five years until the owner changed.**

- **95.6% thumbs up and 15.9 complaints per 100 reviews** — a quarter of Remnant II's complaint rate
  (76) and a fifth of Space Marine 2's (55–71). 47% of all bullets are `unknown`; 948 reviews said
  *good* and nothing else.
- **The number one specific praise is addiction** — `keeps-pulling-you-back`, 172, 9.1 per 100, the
  highest in the corpus. *Crack* six times, *my wife left*, *I left my husband and kids*, *how do I
  have 40 hours on this game*.
- **The number one complaint is a thumbs-down with nothing attached** (22), and the number two is the
  screen you cannot see through (14) — which the reviewers write as a joke twelve times in fourteen.
  **No complaint reaches 1 per 100 over the whole sample.**
- **The complaint line went 22.5 → 13.0 → 9.2 per 100 across five years, then doubled to 18.3 in
  2024 and stayed at 19.0.** The rise is the owner: `owner-puts-players-off` (11), `made-it-worse`
  (10), `frequent-disconnects` (10) and `dlc-not-worth-it` (6) are all zero before 2022 and all peak in
  2024, the *Seekers of the Storm* year. The thumb fell from 98.4% to 92.9%.
- **The tree got the other side of that argument here.** `the-new-owner-is-accepted` (+) was built for
  six reviews that say the new owner has earned trust — *"Gearbox mostly unf**ked it (after really
  f**king it)"* — against eleven that say the name alone is a reason not to buy.
- **The most-helpful review in the sample is a joke** — 324 votes, thumbs-up, 2026-02 — and the two
  most-helped thumbs-down (10 and 9 votes) are the 2025 EULA: *tracking passwords, addresses, IPs*
  and *added after I bought it; now I can neither play nor refund*.

## 5. ⭐ The finding: a complaint line that falls for five years, then the owner changes

**Every large game before this one had a complaint line that moved with the studio's own work —
crashes fixed, prices raised, content added. This one had a complaint line that fell from 22.5 to 9.2
per 100 across five years of the same design, then doubled in one year with nothing about the design
changed.** The 2024 reviewer complains about the owner (1.7 per 100), the expansion (1.2), the
disconnects (1.2) and the patch (1.7); the 2019 reviewer complained about none of those because none
of them existed. **The thumb, which had held between 96% and 98% for five years, fell to 93% and has
stayed there for two.**

**The sample holds both halves of the argument in the same months.** *"For as long as Gearbox has
the IP for this game, I could never recommend this to anyone"* (2025-03, 6 helpful) and *"Gearbox has
released the first of their updates to remedy the faults of Seekers of the Storm; the game is now in
good hands"* (2024-11). Eleven say the name is the reason; six say the repair is the trust; five hold
a grudge under a thumbs-up. **The tree could hold only the first side until this run — the second was
six praise lines homed nowhere — and `the-new-owner-is-accepted` is the twin built for them.**

**And the repair did not bring the line back.** *Alloyed Collective* (2025-11) got three *worth it*
and no thumbs-down; `fixed-what-mattered` outnumbers `made-it-worse` in 2025–26 by 2 to 1 on tiny
numbers; the thumb is back to 93–96% by month. The complaint line is still 19 per 100, twice its
2022–23 floor, because the reviewer who was put off did not come back to say so — **the people who
stayed say *fixed*; the people who left are the missing two points of thumb.**

**Why it matters for a studio that will be bought, or will buy:** in a game this positive, the owner's
name was the biggest single thing that ever happened to the review score — bigger than early access,
bigger than 1.0, bigger than any expansion — and it cost two points. The reviews do not blame the
design; they blame the publisher's other games (*Aliens: Colonial Marines*, *Borderlands*, *Randy
Pitchford*) and they carry that blame into a EULA change a year later.

## 6. The open method questions this game raised

**None is answered here. All are Rico's.**

1. **Flattening.** 223 reviews (11.8%) carry a later edit and are counted on the created date — the
   highest share in the queue. In this game it moves a whole finding: 15 of the 35 Gearbox lines are
   2024–2026 edits sitting on 2019–2023 dates, so the ownership complaint appears in the 2022–23
   period (0.6 per 100) a year before the first line on its own date (2023-01-07). Open since
   Helldivers 2.
2. **The *"the first game had X"* pattern.** Five reviews prefer the 2D game or name what it had
   (*"I love Risk of Rain 1 more, preferred the platformer"*; *"the first game had Chef"*). Filed on
   `positioning.unknown` and `role-design.unknown`. Raised in Remnant II (round 328) and here (rounds
   359, 366); not built.
3. **The missing `game-design.loot` subject (gap 331).** Weaker case here than in Remnant II: the item
   complaints are *some items are trash* (1, `some-options-are-useless`) and *stacking falls off* (1,
   `shallow-options`), and the item-sharing complaint (12) sits on co-op design where it belongs.
4. **The publisher-communication split.** Every line names Gearbox or Hopoo by name; nothing sits on
   `developer-communication` that should be on a publisher subject. Weakest case in the queue.
5. **The friendly-fire subject merge.** One line in 1,885 (81298981). Nothing to add.
6. **The `accessibility.memory-and-attention` subject** Rico cleared on 2026-09-10 got no sighting
   here. One review asks for an epilepsy warning (`accessibility.vision.unknown`, round 369); a second
   ask would build a mode in `accessibility.vision`.

## 7. What this page cannot tell you

1. **What the other languages think.** About 113,000 reviews unread; none pulled. Sixteen of the
   1,885 "English" reviews were in Portuguese, Spanish, Russian, French, Polish, Turkish or Hungarian.
2. **Anything about a single month.** 88 of 91 months are 20 to 39 reviews at 0.4 to 2% of the
   month; the five-period table in the English page is the honest resolution.
3. **Anything about the launch week of either launch.** 5 reviews from the four-day early-access
   month; 37 from the 1.0 month.
4. **Why the reviewer who left, left.** The two-point fall in the thumb is 83 thumbs-down in 1,885,
   18 of them naming the owner, the expansion or the EULA; the rest say *no*, *bad*, *2 ez*, *the game
   is dead*.
5. **What the patch notes say.** None were pulled; expansion dates come from the reviews and the
   month volumes.
