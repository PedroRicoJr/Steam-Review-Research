# Gunfire Reborn — English

**A sample: 1,884 of 43,869 English reviews read, ±2.51% on any whole-sample share.** The pull
(2026-09-27) found 43,869 English reviews across 77 months; Steam's own English count on 2026-09-28
was 43,871. Figures are as of 2026-09-28.

**A co-op (1–4 players) PvE roguelite shooter in first person, on Steam in Early Access from 2020-05 and
released 2021-11-17** (Steam's release date). 103,744 reviews in all languages on 2026-09-28, 93%
positive, *Very Positive*. **It is A18 on `planning/action-roguelike-list.md`, picked by the active loop
as the next closest to Dominion (round 501).** It shares Dominion's four-player PvE runs, builds that grow
inside a run, and a live-service season model. It differs in the camera (first person) and has no
extraction and no PvP.

**How to read the words below.** A **bullet** is one thing one person said. A **tag** is where that
bullet was filed. Tags have three levels: a **division** (who at a studio owns it — game design, art,
engineering), a **subject** (the thing being talked about), and a **mode** (what actually happened).
`unknown` means the person raised a subject and never said what about it.

## 1. The short version

**It is a game people play with friends.** `much-better-with-friends` is **181 bullets**, the most-used
tag after a bare thumbs up. Ten reviews say it is how they play with family. Yet it also stands alone:
`works-solo` is **56**, against **14** on `punishing-solo`.

**Players explain it by naming other games.** `explained-by-naming-other-games` is **147**, the third most
common tag. **114 of 1,878 reviews name Borderlands (4 of them thumbs down)** and **57 name Risk of Rain
(1 down)**. The usual line is "Borderlands as a roguelike". Roboquest is named in 9 reviews, all thumbs up,
usually as the closest rival.

**What they praise is the build.** `deep-and-varied` builds is **110**, `keeps-pulling-you-back` **106**,
a `fair` price **100**, the `appealing-cast` of animal heroes **79**, and a `steady-stream` of updates **73**.

**The top complaint is the English.** `reads-badly` is **36 reviews**, the top complaint, with one more on
text left in Chinese. Three more find the odd wording charming — a mode built for this game.

**The thumb slides, and the complaints double.** Thumbs up falls from **97.9% in Early Access to 88.8% in
2025–26**, and complaint bullets rise from **20.9 to 46.3 per 100 reviews** (section 3). The rise comes from
translation, slow movement and too few players to match with — not from the core loop.

**Recent down votes are about things outside the game.** The most-helpful down review in the sample (91
helpful) is about a clause added to the user agreement. Five reviews from 2026-05 to 2026-09 are thumbs
down over AI-made art, and six complain that the console version was left without updates.

## 3. ⭐ The late slide: translation, movement and an empty lobby

**Complaints per 100 reviews more than double, from 20.9 in Early Access to 46.3 in 2025–26.** Three
clusters carry most of the rise. Counts are reviews, by the month each review is counted in:

| Cluster | Tags | EA (569) | 1.0–2022 (411) | 2023–24 (479) | 2025–26 (419) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Translation | `reads-badly`, `translation-quality.partial` | 7 | 2 | 8 | **20** |
| Movement | `movement.sluggish`, `no-modern-moves` | 3 | 2 | 3 | **14** |
| Too few players | `slow-to-find-games`, `cannot-find-games`, `dead-game`, `the-numbers-are-falling` | 2 | 3 | 8 | **10** |

- **Translation.** New content keeps arriving with its own English, and the later reviews say the new
  text is the worst: *"the translation for some of the free update stuff is pretty shockingly subpar at
  times, though the most recent season has been much improved"* (220774330). One says *"every single item
  in the game [is] at least 3 lines long"*, and that player-made translation packs mostly cover only the
  base game (230540835).
- **Movement.** `sluggish` is 15 and `no-modern-moves` 9 — together **the most repeated complaint about
  how the game feels.** *"You can jump and dash, that is it, not even a sprint"* (230540835); *"you move
  like a snail with a pathetically short dash move"*, with no invincibility on the dash (204410221).
  230540835 adds a design reading: the slow movement is what keeps fairly simple enemies dangerous.
- **Too few players.** **23 reviews** in all. *"your game is dying cause of low player base"*, with a plea
  for deep discounts to bring people in (216633728); *"matchmaking worse than tarkov, not worth playing if
  no friends"* (203253379).

## 4. ⭐ Co-op that works with friends and strains with strangers

**Friends carry this game.** `much-better-with-friends` is 181, and co-op praise holds in every period
(7.1 to 11.6 per 100). But the co-op systems draw specific complaints:

- **Drop and rejoin: 8 reviews** on `cannot-rejoin-a-match` or `a-disconnect-loses-the-run`. The
  second-most-helpful down review in the sample (71 helpful): *"Absolutely disgusting that you can't
  reconnect to your own game session if someone DC's"* (168616519). One review flipped to a thumbs up when
  patch notes said reconnecting had been added (171154399).
- **No join in progress: 3 reviews** want to join a run already under way, one naming Deep Rock Galactic
  as the model (179561786, 13 helpful).
- **Servers moved: 3 reviews** say latency got worse after the studio moved sessions off players'
  machines to its own servers (173877091, 174405754, 178523682).
- **Loot per player is praised.** 4 reviews on `loot-is-shared` like that nobody fights over drops;
  *"You can share nearly everything except for cash"* (223278780, 568 hours).
- **Bots rescued solo for some.** 8 `ai-teammates` bullets on 6 reviews. One player had written the game
  off because solo bosses were *"two hit deaths"* until optional bots came in a patch (192796690); another
  calls bot teammates *"an absolute godsend"* (235865471). The cost is named too: bots deal about an eighth
  of your damage while enemy health scales up (234049844).

## 5. What the sample says about the design itself

- **A guaranteed upgrade every floor keeps runs building.** *"at the end of every floor you ALWAYS get a
  core character upgrade. The pacing is genuinely the best here that I have ever seen in the genre"*
  (235815154). `satisfying-progression` is 55.
- **Breaking the game is the point.** `makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong` is 48 and rises from 1.1 per 100
  in Early Access to 4.8 in 2025–26. Its cost: `progression-outgrows-the-challenge` and `too-easy` sit
  beside one-shot deaths at the top difficulty (`one-hit-kills` 10, rising to 1.2 per 100 in 2025–26).
- **The slow start is the most common complaint about unlocks.** `slow-start` is 22 and `grindy` 18; the
  meta progression that makes early runs weak is also what players praise later.
- **Seasons that never expire are praised.** 6 reviews on `you-can-put-it-down-and-come-back`: old
  seasons stay playable and their rewards are bought later with play currency. *"No FOMO"* (220125902,
  545 hours).
- **Paid characters split opinion.** `dlc-is-fair` is 24, but 10 reviews say DLC characters are stronger
  or that too much of the roster is paid (`power-creep`, `pay-affects-play`,
  `content-behind-a-second-purchase`). One down review complains that full completion needs paid DLC, a
  mode built for it (216038763).
- **Trust issues outside the game itself.** Forced arbitration in the user agreement (207418062, 91
  helpful), AI-made art (5 reviews, all 2026-05 to 2026-09), and a console version left without updates
  (6 reviews on `one-platform-was-left-behind`).

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **The sample is 4.3% of the population.** Whole-sample shares carry ±2.51%; each of the four periods
  carries roughly ±4 to ±5.
- **The first month is the heaviest weight** (2020-05, ×45). Weighted shares lean on it.
- **154 reviews (8.2%) were edited after creation and are counted on the created date.** The flattening
  rule is open with Rico.
- **No patch notes were pulled.** Update names, the server move and the reconnect fix come from the
  reviews.
- **Hours are the hours shown on the review.**
- **The later batches were read in this session, the early ones in earlier sessions.** The tags used for
  movement and population existed throughout, but a reader drift towards naming them more often late on
  cannot be ruled out from the data alone.
- **24 reviews were written in another language**; they were filed on the language tag, not tagged by
  content.
