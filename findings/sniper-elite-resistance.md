## 1. The short version

**Players like being able to invade and be invaded: of 67 reviews that take a side, 43 only praise it, 14
only complain and 10 do both.** 42 reviews name invading as a main reason to play and 14 enjoy being hunted;
the complaints are about fairness (the host's edge, 15 reviews), the invader's kit (6) and invasions that
never stop (6).

**It is judged as an add-on to Sniper Elite 5: 105 notes say it should have been one, 49 that it changes too
little, 47 that it reuses assets.** 63 of 232 thumbs-down reviews give that as a reason.

**Fans forgive it for the same reason: 153 notes say more of the same is the point**, and 62 praise the
level design.

**It is too easy and too empty for its fans:** weak enemy AI (33), too easy (30), small levels (25), too few
enemies (16).

**The launch bugs faded:** 8.9 bug notes per 100 reviews at launch, about 1 per 100 after.

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**Letting a stranger hunt players inside their own mission is liked, by both sides, when it is fair and can
be turned off.** 42 reviews play to invade and 14 enjoy being invaded, against 6 who find it unwelcome; the
praise holds from launch (4.1 per 100) to 2026 (5.2). What the invaders complain about is fairness: hosts who
see them through walls, who clear and trap the map before an invasion can start, who quit to rob them of a
win, and a kit of four rifles with no pickups. The hosts' few complaints are about frequency: one hunter
after another. For Rico's bounty hunter, the lesson is to build it, keep an off switch, and balance what each
side can see and when the hunter can enter, not just their guns.
