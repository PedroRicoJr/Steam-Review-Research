## 1. The short version

**How it feels to play decides everything.** The music (202 bullets), shooting that hits hard (168),
many ways to build a character (144) and quick movement (132) lead the praise; 96% recommend it.

**The one lasting complaint is that only two can play.** 36 bullets ask for three or four players, in
every year of the game, and 34 of those 36 reviews still recommend it.

**"Too little to do" belonged to Early Access.** It fell from 5.5 to 0.3 complaints per 100 reviews
once the game was finished.

**Players judge it by naming other games**, and it wins: 75 call it the best of its kind and 67 say it
beats a named rival, most often Gunfire Reborn (32 wins, 9 losses).

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**A game's feel can carry it past every other weakness — but players still ask to bring more
friends.** Reviews praising the music (11.3 per 100), the shooting (9.4) and the movement (9.2, quick or
skilful) are each the highest of the 20 English groups of 300 or more reviews in the corpus (next:
Risk of Rain 2 3.8 for music; Back 4 Blood 5.8 for shooting; Crab Champions 2.7 for movement). The same
players ask for a bigger group at 2.0 reviews per 100, the highest in the corpus (Aliens: Fireteam
Elite 1.9; no other group above 0.1), and 34 of the 36 still recommend it. Our reading for Dominion: put
the effort into feel first, and treat four-player co-op as something this audience already wants.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- **±2.50% on whole-sample shares**; periods ±3.5 to ±7.8. Most complaint counts are under 20.
- **Weights reach ×16** in several months; the 1.0 month is 14.4% of all reviews.
- **Movement praise falls in the last period** (9.4 → 2.0 per 100); part of that may be how later
  batches filed "fast shooting and movement", not only what players said. Not checked.
- **Not checked:** the publisher history some reviewers name, the start date of the 2025-2026 online
  outage (the fix date, 2026-03-25, is the studio's), prices quoted in reviews.
- **English only.** About 7,200 reviews in other languages; none pulled.
