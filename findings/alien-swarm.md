## 1. The short version

**It is loved as a free game for four friends.** 420 reviews praise the free price, 332 say it is much
better with friends and 136 that it rewards teamwork.

**The complaint is that there is so little of it, and then that no one is left to play it with.** 191
say there is too little content (one campaign) and 85 that updates stopped; over the years "too
little" fell from 9.2 to 2.1 per 100 reviews while "dead game" rose from 1.8 to 5.7.

**Many players came only for a hat in Team Fortress 2.** 304 reviews say so - 8.4% of the sample, and
10 to 13 per 100 reviews after 2017.

**The community's continuation took its place.** 98 say a named rival does it better - 88 of them point
to Reactive Drop, the free fan-made continuation released in 2017.

**Playing alone is the weak spot:** 35 say it cannot be played alone, 27 that solo is punishing, 30
that it works; bots are reached by console commands, by the reviewers' accounts.

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**A four-player squad game is only as good as the squad you can find, and one campaign does not keep
a squad together.** Alien Swarm's design wins nearly every reviewer who plays it with friends: free,
hard in a good way, classes that need each other, friendly fire that makes stories, a tense *Aliens*
mood. But it shipped one campaign and two small updates in sixteen years (Steam news: 2012-07-12,
2020-08-18), so its complaints moved from "too little" (9.2 per 100 in 2012-17) to "nobody plays" (5.7
per 100 after 2024), and 88 reviews send players to
the fan-made continuation instead. Solo
play - the cure for an empty server - is reached only through console commands, by the reviewers'
accounts. Our reading for Dominion: make two, three and solo-with-bots play first-class and easy to
find, keep content coming, and make player-made content easy to share.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- **±2.04% on whole-sample shares**; periods ±1.5% to ±7.3% (the 2010-11 period is the least certain:
  its months were read at the 20-a-month floor, 2011-12 at ×21.1).
- **Reviews are very short**; 1,277 of 5,779 bullets are a bare thumbs up, and many reviews were written
  for the Team Fortress 2 item.
- **Not checked:** the hat's conditions, Reactive Drop's features, player counts, what console commands
  do, Valve's intent ("tech demo"), the game's origins as a mod, and a claim that it inspired Helldivers.
