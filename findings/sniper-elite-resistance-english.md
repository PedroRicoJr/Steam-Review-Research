## 1. In plain words

**Players like the invasions; the complaints are about fairness, not the idea.** Of the 67 reviews that take
a side on invasions, 43 only praise them, 14 only complain and 10 do both. 42 reviews name invading as a main
reason to play; 14 enjoy being invaded. The complaints: the invaded player has an unfair edge (15 reviews),
the invader is short of gear (6), invasions never stop (6), and hosts quit to rob the invader (2).

**The main complaint about the game is that it should have been Sniper Elite 5 DLC.** 105 notes say so, 49
that it changes too little, 47 that it reuses assets; 63 of 232 thumbs-down reviews give that as a reason.

**The main praise is the same thing seen kindly: more of what fans liked.** 153 notes say more of the same is
the point; 62 praise the level design, 41 the looks, 37 the shooting, 26 the stealth.

**It is too easy and too empty for its fans.** Weak enemy AI (33), too easy (30), too few enemies (16), small
levels (25); several say Authentic, the hardest setting, no longer challenges.

**The launch was rough, then the bugs faded.** Bugs fall from 8.9 per 100 reviews at launch to about 1 per
100 after; the add-on complaint falls from 16.1 to 7-10 per 100.

**The surprise: a mode where strangers hunt you inside your own campaign is what many play for.** "Axis
invasions are the star of the show" (189562347, 329 hours shown); one reviewer went "20-0 in invasions until
my luck ran out when i missed the kill shot on a duo" (235363863).

### 3a. The answer

| Mode (reviews carrying it) | Reviews | Who says it |
|---|---|---|
| Invading another player's mission is the draw (+) | 42 | the invader |
| Being invaded is fun (+) | 14 | the host |
| The invaded player has the upper hand (−) | 15 | mostly the invader |
| The invader is kept short of gear (−) | 6 | the invader |
| Being invaded is unwelcome (−) | 6 | the host |
| The invaded player can quit to rob the invader (−) | 2 | the invader |

**67 reviews take a side: 43 only praise invasions, 14 only complain, 10 do both** (counted from the summary
files, round 1090). The keyword scan of all 3,304 reviews found the same split by hand: 130 like, 29
dislike, 24 mixed of 183 with a stance. Six modes were built for it under Rule C (rounds 1068-1073); all are
used in this game only.

**Liked, from the invader's side:** "Invasion mode is the only mode I can derive joy from" (187397935);
hunting a duo "is peak", hunting a solo player is "fairly boring and often one sided" (188700759); "the maps
are much better for invading" - Dead Drop's vantage points and tunnels mean "it's more likely players will
complete objectives in a different order, adding to the challenge of figuring out where they are for the
invader" (187835218). **From the host's side:** invaders "completely change the course of how my mission was
going ... the panic in those first few invasions really can force you to take the loud route" (188845339);
"It will force you to pay attention to every detail of your visual and sound environments" (195423507, 32
found it helpful).

### 3b. What players want fixed

- **The host's edge (15 reviews).** The host's focus ability shows the invader "through walls", plus "a
  sense of direction" (188687204, 202707573); invasions start only after set objectives, so hosts clear the
  AI soldiers first and the invader faces one or two players with no cover (194280585, 1,304 hours shown);
  hosts trap the map and camp: "75% of the games you join are people who cleared the map and set hundreds of
  traps everywhere" (212479232, 66 found it helpful). Co-op hosts camp harder.
- **The invader's kit (6).** "A whopping 4 rifles", and the invader "cant pick up medpacks, ammo
  ..anything..." (187403725); SMGs lost their scopes (192830162).
- **Too often (6 unwelcome).** "As soon as I killed one invading sniper, another invasion would start almost
  immediately" (229873500); "it never stops while it is on" (235325989).
- **Robbed invaders (2).** "50% of the time you invade someone they either just save&quit or die before you
  can kill them" (188728825); "90% of the matches I joined were either at the mission end ... or the person
  just ended it the second I joined" (190055951). Reviewers' estimates, not checked.
- **What they ask for:** invasions possible from the start of a mission (194280585); invasion phones that
  give only a general direction (190207281); a shorter, wider anti-camping timer, limits on traps, strikes for
  repeat trap-mappers (212479232); a way to avoid such hosts (215934628); text, proximity voice or death
  messages with the other player (188845339); a choice of solo or duo hunts and a 2-vs-2 campaign mode
  (188700759, 190207281).
- **What is already there (reviewers' account):** invasions can be set "on / invite only / off" (212489988);
  campers "who stay in one area get punished" (220131240), though others call that system "laughable"
  (215934628).

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play.** The campaign plays in two-player co-op (25 better with friends); Survival takes
  four. Invasions: section 3. Multiplayer is weaker: few maps (three for deathmatch, one for No Cross, by
  many reviews' count), short-range maps that suit SMGs (229110661), cheaters (17 notes) despite Denuvo,
  vote-kicks abused against strong players (188191689, 202707573), lag and refused connections (208571211),
  a lobby that sends you back to the one you left 5-10 times (208571211). In co-op, Denuvo made the
  character keep moving 7-10 seconds after a key was released until removed (206666503, reviewer's account).
- **Combat and feel.** The shooting and slow-motion kill cam are praised (37); the kill cam repeats (2) and
  its setting only changes frequency (235311886). Secondaries are too strong ("Pistol Elite", 205535721).
- **Movement and controls.** Climbing, vaulting, stairs and ladders are unreliable (20); automatic cover
  snags on corners (187749877); a large controller dead zone (187749877).
- **Enemies, bosses and difficulty.** Weak AI (33), too easy (30), too few enemies (16), enemies seeing
  through walls (several). "Enemies never use the alarms" (236461811).
- **Progression, loot and randomness.** Workbenches must be found on each map (several complaints); all
  useful skills unlocked by halfway (224328195); supplies everywhere, so nothing is scarce (236461811,
  235311886).
- **Runs, content and replay.** No runs. Eight missions plus a short ninth; "too little" (41); the timed
  propaganda missions split (25 mode-falls-flat notes, most of them these).
- **Money and price.** Should have been an add-on (105), buy on sale (42), too pricey (24); paid weapons
  and a season pass (18); fair (several, at sale prices).
- **Tech.** Bugs (35), bugs that stop play (21), crashes; Denuvo crashes or input lag in a few reviews.
- **The studio and the community.** Outsourcing claims (not checked); one reviewer says they were blocked on
  the forum for reporting bugs (187385522, reviewer's claim); no update since mid-2025 (214700790,
  224448342, reviewers' claims).
- **What players asked for.** A new war or era (14: the Pacific, the Eastern Front, Vietnam, Korea, the Cold
  War); more enemies and real difficulty; invasion fixes (3b); Karl back as a voiced lead; the paid guns free.

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5. **Rico's idea: players can join a Dominion run as an
enemy bounty hunter.**

1. **Build the bounty hunter: players want this.** 42 reviews name invading as a reason to play, 14 enjoy
   being hunted, against 6 who don't want it. *Strong.*
2. **Keep an off switch and an invite-only option.** The game has "on / invite only / off"; the 6 unwelcome
   reviews want it off, and one advises leaving it off for the first missions. *Strong.*
3. **Space the hunters out.** "As soon as I killed one invading sniper, another invasion would start almost
   immediately." Give the squad a quiet spell after beating a hunter. *Medium.*
4. **Balance information, not just guns.** The host's wall vision and sense of direction are the top fairness
   complaint (15 reviews). Whatever lets the squad see enemies should not show the hunter, or the hunter
   should get the same. *Strong.*
5. **Let the hunter in while the run is live.** Invasions that start only after objectives let hosts clear
   and trap the map first. In Dominion, let the hunter join mid-run, with the AI still there as cover.
   *Strong.*
6. **Give the hunter a real kit and let them resupply.** Four rifles and no pickups are named (6). *Medium.*
7. **Pay the hunter even if the squad quits or dies to the AI.** Reviewers estimate half to most invasions end
   with no reward. *Medium.*
8. **Make anti-camping bite, and limit traps against hunters.** Trap-mapping hosts are the most common
   invader complaint; Dominion's squad of four would camp harder than a duo. *Strong.*
9. **Hunting a group is the fun part.** A duo hunt "is peak", a solo hunt "boring and often one sided"; a
   hunter against a four-player squad needs its own balance (more lives, AI allies, or a target to steal
   rather than a squad to wipe). *Medium.*
10. **Let hunter and squad talk.** Players ask for text chat, proximity voice or death messages. *Weak.*
11. **Anti-cheat that works, and never one that breaks co-op.** Cheaters (17), and Denuvo crashes or input lag
    in co-op (a few reviews). *Medium.*
12. **Don't sell guns that already exist.** The paid M1911 and Kar98k are the sharpest money complaint.
    *Medium.*
