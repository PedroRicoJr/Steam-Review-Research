# EARTH DEFENSE FORCE 5 — English

**A sample: 1,768 of 7,208 English reviews read, ±2.58% on any whole-sample share.** The pull
(2026-09-28) found 7,208 English reviews across 87 months; Steam's own English count on 2026-09-30
was 7,211. Figures are as of 2026-09-30.

**A third-person sci-fi PvE shooter for up to four players online (two on one screen), released on
Steam 2019-07-11** (Steam's release date). 11,553 reviews in all languages on 2026-09-30, 94% positive,
*Very Positive*. **It is C17 on `planning/third-person-shooter-list.md`, picked by the active loop as the
next closest to Dominion (round 542).** It shares Dominion's third-person camera, four-player PvE co-op,
alien invaders and a game hosted by one player. It differs in having missions rather than runs, a long
linear campaign, and no extraction.

**How to read the words below.** A **bullet** is one thing one person said. A **tag** is where that

## 1. The short version

**The camp is the product.** `cheesy-on-purpose` is **169 bullets**, the most-used tag after a bare
thumbs up. `memorable-lines` is **134**: the marching song, *"They look just like us!"* and other
quoted lines. Players praise what other games are marked down for. Rough edges are part of the charm
(**11**), sameness is the point (`more-of-the-same-is-the-point`, **13**), and 3 reviews call it so bad it
is good. The two most-helpful reviews in the sample are one-line quips (186 and 180 helpful).

**It is judged as a sequel.** `successor-framing-accepted` is **133**: better than 4.1. At launch it was
**22.2 per 100 reviews**; from 2024 on it is **3.6**. **125 reviews name 4.1** (11 of them thumbs down).
Against it: the sequel changes too little (**16**), 4.1 was better (`successor-claim-backfired`, **12**),
and the voices and songs are weaker than 4.1's (**16**, a mode built for this game).

**It is played with friends, on one screen or online.** `much-better-with-friends` is **146**.
Split screen is praised in **18** reviews, and 5 more use Steam Remote Play to take the couch game online.
`works-solo` is **39**, against **12** on `punishing-solo`; 8 of those 12 come after 2024-08 (1.6
per 100).

**The top complaint is the price.** `buy-on-sale-only` is **60**, the most common complaint in every
period. `too-high-for-what-it-is` is **20**, and `looks-dated` is **44**. `fair` is **37**.

**Helldivers 2 brought a new comparison.** **36 reviews name Helldivers, all from 2024 on, and none of
them is thumbs down.** `beats-its-rivals` rises from **0 per 100 at launch to 3.2 after 2024-08**. EDF 6
is named in 33 reviews; **7 rate 5 above it, 2 below.**

## 3. ⭐ A new yardstick: Helldivers 2 and EDF 6

**Before 2024 the game was measured against 4.1. From 2024 it is measured against Helldivers 2.**

| | 2019–23 (1,122) | 2024-01 to 07 (140) | 2024-08 on (498) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reviews naming Helldivers | 0 | 10 | 26 |
| Reviews naming EDF 6 | 10 | 8 | 15 |
| Reviews naming 4.1 | 92 | 6 | 27 |
| `beats-its-rivals` per 100 | 0.4 | 2.1 | 3.2 |

- **Helldivers 2 is mostly the foil.** The second most-helpful review in the sample is a joke that
  Helldivers 2 is "EDF at home" (158872972, 180 helpful). Others call EDF5 *"Helldivers before
  Helldivers"* (213662469) or the better Helldivers 2 (218009571), and two use jab names for it. **One
  review says Helldivers 2 does it better** and still recommends EDF5. *"Helldivers 2 improved on the
  formula in pretty much every way but it's still a joy"* (206084077, 7 helpful). That is a mode built
  for this game.
- **Some players came because Helldivers 2 was not sold to them.** 2 reviews, both from 2024-05, came to EDF5
  because Helldivers 2 was pulled from sale in their countries (165319991, 165288608). That is
  another mode built here.
- **EDF 6 split the fans: 7 put 5 above it, 2 below.** *"6 is basically a worse rehash of 5"*
  (225531414); *"still more stable in multiplayer than EDF6"* (229993356). On the other side,
  179277084 says *"just buy EDF6 instead"*. 201902598 (11 helpful) prefers 6 but calls 5 the better
  first game.

## 4. ⭐ Co-op on one player's machine: what it costs online

**The game is hosted by one player, and its online rules draw specific complaints.** Counts are
reviews:

| Friction | Tag | Reviews |
|---|---|---|
| Solo and online progress are separate | `solo-and-online-progress-are-separate` | 7 |
| Online games cap the gear you bring | `online-games-cap-the-gear-you-bring` | 4 |
| Cannot join a mission in progress | `cannot-join-a-match-in-progress` | 4 |
| Each mission caps your weapon level | `each-mission-caps-your-weapon-level` | 2 |
| Online scaling is uneven by group size | `the-scaling-steps-are-uneven`, `the-scaling-outpaces-a-full-group` | 2 |
| The host leaving ends the game for everyone | `the-host-leaving-ends-everyones-run` | 1 |
| Some missions exist only online | `some-missions-need-online-play` | 1 |

- **Scaling by head count is the most detailed complaint.** *"1 player = 4 player scaling, 2 & 3 = 2
  player scaling"* (193922521, 6 helpful), which the reviewer says makes solo online far too hard and three
  players far too easy. These are the reviewer's numbers; they were not checked.
- **The same gear cap is also praised.** 194931079 likes that a room can cap gear at the lowest
  player's level, *"so everyone can enjoy the game in balanced way"*. That is a positive mode built
  beside the negative one.
- **The couch works; the couch online does not.** Split screen is praised in **18** reviews. But it
  cannot go online (1), and the second player starts without the first player's armour (1). **5
  reviews** use Steam Remote Play Together to take local co-op online instead.
- **Allied soldiers split opinion: 4 say they pull their weight, 3 say they are no help.** They also
  sing. 8 reviews on `useless-actions-players-love` are all about singing, chanting or voice lines. 195194393 (5
  helpful) points out that the squad picks songs to fit the moment: a cave song in caves, a sadder verse
  once hurt.

## 5. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Picking up drops by hand is the loot complaint.** Weapons and armour lie where the enemy died.
  **10 reviews** call collecting them a chore, **5** use a cheat to collect them and **4** use a mod. 2
  more lose the drops left on the field when the mission ends. *"you only get like 10 seconds to collect
  ... after mission complete"* (186836235, 8 helpful).
- **The grind is long, and mostly accepted.** `grindy` is 34, `grind-feels-earned` 15. **9 reviews**
  say 100% needs every mission on the top difficulty with all four classes. 191333532 counts that at
  about 580 missions.
- **Four classes that play as four games.** `each-role-plays-its-own-way` is 71 and rises to 5.8 per
  100 after 2024-08. The Wing Diver is 5 of the 11 `role-underpowered` bullets,
  often as weaker than in 4.1, and one player finds the others dull next to it.
- **Destruction and scale are the spectacle.** `destruction-changes-play` is 44 and
  `a-real-sense-of-scale` 22. Players accept plain graphics as the price of hundreds of enemies on
  screen. `looks-dated` is still 44.
- **The campaign is long and padded for some.** `plenty` is 65, but `sprawling-to-a-fault` is 7. One
  review says about 40 missions exist only to introduce one new enemy (191333532).
- **No extra account.** 5 reviews name the absence of a third-party login as a plus. This is a mode
  built here; one reviewer bought EDF5 because EDF 6 launched needing an Epic account (178414607).

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **The sample is 24.5% of the population.** Whole-sample
  shares carry ±2.58%. The two small periods carry about ±8.
- **The heaviest weight is the launch month** (2019-07, ×14). Weighted shares lean on it.
- **102 reviews (5.8%) were edited after creation and are counted on the created date.**
- **No patch notes were pulled.** The Helldivers 2 (2024-02-08) and EDF 6 (2024-07-25) dates are
  Steam's own; the regional delisting and EDF 6's account requirement come from the reviews.
- **Many reviews are jokes.** A joke was filed under what it praises or quotes only when the text said
  so. Otherwise it went to `positive.unknown`.
- **Slurs and jabs were recorded as "a jab" and not repeated. Personal details were left out.**
- **11 reviews were written in another language**; they were filed on the language tag, and summarised
  in English where the text allowed.
