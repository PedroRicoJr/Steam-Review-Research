## 1. In plain words

**Players love it for being itself.** It is cheesy on purpose, and 169 times players say so — the most
common thing said after a bare thumbs up, and no other game gets it. 134 times players quote its lines
back: the marching song, *"They look just like us!"* and others. Players praise what other games are
marked down for: rough edges are part of the charm (11), more of the same is the point (13), and 3
reviews call it so bad it is good. The two most-helpful reviews in the sample are one-line quips (186
and 180 helpful).

**It is judged as a sequel.** 133 times players say it is a worthy follow-up to EDF 4.1. At launch that
was 22.2 per 100 reviews; from 2024 on it is 3.6. 125 reviews name 4.1 (11 of them thumbs down). Against
it: the sequel changes too little (16), 4.1 was better (12), and the voices and songs are weaker than
4.1's (16).

**It is played with friends, on one screen or online.** "Much better with friends" is said 146 times.
Playing two on one screen is praised in 18 reviews, and 5 more use Steam Remote Play to take the couch
game online. 39 say it works well alone, against 12 who find it punishing alone; 8 of those 12 come
after 2024-08 (1.6 per 100).

**The top complaint is the price, not the game.** "Only buy it on sale" is said 60 times, the most
common complaint in every period. "Too expensive for what it is" is 20, and "it looks dated" 44. 37
call the price fair.

**Who complains: mostly fans.** 463 of the 684 complaints (67.7%) come from reviews that still
recommend the game. Only 76 reviews are thumbs down, with a median of 10 hours played.

**Helldivers 2 brought a new comparison without the game changing.** 36 reviews name Helldivers, all
from 2024 on, and none of them is thumbs down. Saying it beats the games like it rises from 0 per 100 at
launch to 3.2 after 2024-08. EDF 6 is named in 33 reviews; 7 rate 5 above it, 2 below.

**The surprise: the design complaints are about playing online on one player's machine.** Progress kept
apart between solo and online (7), gear capped online (4), no joining a mission already running (4).
None is large, but this is the part of the game closest to Dominion.

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **A squad that talks, and a line players can repeat, is worth more than almost any other praise.**
   Evidence: lines players quote 134 (7.6 per 100; every other game 0.6 or less); cheesy on purpose
   169; singing with the allied soldiers 8; songs that fit the moment (195194393). The cross-game page
   says these cost little and are what this game's reviews are about.
   Dominion could: give the squad, the AI handler or the enemies short, quotable lines and maybe a team
   chant; make lines react to the moment (caves, low health, extraction).
   Strength: strong.
2. **But voice lines wear thin if they repeat.**
   Evidence: voice lines grate or repeat 9; voice lines that wear rise to 1.0 per 100 later; voices
   weaker than the last game 16.
   Dominion could: write many variants per moment and limit how often each plays.
   Strength: medium.
3. **Decide early how progress and gear work between solo and online, and say so on the store page.**
   Evidence: solo and online progress separate 7; online gear cap 4; weapon level cap per mission 2;
   some missions only online 1. The cross-game page gives this lesson directly.
   Dominion could: keep one profile for solo and co-op; if gear is capped online, make it a host option,
   not a rule.
   Strength: medium (small counts, but the same hosting model as Dominion).
4. **Let players join a run in progress, and do not end the game when the host leaves.**
   Evidence: cannot join a mission in progress 4; the host leaving ends the game for everyone 1.
   Dominion could: support drop-in joining mid-run; look at host migration or a safe save-and-resume
   when the host quits, since Dominion is hosted by one player too.
   Strength: medium (small counts, direct match to Dominion's setup).
5. **Scale smoothly by the number of players.**
   Evidence: uneven scaling 2; *"1 player = 4 player scaling, 2 & 3 = 2 player scaling"* (193922521),
   which makes solo online too hard and three players too easy (reviewer's numbers); punishing solo 12,
   rising to 1.6 per 100.
   Dominion could: give each group size (1, 2, 3, 4) its own tuning, and retune when someone joins or
   leaves.
   Strength: medium.
6. **A gear cap can be a choice that keeps mixed groups even.**
   Evidence: 1 review praises a room that caps gear to the lowest player (194931079); 4 dislike forced
   caps.
   Dominion could: offer an optional "level the team" setting for mixed groups.
   Strength: weak.
7. **Do not make players pick up loot by hand, and never delete it at the end.**
   Evidence: collecting drops is a chore 10; 5 cheat and 4 mod to collect them; 2 lose drops left on the
   field (about 10 seconds after a mission, 186836235).
   Dominion could: pull loot to players nearby automatically, or credit run loot at extraction; if
   leaving loot behind is part of extraction risk, make that clear.
   Strength: medium.
8. **Classes that feel like separate games are a big draw. Keep them balanced.**
   Evidence: each class plays its own way 71, rising to 5.8 per 100; a class underpowered 11 (Wing
   Diver 5); some options never worth taking 12.
   Dominion could: make each class change how you move and fight, not only numbers; watch that no class
   falls behind.
   Strength: strong.
9. **Hordes, destruction and scale can cover plain graphics.**
   Evidence: destruction changes play 44 (highest in the corpus); a real sense of scale 22; pressure of
   huge numbers 24; good enemy variety 30. Still, looks dated 44.
   Dominion could: favour many enemies and things that break over high-detail models, and budget for it
   on the host's machine.
   Strength: medium.
10. **Price so players do not wait for a sale.**
    Evidence: buy on sale only 60, the top complaint in every period; too expensive 20; fair 37.
    Dominion could: set a launch price players see as fair for what it has, since waiting for a sale is
    the complaint people keep.
    Strength: medium.
11. **Solo must work, and solo becomes more common as new players arrive.**
    Evidence: works solo 39; punishing solo 12, with 8 of the 12 after 2024-08 (from 0.0 to 1.6 per
    100); allied soldiers split 4 helpful against 3 no help.
    Dominion could: tune solo separately, and if AI allies exist, make them useful.
    Strength: medium.
12. **Do not require a third-party account.**
    Evidence: 5 praise needing no extra account; one bought EDF 5 because EDF 6 needed an Epic account
    (178414607).
    Dominion could: if it uses Epic Online Services, let players play with only their Steam account and
    no extra login.
    Strength: medium (small count, very concrete).
13. **Friendly fire can be fun in a PvE co-op game.**
    Evidence: friendly fire makes stories 21.
    Dominion could: consider light friendly fire or knockback as a setting.
    Strength: weak to medium.
14. **Remote play and couch play are wanted by some.**
    Evidence: split screen praised 18; 5 use Steam Remote Play Together.
    Dominion could: make sure Steam Remote Play Together works, even without split screen.
    Strength: weak.
15. **A long grind is accepted if progress is satisfying, but 100% should not need endless repeats.**
    Evidence: grindy 34 against grind feels earned 15 and satisfying progress 39; only repeating
    completes the achievements 9; about 580 missions for 100% (191333532); may never drop 9.
    Dominion could: give achievements new goals, not repeats across every class and difficulty.
    Strength: medium.
16. **Do not pad the start or the length.**
    Evidence: slow to get going 11; too long and padded 7; repetitive 16 (rising later); plenty of content
    65.
    Dominion could: get players into a real fight fast; make each new enemy arrive inside a run, not in
    its own filler mission.
    Strength: medium.
17. **Vehicles, if any, must handle well.**
    Evidence: vehicles handle badly 11.
    Dominion could: skip vehicles or give them the same care as movement.
    Strength: weak to medium.
18. **A new rival changes what reviews are about.**
    Evidence: no review named Helldivers before 2024; 36 do after, none thumbs down; beats its rivals
    0.4 → 3.2 per 100.
    Dominion could: expect to be measured against Helldivers 2 (and EDF), and decide what it does better.
    Strength: medium.
19. **Small conveniences are noticed.**
    Evidence: quality of life looked after 20.
    Dominion could: keep a list of small comforts (auto-loot, quick restart, clear menus).
    Strength: weak.
20. **A game you can relax to is a real draw.**
    Evidence: 66 say they use it to unwind.
    Dominion could: offer an easy setting that still feels like the full game.
    Strength: medium.
21. **A sequel must change enough and keep what was loved.**
    Evidence: worthy sequel 133; changes too little 16; the last game was better 12; voices weaker 16.
    Dominion could: keep for later; it matters only for a Dominion 2.
    Strength: weak (for now).

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **The sample is 24.5% of the population.** Whole-sample
  shares carry ±2.58%. The two small periods carry about ±8.
- **The heaviest weight is the launch month** (2019-07, ×14). Weighted shares lean on it.
- **Every month is read at 16 to 48 reviews**, so single months say little.
- **102 reviews (5.8%) were edited after creation and are counted on the created date.**
- **No patch notes were pulled.** The Helldivers 2 (2024-02-08) and EDF 6 (2024-07-25) dates are
  Steam's own; the regional delisting and EDF 6's account requirement come from the reviews. The
  scaling steps (193922521) and the drop window (186836235) are the reviewers' claims, not checked.
- **Many reviews are jokes.** A joke was filed under what it praises or quotes only when the text said
  so. Otherwise it went to `positive.unknown`.
- **Almost no live service**, so it says nothing about seasons or updates.
- **Slurs and jabs were recorded as "a jab" and not repeated. Personal details were left out.**
- **11 reviews were written in another language**; they were filed on the language tag, and summarised
  in English where the text allowed. About 4,300 reviews in other languages exist on Steam; none were
  pulled.
