# Roboquest — English

**A sample: 1,796 of 17,274 English reviews read, ±2.50% on any whole-sample share.** The grid
(2026-10-03) found 17,274 English reviews across 75 months, the same as Steam's own English count that
day; on 2026-10-04 Steam shows 17,278 (`scripts/steam_counts.py 692890`).

**"A fast FPS Roguelite in a scorched futureworld. You're a rebooted Guardian … Fight with your buddy
or by yourself and annihilate hordes of deadly bots in ever-changing environments"** (Steam store page,
`store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=692890`). First-person, single-player or online co-op,
Windows only on Steam; developer and publisher **RyseUp Studios**; Early Access from **2020-08-20**, full
release (1.0) on **2023-11-07**; **$24.99** list price (shown at $14.99, 40% off, on 2026-10-04). Store
genres *Action, Indie*; categories include Online Co-op, Cross-Platform Multiplayer and Adjustable
Difficulty. The store page does not name the engine or the largest group size; the reviews and the
studio's posts speak of two players. 24,480 reviews in all languages on 2026-10-04, 95% positive,
*Overwhelmingly Positive*. **It is A15 on `planning/action-roguelike-list.md`, picked by the active
loop as the next closest to Dominion (round 650).** It shares the shooting, the sci-fi setting, PvE
co-op online and play built on runs. It differs in its first-person camera, two players rather than
four, and no extraction.

**Dates checked against the studio's Steam announcements**
(`api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=692890`): Early Access 2020-08-20; 1.0 on
2023-11-07 *with a full progress reset* ("Your previous save profile can't be ported to the new
version") and a "Progression Recovery" hotfix on 2023-11-22; the Endless Update (v1.5) on 2024-11-27,
which added Endless Mode and a **single-player-only** save and quit; **"The End of the Journey" on
2025-05-07** ("We're not planning any more updates on Roboquest. The multiplayer and cross-platform will
still remain available"; the game "was designed to be a 25/50 hours gameplay experience"); a
**Multiplayer Hotfix on 2026-03-25** for a failure that left players "unable to play in multiplayer
with your friends", traced with Epic Games to Epic Online Services.

## 1. In plain words

**Players love how this game feels to play, and say so in few words.** 96% of the sampled reviews are
thumbs up. The most named reasons are the music (202 bullets), shooting that hits hard (168), many ways
to build a character (144), quick, smooth movement (132), and a pull to play one more run (129).

**Players explain it by naming other games.** 212 bullets describe it as a mix of others — most often
Borderlands (126 reviews name it), DOOM (113) and Gunfire Reborn (93). 75 bullets call it the best game
of its kind and 67 say it beats a named rival; Gunfire Reborn is the rival most often beaten (32) and
also the one most often named as better (9 of 17).

**The biggest complaint is the group size: only two players.** 36 bullets ask for three or four
players, from the first months of Early Access to August 2026. Only 2 of those reviews are thumbs down:
players like the game and want to bring more friends. Playing with friends is the seventh most named
praise (125).

**The second complaint is how much there is to do, and it faded.** 48 bullets say there is too little
content, 5.5 per 100 reviews in Early Access and 0.3 after updates ended. The studio kept adding
content until 2024-11 and announced the end of updates on 2025-05-07.

**Complaints come from players who stayed.** 533 of the 729 complaint bullets (73%) are in thumbs-up
reviews. The 65 thumbs-down reviews have a median of 5.5 hours, against 13.1 for thumbs up; their most
common reasons are an update that made the game worse (8 reviews) and a named rival that does it better
(7).

**The game ended on purpose, and most players took it well.** After the studio's 2025-05-07
announcement, 9 bullets regret that updates stopped and 4 say the game is finished, not abandoned. Two
thumbs-down reviews name the end of updates. Reviews from late 2025 to early 2026 report online play
breaking, and one reviewer flipped back to thumbs up when the studio fixed it (the studio's 2026-03-25
post confirms the fix).

**The surprise: a run cannot be put down.** 12 bullets ask for a way to save mid-run, 5 of them in
thumbs-down reviews — a high share for so small a count (too little content, with 48 bullets, has 4). The studio added a save
in 2024-11, for solo play only; complaints continue after it, from players who did not find it and from
co-op players (section 4).

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **Four players is a selling point. Say it loudly.**
   Evidence: 36 asks for more than two players, across every period and from players who still
   recommend the game (34 of 36 thumbs up); much better with friends 125.
   Dominion could: put "four-player co-op" on the store page and in the first trailer; make sure every
   system — scaling, revives, rewards — is tested at four, three and two.
   Strength: strong.
2. **Let a dropped player get back into the run.**
   Evidence: a disconnect loses the run 4 (2 thumbs down), *"cannot re-invite"*, online broken for months
   after support ended (studio post 2026-03-25).
   Dominion could: keep a player's slot and state for a time after a drop, and let the host re-invite
   into the same run; test the online service path (Epic Online Services in Roboquest's case) after
   support ends.
   Strength: medium (small count, but it ends runs and is on the host's machine).
3. **Let a group stop a run and come back to it.**
   Evidence: cannot save and come back 12, 5 of them thumbs down; runs of 30-60 minutes; the
   solo-only save left co-op players and some solo players without one.
   Dominion could: offer a save at each extraction or between arenas that works in co-op too, and say
   where it is.
   Strength: strong (high share of thumbs down for its size).
4. **Movement and shooting feel are what players praise first.**
   Evidence: shooting hits hard 168, responsive movement 132, movement that rewards skill 33.
   Dominion could: put the early effort into the gun feel and a short, quick move set; give skilled
   movers something to master.
   Strength: strong.
5. **Do not take control away from the player.**
   Evidence: stuns and control loss 6, including a top-difficulty run lost at the final boss; in a game
   built on movement, *"make you tap a movement key three times"* (153663026).
   Dominion could: keep stuns short and rare, telegraph them, and give a dodge or a cleanse; avoid
   reversed controls.
   Strength: medium.
6. **Do not gate core movement behind long unlocks if the levels need it.**
   Evidence: levels built for moves you have not unlocked 1 (*"10-15 hours"*); gadgets locked behind
   errands 2; slow start 6.
   Dominion could: give the movement tools the maps expect from the first run; put the unlock grind on
   options, not on basics.
   Strength: medium.
7. **The music is a feature in its own right.**
   Evidence: 202 bullets, the most praised named thing; players download the tracks (208087719).
   Dominion could: budget for a strong soundtrack that follows the fight.
   Strength: strong.
8. **Do not wipe players' progress, or offer a way back.**
   Evidence: progress wiped 9, 5 in the 1.0 month; thumbs down from players who lost progress twice.
   Dominion could: plan its save format for the long run; if a reset is ever needed, give a recovery or
   a reward for old progress.
   Strength: medium.
9. **Ending support is accepted when it is announced and the game is complete.**
   Evidence: 9 sad, 4 "finished, not abandoned", 2 thumbs down; the studio's post explained the
   25-50 hour design. But the online service broke later.
   Dominion could: if support ends, say so plainly, keep online play working, and leave the game in a
   finished state.
   Strength: medium.
10. **Players compare by naming games; win the comparison with the closest rival.**
    Evidence: explained by naming other games 212; Gunfire Reborn beaten 32 times and preferred 9 times,
    for progression and replay.
    Dominion could: know its nearest rival and make progression and replay depth its edge.
    Strength: medium.
11. **Fixed levels split players; learning them can be a strength.**
    Evidence: the same levels every run 10 against fixed routes praised 1 and learning the maps 1;
    repetitive 15.
    Dominion could: mix fixed arena layouts with changing routes, enemies and modifiers.
    Strength: weak.
12. **Reward pushing forward, and let melee heal.**
    Evidence: rewards playing aggressively 6 (ranks for speed raise earnings); melee heals on a kill 2.
    Dominion could: tie rewards to tempo and let close-range play restore health.
    Strength: weak.

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **The sample is 10.4% of the population.** Whole-sample shares carry ±2.50%; the periods carry about
  ±3.5 to ±7.8 (the launch month is 157 reviews).
- **Weights reach ×16** (2023-01 and several 2024 months). The 1.0 month is 14.4% of all reviews.
- **16% of bullets say nothing specific**, and 52% of reviews are 15 words or fewer. Most complaint
  counts are under 20.
- **The period drop in movement praise (9.4 → 2.0 per 100) and "the action never stops" (5.2 → 0.3) may
  partly be how bullets were filed**, not only what players said: later batches often filed "fast
  shooting and movement" under shooting feel alone. Not checked; read those two rows with care.
- **200 reviews (11.2%) were edited after creation and are counted on the created date**, so edits
  about the end of updates sit in earlier months (154927269 and 162952721, both edited in 2025).
- **Dates and studio statements come from Steam's announcements for app 692890**; their text was read
  for the 1.0, Endless Update, End of the Journey and Multiplayer Hotfix posts only.
- **Not checked:** the publisher history some reviewers name (the store lists RyseUp Studios as both
  developer and publisher), the dates of the online outage (only the fix date is the studio's), prices
  reviewers quote, and claims about crashes caused by a patch.
- **Italic phrases with an id are mostly the wording of the summary bullet, not the review's own words.**
  The exact review text is in `raw/roboquest/english/sample/*.json`; `DOMINION-TAKEAWAYS.md` quotes the
  review text itself.
- **Slurs and jabs were recorded as "a jab" or "a crude remark" and not repeated. Personal, family and
  health details were left out.**
- **7 reviews were written in another language** (Portuguese 3, Russian 2, Polish 1, French 1), filed on
  the language tag. About 7,200 reviews in other languages exist on Steam; none were pulled.
