## 1. The short version

**Players judge it against Pokémon and say it wins: 230 of 1,325 reviews say the word, and 62 of the 77
who say it beats its rivals mean Pokémon.**

**It is hard to put down: 75 reviews say they can't stop, and the median review shows 41 hours played.**

**Only 85 reviews are thumbs down; their top named reasons are a lost save or world (9), the late-game
grind (8), crashes (6) and not being able to connect at launch (6).**

**After Nintendo's lawsuit, 42 thumbs up were cast as a vote against Nintendo - none before it.**

**The 1.0 release lifted praise from 42-47 to 60.5 per 100 reviews (bare thumbs left out) and the thumb to
96.0%.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**The thing players love most can also be what wears them out - and the thing they can never forgive is
losing their progress.** Palworld's Pals are why people buy it: they fight, work, carry you and make you
laugh, and 75 reviews can't stop playing. But the same Pals, as workers, get stuck, starve and need
feeding (11 and 7 complaints), and the late game turns into breeding and grinding (19). Above all, the
most common named reason for a thumbs down is a lost save, world or base (9 of 85) - from launch
servers, a host's power cut (169961983) or a move to a dedicated server (156738837). A game played over
hundreds of hours with friends has to protect what the group built before it adds anything else.
