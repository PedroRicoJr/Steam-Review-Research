## 1. In plain words

**People love it because playing it with friends is funny.** The most common thing reviewers say,
after a bare thumbs up, is that the game makes them laugh: 294 bullets, about 20 for every 100 reviews,
describe friends dropping loot, falling into pits, screaming, hitting each other with the cart, or
getting killed because someone talked too loud. Another 246 say it is much better with friends.

**The laughs come from the players, not from jokes the studio wrote.** Reviewers credit three things
the game gives them: voices you hear only up close, which also draw the monsters (32 bullets); loot that
breaks if you carry it badly (20); and robots with silly faces and bodies (21). Several say the game
itself is simple and the fun is what the group does with it.

**It is scary too, and that helps.** 104 bullets praise the mood and the scares, usually in the same
breath as the laughs: "funny with friends, scary on your own" is one of the most common short reviews.

**The top complaint for four months was that you could not play with strangers.** 25 reviews asked for
public games between March and June 2025; the studio added a server list and random matchmaking on
2025-06-26, and the complaint never appears again. After that, reviewers start to report bad strangers
(8) as well as good ones (20).

**It is measured against Lethal Company.** 57 kept reviews name it; 23 say this game is better, 6 say
a named rival does it better and 4 call it a copy.

**Later reviewers find it harder and more punishing.** Complaints that it is too hard rose from none
in the first four months to about 2.4 per 100 reviews in 2026, after the monster update; asks for a way
to save or retry a level rose to 2.4 per 100 in the last period. The thumbs down in 2026 are about one
hit killing you, too many monsters at once, and losing the whole run when the team dies.

**The surprise: almost nobody complains about anything big.** Only 18.6 complaint bullets per 100
reviews, the second lowest of the 22 games counted this way. The game's faults are named, but mostly
by people who still recommend it.

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play.** Friends are the point (246). Up to six players; one group of seven wants
  more (1); two players "is ok, but tough" (3, `small-groups-have-it-hard`). No public games until
  2025-06-26 (25); then strangers good (20) and bad (8), plus trolls and griefers (2), cheaters (2), a
  jab over voice chat (1). Hosting needs a strong PC for one reviewer (209330719). Disconnects (3),
  failing to join (1), a wish for dedicated servers with a promise to flip the thumb (197920022).
  Friendly fire is fun (7). Dead players: a fallen teammate's head is carried back to the truck to
  revive them (3 praise, `extracting-brings-the-dead-back`), but the dead can wait 10-15 minutes
  (5 `a-dead-player-spectates…`, 2 `too-much-waiting`); asks for "something for spectating players to
  do" (191034649) and "some kind of revive" beyond checkpoints (208662440).
- **Combat and feel.** Mostly running and hiding: "most monsters have the same counterplay: hide and
  wait" (2 `all-fought-the-same-way`); no way to fight back (3 `no-counterplay`); weapons named — the
  tranquilliser gun and the bat (2). One wants the fighting removed (1). Movement: slow (2) and
  responsive (2); stamina too short (1).
- **Movement and controls.** No controller support (2; the store lists none). The mouse drifting on
  its own in this game only (1). Nothing else said.
- **Enemies, bosses and difficulty.** Memorable monsters (28) and good variety (10) against one hated
  monster (18), one too strong (8), poor AI (2), monsters that find you while hidden (1), silent
  one-hit kills (2 `no-warning-sounds`), unfair spawns (1). Too hard 13 against satisfyingly hard 9 and
  too easy 2. Not scary enough 7, too scary 1.
- **Progression, loot and randomness.** The shop and upgrades satisfy (8) but the shop rarely has
  anything worth buying (1) and strength upgrades may never appear (1). Nothing carries over between
  runs (2); a team wipe ends the run (10 `harsh-restart`; asks for a save, a retry or an autosave after
  level 10). Luck decides runs (3). Loot can all be destroyed, leaving the level unwinnable (1).
  Cosmetics since 2026-05-07 give a goal (4).
- **Runs, content and replay.** Keeps them coming back (76); varied runs (5) against repetitive (9) and
  gets boring fast (7); too few levels at launch (3); a level drags (1); good in short sessions (2).
- **Money and price.** Fair (27), at $9.99 list; too high (1); price raised without a verdict (2). Tried on the
  free weekend (2).
- **Tech.** Runs well on modest hardware (5), small download (2); demanding (2); game-breaking faults after
  updates (2 `breaks-play`: a crash before the menu, no way past level 1); bugs rare and minor (5); Linux via Proton works (2, Steam lists Windows
  only). Mods: they add to it (22) and sometimes break it (2).
- **The studio and the community.** Liked as people (17), open about their plans through devlogs (4),
  listening (1); steady updates (7) and updates that made it better (13) against made it worse (3) and
  retuned for the hardcore (1). Waiting for promised content (21). A Fortnite outfit tie-in disliked
  (1). Store art off-putting (6) or deliberately odd (2); two say the cover was changed (not checked).
- **What players asked for.** Public games (25, met on 2025-06-26); a save or retry (10); controller
  support (2); achievements (2); trading cards (1); more players than six (1); dedicated servers (1);
  a competitive mode (1); a separate last-loser-standing mode (1); a mimic monster that repeats players' voices (1); more cosmetics and boxes
  (3); solo aids — a back-up robot, a monster tracker (1); a beginner's guide to the monsters (2);
  console versions (2).

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **Let the players make the comedy.** Evidence: 294 slapstick bullets, 32 for close-range voices that
   monsters hear, 20 for fragile loot. Dominion could give players tools whose failures are funny and
   shared — loot or objectives that react to rough handling, and enemies that react to player noise.
   **Strong.**
2. **Ship with a way to play with strangers.** Evidence: the top complaint for four months (25) and
   gone once it came. Dominion could launch with matchmaking or a server list alongside invites.
   **Strong.**
3. **Give hosts control over strangers.** Evidence: once public games came, bad strangers rose from 0
   to 1.2 per 100 (8 in all; children with soundboards, trolls, hackers). Kick, mute and
   friends-only lobbies. **Medium.**
4. **Do not make a team wipe cost everything.** Evidence: 10 `harsh-restart`, rising to 2.4 per 100 in
   2026; "delete a save on team death" in a thumbs down. A checkpoint between levels or one retry.
   **Medium.**
5. **Every threat needs a warning and an answer.** Evidence: too hard rose to 2.3-2.4 per 100 after
   the monster update; luck (3), no counterplay (3), silent one-hit kills (2). Telegraph attacks with
   sound and give at least one counter to each enemy. **Medium.**
6. **Give the dead something to do.** Evidence: 5 + 2 complaints about waiting while dead; 3 praise
   for carrying a dead teammate's head home to revive them. **Medium.**
7. **Tune two-player and solo runs.** Evidence: solo fine (28) against worse or unplayable alone (19);
   two players "ok, but tough" (3). Scale enemies to the group. **Medium.**
8. **A low price makes friends buy together.** Evidence: a fair price 27 at $9.99, often bought as a
   group. **Weak** for Dominion's pricing, which is not decided here.
9. **Support mods.** Evidence: 22 say mods add to it, including a save mod and voice-mimicking
   enemies. **Weak.**
10. **Cosmetics as a goal.** Evidence: 4 welcome outfits as "something to work for". **Weak.**
11. **Show the studio at work.** Evidence: 17 like the people, 4 name the devlogs. **Weak.**
12. **Controller support.** Evidence: 2 ask. **Weak.**

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **±2.52% on whole-sample shares**; periods ±4.4% to ±8.1%. Most complaint counts are under 25, so
  per-period rates rest on a handful of reviews.
- **The launch month is read at 0.31% and weighted ×325**; it holds 0.9% of all reviews, so the effect
  is small. 2025-03 is 22.2% of the group.
- **75 reviews (5.1%) were edited later** and are counted on the date they were written.
- **Not checked:** that the store cover was changed (two reviewers say so); the claim that enemies
  could once be held indefinitely (223872889); "the tricycle", "the Reaper" and "the cauldron" as
  things in the game; hacker and cheater reports; any hosting or server detail; prices reviewers quote
  ($10, 10 euros, 22 riyals).
- **Checked on Steam:** release date, price, store text, platform list, the update dates above, the free
  weekend, the Fortnite tie-in, and that Bella and Loom are enemies.
- **Slurs, jabs and crude remarks** are recorded only as "a jab" or "a crude remark"; family and
  personal details are left out; 29 reviews with nothing about the game were excluded.
- **Two reviews describe a game that is not this one** in polished general praise (a story game about a
  repo agent); they are filed at `review.reads-like-a-store-blurb` and carry no other bullets.
- **English only.** About 251,000 reviews in other languages (427,270 in all less 176,247 English);
  none pulled.
- A blind re-grading of 30 summaries in round 748 found no fall in quality across the reading.
