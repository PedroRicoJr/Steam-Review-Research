## 1. The short version

**What decides how players feel is whether the game works:** 11 times a reviewer said a bug stopped
play — stuck goals, a helicopter that will not come, enemies that stop appearing — and 10 of those 11
voted thumbs down. **The only real case for buying it is the low price:** 11 say it is fair, all
thumbs up. **Early access was praised for steady updates in 2021; after full release the reviews are
bug reports.** **Its text is the second most negative in the study**: 132 complaints against 56
pieces of praise.

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**A small game is forgiven for being small but not for being broken.** In ZCREW, 10 of the 11
reviewers who said a bug stopped a mission voted thumbs down; in Zombie Girl, 12 of 14 who said the
game was too short still recommended it. The bugs that stop ZCREW include the end of the mission
itself — a helicopter that will not come — and a timer to reach it that 3 more reviewers dislike. For
an extraction game, getting out is the moment that must always work.
