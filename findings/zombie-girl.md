## 1. The short version

**What decides how players feel is whether the store page told the truth:** 19 of 89 reviews argue
about the adult store tags, and the two sides split the thumb — 9 of 12 who say the tags lied vote no,
all 8 who say buyers did not read vote yes. **The case for buying it is "short but cheap":** 14 say the
price is fair (all thumbs up) and 14 say there is too little game (12 still recommend it). **Players
who recommend it write most of the complaints** — 86 of 136 — like a wish list to the maker. **The
maker was praised for quick updates in 2023 and called gone by 2026.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**When the store page and the game disagree, the review pool becomes an argument about the store
page.** One review in five here is about whether the adult tags lied, and the side a reviewer takes
almost decides the thumb: 9 of the 12 who say the tags lied vote no, and all 8 who say the buyers did
not read vote yes. The most-helpful review of the game (147 helpful) is the claim that the tags lied.
Without those 19 reviews the game is 77.1% thumbs up. FULL METAL SCHOOLGIRL has the same shape of
argument (`findings/cross-game.md` section 15).

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- Every English review was read, but English is only 9% of the audience; the other 890 reviews (91%)
  were not read and are far less positive (51.0% across all languages).
- 2025 and 2026 are 17 reviews together; their rates are shaky.
- Who set the adult store tags in 2023 cannot be settled from reviews.
- Prices and the update history are what reviewers say, not checked.
