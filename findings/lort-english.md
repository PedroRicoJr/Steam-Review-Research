**A sample: 774 of 3,287 English reviews read, ±3.49% on any whole-sample share.** The grid was pulled
on 2026-10-07 (`raw/lort/english/MANIFEST.md`): 1,057 reviews were planned and 774 came back. The game

ridiculous powerups across a cursed fantasy world. Fight toward escape, revive your homies..."** (Steam
store, `store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=2956680`, 2026-10-07). Developer and publisher Big
Distraction; released 2026-01-21 in **Early Access** (store genres: Action, Early Access). The store
promises "Swords, wands, bows, or the age-old gun", "over 100" power-ups, bosses that "hit hard", and

Categories: Single-player, Online Co-op, Full controller support, Steam Cloud. Full price $14.99,
$10.49 on the day (30% off). The store does not name the camera or the engine. All languages: 4,607
reviews, 78% positive, *Mostly Positive* (`steam_counts.py`). **B20 on
`planning/action-roguelike-list.md`, picked by the active loop after Risk of Rain (2013) (round 945):
co-op runs against the game's own enemies that end in an escape, power-ups that stack, and revives.**
It differs from Dominion in its fantasy setting, its 8-player cap and its lack of extraction.

## 1. In plain words

**Players like LORT most as something to do with friends.** "Much better with friends" is the top
specific praise, 103 times; 20 more praise the 8-player lobby in its own right ("Lobby sizes of 8 on
games like this is unheard of", 235132133). The game sits at 80.7% thumbs up in the sample, below most
co-op games read here.

**The thumbs down are about the numbers, not the idea.** The most common named reasons are that a
rival does it better (19 of the 149 thumbs down, almost always *Risk of Rain 2*), that the difficulty
is badly scaled (19), that money and drops are too scarce (13), and that enemies soak too much damage
(12). The most-helpful review in the sample (217 found it helpful, thumbs down) says the items are
"mainly stat upgrades" and the main difficulty is "how ... long it takes for the bosses to die".

**Playing alone is the second-biggest fight in the reviews.** 27 reviews say solo is punishing or
unplayable (13 of them thumbs down); 10 say it works solo. Reviewers who stay say the game is fine once
you learn its attribute system; 19 notes answer the complaints, most often as a "skill issue".

**There is not enough of it yet.** 31 say there is too little content, 12 that it runs out fast, 11
that nothing is left to chase after a win, and 10 ask for an endless or looping mode. 48 more ask for
more *because* they like it, and 31 say the potential is there.

**Everyone compares it to something.** 83 reviews name *Risk of Rain*; 25 name *Megabonk*; 12 name
*Muck*. 23 say a named rival does it better (19 of them thumbs down) and 11 call it derivative; 9 say
it beats its rivals.

**The thumb fell after the first two months, then recovered.** 82-85% up in January and February, 74%
in March-April as difficulty settings arrived and players found the content's end, 77-80% after.
Complaints per 100 fell from 85-92 to 56 in August-October, after an update reviewers say widened the
builds and the weapons.

**The surprise: co-op in LORT can make you weaker.** Three reviewers say that because each character
grows from its own stat and most camps on a map give one stat, a mixed group finds less of what each
player needs than one player alone ("we're practically playing solo", 218819580). This mode was built
for this game and appears in no other.

The store sells "1–8 player co-op" and "bravely solo it". Reviewers say the numbers favour the full
lobby: "the HP of the boss is the same whether you are 1 or 8 players" (222135067, reviewer's account);
"normal mode feels only balanced for 8 players" (221420944); "Boss health does not scale with
cooperative play. In solo it can take 10-15 minutes" (217279663, 8 found it helpful). Small groups
say the same from the other side: "even with 2-3 players, difficulty is seeing 8" (217231436). And one
review finds the opposite: "Multiplayer makes the game really easy" (218969826). The solo complaint fell
over the months (6.9 per 100 at launch, 1.3 after August); reviewers credit the easy setting added in
March ("for solo play it's virtually the only option", 225450054).

**The stat split.** Each character scales from one of three attributes (red, green, blue), raised at
camps of that colour. Three reviewers say a mixed group must share the same camps: "if I focus my main
stat and my friend focuses their main stat then we're practically playing solo" (218819580, 10 a stage
in a duo against 15-17 alone); "it's often more optimal to split up from your friends" (220419030).
**Checked:** the store does not describe the attribute system; this is the reviewers' account.

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play.** Friends are the reason to play (103). The 8-player lobby is praised by 20.
  Public matchmaking arrived after launch (218744090, 2026-02-20: "I enjoy that they finally put public
  matchmaking into the game"); 7 note no public matchmaking at some point and 5 note few players (2 never found a public game at all). Hosting: a public host kicked everyone in the final boss's last phase twice
  (218744090); the host crashes "about a 50% chance" loading the next map with no reconnecting
  (223654623); the final boss's area attacks showed only on the host's screen (223218666, 18 found it
  helpful). 3 cannot rejoin a dropped game. Revives: one says reviving takes too long to survive a boss
  fight (218819580); another that lingering attacks on graves blocked revives (223218666); a boss cannot
  start unless every player is alive at the item (219753592). Little shared play: 3 say working together
  buys nothing ("There is very minimal interaction between players", 222139684, 21 found it helpful);
  2 praise pooling money for a teammate's upgrade (222154921); 1 wants to drop items for teammates.
  The stat split (3a). No friendly fire or loot-sharing disputes beyond one "loot hog" joke.
- **Combat and feel.** 12 say movement is sluggish, 13 that it is responsive; sprint is toggle-only
  (222720273); you slow down while attacking (223284827). The dodge has a long cooldown (217296763: "a
  4-second dodge cooldown against bosses that can hit 6 times in 2 seconds"). Healing from the flask is
  too slow and roots you (5). Melee is called weak by 18 and the mage strong by many (one option
  dominates, 18). One reviewer finds hits "like hitting a brick wall" (225456554).
- **Movement and controls.** Bounce pads, flowers and air dodges make speed the key skill (217719665,
  218686752); 13 praise the movement, 12 call it slow. A taunt key sits next to heal (223290343). Camera
  effects cannot be turned off (223284827); the archer's centred crosshair is hidden by your own
  character up close (220419030); one wants a first-person view for ranged characters (218843206).
- **Enemies, bosses and difficulty.** 23 badly scaled, 15 damage sponges, 9 bosses a chore, 12 hate one
  enemy — the snipers most (10 reviews mention snipers), then goblin thieves and invisible goblins. 28
  like the challenge. Difficulty settings (easy, normal, hard) arrived by reviewers' account in March;
  then "Easy is far too simple" and the developers "didn’t properly test the Normal and Hard difficulty modes" (224843487), and hard "only
  changes the numbers" (3). 8 say it became too easy, 2.6 per 100 after August.
- **Progression, loot and randomness.** Attributes from camps drive power (217799915, a long guide, is
  the clearest account). Meta upgrades bought with rune juice ("Soul Juice") feel tiny to 10. 10 say luck
  decides the run; 5 want to remove or sell unwanted items; 7 want to choose the starting weapon; 6 say
  items change numbers, not play. 2 say weapons decide the fight, and 1 that drops favour classes nobody in the group plays.
- **Runs, content and replay.** A run of five stages (218984136), each three in-game days, "like 20 mins" (223811425, reviewer's account); the clock pushes you on (5 dislike it; an invulnerable Death
  ends a stage, 233996727). Content is the top complaint (3b). 35 keep coming back.
- **Money and price.** 28 fair, 7 too high, 6 buy on sale; 4 refunded; 1 says the refund was refused
  (219239349, reviewer's account). Reviewers quote $10, $15, £12.79 and €15 — the store's full price on
  the day is $14.99.
- **Tech.** 44 reviews raise any technical point (15 thumbs down); 4 poorly optimised, against 5 that it
  runs well; crashes loading the next zone (223654218, 223654623); a 30% frame drop late in a level
  (218853787); bugs that block camp rewards (2).
- **The studio and the community.** 6 say the devs listen and act; 5 say early access grew into its
  promise; 4 say an update made it worse (236369657, 7 found it helpful: the stat colours "scrapped").
  One reviewer says the devs pasted the same reply on every review about difficulty (220419030,
  reviewer's account). The Discord is where players find groups (6). Humour splits: 29 like it, 7 say
  it tries too hard. One calls it GPT-made (216713749) and 3 say it looks made from asset packs.
- **What players asked for.** More content (48 "wants more", 31 "too little"); an endless or looping mode
  (10); public matchmaking (7); difficulty settings (5); to choose the
  starting weapon (7); to remove or sell items (5); a map and mini-map, crosshair options, chest prices
  on ping (quality of life, 7); a healer or support class (2); hold-to-sprint (1); reconnecting (3).

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **Tune every number for 1, 2, 3 and 4 players, not for the cap.** 27 say solo is punishing; 7 say a
   small group faces numbers set for a full one; the boss's health is reported the same for 1 or 8.
   Dominion should scale enemy health, count and economy per player and test each count. *Strong.*
2. **Never let co-op split what each player needs.** The stat split (3 reviews, a mode no other game
   has) shows a group made weaker by playing together. If Dominion's classes need different resources,
   each player's drop should be theirs. *Medium.*
3. **Ship enough for a second and third week.** Content is the top complaint (31) and 11 say nothing is
   left after a win; 10 ask for an endless mode. Dominion's first release needs a reason to run again
   after the first extraction. *Strong.*
4. **Make power come from choices, not only stat bumps.** Items "mainly stat upgrades" (220590118, 217
   found it helpful); 10 say upgrades change nothing. *Medium.*
5. **Let the host's game survive the host's problems.** A host crash or kick ends everyone's run; no
   reconnecting; effects shown only on the host's screen. With a listen server, Dominion needs host
   migration or rejoin, and every effect replicated to clients. *Strong.*
6. **Death penalties that stack can spiral a group.** A permanent max-health loss per death (8 notes)
   "ruins the fun for the entire group when 1 person keeps dying" (218888736). *Medium.*
7. **Give a reason to stand together.** "very minimal interaction between players" (222139684, 21 found
   it helpful); the one loved moment is pooling money for a teammate (222154921). *Medium.*
8. **Public matchmaking at launch.** It came a month late and players still could not find games;
   20 praise the group size, but only if the group can form. *Medium.*
9. **Readable, fair ranged threats.** Snipers are the most hated enemy; "hitscan" enemies and poorly
   telegraphed attacks recur. *Medium.*
10. **Difficulty settings early, with a sensible middle.** They arrived in March; then the gap between
    easy and normal became the complaint. *Medium.*
11. **Expect to be judged against the genre's best.** 23 say a named rival does it better. *Weak* as a
    design lesson, *strong* as a positioning one.
