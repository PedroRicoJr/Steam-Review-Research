# Alien Swarm — English

**A sample: 3,607 of 10,323 English reviews read, ±2.04% on any whole-sample share.** The grid
(2026-10-06) found 10,323 English reviews across 193 months; later the same day Steam shows 10,324
(`scripts/steam_counts.py 630`). The floor of 20 reviews a month sets the size: every month but one
holds under 400 reviews, so almost every month is read at its floor, and 42 months came back a few
short of 20 (the shortest, 2010-11, with 10).

**"Co-operative multiplayer game and complete code base available for free"** (the store's short
description, `store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=630`). Developer and publisher **Valve**;
released **2010-07-19**; **free**. Store genres *Action, Free To Play*; categories include
Single-player, Multi-player, Co-op, Steam Achievements, Adjustable Difficulty, Stats and **Includes
level editor** - but **not Steam Workshop**; **Windows only** (no Mac or Linux); the store lists no
controller support. Steam's Steam Deck report for the game
(`store.steampowered.com/saleaction/ajaxgetdeckappcompatibilityreport?nAppID=630`) gives resolved
category 2 with failing notes such as *DefaultControllerConfigNotFullyFunctional* and
*InterfaceTextIsNotLegible*. All languages: **21,639 reviews, 95% positive, *Very Positive***
(2026-10-06). **It is C11 on `planning/third-person-shooter-list.md`, picked by the active loop as the
next closest to Dominion (round 760): four-player sci-fi co-op against the game's own enemies.** It
shares with Dominion a four-marine squad with classes, swarms of aliens, friendly fire, and missions
with objectives; it differs in a top-down camera, one fixed campaign rather than runs, no extraction,
and almost no updates after release.

**Dates checked against Steam's news feed for the game**
(`api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=630`): a product update on **2012-07-12**
("Added latest localization files", a crash fix on the Achievements screen) and **"Alien Swarm update
released" on 2020-08-18** ("Security fixes to client/server file transfers", the "Out of thread ids"
error on 32 or more logical processors, "Updated localization files"). Nothing else in the feed is an
update from Valve. **The community's continuation, *Alien Swarm: Reactive Drop* (appid 563560), was
released on 2017-04-20** and shows 23,836 reviews, 95% positive, *Very Positive*
(`scripts/steam_counts.py 563560`, 2026-10-06). **HELLDIVERS 2 (appid 553850) was released on
2024-02-08** (Steam store data) - the date the reviews start naming it.

## 1. In plain words

**Players love it as a free, four-player team game against alien swarms, and most of the praise is
"free, fun, and better with friends".** 420 reviews call the price fair or praise it for being free,
332 say it is much better with friends, and 136 say it rewards teamwork. Classes that each play their
own way (76), difficulty that is hard in a good way (89) and a tense, film-like mood (88) come next.

**The top complaint is how little there is: one campaign of seven missions.** 191 reviews say there is
too little content, and long reviews put the campaign at "less than 2 hours" (223760264) or "2 hours
long at best" (116902512). 85 say updates stopped, and Steam's news feed shows only two small updates,
in 2012 and 2020.

**Over the years the complaint changed from "too short" to "nobody plays".** Too-little-content
bullets fell from 9.2 per 100 reviews in 2012-17 to 2.1 per 100 after February 2024; "the game is
dead" rose from 1.8 to 5.7 per 100 over the same span, and 98 reviews say a named rival does it better
- 88 of them name the community's continuation, Reactive Drop.

**A large share of players came for a hat in Team Fortress 2, not for the game.** 304 reviews say so.
That share tripled after 2017 (3.6 per 100 reviews in 2012-17, 10.3 to 13.4 per 100 after). Many of
them then praise the game ("Came for the Team fortress 2 hat. Stayed because the game is actually
pretty damn good", 72060435, 80 found it helpful); a few say it was a chore.

**Playing alone is the game's soft spot, and players disagree about whether it is even possible.** 35
reviews say it cannot be played alone, 27 that solo is punishing, and 30 that solo works. The store
lists "Single-player"; reviewers describe an offline practice mode of the first levels, and bots or
solo online play reached only through console commands - and several reviews
teach the commands.

**Those who stay are not the ones who complain.** Thumbs-down reviews show a median of 1 hour played
against 5 for thumbs up; the complaints come mostly from people who tried it briefly, often for the
hat, and found no one to play with.

**The surprise is how kind the remaining players are.** Several later reviews describe strangers
teaching them the game, carrying them, or becoming friends, while five describe being kicked from
games, one for being level 1 (232726539).

## 3. ⭐ The hat

**304 reviews (8.4%) say they installed Alien Swarm to unlock an item in Team Fortress 2**, filed at
`marketing.discovery.installed-it-to-claim-an-outside-reward`. Per 100 reviews by period: **3.5, 3.6,
10.3, 13.4, 12.4** - the share tripled once the game had stopped changing (counted with a grep over
the summaries by month; the command is in section 8). Reviewers describe the condition as an
achievement for finishing two missions online with others; the item's terms were not checked against
a primary source.

- Many turn into fans: "Came for the Team fortress 2 hat. Stayed because the game is actually pretty
  damn good" (72060435, 80 found it helpful); "i honestly just got this for the tf2 alien hat but
  THIS GAME IS ACTUALLY PRETTY FUN!!!!" (142103294).
- Some say it brought the wrong players: Valve "missed the mark with who they tried to market the game
  to" (141558557); "Play this game. Get a hat. Leave." (229252733) - both filed at
  `a-reward-campaign-farms-installs`.
- It shapes play: 2 used an achievement-unlocking tool instead (125750302, 145006587); reviews teach how to get the item alone with console bots (231087688,
  236442029, 138975819); and "half of the periodically active players are
  Russians who kick everybody who don't speak Russian" (56698566) - an empty game, a reward that needs
  two players, and strangers who gatekeep it.

## 3. ⭐ Reactive Drop and the empty servers

**98 reviews say a named rival does it better; 88 of those name the community's continuation,
*Alien Swarm: Reactive Drop*,** or call it "the remaster" or "new version". **31 more recommend another game
alongside this one, 27 of them Reactive Drop or "the remake".** The mode was 0.0 per 100 before 2012, 0.5 in 2012-17, then **4.9 per 100** once
Reactive Drop was out (released 2017-04-20, Steam store data). Its reviewers name what it adds: "more
of everything: maps, aliens, game modes, guns... Steam Workshop support... it gets updates too"
(115029799, 14 found it helpful); "LITERALLY the same game,but with post-launch support(new campaigns,weapons
and game modes,also lets you play solo with bots)" (163516321); "like L4D1 vs L4D2" (203922449, 17 found it helpful). These are the
reviewers' words; Reactive Drop's own features were not checked.

**138 reviews say the game is dead and 77 that it is thinner than hoped but playable.** "Dead game"
rose from 1.8 per 100 in 2010-11 to **5.7 per 100** after 2024. "It's really sad nobody plays this
game anymore, even the profile stats seems to be reset" (62891056, 45 found it helpful); "the player
base maxes out at 20 players on a good day" (206169848, the reviewer's estimate). A few disagree:
"surprisingly active in 2025!" (197562060); weekends are best (193736079, 194419739).

**From February 2024 the comparison is HELLDIVERS 2:** 17 reviews in that period explain the game by
naming Helldivers - "Helldivers before it was cool" (161102970, 136 found it helpful), "Helldivers at
home" (161037720), "helldivers if it was good" (168427482). A claim that it inspired Helldivers
(197359312) was not checked.

## 3. ⭐ Left as it was: one campaign, no updates

**191 say there is too little content and 85 that updates stopped.** Reviewers count "only 7 missions"
(133186307) of "avg. 3 minutes" each (138975819) or "9-20 min" (223760264); estimates of the whole
campaign run from "about 2 hours in co-op" (193736079) to "4–6 hours" (225005600). Some blame Valve:
"Valve hasn't updated this in years" (55678305); "yet another multiplayer IP abandoned by valve"
(169186703). Steam's news feed shows two small updates (2012-07-12 and 2020-08-18, above); a reviewer
who quoted the 2020 notes called it "the first ever update" (119205695), which the feed contradicts.
The store's own words - "complete code base available for free" - fit the reviewers who call it a tech
demo (147804079, 210987665, 116902512), but Valve's intent was not checked.

**What players did instead was make their own.** 78 say community maps and mods extend it, mostly
before 2017 (5.3 per 100 in 2010-11, 3.6 in 2012-17); 8 value it as a free kit of engine tools for
other projects (`used-as-a-free-toolkit-for-other-projects`, built in round 810); one learned the
Hammer editor in 10 hours to build a map for the kill achievements (133186307). But 23 ask for Workshop
support and 8 say custom maps are a hassle: the game "predates the Steam Workshop" and "can't download
custom content, which blocks many players from even joining custom campaigns" (116902512). The store
lists "Includes level editor" and no Workshop (checked above).

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play.** 332 better with friends; 136 rewards teamwork; 22 say small groups
  struggle ("doesn't scale well for less than 4 players", 121752237); 12 need a group. Solo: 35
  unplayable alone, 27 punishing, 30 works, 3 say offline play gives no progression ("no achievements,
  no level ups, no rewards", 212920097). Reviewers say online needs at least two players (53946107,
  69962109) and that bots come only through console commands (several reviews give the steps; 206169848 names the command); these are the reviewers' accounts. Bots: 6 useless in combat, 5 "no bots", 2 that
  bots make solo possible. **Friendly fire: 57 bullets** - 37 say it makes stories ("Has friendlyfire.
  11/10", 70240754), 15 treat it as a cost or hate it ("Trash with friendly fire", 195265204), 3 say it
  lets players grief. Matchmaking: 11 slow, 3 cannot find games, 2 fast. Servers: 5 lag (player
  hosting blamed, 55605139, 67373670), 5 note dedicated servers. Hosts and strangers: 5 kicked from
  games (`the-host-can-remove-you-at-will`), 1 kicked for not speaking Russian, 6 teammates run ahead,
  4 can draw on the shared map ("Writing on the map was the best part", 73680129). Kindness: 6
  welcoming, 4 made friends, 3 taught each other.
- **Combat and feel.** 15 say hits feel good ("the gun play is reasonably more in-depth than just
  holding left click", 116902512); **5 single out reloading that throws away the rest of the
  magazine** ("the biggest annoyance of all time", 93601907) - a mode found in no other game; 1 says
  melee does not land, 1 that melee "felt fantastic" (222913178).
- **Movement and controls.** 13 responsive and clear, 4 missing bindings, 2 take hours to learn, 9
  unclear; 11 bullets mention controllers - missing support (185724341, 219031288,
  223372985), prompts that show the wrong buttons (222913178), or a request for better support
  (182190512); the store lists none. Camera: 7 like it, 5 say it gets in the way, 7 want another view,
  and 8 say it can be played in first person - by console command, a mod or a setting, in the
  reviewers' words (217208481: "firstperson, and controls 0"); a first-person mod was covered by Rock,
  Paper, Shotgun on 2013-07-18, per Steam's news feed.
- **Enemies, bosses and difficulty.** 89 hard in a good way, 29 well graded, 3 can tune it in detail
  (Onslaught and a hardcore friendly-fire setting, 138975819), 10 too hard, 3 "one part is far
  harder" (the last mission). 10 good enemy variety, 6 lacking; 9 hate one alien - the parasite
  ("parasites are OP", 168650517). 5 say the swarm pressure feels good ("these 10-20 seconds will be
  the most longest seconds", 119205695). Readability: 5 too dark to see, 2 threats unclear, 1 cannot
  tell friend from foe ("Firing At Your Own Team", 223372985).
- **Progression, loot and randomness.** 61 deep and varied gear, 42 satisfying unlocks, 11 tools
  combine into tactics (the Tesla gun "makes any swarm stop in their track", 223760264), 4 any gear
  can carry you, 21 plenty of achievements, 4 say only repetition completes them (100,000 kills,
  147804079). Classes: 76 each plays its own way, 10 every role needed, **9 say one role is required
  to progress** - the tech, the only one who hacks doors (93601907, 137498968). One says each class has
  "a superior member" (171470107); another says the gaps are small so "you cant accidentally mess up by
  picking 'wrong' one" (223760264). Randomness: 3 say a director varies each run (119205695,
  171470107, 146105225).
- **Runs, content and replay.** 191 too little, 40 runs out fast, 18 repetitive, 4 repetitive but fun,
  13 more to do than fighting (objectives), 80 keep coming back. No runs: one fixed campaign.
- **Money and price.** 420 praise the free price; 14 note there is nothing to buy; no shop.
- **Tech.** 24 run well on modest hardware, 5 well optimised, 5 small install ("only 2 gigs"); 4
  demanding; **8 say it will not start** - two needed to reset Windows' WMI service (185464912,
  185464747); 4 crash repeatedly; 3 say it destabilises the system. 9 say it vanished from their Steam
  library and 2 cannot remove it (`the-free-game-cannot-be-removed-from-the-account`, built in round
  820). No Mac or Linux (7 ask for another platform); the Steam Deck report lists failing items.
- **The studio and the community.** 85 say updates stopped; 2 note known bugs never fixed; 27 want a
  sequel. The community's culture: 12 reviews teach play, 11 copied meme texts, 5 checkbox templates,
  2 copies of earlier reviews.
- **What players asked for.** More content or campaigns (34 "want more"; 191 too little), a sequel
  (27), Workshop support (23), controller support (11 bullets mention controllers), solo play or bots without the console
  (35 unplayable alone), a Mac or other platforms (7), more players (77 thin).

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **A four-player squad game lives or dies on getting four people together.** Evidence: 138 "dead",
   77 thin, 22 small groups struggle, 12 need a group, 35 unplayable alone. Dominion could make two-
   and three-player games and solo with bots first-class, scaling the threat to the squad. Strength:
   **strong**.
2. **Bots that only exist behind a console command do not count as solo play.** Evidence: reviewers teach each other the console steps (7 of the
   12 teaching reviews mention the console or bots), 6 call the bots useless, and one wishes for "xcom but in real time" squad
   orders (223760264). Dominion could ship bots in the menu, with simple orders. Strength: **strong**.
3. **One campaign is not enough, however good.** Evidence: 191 too little, 40 runs out fast, and the
   fall of praise from 111 to 40 per 100. Runs that change are Dominion's answer; keeping new content
   coming matters as much. Strength: **strong**.
4. **Friendly fire splits players but makes the stories.** Evidence: 37 stories vs 15 cost and 3
   griefing; complaints about it rose after 2024. Dominion could keep friendly fire with a setting, and
   watch for griefing in public games. Strength: **medium**.
5. **Kicks and gatekeeping hurt newcomers in a small population.** Evidence: 5 kicked (one for being
   level 1, 232726539), 1 kicked for language. Dominion's hosts can remove players; a host-kick reason
   or a cooldown could help. Strength: **weak** (small counts).
6. **A role only one class can fill is a single point of failure.** Evidence: 9 say the tech is required
   to progress, and "If the tech dies... you will have to restart the level" (93601907). Dominion could
   let any class do a slower version of key tasks. Strength: **medium**.
7. **Reload rules that throw away ammo annoy players.** Evidence: 5, a mode seen in no other game.
   Strength: **weak**.
8. **Dark, grey levels make enemies hard to read.** Evidence: 5 too dark, 2 threats unclear, 1 shot
   teammates in the dark. Dominion's sci-fi interiors need readable enemies. Strength: **medium**.
9. **Player-made content keeps a small game alive - if it is easy to share.** Evidence: 78 mods extend
   it, but 23 ask for Workshop support and 8 say custom maps are a hassle. Strength: **medium**.
10. **A reward in another game brings players who leave.** Evidence: 304 came for the hat; median 1
    hour on thumbs-down reviews. Strength: **medium** (a marketing lesson).
11. **Controller support and launching cleanly matter years later.** Evidence: 11 bullets mention
    controllers, 8 will not start. Strength: **weak**.

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **Sample:** 34.9% of the group, ±2.04% on a whole-sample share. Per period: ±7.3 (2010-11), ±2.8,
  ±2.9, ±1.9, ±1.5. The 2010-11 period is the least certain: its months hold up to 422 reviews but
  were read at the 20-a-month floor (2011-12 at ×21.1).
- **Counts on this page are unweighted** (one bullet = one); the weighted shares are in the stats file
  (section 8) and agree on the top of the list: free 9.5%, friends 6.3%, too little 4.4%, the hat 3.2%.
- **Edited reviews:** 281 (7.8%) are counted on their created date.
- **Not checked against a primary source:** the hat's conditions; Reactive Drop's features; player
  counts ("20 players on a good day"); Valve's intent ("tech demo"); console commands and what they do;
  the claim that Helldivers was inspired by it; that profile stats were reset; that the original came
  from an Unreal Tournament 2004 mod and that Valve bought its studio (55694801, 113291025).
- **Checked:** release date, developer, publisher, price, categories, platforms (store data); updates
  (news feed); Reactive Drop's and HELLDIVERS 2's release dates; the Deck report; Lambda Wars as an
  Alien Swarm mod (its store page, round 803); Left 4 Dead's release date (round 820).
- **Slurs and personal details:** crude jabs and slurs are recorded only as "a crude remark" or "a
  crude jab"; a review about a friend who died (130868378), family details in joke reviews, and named
  players are left out.
- **Other languages:** 147 reviews in other languages are counted with one bullet each saying so,
  plus their content where it was clear.
