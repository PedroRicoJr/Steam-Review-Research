# Immortal: Unchained — the whole game, ranked

**One game, one language group read whole — a census. The ranked lists this page exists for.** Full
read: `findings/immortal-unchained-english.md`. Plain-words lessons for Dominion:
`DOMINION-TAKEAWAYS.md`.

**The genre test of this study.** Eight co-op shooters built the tag tree. This is a single-player
soulslike third-person shooter from 2018, read as a **census — every English review, 435 of 435, no
margin of error.** The question it was chosen to answer is not "is this game good" but **"does a tree
grown from co-op shooters still work when the co-op is taken away?"**

## 1. The short version

**People who recommend it are the ones listing its faults.** A thumbs-up reviewer writes 1.53
complaints — second in the study only to Redfall — and 45.5% of all complaints come from people who
tell you to buy it.

**Faults about numbers are forgiven; losing control is not.** Bad balance, one weapon that beats the
rest and the price are mostly raised by fans. Stuns, slow weapon handling, attacks with no answer and
enemies that appear without warning are mostly raised by people who left.

**The levels are the top praise (46), hard-but-fair difficulty is next (39).**

**Nobody describes it without naming another game** — 76 times, the most common thing said.

**With no other players, more than half of everything said is about how the game plays** — 53.6%,
against 30.3% in the eight co-op games.

## 5. ⭐ The finding

### 1. The tree held — 240 of the 245 modes it needed were already there

**A tree built entirely from co-op shooters absorbed a single-player soulslike with five new
sightings and no structural change.** No new division. No new subject. Five modes, three of them
built in this run. **That is the strongest evidence for the universality claim this study has
produced.** Full list in `immortal-unchained-english.md` section 3.4a (before the 2026-10-03 rebuild,
§4a).

### 2. But 49 subjects went untouched, and they are two different kinds of absence

**Twelve are about other players** — `co-op-design`, `ai-teammates`, `matchmaking`, `population`,
`player-conduct`. **Eleven are about a live service** — `abandonment`, `update-cadence`,
`early-access`, `monetisation-practice`, `refund`. **The first cluster is empty because the genre
changed. The second is empty because the business model changed.**

⚠️ **The tree cannot tell those apart. Both read as zero.** A reader asking what the tree covers
sees 49 gaps and no way to know which kind each one is. **This is about how divisions are organised,
and it is Rico's call.** English page section 3.4b (§4b before the rebuild).

### 3. Take the other players away and half of everything said becomes game design

`game-design` is **53.6%** of this game's bullets against **30.3%** for the eight co-op games.
`community` fell from 13.6% to **0.8%**. **The share did not vanish — it moved.** English page section
3.4c (§4c before the rebuild).

### 4. The people who recommend it are the ones cataloguing its faults

**A recommending reviewer writes 1.53 complaints — second in the corpus only to Redfall.** 45.5% of
every complaint was written by somebody who told you to buy it. **Three of the five biggest complaint
modes are majority thumbs-up.** English page section 3.1 (§1 before the rebuild).

🔑 **The dividing line is control.** Faults about numbers — balance, a dominant weapon, price — are
forgiven. Faults about **the game taking the controller away** — stuns, sluggish reloads, no
counterplay — are not.

### 5. Nobody describes it without naming another game

`explained-by-naming-other-games` is **76 bullets, the largest mode by a factor of 1.6.** And its two
neighbours are the same observation with opposite signs: 27 say *nothing else plays like it*, 25 say
*Remnant already did it better*. English page section 3.2 (§2 before the rebuild).

### 6. Where the guns meet the souls-like, and lose

**39 corpus-wide word hits for lock-on and aim assist; 22 are this game.** No co-op shooter ever had
to bolt a melee game's lock-on onto a shooter with weak points. **The new mode
`the-lock-on-holds-the-body-not-the-weak-point` is the collision, named.** English page section 3.6
(§6 before the rebuild).

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- **Whether the genre finding generalises.** One soulslike is one soulslike. The ten roguelikes
  queued next (when this was written) are a different single-player genre and will test the same
  claim again.
- **Anything about the last three years.** 2024–2026 is **31 reviews**. A census has no sampling
  error and still cannot invent reviews nobody wrote. English page section 3.3 (§3 before the
  rebuild).
- **The non-English audience.** 455 reviews unread, 51% of the game's total.
- **Whether `developer-communication` belongs under `community`.** It is the only community subject
  a single-player game touched — 13 of 14 community bullets. **Rico's ruling, 2026-09-10: it stays
  under `community`.** English page section 3.4d (§4d before the rebuild).
- **Co-op and live-service lessons.** None can come from a single-player, finished game.
- **Unchecked claims.** A free copy from the studio and a platform reward are what reviewers say.
