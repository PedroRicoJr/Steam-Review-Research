**A sample: 1,512 of 27,193 English reviews read, ±2.50% on any whole-sample share.** The pull
(2026-09-30) found 27,193 English reviews across 42 months; Steam's own English count on 2026-10-01
was 27,196. Figures are as of 2026-10-01.

**A third-person roguelike shooter built on runs, for one player or a co-op group online, in Early
Access on Steam since 2023-04-01** (Steam's release date). Made and published by Noisestorm, the
musician behind *Crab Rave* (Steam store page). 31,211 reviews in all languages on 2026-10-01, 98%
positive, *Overwhelmingly Positive*. **It is A7 on `planning/action-roguelike-list.md`, picked by the
active loop as the next closest to Dominion (round 581).** It shares Dominion's third-person camera,
co-op online and runs that build power. It differs in its cartoon look, no sci-fi, and no extraction.
The pages do not give the price, the engine or the largest group size.

## 1. In plain words

**Almost nobody dislikes it, and few say why they like it.** 97.5% of the reviews read are thumbs up,
and there are only 18.7 complaints per 100 reviews, second lowest of the nine games counted this way in
`cross-game.md`, after Risk of Rain 2 (18.0). 61 of every 100 reviews are a thumbs up with nothing
specific, often just "crab". The most-helpful review in the sample is *"I wish crabs were real"*
(158886230, 781 helpful).

**It is a game for playing with friends, more so every year.** "Much better with friends" is said 86
times, the most common specific praise. It goes from 4.1 per 100 reviews at launch to 10.0 after the
November 2025 update. 19 say it works well alone, against 1 who calls it punishing alone.

**When players do say why, it is about feel.** Quick, responsive movement is praised 39 times — only
Warframe gets praise like that in the study. Feeling far too strong is praised 37 times and rises from
1.5 to 3.9 per 100 reviews.

**It is measured against Risk of Rain 2.** 43 reviews name Risk of Rain (2 of them thumbs down). Every
one of the 9 times a reviewer says it beats the games like it, the game it beats is Risk of Rain. The
second most-helpful review is the pun *"Risk of Rangoon"* (136295436, 396 helpful).

**Players like the price and keep coming back.** The price is called fair 69 times, "it keeps pulling
you back" 78 times, and the builds deep and varied 41 times.

**It looks like a meme and plays like a real game.** 20 reviews say so in those words. 23 reviews name
*Crab Rave*; 3 came because of the musician, and 3 miss the song in the game.

**Who complains: mostly fans.** 182 of the 280 complaints (65.0%) come from reviews that still recommend
the game. Only 37 reviews are thumbs down.

**The one big update that split players was the Island Update (2025-11-02).** Complaints rise from 12.7
to 24.8 per 100 reviews after it, led by missing modes, slow updates and "it made it worse", while
thumbs up holds at 97.8%.

## 3. The big stories

### 3.1 ⭐ Movement and power are the feel players name

**The named praise is about feel**: responsive movement (39), feeling far too strong (37), combat with
impact (22), music that fits (39).

| What players say | Tag | Bullets |
|---|---|---|
| Movement is quick and responsive (+) | `responsive` | 39 |
| You feel far too strong (+) | `makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong` | 37 |
| Combat hits hard (+) | `impactful` | 22 |
| The run can loop on and on (+) | `the-run-can-loop-endlessly` (game-only) | 7 |
| Power outgrows the challenge (−) | `progression-outgrows-the-challenge` | 6 |
| The balance assumes you restart for a god build (−) | `the-balance-assumes-you-restart-for-a-god-build` (game-only) | 4 |

- **Movement is praised as in no other game but Warframe.** Reviews praising movement
  (`game-design.game-feel.movement.*`, any good mode) are **2.7 per 100 here and 2.6 in Warframe; every
  other English group of 300 or more reviews is at 0.5 or less**. In Warframe it is `rewards-mastery`
  (69). In the other sixteen groups, movement is praised in at most 1 review in 200; the commonest
  movement complaint elsewhere is `sluggish` (Gunfire Reborn 15, DRG: Rogue Core 11, Immortal: Unchained
  10) (`cross-game.md` section 24).
- **Getting too strong is praised in both runs-based games**: `makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong` is 3.8
  per 100 in Risk of Rain 2 and 2.5 here, behind only Space Marine 2 (4.5).
- **The turn over time:** responsive movement falls 3.2 → 1.3 per 100 while feeling too strong rises
  1.5 → 3.9 per 100.
- **The complaints are the other side of the same thing**: power that outgrows the challenge (6), a
  balance that assumes you restart for a god build (4), runs that run out (8). Section 4 has the detail.

### 3.2 ⭐ Risk of Rain 2 is the yardstick

**43 of 1,499 reviews (2.9%) name Risk of Rain, in every period: 13 at launch, 11 in the first year, 15 in
2024-01 to 2025-10, 4 after the Island Update.** Two of the 43 are thumbs down.

- **When it is compared, it wins.** All 9 `beats-its-rivals` bullets are against Risk of Rain: *"Risk of
  Rain but good"* (137524432), *"Risk of Rain if it were fun"* (150154818), *"ror2 but better"*
  (233528238). The 2 thumbs down that name it call it a copy (154240327) or say to play Risk of Rain 2
  instead if you want to finish everything (157234469).
- **Some came because Risk of Rain 2 got worse for them.** Two 2024-10 reviews say so: one calls this
  *"the definitive third-person roguelike shooter"* now that Risk of Rain 2 has declined under its new
  owner, *"at a quarter of the price"* (177078102); another plays it *"until that game stops being bad"*
  (178129296). A 2025-01 review says Risk of Rain 2's *Seekers of the Storm* expansion *"should have been
  this"* (186213805).
- **One reviewer sets out the difference** (175059509). It is like Risk of Rain with stacking power-ups and loops,
  but the danger rises with how far you go, not with a ticking clock.
- **New names appear in 2026.** Megabonk is named in 2 reviews, both 2026: one thumbs down wishes the
  game had *"the same dev attention as like megabonk"* (229868355); one recommends it to Megabonk fans
  (233534608). Gunfire Reborn is named twice.
- **Co-op is part of the pitch.** 3 reviews call co-op rare in the genre
  (`co-op-is-rare-in-its-genre`, a mode built here): *"Co-op, which is rare for the genre"* (142668300).
- **Two runs-based games side by side** (`cross-game.md` section 24): Crab Champions and Risk of Rain 2
  have the two lowest complaint rates on this count (18.7 and 18.0 per 100) and the two highest
  bare-thumbs-up rates (61.0 and 50.3 per 100).
- **Checked:** nothing here was checked against a primary source; the comparisons and the Risk of Rain 2
  ownership claim are the reviewers'.

### 3.3 ⭐ The Island Update and what Early Access costs

**One developer, updates every few months, and players follow each one.** Steam lists 11 Early Access
updates from 2023-04 to 2025-11. The last, the Island Update (2025-11-02), replaced every island,
replaced the blue boundary walls with a new out-of-bounds system, and reworked difficulty with a new
top rank, Prismatic (Steam announcement, 2025-11-02). Hotfixes on 2025-11-03 and 2025-11-22 widened
the out-of-bounds zones, made the Immortal Snail purple to see it better, and moved two modifiers out of
the medal path after feedback. On 2026-03-18 the developer said the next update had no firm date.

**What the reviews say about it** (counts are reviews):

| Reaction | Tag | Reviews |
|---|---|---|
| An update made it worse | `made-it-worse` | 7 (3 after 2025-11, plus one 2025-02 review edited 2025-11-12 that blames patch 11.0) |
| The old islands were removed | `removed-a-feature` | 3 of 4 (the fourth is keys, 2023-11) |
| Wants the old version back | `wants-the-old-version-back` | 3 (a mode built here) |
| The map edge can't be seen | `the-map-edge-cannot-be-seen` | 2 (a mode built here) |
| An unkillable snail hunts you | `an-unkillable-enemy-hunts-you` | 2 (a mode built here) |
| Favourite items were nerfed | `nerfs-what-players-liked` | 5 (2 after 2025-11) |
| Updates are too slow | `too-slow` | 6 (4 in 2026) |

- **The longest complaint is a thumbs down from a fan.** 226203004 (2026-05) loves the game but says the
  updates are making it worse: the old islands each had a gimmick, the new ones *"feel like simple
  arenas"*, the out-of-bounds teleport is *"an awful idea"*, and it now takes too little time to become
  unstoppable. It asks for a harder game with fewer upgrades, not more content.
- **The developer is still praised.** `listens-and-acts` is 5; 3 of them are from 2025-11 and 2025-12.
  *"An amazing developer who listens and keeps updating it"* (209872720).
- **The hardcore and the casual player pull apart.** The developer's own 2025-11-22 note splits the
  feedback into players who want a fair new challenge and players who want the hardest possible
  setting. One 2023-11 review had already said the game was made harder for the most dedicated players
  at casual players' cost (151186055, `retuned-for-the-hardcore-at-casual-players-expense`).
- **Waiting is part of liking it.** `awaiting-promised-content` is 27, `steady-stream` 24. Two launch
  reviews want the parkour and one-on-one modes an earlier trailer showed.
- **Checked:** update dates and contents come from Steam's announcements for app 774801; the reviews'
  own accounts of what changed are theirs.

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play.** **It is a friends game, more each year:** `much-better-with-friends` 86,
  rising 4.1 → 10.0 per 100; `works-solo` is 19, against 1 bullet that calls it punishing alone; co-op called
  rare in its genre 3. **Online play draws almost no complaints.** In 1,499 reviews there is 1 bullet on
  a disconnect losing the run, 1 on a disconnect that did not (the save kept it,
  `a-disconnect-does-not-lose-the-run`), 2 on matchmaking, 2 on smooth online play and 2 that name online
  play without saying what. One review asks for the invite to drop a friend straight into the game
  (226199324). Teammates can trade items (`teammates-can-trade-items`, 2, praised); who gets the loot is
  unclear (`who-gets-the-loot-is-unclear`, 1); one save slot for solo and co-op together
  (`only-one-saved-run`, 1); bugs mostly in multiplayer (`mostly-in-multiplayer`, 1); it slows late in a
  multiplayer run (`slows-late-in-a-multiplayer-run`, 1). One review says it can be played with as many
  as 40 friends (232914887); that is the reviewer's claim, not checked. 2 want split screen. **Nothing
  said** on who hosts, bots, friendly fire or crossplay.
- **Combat and feel.** **Too strong is the fun, until it is boring.** `makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong`
  is 37 and `impactful` 22; `every-option-plays-differently` 14. Complaints: hitboxes off
  (`the-hitboxes-are-off`, 4, game-only); attacks you cannot answer (`no-counterplay`, 5); one option
  dominates (5); upgrades make aiming unnecessary (`upgrades-make-aiming-unnecessary`, 1); a damage cap
  stops you killing first (`a-damage-cap-stops-you-killing-first`, 1).
- **Movement and controls.** Quick, responsive movement is the feel players name: `responsive` 39 (2.6
  per 100), falling 3.2 → 1.3 per 100 over time (section 3.1). One wants to cancel a move early
  (`wants-to-cancel-a-move-early`, 1); one says the cursor escapes to another monitor
  (`the-cursor-escapes-to-another-monitor`, 1). **Nothing said** with a count on controller support or
  rebinding.
- **Enemies, bosses and difficulty.** **Difficulty you set yourself** is praised:

  enemy type hated 6; bullet sponges 5; no counterplay 5; unfair spawns 3; threats unclear 3; badly
  scaled difficulty steps 3; bosses just bigger enemies 3 (game-only); slow shots still find you
  (`slow-shots-still-find-you`, 2). The Immortal Snail is an unkillable enemy that hunts you (2); the
  developer made it purple to see it better. One reviewer says the danger rises with how far you go, not
  with a clock as in Risk of Rain (175059509). Made harder for the dedicated at casual players' cost
  (1, 151186055). Drawbacks you choose for bigger rewards are praised once
  (`drawbacks-you-choose-for-bigger-rewards`, 1).
- **Progression, loot and randomness.** **Nothing carries over between runs, and some want it to.** 4
  reviews want a talent tree or upgrades between runs (game-only); 1 more says losing everything makes
  play feel worthless (`nothing-from-a-run-carries-into-the-next`). One review says only new weapons and
  cosmetics carry over (203287249). **One gun per run.** 2 reviews want to change or carry a second gun;
  one gun gets boring after many loops (`the-weapon-is-fixed-for-the-run`, game-only); 2 want more than one
  crab, each with its own playstyle (`only-one-character-to-play`). **Repeated rewards are noticed.** 5 reviews say
  several achievements give the same skin (`two-achievements-give-the-same-reward`, game-only). 1 says
  the best skin is nearly impossible to get. `plenty-to-hunt` 16; builds `deep-and-varied` 41. Luck:

- **Runs, content and replay.** `keeps-pulling-you-back` 78. **The run can loop on and on**

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **How it feels to move is judged by name. Make movement quick.**
   Evidence: quick movement praised 39 times (2.6 per 100). Reviews praising movement are 2.7 per 100
   here and 2.6 in Warframe; every other large group is at 0.5 or less, and "sluggish" is the common
   complaint elsewhere. The cross-game page says: in a third-person shooter, a slide, dash or jump that
   feels quick is praised by name, and a slow one is the complaint.
   Dominion could: make sprint, slide, dodge and jump snappy, with little delay, and test them early with
   players.
   Strength: strong.
2. **Let players get absurdly strong inside a run.**
   Evidence: feeling far too strong praised 37 times, rising 1.5 → 3.9 per 100 (Risk of Rain 2 3.8);
   builds deep and varied 41; players enjoy the frame rate dropping as the build explodes (3 of 5).
   Dominion could: let run power stack into big, visible effects near the end of a run.
   Strength: strong.
3. **But keep the danger rising with the power, or end the run.**
   Evidence: power outgrows the challenge 6 (*"going on forever feels pointless"*, 150108738); runs out
   fast 8; balance assumes restarting for a god build 4; the fan's thumbs down asks for a harder game
   with fewer upgrades (226203004). One reviewer likes that danger rises with distance, not a clock
   (175059509).
   Dominion could: tie difficulty to how deep the run goes; use extraction as the natural end so a run
   does not go stale; make normal builds win, not only god builds.
   Strength: medium.
4. **Big effects must not kill the host's frame rate.**
   Evidence: slows at the highest levels 5 (only in this game). Most enjoy it here, but Dominion is
   hosted on one player's machine.
   Dominion could: cap effects and projectile counts, and merge damage numbers, so a strong build does
   not lag the host and every client.
   Strength: weak (small count), but the risk is larger on a listen server.
5. **Co-op is the reason people stay, and solo must still work.**
   Evidence: better with friends 86, rising 4.1 → 10.0 per 100; works solo 19 against 1; co-op called
   rare in its genre 3.
   Dominion could: put co-op at the center of the pitch and keep solo runs fair.
   Strength: strong.
6. **Protect the run when someone drops, and make joining one click.**
   Evidence: online play draws almost no complaints, but 1 lost a run on a disconnect and 1 kept it
   because of the save; 1 asks for an invite that drops a friend straight in (226199324); 1 has one save
   slot for solo and co-op.
   Dominion could: save run state so a dropped player can rejoin; let invites join a run in progress;
   keep solo and co-op saves clear.
   Strength: weak to medium.
7. **Be clear about who gets the loot, and let teammates share.**
   Evidence: trading items praised 2; who gets the loot unclear 1.
   Dominion could: show on screen who a drop is for, and allow trades inside the team.
   Strength: weak.
8. **Many difficulty dials win praise.**
   Evidence: difficulty tuned in detail 16 (highest in the corpus), rising 0.0 → 2.2 per 100; hard in a
   good way 20, rising 0.3 → 2.2. The developer saw fans split between a fair challenge and the hardest
   possible; one review says it was made harder for the dedicated at casual players' cost (151186055).
   Dominion could: offer separate dials (enemy count, damage, modifiers) and a top rank for the
   hardcore, without making the normal game harder for everyone.
   Strength: medium.
9. **A big update that removes loved content splits fans.**
   Evidence: after the Island Update complaints went 12.7 → 24.8 per 100; made it worse 7; old islands
   removed 3; wants the old version back 3; map edge cannot be seen 2; unkillable snail hunting you 2;
   the fan review says new maps *"feel like simple arenas"*.
   Dominion could: add new maps beside old ones rather than replace them; give each map its own gimmick;
   make map edges easy to see; test hunter-type enemies before shipping.
   Strength: medium.
10. **Some players want something to carry between runs.**
    Evidence: 4 want a talent tree between runs; 1 says losing everything makes play feel worthless; only
    weapons and cosmetics carry over, one reviewer says (203287249).
    Dominion could: let extraction bank something lasting (unlocks, a light talent tree), as Dominion
    plans.
    Strength: weak to medium.
11. **One fixed gun per run gets boring after many loops.**
    Evidence: 2 want to swap guns or carry a second.
    Dominion could: allow at least one weapon swap or a second slot in a run.
    Strength: weak.
12. **Do not repeat rewards across achievements.**
    Evidence: two achievements give the same reward 5; best skin nearly impossible 1; plenty to hunt 16.
    Dominion could: give each challenge its own reward and keep the hardest reachable.
    Strength: weak.
13. **Make threats readable.**
    Evidence: attacks you cannot answer 5; enemies spawn with no time to react 3; cannot tell what is
    attacking 3; hitboxes off 4; the developer recoloured the snail to make it easier to see.
    Dominion could: give each attack a clear tell, keep spawns out of sight lines near players, and match
    hitboxes to models.
    Strength: weak to medium.
14. **Make bosses more than bigger enemies.**
    Evidence: bosses just bigger enemies 3; one enemy type hated 6; bullet sponges 5.
    Dominion could: give each boss its own moves and rules.
    Strength: weak.
15. **A fair price earns goodwill.**
    Evidence: fair price 69; one reviewer says it costs a quarter of Risk of Rain 2.
    Dominion could: price low enough that friends will buy it together.
    Strength: medium.
16. **In Early Access, a steady update rhythm is praised and silence is noticed.**
    Evidence: steady stream 24; awaiting promised content 27; too slow 6 (4 in 2026); the developer said
    the next update had no firm date (2026-03-18).
    Dominion could: post a simple roadmap and keep a regular rhythm; say early when a date slips.
    Strength: medium.
17. **Do not show modes in a trailer you will not ship.**
    Evidence: 2 launch reviews want the parkour and one-on-one modes an earlier trailer showed.
    Dominion could: show only what is coming soon.
    Strength: weak.
18. **Some co-op players want PvP or extraction next to the main game.**
    Evidence: 5 of 8 missing-mode complaints want PvP, duels or teams; 1 asks for an extraction version
    (230547405); 2 want split screen.
    Dominion could: note the interest; it does not mean PvP belongs in Dominion.
    Strength: weak.
19. **Music can carry identity, but it fades as a talking point.**
    Evidence: music fits 39, falling 3.5 → 0.9 per 100; 3 miss the creator's song.
    Dominion could: give runs a strong sound identity.
    Strength: weak.
20. **A rival that slips sends players your way.**
    Evidence: 43 reviews name Risk of Rain; all 9 "beats its rivals" bullets are against it; 2 came
    because Risk of Rain 2 got worse for them.
    Dominion could: know which game it will be compared to and what that game's players miss.
    Strength: weak to medium.

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **The sample is 5.6% of the population.** Whole-sample
  shares carry ±2.50%. The periods carry about ±4 to ±7. The after-update period is only 230 reviews
  (about ±7): the direction is clear, the size is not.
- **Two months carry ×20**: the launch month (2023-04) and 2025-03. Weighted shares lean on them.
- **41% of bullets say nothing specific.** The specific counts are small: no complaint reaches 10. Most
  months are read at 20 to 54 reviews.
- **89 reviews (5.9%) were edited after creation and are counted on the created date.** So an edit about
  the Island Update can sit in an earlier month (188959484, created 2025-02, edited 2025-11-12).
- **Update dates come from Steam's announcements for app 774801**
  (https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/774801). Only the titles and opening text were read.
- **Not checked:** the 40-player co-op claim (232914887), what carries between runs (203287249), and
  the reviewers' account of Risk of Rain 2's decline.
- **A cartoon game about crabs**; its humour is part of the praise, so tone lessons may not carry to a
  serious sci-fi game.
- **Slurs and jabs were recorded as "a jab" and not repeated. Personal and family details were left out.**
- **1 review was written in another language** (Norwegian), filed on the language tag. About 4,000
  reviews in other languages exist on Steam; none were pulled.
