## 1. The short version

**Players love it for being hard (333 remarks), for its music (284), for the pull of one more run (236)
and for the clock that makes every minute harder (116 who call it the thrill, against 9 who dislike
being rushed).**

**The top complaint is getting a friend into your game: 115 say you need the host's IP address, opened
ports or Hamachi, and 187 reviews raise some online-play problem.** It peaked in 2016-2018 at about one
review in ten.

**From 2019 the game is measured against its own sequel (80 say *Risk of Rain 2* is better, 35 the
reverse), and from November 2023 against its remake (56 say buy Risk of Rain Returns instead, 5 that
the original is better).**

**The thumb fell twice: to 81% once the remake was out, and to 60% in April-August 2025, when 30 of 39
thumbs down protested a new end-user agreement or the owners.** It came back to 84% after.

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**A co-op game whose online play takes setup outside the game loses players at the door for its whole
life — and the fix that finally works is a new game.** From 2013 to 2018 one review in twenty to one in
ten said joining a friend needed the host's IP address, opened ports or a program like Hamachi (115 notes,
187 reviews with an online-play problem, 43 of them thumbs down); 6 called the "play with friends" pitch
deceptive. Players kept the game and the four-pack because the run itself — hard, timed, a climb to
godlike power — was loved: 333 hard-and-fun, 236 one-more-run, 177 better with friends. The complaint
faded only as players left for the sequel (online play that works) and the remake (released 2023-11-08,
which 56 reviews recommend in its place). Three reviewers object that owners had to buy again to get it (55235122, 140513766, 165912172).

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- The sample covers 156 months; most hold 20 reviews read, so any one month is ±22%. Use the six periods
  on the English page.
- The end-user agreement (date, terms, "spyware") is not checked; it is not on the store page. The
  Visual C++ fix, the Spike Strip cause, the console versions' online play and the game's origin story
  are reviewers' accounts.
- 978 of the 3,104 reviews say nothing but a thumbs up; most later reviews are 15 words or fewer.
- Batches 1-48 were read at twelve a firing; a blind audit found no drop in accuracy against six a firing
  (`qc/pace-test-2-2026-10-07/`).
