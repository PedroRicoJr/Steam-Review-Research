## 1. ⭐ The headline number is an average of two opposite trends

**Split by the month the review was written, this game has a best month and a worst month, and they
are three months apart.**

| Review month | Thumbs up | Thumbs down | % up | `patch-quality` good / bad |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-05 launch | 343 | 139 | 71.2% | 24 / 5 |
| 2026-06 Update 1 | 162 | 56 | **74.3%** | 29 / 5 |
| 2026-07 hotfix 01.02 | 40 | 23 | 63.5% | 4 / 4 |
| 2026-08 | 21 | 18 | 53.8% | 2 / 2 |
| 2026-09 (partial) | 6 | 6 | **50.0%** | 2 / 2 |

**A 24-point fall from June to September.** The aggregate 70.3% is dominated by the launch month,
which is 59% of the sample.

**The order is checkable and the text moved first.** `live-ops.patch-quality` was 5.8 : 1 positive in
June and 1 : 1 in July. **The thumb rate turned in July and kept falling.** What people wrote changed
a month before how they voted.

> *"Patch RC: EA: 01.02 broke the game. No one bothered to actually playtest their patch… Their first
> big patch was a great one, but they decided to absolutely decimate the game with a 'hotfix'."*
> (`229967213`)

## 2. ⭐ The most-named thing in the game is a disagreement, not a complaint

`game-design.session-flexibility.a-clock-decides-when-you-leave` — **112 bullets, the largest mode in
the game**, ahead of every observation about combat, art, price or progression.

| Who raised the run timer | Bullets |
|---|---|
| Reviewers who gave a thumbs **up** | **69** |
| Reviewers who gave a thumbs **down** | 43 |

**61.6% of the people who raise the timer recommend the game.** Across ten batches the split stayed
inside **60.7 – 62.7%** — the most stable measurement in this study — **and it did not move when the
thumb rate collapsed.**

> *"I also like the timer, because it actually put pressure on me"* (`226877922`)
> *"The timer makes this game impossible especially if solo"* (`227471291`)

**The mode is named for the complaint and carries a 3:2 positive majority.** A study that had tagged
the timer as a complaint would have reported the opposite of what these reviews say.

## 4. ⭐ The third-largest mode is a defence of the game

**`marketing.reputation.judged-unfairly` at 83 bullets** — ahead of every complaint except the timer
and the shallow upgrades. **Roughly one review in ten is written to argue with this game's own review
page rather than to describe the game.**

> *"I really struggle to understand the majority of the reviews for this game."* (`226862716`)
> *"I think you should be required to take a written test before leaving a review for this game."*
> (`227460186`)

**No other game in this corpus has this.** The mode was built during Back 4 Blood and sat mostly
unused until now.

## 6. ⭐ The cast complaint is 0.4% of the corpus

Three modes carry it: `characters-writing.cast-politics-put-me-off` (4),
`world-and-setting.politics-put-me-off` (3), `art.character-design.cast-is-off-putting` (2).

**Nine bullets out of 2,643.** The run timer alone is 112.

**It is loud inside individual reviews and a rounding error in the counts.** A studio reading its
forum would weight those two things similarly. **The counts do not.**

## 7. The shared upgrade pool, and the constituency the loudest complaint hides

**62 bullets complain about it** — `teammates-can-take-your-things` 47 and
`the-design-sets-players-against-each-other` 15 — the most-complained-about system in the game.
Update 1 loosened it. **Two reviewers then filed thumbs down over the fix.**

> *"the devs bowed to people who play with randoms and took out all the teamwork and strategizing so
> now you just play by yourself with other people"* (`228588560`)
> *"they turned a fun team game into a solo game you can play with friends"* (`228588082`)

**`co-op-design`'s complaint share fell from 68% to 54% across the run**, entirely because of when
the reviews were written, not because the tagging changed.

## 8. What this game is not being criticised for

**Read against the complaint list, the absences matter as much as the presences.**

- **`engineering.stability`** — 9 bullets in 814 reviews, of which 4 are repeated crashes.
- **`publishing.monetisation-practice`** — **4 bullets**, three of them people buying in to support
  the studio and **one** complaint about microtransactions in a paid game.
- **`localization`** — 1 bullet.
- **`accessibility`** — 2 bullets.

**A studio triaging by volume would not touch any of these.**

## 9. The limits of this document

1. **English only.** 7,990 of 15,210 all-language reviews. Nothing here speaks for the other 7,220.
2. **±3.26%, not ±2.5%.** The launch month is covered at 8.0% and every other month at about 16%.
   **The under-covered month is 75% of the group**, so month-level figures for 2026-05 are the
   weakest numbers here.
3. **The game is in early access and still changing.** Every figure is a photograph taken 2026-09-02.
   Section 1 exists because the photograph moved while it was being taken.
4. **19.4% of these reviews were edited after posting** and the summariser flattens each one onto its
   creation date. A review written in May and rewritten in August is counted in May.
