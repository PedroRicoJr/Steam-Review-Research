page exists for.** Full read: `findings/scp-abhorrent-english.md`. Plain-words lessons for Dominion:

| Read | 69 of 69 English reviews at the pull — a census, no margin of error — 2022-03 to 2026-07 |

---

## 1. The short version

**Players are laughing at it:** the most common real praise is funny writing (11), but 4 of those 11
are thumbs down — the opening scene is "so bad it's good". **The only sincere praise is the music**
(7, all thumbs up). **The one honest complaint is the dark** (6, 4 thumbs down), and a brightness
setting added after one review turned that reviewer's thumb up. **28% of reviews say nothing at all.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**A count of praise cannot tell when players are laughing at a game.** This is the only game in the
block with more praise than complaint, and the reason is that its largest real praise — funny writing,
11 times — is mostly people enjoying how bad it is, with 4 of the 11 voting thumbs down. Read as a
number, it looks better than Helldivers 2's ratio; read as text, it is a joke told straight.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- Every English review at the pull was read; one more has arrived since.
- 42 of 69 reviews are from 2022; no trend is claimed.
- 42 reviews in other languages were not read.
- The brightness patch is what a reviewer says, not checked.
- The study has no way to mark praise as ironic.
