## 1. The short version

**Players love it as a real military simulation and stay for what other players build on it: 319 notes
praise the realism, 256 the mods, 171 a fan-made mode such as Altis Life, King of the Hill or Antistasi.**

**The top complaint is performance: 156 notes say it runs badly and 79 that it needs a strong computer,**
though both fall by about two thirds after 2019.

**Paid add-ons are the sharpest complaint: 111 reviews object to locked gear, "buy this" pop-ups in play,
servers that need add-ons, or the 2026 subscription,** and 63 of them are thumbs down.

**It is hard to learn (107 reviews), and the advice is always the same: play with friends (112) or join a
group of players (43).**

**One player running the match for the others, Zeus, is praised in 31 notes and named in 35 reviews, with
no complaint about the idea.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**A game that hands players the tools to run it for each other lasts for over a decade, as long as it
doesn't charge them to play together.** Zeus, where one player directs the enemy side live, is praised in 31
notes with no complaint about the idea, and fan-made modes carry most of what people play (171 notes). What
turns players away is anything that splits a group by what each person paid for: servers that need add-ons
(21), gear locked behind purchases (36), "buy this" pop-ups mid-fight (25) and, in 2026, a monthly pass
(6 notes, one with 296 helpful votes). For Rico's bounty hunter, this is the strongest evidence yet that a
fifth player who runs or hunts the squad is wanted; it also says such a role must never sit behind a
purchase the squad lacks.
