## 1. The short version

**The game's public face is a fight about fan service:** 12 reviews call the shadow under the skirts
censorship, 9 of them thumbs down, and they carry 52% of every helpful vote. **Under the fight, the
real complaint is the price** — 22 say $49.99 is too much, 14 of them still recommending it. **The
real design complaint is the floor key that is used up when you use it** (9), with runs of two to
seven hours (8) and no save. **What players like is the jokes, the shooting and that it runs**, every
one of those bullets a thumbs up.

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**A handful of one-line reviews can become the whole store page.** Twelve reviews, five of them under
ten words, carry 750 helpful votes — 52% of all the helpful votes on the game — and the seven
most-helpful English reviews are all the same claim. A buyer sorting by "most helpful" learns nothing
about how it plays: that the floor key is used up, that runs last hours, that the shooting is good.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- Every English review was read, but only eleven months exist and the last four months are 8 reviews.
- 119 reviews in other languages (51% of the audience) were not read.
- Whether the shadow is censorship cannot be settled from reviews.
- The aim-speed patch and the bundle prices are what reviewers say, not checked.
