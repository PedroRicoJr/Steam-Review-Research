## 1. The short version

**The other players are the best thing and the worst thing, and the studio's answer to them is the
argument.**

- **The most-said specific thing is praise for the tension of not knowing who to trust** — 95
  bullets, 6.4 per 100 read.
- **Five of the top eight complaints are about the other players**: the wish for a mode without them
  (55), griefers (42), the false friendly (38), cheaters (38), exit campers (27). **The first complaint
  about the game's own systems is ninth.**
- **The thumb went from 86% in the launch month to 52% from 2026-05 on, and the thing that rose was
  cheating** — 0.9 to 12.4 per 100, with *the studio does nothing about it* one step behind.
- **The conduct matchmaker has six modes in the tree now, one praise and five complaints, and all six
  came from this game.**
- **31% of bullets are `unknown`**, 591 of them a wordless thumbs up — 40% of the sample.

## 5. ⭐ The finding: the cheating line, and the two populations

**Praise and complaint per 100 readers, by period:**

| Period | Reviews | Thumbs up | Praise/100 | Complaint/100 | Mean hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025-10 to 11 | 661 | **85.8%** | **100.3** | 46.3 | 58 |
| 2025-12 to 2026-01 | 456 | 81.1% | 79.6 | 50.4 | 89 |
| 2026-02 to 04 | 243 | 73.3% | 75.7 | 67.1 | 140 |
| 2026-05 to 09 | 113 | **52.2%** | 59.3 | **112.4** | 282 |

**`cheaters-spoil-matches` goes 0.9 → 2.4 → 2.9 → 12.4 per 100 across the four periods.**
`does-nothing-about-the-cheaters` goes 0.2 → 1.5 → 2.5 → 7.1. `exploit-ruins-the-game` — the
duplication bug and the unprotected API — is 0.1 per 100 in 2025 and 1.2 in 2026. **Design complaints
fall over the same span** (the third player swooping 1.1 → 0.5, *dying costs nothing* 1.0 → 0.3, lag
1.0 → 0.2). **The 2026 reviewer argues with the lobby, not with the design.**

**The two populations.** 147 reviews carry only positive conduct modes, 158 only negative ones, and
**11 carry both**. The people who write *"be careful who you trust"* as praise and the people who
write *"gunned down for three matches straight"* as complaint are not, in this sample, the same
people. The tree records what the player felt, so both stand — and the game's reputation is the
ratio between them, which the sample puts at about 1 : 1 and falling.

**The matchmaker is the design's attempt to keep the two apart, and the sample is its failure
analysis.** Section 4 of the English page lists all six modes with quotes. In one line: it works when
conduct is consistent; it misreads self-defence; it can be gamed; it concentrates the cheaters where
the fighting is; and it degrades as the population falls.

## 6. The open method questions this game raised

**None is answered here. All are Rico's.**

1. **Flattening.** 172 reviews (11.7%) carry a later edit and are counted on the created date. In a
   game whose story is *good at launch, cheaters later*, the edits are mostly negative and land on
   2025-11. **The cheating line above is understated by this.** Open since Helldivers 2.
2. **The missing `game-design.loot` subject (gap 331).** This game is where the evidence lives:
   loot rooms, blueprint drop rates, the dupe economy. Filed on `level-design.unknown`,
   `nerfs-what-players-liked` and `exploit-ruins-the-game` for now.
3. **Ethics complaints about generated voices with no refusal attached (gaps 327/334).** Four voices in
   this game; `the-voices-are-generated-and-it-shows` records the quality complaint and loses the
   ethics one. Home undecided.
4. **The publisher-communication split** does not arise here — Embark is both studio and publisher.

## 7. What this page cannot tell you

1. **What the other languages think.** About 164,000 reviews unread; none pulled.
2. **Anything about launch day.** 19 reviews for two days; 2025-11 is the launch month in practice.
3. **Whether the last five months are real.** 113 reviews; a single review moves 2026-09 by ten points.
   The direction is consistent across all five months, which is the only reason it is reported.
4. **Whether the studio did anything.** No patch notes were pulled. Two events the reviews name —
   a 2026-03-31 balance patch and a currency wipe around 2026-04 — are unconfirmed from the studio's side.
5. **Whether the conduct matchmaker is broken or the population fell below what it needs.** Five
   reviewers tie the failure to the falling count; the sample cannot separate the two causes, and by
   the settled rule (2026-09-10) the tree records what the player felt, not the root cause.
