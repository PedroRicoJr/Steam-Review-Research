## 1. ⭐ The thumb on this game is a vote on whether the store tags lied

**Nineteen of 89 reviews — one in five — argue about the adult tags, and the two sides split the thumb
almost perfectly.** (Twenty bullets; one review makes the claim twice.)

| Claim | Mode | Bullets | Thumbs up | Thumbs down |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The tags promise adult content the game does not have | `marketing.positioning.sold-as-a-different-kind-of-game` | 12 | 3 | **9** |
| The tags never promised it; buyers did not read | `marketing.expectation-management.the-mismatch-was-the-buyers-fault` | 8 | **8** | 0 |

> *"It promises nudity, sexual content, NSFW and fails to deliver."* (`148135531`, 147 helpful — the
> most-upvoted review of the game)
> *"Game literally does not say that is has sex or nudity in it, if you read the description."*
> (`148267026`)
> *"people are judging the game because the user tags says nsfw... the 'user tags'"* (`168049101`)

🔑 **The disagreement is not about the game. It is about who wrote the tags.** Three reviewers say
outright that the tags are Steam's user tags, not the studio's. One (`163896162`) argues the game
cannot be pornographic because it is sold in Germany. **The tree can record both positions and
cannot settle which is true** — the store page as it stood in 2023 is not in the corpus.

**This is the game's entire argument.** Strip the nineteen tag reviews out and the remaining 70 are
77.1% positive with almost nothing to fight about.

## 2. "Short, but cheap" is the whole case for buying it

| Mode | Bullets | Thumbs up | Thumbs down |
|---|---|---|---|
| `publishing.price.fair` | 14 | **14** | 0 |
| `production.content-amount.too-little` | 14 | **12** | 2 |

**The two largest modes in the game are the same sentence.** Twelve of the fourteen people who say
it is too short recommend it anyway, and every one of the fourteen who mention the price says it is
fair. The playthrough is two hours by most accounts; one reviewer finished the story in thirteen
minutes (`154333287`).

> *"I beat the game in 2 hours, so i wouldnt necessarily recommend it, but its not bad enough to not
> recommend it at the price its being sold at."* (`148299530`)

**Eight reviews (9%) say outright that they are grading against a one-person studio** —
`review.grades-it-against-the-studios-size`, 7 of 8 thumbs up. Nobody in the nine games before this
used that ruler as often, in share.

## 3. The text is more negative than the thumb, again

**73.0% positive, and 136 complaints against 72 pieces of praise.** The ratio of 0.53 is the fourth
most negative text in the corpus, below every game except Terminull Brigade, Redfall and The
Anacrusis — all of which are under 58% on the thumb.

**63.2% of the complaints were written by people who recommend the game** — 86 of 136. Only Deep
Rock Galactic is higher, at 68.5%, and its recommenders write 0.17 complaints each against 1.32 here.

🔑 **Every one of the top complaints except the tag argument is majority thumbs-up:** too little
content 12 of 14, poorly explained where to go 4 of 7, bullet sponges 2 of 4. **Recommenders here
are writing a wish list to the developer, not a verdict** — see §4.

⚠️ **2025 and 2026 are 17 reviews together.** The direction is clear; the percentages are not
load-bearing.

**Five reviews are addressed to the developer rather than to a buyer**
(`developer-communication.written-to-the-studio-not-to-the-buyer` ×3 and two more that read that
way). One says outright that the thumb is encouragement: *"give a good review first... a little
encouragement to the production team"* (`148210236`). **That claim has no mode; it is gap 319.**

## 5. What the tree learned — almost nothing, which is the point

**118 distinct modes; two used by no other game; zero built.** The two are
`localization.voice-localization.no-dub-available` (all in-game audio is Chinese or Japanese, 2
sightings) and `accessibility.phobia.unknown` (spider sections, 1 sighting).

**`game-design` is 33.7% of bullets** — close to the co-op average of 30.3%, and far below
Immortal: Unchained's 53.6%. A game with two hours of content generates two hours of design
commentary. **What fills the gap is `marketing` (9.5%), `publishing` (7.6%) and `production` (8.0%)
— the tags, the price, and how much you get.**

**The ninth game's genre test holds on the tenth.** A second single-player game, from a different
genre and a one-person studio, needed nothing new.
