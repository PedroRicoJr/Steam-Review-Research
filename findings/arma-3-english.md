## 1. In plain words

**Players love it for being a real military simulation, and they stay for what other players build on
top of it.** 319 notes praise it as a true simulation rather than an arcade shooter, the top named praise.
256 say mods extend the game and 171 that a fan-made mode is what they play: Altis Life role-play (39
reviews name it), King of the Hill (33) and Antistasi (14). 413 reviews mention mods or the Workshop.

**It is well liked, and the people who stay, stay for years.** 90.7% thumbs up; the
median review shows 98 hours, and 438 reviews show 1,000 or more. Thumbs-down reviewers have a median of 15
hours: those who complain mostly left early.

**The top complaint is that it runs badly, on any computer.** 156 notes say it is poorly optimised and 79
that it needs strong hardware; 233 reviews raise one or both, 51 of them thumbs down. Players blame the CPU
and the old engine ("the best CPU and GPU ... and still only get 50 frames", 178567584).

**Paid add-ons are the sharpest complaint, and they split the servers.** 111 reviews complain about paid
content: gear locked behind add-ons, pop-up "buy this" ads in the middle of play, servers that need add-ons
to join, and since April 2026 a monthly subscription. 63 of the 305 thumbs-down reviews carry one of these.
34 reviews call the add-ons fair, many of them about the free-to-try system.

**It is hard to learn, and the game does little to help.** 107 reviews say it is overwhelming, poorly
explained, or has too many keys; 65 that it is rewarding once learned. The advice players give each other
is the same every year: join a group ("unit") of players (43 notes), or play with friends (112).

**The surprise: one player running the game for the others is a draw of its own.** 31 notes praise Zeus,
the mode where a player acts as game master, spawning enemies and setting objectives live; 35 reviews name
it. "Zeus is like D&D" (25913914); "one of the best game modes to ever grace a multiple player game"
(132862728).

**Over thirteen years, the praise thinned but the thumbs stayed up.** Praise per 100 reviews fell from 96
in 2013 to 42-44 since 2020 as reviews got shorter; complaints fell from about 50 to about 25 per 100;
thumbs up stayed at 88-92%. Performance complaints fell by about two thirds as computers caught up.

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play.** Friends (112) and groups (43) carry it; 22 say you need a group, 7 that it is
  poor with strangers, 15 that the fanbase puts them off, 12 griefers. Servers: 17 can't find games, 16 lag
  and desync, 5 blocked by the anti-cheat, a few can't connect or face hundreds of ping (237157970). The quick-play
  button is "completely broken" (233542929); 4 can't start their own game. 1 wants co-op in the main
  campaign, as Apex has (26746541).
- **Combat and feel.** Ballistics and weapons behave as expected (17); one shot kills ("one bullet is a death
  sentence", 136757030); stamina ruins aim (10); hitboxes (4); "no spray-and-pray" is praised. Shooting
  "intentionally feels clunky; team work, positioning, and tactics are vastly more important" (202196105).
- **Movement and controls.** Too many keys (28), takes hours to get used to (13), awkward default layout (6),
  no jump or vault (6), rebinding breaks other keys (3); vehicles handle badly (9). One player with a motor
  limitation could not play on mouse and keyboard and wants controller support (219479274).
- **Enemies and difficulty.** The AI sees through grass, smoke and walls (26) or is dumb (20); one
  long review (213998490, 11 found it helpful) says both, and that friendly AI works only when commanded.
  Satisfyingly hard (23), too hard (5).
- **Progression, loot and randomness.** None in the usual sense. The Virtual Arsenal is play in itself: "60-
  70% of these hours were spent in the 'virtual armory' playing soldier barbie" (196098637, 22 found it
  helpful); 15 praise deep loadouts.
- **Runs, content and replay.** No runs. Keeps pulling you back (75); stories from matches (55); plenty (14)
  against too little (10). The maps are too big: long walks and helicopter waits (36).
- **Money and price.** Section 3b. Price fair (36) against buy on sale (36); "$9 is way less than you expect"
  (151185607).
- **Tech.** Poorly optimised (156), demanding (79), runs fine on modest hardware (12), better than it used to
  (5: "runs NOTICABLY better than how it did a few years ago", 213998490); bugs (43) but many "harmless and
  funny" (45: trucks that explode on pebbles, tanks that fly); old bugs never fixed (14); saves lost
  (222756109); loading screens (3); a huge install with mods (5).
- **The studio and the community.** Steady updates (21) and listening (4) against lost trust (15), and one
  claim that the studio threatened a modder with legal action (49573340, reviewer's claim). Reforger is
  named as worse by 7 ("more playable than arma reforger", 237259997); 12 want Arma 4.
- **What players asked for.** Arma 4 (12); jump and vault (6); co-op in the main campaign; better
  optimisation; AI that can't see through grass and smoke, built in rather than modded (213998490); no
  subscriptions, battle passes or loot boxes in Arma 4 (224467725); controller support (219479274).

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5. **Rico's idea: players can join a Dominion run as an
enemy bounty hunter.**

1. **A player who directs the enemy side is a loved mode in its own right.** 31 notes and 35 reviews praise
   Zeus; no review complains about the idea. A Dominion "director" role (spawning enemies, setting a bounty,
   steering a run) would sit beside the bounty hunter as a second way for a fifth player to join. *Strong.*
2. **The fun depends on who runs it, so give the director limits and tools.** "Who you play with matters";
   Zeus is "harder than fighting on the field". A budget, cooldowns and a short list of tools would keep a
   new director from ruining a run. *Medium.*
3. **Let the director play alone against the AI too.** Several of the keenest Zeus players use it solo
   (83688330, 222180917). A sandbox where one player builds a fight against bots doubles as a tutorial for
   the role. *Weak.*
4. **Never put a "buy this" pop-up inside play.** The pop-ups are the most quoted money complaint in the
   sample, from 2014 to 2024, and the subscription drew a thumbs down with 296 helpful votes. *Strong.*
5. **Never split the group by what each player owns.** Servers that need add-ons to join (21), and a squad
   member who can't use the gear, are a reason to leave. If Dominion sells content, let anyone join any
   run, and let a host's content be usable by guests. *Strong.*
6. **Fan-made modes outlived the official ones.** 171 notes play a mode the studio never made. Even a small
   set of hooks for players to make their own runs or rules can keep a game alive for years. *Medium.*
7. **Teach in the game, not on YouTube.** 107 reviews find it overwhelming or poorly explained; the showcases
   and pop-up tips are praised when they exist (214761932, 233542929). Dominion's first run should teach the
   controls without a wiki. *Strong.*
8. **Keep the controls few.** "2-4 controls bound to each key" (182847146); too many keys (28). *Medium.*
9. **Run well on ordinary computers.** The top complaint for thirteen years; thumbs-down reviews quote frame
   rates. Unreal Engine 5 on a host's machine needs a performance budget for the host. *Strong.*
10. **Make deaths feel fair and readable.** Enemies that see through bushes and smoke (26) are the main AI
    complaint; "the only game that doesn't make me mad when I die" (213335318) is praise for the reverse.
    *Medium.*
11. **Funny physics is forgiven; lost progress is not.** 45 notes enjoy the bugs; saves lost and runs broken
    by bugs draw thumbs down. *Medium.*
12. **Price a friend's way in.** "Got in only because friends bought it" (214761932); much better with
    friends (112). A friend pass or a cheap way to join a host's run fits a four-player game. *Weak.*

## 7. Confidence and limits

- 3,264 of 140,386 reviews, ±2.00%; periods ±3.7% to ±6.9%; each review in 2019-11 stands for about 166.
- 2,078 reviews are 15 words or fewer, so most modes rest on the minority of longer reviews. Notes per
  review drift from about 1.75 in the first eleven batches to 1.1-1.5 in later ones, a content effect noted round by
  round (`batch_quality.py`).
- 420 reviews (12.9%) were edited later and are counted on the day first written; a few edits (for example
  171190279, edited 2026) speak about later events.
- **Checked:** the store facts (Bohemia Interactive, 2013-09-12, $29.99 and $49.99 editions, Apex and Contact
  included, the Arma 3 Pass at $8.99 a month, Zeus in the store text, the categories), Steam's counts.
- **Not checked:** when the subscription began, frame rates and hardware claims, BattlEye bans, the legal
  threat claim, AI behaviour claims, how many servers need which add-on, mod sizes, player counts.
- Slurs, jabs and political remarks are recorded only as such; personal, family and health details are left
  out (one motor limitation is kept only as that).
- 6 reviews excluded: two describe other games, three are copied texts (one, the "this game saved my life"
  text, appears word for word in Back 4 Blood, Earth Defense Force 5 and Mass Effect 3, where it was
  summarised at face value), one is a travel essay.
