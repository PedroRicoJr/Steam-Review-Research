## 1. In plain words

**This game is judged as a co-op game first.** "Much better with friends" is the most named praise
(125 bullets), more than four times the next praise of who you play with (fine with strangers, 28). Players who stay play a lot: half the
reviews show 43 hours or more, and 275 show over 100.

**The praise is for the challenge, the bosses and the characters.** Hard in a good way (61), keeps
pulling you back (60), the bosses are a highlight (47), each character plays its own way (38), and a
fresh mix of the studio's combat with runs that start over (33).

**The top complaint is that you cannot talk to your team.** 36 bullets say there is no voice or text
chat, only map markers. It is the second-highest rate of the 21 large English groups in the corpus
(2.8 per 100 reviews, after Aliens: Fireteam Elite at 3.4). Two players say the missing chat keeps
the game civil.

**At launch, two friends could not play alone together.** 26 bullets asked for a two-player mode, all
in the first two months. The studio added it on 2025-07-31, and the complaint stopped: not one after
July 2025.

**Many players feel they paid again for Elden Ring.** 28 bullets say the game is built from reused
Elden Ring parts — the highest rate in the corpus (2.2 per 100). 189 kept reviews name Elden Ring, and
37 of those are thumbs down. 16 say the game assumes you played Elden Ring, and 14 say it falls short
of the studio's earlier games; 16 others say it is made for those fans and better for it.

**Dropped connections cost whole runs.** 22 bullets report frequent disconnects, 8 say a drop lost
the run, 5 say they were penalised for leaving when they had no choice, and 2 that a crash is
punished like quitting. Most of these are from the launch summer.

**A year on, players say the game was left behind.** The add-on came on 2025-12-03 and the last patch
on 2026-01-16. Six of the seven "updates stopped" bullets come from July to October 2026. Players who
stayed beg for more (31 "more, please" bullets, the highest rate, 4.9 per 100, in the last period).

**The surprise: thumbs-down reviews come from players with real hours.** The 159 thumbs-down reviews
have a median of 25 hours; their most named reasons are reused content (14 reviews), luck deciding
the run (12), falling short of the studio's earlier games (12) and server drops (11). 54% of all
complaint bullets sit in thumbs-up reviews.

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play.** Much better with friends 125; needs a group 11; fine with strangers 28,
  poor with strangers 21; careless teammates 16, quitting 8, griefers 4, cheaters 3. No chat 36; good
  tools for coordinating 2; missing chat keeps it civil 2. No way to play as two 26 (fixed 2025-07-31);
  group too small 2. **Scaling**: the enemies stay scaled for players who left 2 (game-only: when
  teammates leave or get lost on the add-on map, the rest face bosses scaled for three, 222216652);
  scales to the number of players 2; more players makes it trivial 2 (a coordinated trio chain-staggers
  bosses, 201670146). **Character quests**: solo in practice 5 (only one player's quest can progress in
  a party; players with active quests could not be matched, 197828923). Frequent disconnects 22, run
  lost 8, cannot rejoin 3, punished for leaving 5; peer-to-peer rather than dedicated servers 2; no
  crossplay 15 (the highest rate in the corpus, 1.2 per 100) and no cross-save 1 (225610557). Revives:
  each revive gets harder 1; the boss turns away so melee must chase 3 (game-only).
- **Combat and feel.** Hits feel weighty 19; abilities that double as dodges 1 (197828923: *"most
  class abilities make you briefly untouchable"*). Stamina runs out too fast 1; hit while getting up
  1; a sword cannot block without a shield 1. **Lock-on**: switching targets is unreliable 4
  (game-only: it grabs a far target over the downed friend in front, 198146831). One character
  handles its own heavy weapons badly 1 (199895754, July 2025); the studio's 1.03.2 notes (2026-01-16)
  list faster attacks with greataxes, great hammers and colossal weapons among the character changes.
- **Movement and controls.** Responsive 11 — the sprint, climbing and no fall damage are praised

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **Ship voice and quick text chat, or a very rich ping system, on day one.**
   Evidence: no way to talk 36, the most named complaint, second-highest rate in the corpus; strangers
   poor 21 and careless 16; only 2 prefer silence.
   Dominion could: offer push-to-talk voice and a short set of quick messages, with mute and report;
   make pings mark enemies, items and intentions.
   Strength: strong.
2. **Support every group size from launch: one, two, three and four.**
   Evidence: 26 duo requests in two months, then none once patch 1.02 added it; balance "adjusted to
   the current number of players" (studio post).
   Dominion could: test and tune solo, two, three and four players before release and let any of them
   queue.
   Strength: strong.
3. **When a teammate leaves, rescale the fight.**
   Evidence: enemies stay scaled for players who left 2; players lost on the add-on map leave two to
   fight a boss built for three (222216652); griefed at a boss with no way out (197497249).
   Dominion could: rescale health and damage on a leave, and let late joiners fill the slot.
   Strength: medium.
4. **A dropped player must get back in, whole — and a crash is not quitting.**
   Evidence: disconnects 22, run lost 8, rejoin a level down 2, cannot rejoin 3, crash punished like
   quitting 2, punished for leaving when trapped 5. Runs are 30-45 minutes.
   Dominion could: hold a dropped player's slot and gear on the host for a time; never apply a leave
   penalty to a crash or a host drop; let a player leave without penalty once the others have gone.
   Strength: strong (Dominion's host-run sessions make drops more likely, not less).
5. **Let a lost boss be retried without replaying the whole run.**
   Evidence: harsh restart 12; *"why not retry the boss without the reward"* (208671319, 9 helpful);
   no time to explore 8.
   Dominion could: offer a practice retry or a checkpoint before the arena that costs the reward but
   not the time.
   Strength: medium.
6. **Bosses are the draw; give them openings and clear reads.**
   Evidence: bosses a highlight 47; hard in a good way 61; against that, no opening 2, ignores who has
   its attention 2, punishes standing close 1, one-hit kills 4, bullet sponges 6, a hated roaming boss
   26.
   Dominion could: give every boss attack a gap to punish, keep threat rules readable, and test roaming
   elites against fresh players.
   Strength: strong.
7. **Randomness must change what you do, not just what you get.**
   Evidence: not random enough 20, luck decides the run 19, the needed item never drops 15, level-ups
   change numbers not play 12; one map with fixed variants.
   Dominion could: vary arenas, objectives and enemy mixes per run; give a pity or reroll for key
   drops; make upgrade choices change how a weapon plays.
   Strength: strong.
8. **Reusing assets is fine if the game is plainly new — say what is new.**
   Evidence: recycled assets 28, the highest rate in the corpus, 14 of them thumbs down; fresh mix 33.
   Dominion could: show new enemies, maps and systems first in its store page and trailers.
   Strength: medium.
9. **Plan the support after launch, and say it out loud.**
   Evidence: steady updates praised through 2025 (made it better 14); after the last patch, updates
   stopped 7 and "more, please" peaks at 4.9 per 100; the add-on period has the lowest thumbs up.
   Dominion could: publish a road map, keep a steady beat of small additions, and say plainly when
   support ends.
   Strength: medium.
10. **Do not teach the game only through guides.**
    Evidence: assumes the earlier games 16; overwhelming at first 24; poorly explained 5; needs outside
    research 5; a steep start then praise (rewarding once learned 10).
    Dominion could: teach routing, threat and builds in the first runs; mark which bosses are beyond a
    level.
    Strength: medium.
11. **A lasting goal after the credits.**
    Evidence: nothing left to chase 8; runs out fast 8; beating the game switches off map events 1;
    the harder mode is the only reason left (208660420).
    Dominion could: keep variety on after the final boss and give a long goal that does not punish
    players for strangers' mistakes.
    Strength: medium.
12. **Crossplay and cross-save matter even for a PC-first game.**
    Evidence: no crossplay 15, the highest rate in the corpus; one wants cross-progression in 2026.
    Dominion could: plan the online layer so platforms can mix later.
    Strength: weak (Dominion is PC first; the evidence is about friends on consoles).

Questions for Rico were not raised by this page.

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **The sample is 1.3% of the population.** Whole-sample shares
  carry ±3.18%; the periods carry about ±3.4 to ±10.1 (the add-on period is 95 reviews).
- **The launch month is read at 0.38% and weighted ×264**, because the game came out on 2025-05-29 and
  three of four sampling windows were empty. Rico ruled on 2026-09-04 that short launch months are read
  as pulled; a re-pull is parked in `OPEN-WITH-RICO.md`. Every other month is ×17 to ×66.
- **25% of bullets say nothing specific**, and 63% of reviews are 15 words or fewer. Most complaint
  counts are under 30.
- **139 reviews (10.7%) were edited after creation and are counted on the created date**, so later
  verdicts (the duo patch, flipped thumbs) sit in earlier months.
- **Checked against primary sources**: the store page (developer, publishers, date, price, add-on
  contents and price) and the studio's Steam announcements (patch dates, the solo changes in 1.01.1,
  the duo mode in 1.02, Deep of Night, the add-on, the last patch 1.03.2). The director named in two
  reviews, Junya Ishizaki, was confirmed in round 703 (PlayStation Blog interview, 2025-05-07; PC
  Gamer).
- **Not checked:** the leave-penalty rules (reviewers disagree), the boss health cut for solo
  (197495319's account), "about 5% new content", the forum report of save corruption (199240157), a
  reviewer's claim that cheaters use VPNs, and the game a reviewer says the studio is making next.
- **Italic phrases with an id are mostly the wording of the summary bullet, not the review's own
  words.** The exact review text is in `raw/elden-ring-nightreign/english/sample/*.json`;
  `DOMINION-TAKEAWAYS.md` quotes the review text itself.
- **Slurs and jabs were recorded as "a jab" or "a crude remark" and not repeated. Personal, family,
  health and place details were left out** (several reviews named relatives, partners, towns or health
  complaints).
- **22 reviews were written in another language**, filed on the language tag. Steam shows about
  94,700 reviews in other languages; none were pulled.
