## 1. The short version

- **90.9% thumbs up, 40.4 complaints and 134.6 praise per 100 reviews; praise to complaint 3.3 : 1.**
  Between Risk of Rain 2 (5.8 : 1) and Warframe (2.5 : 1) on the same count (`cross-game.md` section 21).
- **It is judged against Tarkov and wins.** 135 of 1,160 reviews name Tarkov; 6 of them are thumbs
  down. `beats-its-rivals` (71) is the third most common tag.
- **No PvP is the feature.** `no-pvp-is-a-feature` 51, all thumbs up; `won-over-someone-who-avoids-the-
  genre` 16, all thumbs up.
- **The top complaint is a wish from fans**: `expected-mode-missing` (39, mostly co-op), 36 of the 39
  thumbs up. The second is no controller support (33), and that one costs thumbs: 12 of the 33 are down.
- **The end game is where the game itself sours** — late-game grind (14), the part you need never
  dropping (23), the last area far harder than the rest (5).
- **The one fall in the thumb came from outside the game**: 96.7% in 2025-11, 74.4% in 2025-12, when
  a workshop mod dispute brought protest votes (13 of December's 34 thumbs down).
- **255 of the 469 complaint bullets (54.4%) come from reviews that recommend the game.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding: an extraction game that works without other players

**Every specific praise line near the top is about what taking the other players out allowed.** No
PvP (51), a game to unwind to (48) and satisfyingly hard (49) at once, a difficulty dial (`well-graded`
29), stakes worth the risk (33) with a corpse run to recover from. **The complaints are about what is
still missing without other players** — someone to play with (co-op, 39) — **and about what chance does
when nobody can trade you the part** (the end-game drops, 23). The one outside shock, the mod dispute,
peaked in 2025-12 and 2026-01; copies of one protest statement kept appearing until 2026-07.
