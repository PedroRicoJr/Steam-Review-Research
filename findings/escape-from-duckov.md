## 1. The short version

**No tag names in this section.**

- **Players like it, and most complaints come from fans.** 90.9% thumbs up, 40.4 complaints and 134.6
  praise per 100 reviews; praise to complaint 3.3 : 1, between Risk of Rain 2 (5.8 : 1) and Warframe
  (2.5 : 1) on the same count (`cross-game.md` section 21). **255 of the 469 complaint bullets (54.4%) come
  from reviews that recommend the game.**
- **It is judged against Tarkov and wins.** 135 of 1,160 reviews name Tarkov; 6 of them are thumbs down.
  "It beats its rivals" (71) is the third most common thing said.
- **Having no other players to fight is the feature.** Said 51, all thumbs up; won over people who avoid
  this kind of game: 16, all thumbs up.
- **The top complaint is a wish from fans**: a missing mode (39, mostly co-op), 36 of the 39 thumbs up.
  The second is no controller support (33), and that one costs thumbs: 12 of the 33 are down.
- **The end game is where the game itself sours** — late-game grind (14), the part you need never
  dropping (23), the last area far harder than the rest (5).
- **The one fall in the thumb came from outside the game**: 96.7% in 2025-11, 74.4% in 2025-12, when a
  fight over a workshop mod brought protest votes (13 of December's 34 thumbs down).

## 5. ⭐ The finding: an extraction game that works without other players

**Every specific praise line near the top is about what taking the other players out allowed.** No
fighting other players (51), a game to unwind to (48) and satisfyingly hard (49) at once, a difficulty
dial (well-spaced levels, 29), stakes worth the risk (33) with a corpse run to recover from. **The
complaints are about what is still missing without other players** — someone to play with (co-op, 39) —
**and about what chance does when nobody can trade you the part** (the end-game drops, 23). The one
outside shock, the mod dispute, peaked in 2025-12 and 2026-01; copies of one protest statement kept
appearing until 2026-07.
