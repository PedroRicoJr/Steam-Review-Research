## 1. The short version

**Players liked it at launch and liked how fast the studio patched it, then watched the studio go
quiet:** thumbs up fell from 74% of 2023's 137 reviews to 45% of 2025's 11, and 10 reviews praising
the studio for listening (all 2023) gave way to 9 calling the game abandoned. **The top complaint is
that you move too slowly** — 17 times, from both sides of the thumb. **Finishing the game wipes your
permanent upgrades**, and 7 of the 12 people who said so still recommend it. **Almost everyone
describes it by naming Risk of Rain 2**: 23 times another game is used to explain this one, and 10 of
the 11 who say another game is better vote thumbs down.

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**A small studio's quick patches at launch were the most-praised thing about it and told players
nothing about what came next.** Ten reviews in the first weeks of 2023 praise the studio for
listening; from 2024 on, nine call the game abandoned, sometimes in the same review edited later
(`137512385`). Thumbs up fell from 74% to 56% to 45% by year. One reviewer votes thumbs down while
saying the game is fun, as a protest against the studio (`189831000`). Five of the nine real games in
the small-game block made the same turn (`findings/cross-game.md` section 15).

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- Every English review was read, so there is no margin — but 2025 and 2026 are only 13 reviews, so
  their rates are shaky.
- 17 reviews (9.6%) were edited later and are counted on the date first written; three belong to a
  later year.
- 59 reviews in other languages (25% of the audience) were not read.
- Patches, the change to the upgrade wipe and the end of updates are what reviewers say; none was
  checked against Steam news.
- The studio name differs between this page (Trickjump Games) and the Die After Sunset page; not
  settled.
