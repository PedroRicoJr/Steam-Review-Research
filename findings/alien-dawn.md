## 1. The short version

**The two developers are the most-praised thing about the game:** 21 of 74 reviews mention them and
20 are warm; 6 bought it to fund the studio. **It is the one block game nobody called abandoned.**
**VR is the fault line**: playing VR and flat-screen players together is loved (5, all thumbs up), but
VR that will not launch or misreads your hands drives people away (8, 7 thumbs down). **Finding other
players is hard** — 5 say nobody is playing.

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**A small studio that keeps showing up is praised more than its game.** Twenty of the 21 reviews that
mention the two developers are warm; they listen, invite unhappy players to talk, are known by name and
played with their community. Five other games in the small-game block turned from "they listen" to
"they left" inside two years; this one kept updating, and a 2025 edit says so. The same pool shows the
limit: a VR setup that does not launch ends the review in a thumbs down, whatever the studio is like.
