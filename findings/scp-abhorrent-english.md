# SCP: Abhorrent — English

**A census: all 69 English reviews read. No margin of error.** Every English review there was at the
pull was read, so there is no sample and no margin. Steam's own English count is 44 up and 26 down as
of 2026-09-10 — one review arrived after the pull. Ours is 44 up and 25 down. The tables in section 2
were re-run on 2026-10-03.

**A free thirty-minute SCP horror game from 2022, made by students.** A pitch-black procedural
corridor, a glock that only kills with headshots, a flamethrower, and an opening cutscene the
reviews cannot stop talking about. The camera, engine, player count and hosting are not recorded on
this page. Steam, all languages: 111 reviews, 64 up, 57.7% — English is 62%. It was read as the sixth
of the ten-game block. It is not close to Dominion: it shares shooting and a corridor the game builds
for you, but it is free horror, very short, with no runs, co-op or extraction.

## 1. In plain words

**What decides how players feel is that it is free and short, and they are laughing at it.** 11 times
a reviewer said the writing is funny or memorable — mostly the opening scene — but 4 of those 11 voted
thumbs down. People enjoy how bad it is.

**The only praise that is not a joke is the music.** 7 times a reviewer said the music fits, and all 7
recommend the game.

**The one honest complaint is that it is too dark to see.** 6 times it was said, 4 from thumbs-down
reviews. One reviewer's screen showed nothing; the makers added a brightness setting after he wrote,
and he changed his thumb to up.

**Players also find it hard and unclear.** 4 said it is too hard — enemies only die to headshots — and
3 said nothing tells you what to do.

**Many people say nothing at all.** 19 of the 69 reviews (28%) give no reason for their thumb.

**It got less liked after launch.** 73.8% of the 2022 reviews are thumbs up; 48.1% of the later ones.

**The surprise: this is the only game in the block with more praise than complaint, and the praise is
mostly ironic.** That number does not mean the game is good.

## 3. The big stories

### 3.1 ⭐ The praise is for how bad it is

`narrative.characters-writing.funny-or-memorable` — **11 bullets, the largest real mode in the game,
and 4 of the 11 are on thumbs-down reviews.**

> *"greatest opening cutscene of all time. completely unplayable tho"* (`158938519`, thumbs down)
> *"I was excited because the intro was so bad it's good, then I was disappointed because the game
> was so bad it's bad"* (`131402396`)
> *"It's free, so get it to get omegalulz out of how bad it is."* (`112645927`, thumbs up)

🔑 **This is the one game in the corpus whose praise-to-complaint ratio is above one, and the praise
is ironic.** The mode records that the writing landed. It cannot record *why* it landed — the
reviewers are laughing at the game, and the tree has no direction for that. `audio.music.fits-the-game`
(7, all thumbs up — *"Music slaps"*) is the only unironic praise the game gets.

⚠️ **The ratio is a warning about the ratio.** Across nine games the text was more negative than the
thumb and more informative. Here the text is *less* negative than the thumb because a quarter of the
bullets are a joke told with a straight face. **A 1.18 does not mean this game is better than
Helldivers 2 (1.95). It means the corpus has met sarcasm at volume.**

### 3.2 ⭐ The one real complaint is the dark

`game-design.readability.too-dark-to-see` — 6 bullets, 4 down. *"Visibility was too limited for me to
continue playing."* (`117699810`)

**And the one real developer story:** `112105387` did not recommend the game because his monitor
showed nothing; *"As you can see from the comments to my review, the developers quickly released a
patch fixing this issue using a brightness slider."* He flipped to a thumbs up. **That is the fourth
sighting of `developer-communication.replied-to-my-review` in the block**, and the second where the
reply changed the verdict.

Not checked against a primary source: the patch and the reply are as the reviewer gives them.

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **Darkness must never stop players from seeing.**
   Evidence: 6 times it was said that it is too dark, 4 thumbs down; a brightness slider turned one
   thumbs down into a thumbs up.
   Dominion could: ship with a brightness and gamma setting and a calibration screen; test dark areas
   on cheap monitors; keep enemies readable in the dark (outlines, glow, sound).
   Strength: medium (small count, but a cheap fix that flipped a verdict).
2. **A fast, visible fix after a public complaint can change the review.**
   Evidence: `112105387` flipped to thumbs up after the studio replied and patched.
   Dominion could: read reviews in the launch weeks, reply where a fix is coming, and point to the
   patch when it lands.
   Strength: weak (one review), but repeated across the block (see the block entry).
3. **Laughs are not the same as liking.**
   Evidence: 4 of 11 "funny writing" comments are on thumbs-down reviews; the high praise number is
   ironic.
   Dominion could: when reading its own playtest or review praise, check whether people laugh with the
   game or at it.
   Strength: weak.
4. **Music can carry a weak game.**
   Evidence: 7 times it was said that the music fits, all thumbs up, the only sincere praise.
   Strength: weak.
