## 1. The short version

**Players judge it as a remake, and nearly all say it is the better way to play the old game** — 265
remarks, against 26 that it changed too little, left out what the sequel added, or that the old game
was better.

**Next they love it for being hard (110), its looks (92), its music (81) and the pull of one more run
(50).**

**The top complaints are the unlock challenges (42 a chore, though 36 call them fun) and aiming (35:
you can only shoot left or right).** A patch a month after launch added left-right mouse aim and the
complaint stopped for two months; it came back because shooting up or down never came.

**The thumb fell only once, and not over the game.** In April to August 2025 it dropped to 74.0% as 17
thumbs down protested a new end-user agreement or the owners; after the studio's return patch (1.1.0,
2025-08-29, after 19 months with no patch) it was back at 93.6%.

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**A co-op game that ships without the basics players already know from its genre pays for it for
years — and fixing them late helps, but only the half that gets fixed.** Players asked for free aim,
a visible exit, pings and a way back after a disconnect. The studio answered the aim and exit
complaints within five weeks (left-right mouse aim, a wider teleporter signal) and the pings and
gentler disconnects only 21 months later, in patch 1.1.0 (all checked in the studio's Steam news).
The left-right half of aiming stopped being a complaint at once; the up-down half, never added, came
back at 2–3 per 100 reviews; the exit is still a complaint in 2025. Meanwhile the one real fall in
the thumb came from outside the game — an end-user agreement change — and the most-helpful review in
the sample is about it.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- The launch month is 75.4% of all English reviews and is read at ×19; later months hold 20 reviews
  each (±22% for any one month). Use the five periods on the English page.
- The end-user agreement (date, terms, "spyware") is not checked; it is not in the game's Steam news
  or on its store page. The classic-rules setting and "Drone Life" are named by reviewers only.
- 682 of the 1,533 reviews say nothing but a thumbs up.
- The reading pace changed at batch 13 (three to six batches a firing); a blind audit found no drop in
  accuracy (`qc/pace-test-2026-10-07/`).
