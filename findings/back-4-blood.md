# Back 4 Blood — the whole game, across languages

**One game, two language groups read end to end, ranked into one list.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Language groups pulled | **6** — english, latam, spanish, brazilian, russian, schinese |
| Language groups read | **2** — english and latam |
| Reviews read | **2,375** |
| Months covered | **59** — 2021-10 to 2026-08 |
| Tagged observations | **5,703** |
| Observations per review | **2.40** |
| Thumbs up | **1,729 of 2,375 — 72.8%** |
| Observations with no home in the tree | **0** |

**What is not in here.** European Spanish was started and stopped at 50 of 1,175. Brazilian
Portuguese, Russian and Simplified Chinese were pulled and never read. **Four language groups
covering 23,734 reviews on Steam are unread**, and 5,060 of those were already sampled to disk and
are waiting. Nothing on this page speaks for them.

---

## How the two groups were joined

The two groups were sampled in different ways, so their raw counts cannot simply be added.

| Group | On Steam | Read | Method | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| English | 33,766 | 1,663 | 4.9% sample | **×20.30** |
| LatAm Spanish | 712 | 712 | census — every review | **×1.00** |

**The "Est. people" column below is `raw count in the group × that group's weight`.** It answers
*how many people on Steam, in these two languages, wrote this*. One English bullet stands for about
twenty people. One LatAm bullet stands for one person.

**Read that column as a scale, not a headcount.** It is rounded to the nearest ten, and a mode with
20 raw bullets is still 20 people who typed it, however large the estimate looks.

**Every table also shows the raw counts**, so any row can be checked by hand.

## 1. The short version

**The reviews are an argument about a game that was never made, and about what the game cost.**

- **2,824 negative bullets against 2,695 positive** — more complaint than praise, in a corpus that
  is **72.8% thumbs up**. The thumb and the text disagree, at scale, in both languages.
- **The single most common thing anyone says is that this is the successor to another game.**
  `successor-framing-accepted`, 319 raw bullets, in both languages.
- **The top two complaints are both about money, not play** — wait for a sale (212) and the
  comparison the game invited (176). The first complaint about how the game *plays* is eighth.
- **`marketing` and `publishing` together are 24.5% of everything said.** A quarter of the record is
  about how the game was sold and what it cost.
- **15.5% of all bullets are `unknown`** — the person raised a subject and never said what about it.

**And one thing is missing that both other games have.** See section 5.

## 5. ⭐ The finding: this game has no player culture

**`community.culture` has zero bullets in 2,375 Back 4 Blood reviews.**

Not a low count. **Zero.** No catchphrase written unprompted. No in-joke. No identity a player
claims out loud. Both other games in this corpus are saturated with it — Deep Rock 28.6 bullets per
100 reviews read, Helldivers 29.7. **Back 4 Blood: 0.00.**

**This is not a hole in the tagging.** `community.culture.shared-ritual` was built in **round 5**.
Back 4 Blood summarising began in **round 27**. The mode existed, and stayed empty, for every one of
these 2,375 reviews.

**What sits in its place is the comparison.** 580 bullets of `marketing.positioning`, arguing about
which game this is a sequel to. **Where the other two games grew a language of their own, this one
kept borrowing somebody else's.**

## 6. Year by year, both languages together

| Year | Reviews | Thumbs down | Bullets/review | Praise per 100 | Complaint per 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 513 | 25.9% | 2.46 | 104.9 | **134.7** |
| 2022 | 395 | **34.9%** | 2.43 | 100.8 | 131.9 |
| 2023 | 350 | 30.3% | 2.58 | 115.7 | **135.1** |
| 2024 | 478 | 21.1% | 2.25 | 117.8 | 99.8 |
| 2025 | 409 | **23.0%** | 2.30 | **124.7** | **95.8** |
| 2026 | 230 | 32.2% | 2.46 | 122.2 | 117.4 |

**The game's best year is 2025 — two years after the updates stopped.** Praise per 100 reviews peaks
and complaint bottoms out in the same year. **2021 and 2023 are tied for the most complaint per
reader** (134.7 and 135.1), and 2023 is the year support ended. **The most thumbs down came in 2022**,
at 34.9%.

**2026 turns back down.** Thumbs down returns to 32.2% and complaint rises 23%. The corpus records
that it happened; it does not say why.

## 7. Where the two languages disagree

The full comparison is in `back-4-blood-latam.md` section 3. **Three things, in short:**

1. **Price is a condition in LatAm, not a complaint.** `buy-on-sale-only` runs 1.6× the English rate
   and `price.fair` has one bullet in 712 reviews.
2. **Nobody in LatAm blames the studio.** `studio-lost-my-trust` — 20 English bullets, **zero** LatAm.
3. **The card system's deep critiques are English-only** — 66 English bullets, zero LatAm.

## 8. What this page cannot tell you

1. **What four of the six languages think.** 23,734 reviews on Steam, unread. The Chinese group
   alone is 14,997 reviews — nine times the English sample.
2. **Whether the estimate column is right at the edges.** A mode with 20 raw bullets carries a wide
   error bar however it is scaled.
3. **Why 2024 and 2025 improved.** Deep sales, the copy protection removed in May 2024 and press
   coverage of a player revival all land in the same window.
4. **What the reviewers actually wrote.** These are tags. Pulling the raw text behind the top
   entries is a separate pass — see plan §7, "The two-stage read".
