# Risk of Rain (2013) — English

**A sample: 3,104 of 21,329 English reviews read, ±2.53% on any whole-sample share.** The grid was
pulled on 2026-10-07 (`raw/risk-of-rain-2013/english/MANIFEST.md`): 3,161 reviews were planned and
3,104 came back, because in 18 months Steam returned fewer reviews than its own count. The grid's
month-sum is 21,329; `scripts/steam_counts.py 248820` gave 21,330 English reviews later the same day.

**A 2D side-on sci-fi roguelike from Hopoo Games, released 2013-11-08, now published by Gearbox
Publishing and 2K** (Steam store, `store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=248820`, 2026-10-07).
The store calls it "an action platformer with roguelike elements" with "permanent death", "over 100
items" and randomly spawning enemies and bosses, played "either alone or with 3 friends in online
co-op". Its three stated goals: a game "randomly generated every play-through", "Time = difficulty",
and "Players should not blame the game for their loss!". Store categories include Shared/Split Screen,
Remote Play Together, **Partial Controller Support** and **Adjustable Text Size**. Full price $9.99,
$1.99 on the day (80% off). All languages: 29,841 reviews, 93% positive, *Very Positive*
(`steam_counts.py`). It is the original of *Risk of Rain Returns* (row 17, read in rounds 837-874) and
the 2D forerunner of *Risk of Rain 2* (released on Steam 2020-08-11, store). **A19a on
`planning/action-roguelike-list.md`, picked by the active loop after the remake (round 875): the same
four-player sci-fi run loop, and Rico wants both versions read side by side.** Like the remake it is 2D
and has no extraction; unlike it, its online play was built in 2013 around one player hosting by IP.

## 1. In plain words

**People love this game for being hard.** "Hard, and that is the fun" is the most common specific
praise: 333 bullets, 10.7 per 100 reviews. Close behind come the music (284), the pull of one more run
(236), playing with friends (177), characters that each play their own way (165) and the clock that
makes the game harder every minute (116, against 9 who dislike being rushed).

**The biggest complaint is getting a friend into your game.** 115 reviews say online play needs the
host's IP address, opened ports, or a program such as Hamachi; 187 reviews raise some online-play
problem, and 43 of them are thumbs down. It was worst from 2015 to 2018, when about one review in ten
raised it, and it faded as players moved to the sequel and the remake.

**From 2019 the game is judged against its own sequel, and from November 2023 against its remake.** 80
reviews say *Risk of Rain 2* is better (33 of them thumbs down), against 35 that prefer this one. Once
the remake came out, 56 reviews told buyers to get it instead — 9 per 100 reviews in its first year and
a half — while 5 said the original is better.

**The other complaints are about fairness and pace.** Luck deciding the run (49 bullets), enemies that
soak up damage (26), a slow and lonely start with one character (42), having to hunt down the last enemy
before you can leave (15), shooting only left or right (12), and a view so far out the character is a
few pixels tall (27).

**The game shows its age in its tech.** 166 reviews name a technical fault: crashes that lose a run of
an hour or more, lost saves and settings, controllers that do not work, and a Linux version that
freezes when one item, Spike Strip, triggers (9 reviews over ten years). 15 say the faults were never
fixed.

**Who complains: mostly people who left early.** The 339 thumbs down show a median of 3 hours played;
the 2,765 thumbs up show 20.

**The thumb fell twice, and only once over the game.** It went from 91% up before 2023 to 81% once the
remake was out, and to 60% during the 2025 protest over the publisher's new end-user agreement; after
the protest it recovered to 84%.

**The surprise: the 2013 game is praised for the same things as the 2023 remake, and it holds up.** 58
reviews say it still holds up years later, rising from 0.5 per 100 before the sequel to 3.9 per 100 in
2025-26.

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play** — section 3. 177 much better with friends; 27 praise local play on one
  screen; 115 host-IP; 29 could not connect; 4 disconnects, 2 could not rejoin; 3 loot-sharing notes
  (items are shared among players); 11 say it is punishing solo, 2 unplayable alone, 48 that it works
  solo. 2 call the online population dead. **A dead player waits for the next stage to come back**
  (13884611, 28568663, 41578468).
- **Combat and feel** — 83 end a run feeling godlike. Against: shooting only left or right (12, 11
  thumbs down), kiting as the only way to fight (14, `only-one-way-to-play-it`), standing still to shoot
  (138124757), a dodge every four seconds (120515312), attacks that feel "pitiful" (120515312).
- **Movement and controls** — 13 call movement sluggish; 6 say the default keyboard layout is awkward
  ("X to move left", 159484498); 12 miss mouse binds or a drop-through key; 5 praise full rebinding.
- **Enemies, bosses and difficulty** — 333 hard and fun, 26 too hard; 26 bullet sponges; 10 unfair
  spawns, 9 overwhelming numbers; 19 hate one enemy (the Magma Worm, jellyfish, Whorls, spitters); the
  final boss is praised (153133821, 206135415). The death message is mocking (4, all disliked; "sucks to
  suck", 189388234).
- **Progression, loot and randomness** — 103 deep builds, 92 satisfying unlocks, 72 welcome the
  artifacts (Command to choose items, Glass, Sacrifice); 49 luck decides, 42 slow start, 11 grindy, 6
  find unlocks gated behind a mode (3 the easiest difficulty, 3 single player or multiplayer), 5 monster logs a chore ("monster logs ... have a very low drop
  chance", 223209265).
- **Runs, content and replay** — 236 one more run; 16 love looping the stages; 23 repetitive; 19 say the
  randomised levels are only surface (2-3 layouts each, 54559869); 37 good in short sittings, but
  runs of 2-5 hours lost to a crash (49371144).
- **The clock** — 116 call it the thrill; 9 dislike being rushed; 6 say the scaling is badly tuned; 1
  says the game never tells you that you must hurry (217661377).
- **Money and price** — 117 fair; 28 on sale only; 11 too high. Sold at $1.99 on the day.
- **Tech** — section 3.4.
- **The studio and the community** — the studio's move to *Risk of Rain 2* is named as why bugs stayed
  (8, `diverted-to-other-projects`); 29 praise the mods (Starstorm, Rainfusion); 2 say the community
  answers complaints with "git gud".
- **What players asked for** — easier online play (most of the 187), checkpoints or saving mid-run
  (137479643; 7 notes say there is no way to save a run), monster logs that drop on first kill (223209265), a zoom slider (231657264), a
  text-size setting (78427966; the store now lists Adjustable Text Size), a difficulty between easy and
  normal (231657264), aiming up and down (138124757, 129076331), mouse binds (12).

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **Joining a friend must take no more than an invite.** 187 reviews raise an online-play problem, the
   single largest complaint, and the setup (IP, ports, Hamachi) drove it. Dominion is a listen server like
   this game: use the platform's invites and relay so no player opens a port. *Strong.*
2. **A dropped player must be able to rejoin the same run.** 4 disconnects and 2 "could not rejoin"
   reviews, and 143498280 lost whole sessions. *Medium.*
3. **Do not sell online co-op the setup cannot deliver.** 6 thumbs down called the four-pack and the
   store's promise deceptive (47549942, 106 found it helpful). *Medium.*
4. **A clock that raises the difficulty is loved — and must be explained.** 116 call it the thrill
   against 9; one says the game never tells you to hurry, and 77583153 calls the hidden "rush the
   teleporter" rule a filter for new players. *Strong.*
5. **Do not make players hunt the last enemy.** 15 complain, with stories of "14 enemies left on a tiny
   platform" (185953137). *Medium.*
6. **Give the first character and the first hour more.** 42 call the start slow; 20-30 deaths before the
   first unlock (158584785); the thumbs-down median is 3 hours played. *Strong.*
7. **Let players aim where enemies are.** The left-right-only complaint carries 11 of its 12 bullets
   as thumbs down. A third-person shooter has this already; keep it for every class. *Medium.*
8. **Stacking items to absurd power is the payoff players remember — budget for it.** 83 feel godlike;
   29 say the game slows, and crashes end runs. Plan the engine and the server for the late-run load.
   *Strong.*
9. **Save the run.** 28 lost a run to a crash with nothing saved; 7 say there is no way to save a run
   and come back. *Medium.*
10. **Make controllers and readability first-class.** 15 controller failures; 27 say the view is too
    small; a text-size request. *Medium.*
11. **Do not change the terms after people have bought.** The 2025 agreement brought 30 of 39 thumbs down
    in five months, and three of the eight most-helpful reviews in the sample. *Strong.*

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **Sample:** 3,104 of 21,329 (14.6%), ±2.53% on whole-sample shares; periods carry ±2.7% to ±11.2%.
- **Weighting:** the tables on this page count each review read once. The weighted shares are in the
  stats file; 2013-11 is read at ×36.
- **Edits:** 244 reviews (7.9%) were edited later and are counted on the date they were first written.
- **Reading pace:** batches 1-48 were read at twelve a firing; the second pace test (round 924) found no
  drop in accuracy against six a firing (`qc/pace-test-2-2026-10-07/`).
- **Not checked:** the end-user agreement and its terms; every patch, fix or "the latest update" claim;
  the Visual C++ fix; the console versions' online play; the origin story (two students, a Kickstarter);
  Hopoo's closure and its founders' moves; the Spike Strip cause; mod-support changes. All are marked as
  reviewers' accounts.
- **Store facts checked** (store API, 2026-10-07): developer, publishers, release date, price, the
  categories quoted, and *Risk of Rain 2*'s release date.
- **Handled:** jabs and slurs recorded only as "a crude jab"; personal, family and health details left
  out, including a copied "single father" story that appears in other games' reviews; named people left
  out except the studio's composer, credited by name in many reviews.
- **Other languages:** 21 reviews, summarised in English.
