# Risk of Rain Returns — English

**A sample: 1,533 of 19,399 English reviews read, ±2.77% on any whole-sample share.** The grid was
pulled on 2026-10-07; its month-sum, 19,399, is exactly Steam's own English count on the same day
(`scripts/steam_counts.py 1337520`). Steam returned 777 of the 973 reviews wanted for the launch month
(the game came out on 2023-11-08, so the first windows of the month hold none), and 6 for 2026-10, a
month seven days old.

**A 2D side-on sci-fi roguelike from Hopoo Games, published by Gearbox Publishing and 2K, released
2023-11-08** (Steam store, `store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=1337520`, 2026-10-07). It is
the remake of Hopoo's *Risk of Rain* (2013). The store calls it "the iconic roguelike" remade, with "15
unique Survivors" (characters), items that stack into "unpredictable combinations", "more than 10
Artifacts that each mutate your run", and "up to four-player online and couch co-op". Store tags
include Online Co-op, Shared/Split Screen Co-op, Remote Play Together and Full controller support. Full
price $14.99, $7.49 on the day (50% off in a 2K sale). The studio's launch post lists "Revamped
Multiplayer", "Completely reimagined pixel art", "3 New Survivors" and a new challenge mode, "Providence
Trials" ([Steam news, 2023-11-08](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5302486387995332510)).
Gearbox Entertainment bought the *Risk of Rain* series from Hopoo in late 2022; Hopoo stayed the
developer of this game ([Dev Thoughts #28, 2022-12-20](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5623338145769653434)).
All languages: 28,739 reviews, 90% positive, *Very Positive*. **A19b on
`planning/action-roguelike-list.md`, picked by the active loop as the next closest to Dominion (round
837): four-player sci-fi co-op against the game's own enemies, built on runs that end in an escape from
the planet. It differs in camera (2D, side-on) and has no extraction.** Its 3D sequel, *Risk of Rain 2*,
is row 4 of the large-games queue, so the two can be read side by side.

## 1. In plain words

**Players judge this game as a remake first.** 265 remarks say it is the better way to play the 2013
game, and 46 more that it stays true to it; only 26 say it changed too little, left out what the sequel
added, or that the old game was better. The biggest single reason to recommend it is "the old game,
done right" — *"the gold standard what a remaster/rework should do"* (`172134848`, 154 found it
helpful).

**After that, players love it for being hard and for how it looks and sounds.** Hard but worth it is
said 110 times, good looks 92, the music 81, and the pull to play one more run 50.

**The top complaints are about unlock challenges and aiming.** 42 remarks call the Providence Trials
(set challenges that unlock skills and skins) a chore — though 36 others call them fun. 35 say you can
only shoot straight ahead, left or right, so flying enemies are hard to hit. The studio added left-right
mouse aim a month after launch, and the aiming complaint vanished for two months, then came back at a
lower level, because shooting up or down never came.

**The sequel is the yardstick.** 43 remarks say this is better than *Risk of Rain 2*, 31 that it is
worse, and 41 explain the game by naming other games (nearly always *Risk of Rain 2*). Those who prefer
the sequel miss its smooth movement, its easy-to-find exits and its item printers.

**Complaints come half from people who stayed.** 260 of the 503 complaint remarks (51.7%) are in
reviews that still recommend the game. There are 32.8 complaints and 122.6 praise remarks per 100
reviews — more complaints than *Risk of Rain 2* (18.0) and about the same as Gunfire Reborn (33.1).

**The thumb fell only once, and not because of the game.** Thumbs up held at 94–98% in every period
except April to August 2025, when it fell to 74.0%: a wave of thumbs down over a new end-user agreement
from the owners (Take-Two, 2K, Gearbox), often from people who praise the game in the same breath. Take
those reviews out and the period is at 89%.

**The surprise: the studio went quiet for 19 months, then came back.** Four patches came in the first
two months; then nothing from 2024-01 to 2025-08. Players called it abandoned (*"Devs just gave up on
this game"*, `166384740`). The 2025-08 patch added pings, an option for more than four players, and
gentler disconnect rules — and after it, the share of reviewers calling this better than *Risk of Rain
2* tripled (1.9 per 100 at launch, 6.0 after).

| Mode | Bullets | Direction |

| `game-design.game-feel.reward-moment.gives-a-nostalgia-hit` | 26 | + |
| `game-design.modes.the-original-rules-are-an-option` | 13 | + |

| `marketing.positioning.the-original-was-better-than-the-remake` | 4 | − |

**350 for the remake against 26 against.** All seven modes but the nostalgia one were built for this
game (section 6). The praise names the same things the store lists: new art, new characters and items,
online play that works, and fixes to old annoyances:

- *"this is the gold standard what a remaster/rework should do ... its the old game polished, optimized
  and enhanced"* (`172134848`, 154 found it helpful — the third most-helpful review in the sample).
- *"Improves on the original in almost every way"* (`152535252`).
- A player who could never connect to the 2013 game online says *now* it is a breeze (`152598799`);
  another says multiplayer works through Steam, without an outside program like Hamachi (`150033540`).

**The case against is "the same game in a pretty wrap".** The most-helpful thumbs down of the launch
month (`152611852`, 154 hours, 64 found it helpful) says: *"Same (mostly) game with duct-taped additions
in a pretty wrap. I hoped it would be akin to Binding of Isaac: Rebirth, but it is pretty much just a
visual update"*, and sends players to the 2013 game with the *Starstorm* fan mod instead. `149839599`
(9 found it helpful): *"the actual play is almost unchanged"* (our summary of its words).

**Thirteen reviewers say the original rules can be switched back on** (`149836905`: *"u can always play
RoR 1 classic style in the settings"*; `172134848`: *"you can play with the old ruleset and items or the
new"*). This is the reviewers' account: the store page does not name such a setting. When patch 1.1.0
came out, the studio also kept the previous build as a Steam beta branch, "1.0.5 Legacy post-release
build", for anyone who wanted to "downpatch" ([Steam news, 2025-08-29](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1809235871629924)).

**The sequel features players missed** (8 bullets): scrappers and 3D printers that turn unwanted items
into wanted ones, the bazaar, lunar items and the choice of stages (`177101312`, `150188755`,
`201506652`).

## 3.2 ⭐ Aiming, and what the patches changed

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `you-can-only-shoot-straight-ahead` | 35 | 25 | 0 | 1 | 3 | 6 |
| `one-enemy-type-is-hated` | 23 | 10 | 1 | 4 | 1 | 7 |
| `game-feel.movement.sluggish` | 9 | 6 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| `the-way-onward-is-hard-to-find` | 14 | 6 | 0 | 3 | 1 | 4 |

*(Counts by grep on the summary folders; month by created date.)*

**At launch, characters fired only the way they faced.** The complaint came in two halves: you could
not turn while firing, and you could not shoot up or down. *"A 2023 remaster of a 2013 game with a
control scheme from 2003. Please just let me aim with the mouse"* (`150380205`); *"don't put flying
enemies in a game where you can't aim up or down"* (`150032341`, thumbs down); *"You cant aim at all
... the flying enemies are unable to be damaged by you most of them time"* (`152548522`).

**Checked against the studio's Steam news.** Two days after launch, patch 1.0.2 promised "independent
horizontal (not full 360) aim options" ([2023-11-10](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5302486388003141718)).
Patch 1.0.4 delivered it: "its biggest feature is the inclusion of horizontal mouse aiming! To be clear -
this does not allow you to have full 360 degree aiming/shooting" ([2023-12-12](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5395938616813611402)).
The same patch "Normalized survivor movement speed. All slower survivors now move faster", and 1.0.3
had "Increased Teleporter particle visibility range" ([2023-11-17](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/6560117310265889225)).

**What the reviews show after:** the aiming complaint dropped from 25 in the launch month to none in
the next two months; `150380205` edited its review to *"Edit: They did it"*, and `162384583` came back
after four months away because of *"the aiming update, a handful of patches, and several providence
trials being re-balanced"*. But the up-and-down half was never fixed, and it returns: *"Just please let
us shoot up"* (`158337597`, 2024-02); *"the survivors can't raise their arms to shoot upwards"*
(`220568186`, 2026-03). Ten aiming bullets come after the patch. **Flying enemies carry the cost**: of
the 23 hated-enemy bullets, 9 name flying enemies or birds. The teleporter
and movement complaints also go on after their patches (`166384740`, 2024-05; `205446707`, 2025-09).

## 3.3 ⭐ A 19-month silence, then an agreement protest, then a return

**Checked against the studio's Steam news** ([announcements feed](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=1337520),
2026-10-07): patches 1.0.2 (2023-11-10), 1.0.3 (2023-11-17), 1.0.4 (2023-12-12) and 1.0.5
(2024-01-11), then nothing about the game until "Risk of Rain Returns... returns! (V1.1.0)" on
2025-08-29 — a gap of about 19 months.

| Mode | Bullets | 2023-11 | 2023-12 to 2024-01 | 2024-02 to 2025-03 | 2025-04 to 2025-08 | 2025-09 on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `publishing.data-and-privacy.the-agreement-strips-buyer-rights` | 18 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 16 | 1 |
| `publishing.ownership.owner-puts-players-off` | 8 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 5 | 1 |
| `live-ops.abandonment.updates-stopped` | 6 | 0 | 0 | 3 | 3 | 0 |
| `marketing.reputation.studio-earned-my-trust` | 9 | 8 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 |

*(Per-100 rates for the first three in the complaint table above; these are bullet counts. The one
2023-11 agreement bullet is `151702556`'s 2025-06-11 edit, counted on its created date.)*

**First, trust.** At launch 8 reviewers trusted the studio to fix things (*"Expects the studio to fix it
- it is very good with post-launch updates and balance"*, `150030740`, our summary). Several read the
game as Hopoo's farewell to the series (*"A farewell letter from Hopoo games with a bittersweet taste"*,
`150387792`). Gearbox's purchase of the series is a fact (Dev Thoughts #28, above); the claim that
Gearbox was making *"a microtransaction-heavy mobile game"* (`150182603`) was not checked.

**Then the gap.** From 2024-05, reviewers say the game is abandoned: *"Devs just gave up on this game"*
(`166384740`); a Commando slide bug *"after 6 months of devs being aware"* (`165404589`, 280 hours, 44
found it helpful; patch 1.1.0 later "Fixed Commando's 'Tactical Slide' frequently entirely failing to
cast"). From 2024-08, when Gearbox's own *Risk of Rain 2* expansion *Seekers of the Storm* came out
(Steam store, appid 2306620: developed by Gearbox Software, released 2024-08-27), some blame Gearbox for
both (*"Gearbox kills Games"*, `173928825`).

**Then the protest.** 17 thumbs down object to a new end-user agreement that they say was forced on
owners after purchase — 15 written from 2025-04-29 to 2025-08, one in 2025-09 (`204360768`) and one a
2025-06 edit of a launch review (`151702556`) (`193855090`: *"Take-Two forced a new end-user agreement
after purchase"*, our summary). The most-helpful review in the whole sample is one of them: *"I cant
believe I have to call this game Abandon-ware AND NOW Spyware"* (`201947635`, 224 found it helpful).
Others: *"I love losing consumer rights!"* (`201589243`); *"great game but terrible, terrible decision to
take away consumer rights ... HOPOO GAMES, FIND A NEW PUBLISHER"* (`200392338`, 113 found it helpful).
Most praise the game in the same review — *"An amazing remake, 10/10"* then a thumbs down (`197321716`,
199 hours, our summary). **17 of the 26 thumbs down in 2025-04 to 2025-08 are about the agreement or the
owners (15 the agreement, 2 the owners alone); without those 17 reviews the period's thumbs up is 74 of
83, 89.2%.** **Not checked:** the
agreement itself, its date and its terms are not in the game's Steam news or on its store page, so
"spyware", the risk to bank details (`198896654`) and the claim that it forbids playing through Steam
(`151702556`) stay the reviewers' words.

**Then the return.** The 1.1.0 post opens: "Rumors of Risk of Rain Returns' death have been greatly
exaggerated", and lists "An 'experimental' option to allow more than 4 players", "A ping and emote
system", and Steam Deck work ([2025-08-29](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1809235871629924)).
The full notes add a host option "Player Limit. May be increased up to 32" with a warning above 4, and
"Adjusted client disconnection code to be much more lenient" ([Patch Notes 1.1.0](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1809235871629922)).
`198466600` had written two months earlier that no updates would come (41 found it helpful). **No review
read after 2025-08-29 mentions the pings or the larger lobbies**; three ask for more updates
(`209127244`, `218131964`, `226859370`). After 1.1.0, thumbs up returns to 93.6% and *better than the
sequel* to its highest rate (6.0 per 100).

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play.** 34 much better with friends; 28 getting friends in now works (the
  2013 game needed outside programs — `150033540`, `152598799`); 2 praise couch co-op, 1 joins
  strangers' lobbies happily (`149839014`). Against: 6 frequent disconnects and 5 cannot rejoin after
  one (`149838431` begs for a way to rejoin); 1 desync (`152611852`); 1 relays that the run ends
  when the host leaves (`186710992`, reviewer's account); 2 say the online lobbies are empty
  (`186710992`, `220568186`) and 2 more that they are thin. **Loot:** 3 say teammates take your items
  (*"Like why doesnt it make a separate instanced chests for each player?"*, `150031848`), and the
  most-helpful line preferring this game to its sequel is about exactly this: in *Risk of Rain 2* a
  friend takes every chest (`225615450`, 42 found it helpful). **Death:** 2 praise "Drone Life" —
  a dead player comes back as a drone that can still shoot and pick up items: *"a stroke of genius on
  their part"* (`149838153`); *"a massive improvement to the multiplayer"* (`152300415`). The name is
  the reviewers'; the store page does not list it. 1 says some achievements unlock only for the lobby

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **Never limit where players can shoot, and give every enemy a way to be hit.** Evidence: 35
   aiming complaints, the second biggest; flying enemies named in 9 of 23 hated-enemy bullets; the
   complaint fell to zero when left-right mouse aim arrived and came back because up-down never did.
   Dominion has free third-person aim already; the lesson is for enemies that fly, hide above or sit
   out of reach — every class needs an answer to them. Strength: **strong**.
2. **Make the way out visible from anywhere.** Evidence: 14 cannot find the teleporter, 6 say zooming
   out to find it makes the fight unreadable, a patch widening the teleporter's visibility did not end
   it, and *Risk of Rain 2*'s visible teleporter is the comparison. Dominion's extraction point and
   arena objectives could show on the HUD or in the world from the start. Strength: **strong**.
3. **A dead player should keep playing.** Evidence: 2 praise "Drone Life" in strong words; the review
   explaining it says the sequel's long runs leave dead players *"just watching other people play"*
   (`149838153`). Dominion could give downed or dead players a small role (a drone, pings, buffs) until
   revive or extraction. Strength: **medium**.
4. **Ship pings, rejoin and gentle disconnect rules on day one.** Evidence: 1 no ping, 6 disconnects, 5
   cannot rejoin, 1 desync, 1 host-leaves-ends-run — all things the studio fixed or eased only in
   patch 1.1.0, 21 months after launch. A listen-server game needs a rejoin path and, ideally, host
   migration. Strength: **strong** (counts small here, but the same in many other games in this study).
5. **Do not let one player take everyone's loot.** Evidence: 3 complaints here, and a 42-vote review
   that prefers this game because a friend takes every chest in the sequel. Dominion could give each
   player their own drop, or a shared pool with a pass button. Strength: **medium**.
6. **Unlock challenges need a second road.** Evidence: 42 chore against 36 fun; the complaints are about
   luck, tight times and a forced top difficulty; 6 praise the slower route through normal play.
   Dominion could keep challenge unlocks, with a slower normal-play route for every one, and test the
   hardest by hand. Strength: **strong**.
7. **Base movement must feel good with no items.** Evidence: 9 slow, 8 stages too big for the speed, 5
   levels that do not suit the movement; a patch that sped up the slow characters did not end it.
   Strength: **medium**.
8. **Hard is a selling point when it is fair.** Evidence: 110 hard but worth it against 9 too hard and 4
   too fragile; the hated enemies are the ones you cannot answer. Strength: **strong**.
9. **Keep each class viable, and keep fixing the weakest.** Evidence: 20 too weak (Artificer 12, 7 of
   them after a buff), 9 too strong, 41 each plays its own way. Strength: **medium**.
10. **A long silence after launch reads as abandonment.** Evidence: 19 months with no patch; "gave up",
    "abandoned", "in limbo" (6 updates stopped, 4 too slow); the return post itself jokes about
    "Rumors of ... death". Dominion could post even small updates on a steady beat. Strength:
    **medium**.
11. **Changing the terms after people bought the game costs more than any design flaw.** Evidence: the
    only drop in thumbs up (74.0%) came from the agreement, not the game; the most-helpful review in
    the sample is about it. Dominion should settle accounts, data use and terms before launch.
    Strength: **strong**.
12. **Players will measure Dominion against the genre's best, feature by feature.** Evidence: 31 worse
    than the sequel; 8 miss named features (printers, scrappers); 41 explain the game by naming others.
    Strength: **medium**.
13. **When a big rules change lands, keep the old rules as an option.** Evidence: 13 praise the classic
    setting; the studio kept the old build as a beta branch for 1.1.0. Strength: **weak**.
14. **Let a run be saved and resumed.** Evidence: 6, one tying it to the Steam Deck. Strength:
    **weak** (Dominion's runs may be short enough not to need it).

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **Sample:** 1,533 of 19,399 (7.9%), ±2.77% on whole-sample shares. Period margins: launch ±3.5%,
  aim patches ±9.3%, update gap ±5.9%, protest ±9.8%, after 1.1.0 ±6.0%. Any single later month (20
  reviews) is ±22% — use the periods.
- **The launch month is weighted ×19** and is 75.4% of the group. Weighted shares (the stats file) lean
  on it: *remake improves* is 11.3% weighted against 10.4% of raw bullets.
- **Edited reviews:** 107 (7.0%) are counted on the day they were created. `151702556`'s agreement
  complaint is a 2025 edit sitting in 2023-11; `150380205`'s *"They did it"* is an edit too.
- **The pace of reading changed during the game** (three batches a firing, then six from batch 13).
  A blind audit of 40 reviews a side found no drop in accuracy (wrong notes 1 of 66 against 0 of 69;
  `qc/pace-test-2026-10-07/`); the three reviews with a missed point were all on the faster side, and
  four audit findings were fixed (round 869).
- **Checked against primary sources:** the store facts; the patch dates and what 1.0.2, 1.0.3, 1.0.4
  and 1.1.0 changed; Gearbox's purchase of the series; *Seekers of the Storm*'s developer and date.
- **Not checked:** the end-user agreement (date, terms, "spyware"); the classic-rules setting and
  "Drone Life" (named by reviewers, not on the store page); the 60-frame lock; that the run ends when
  the host leaves; empty lobbies; any price a reviewer quotes; Gearbox mobile-game claims.
- **Slurs and crude remarks** are recorded only as "a crude remark" or "a crude jab". Personal, family,
  health and named-person details are left out — including the names of friends in `225615450` and a
  bereavement in `207362979`, whose co-op line is kept without them.
- **12 reviews in other languages** were read and kept; most are one or two words.
