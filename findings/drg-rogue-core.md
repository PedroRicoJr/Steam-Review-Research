# Deep Rock Galactic: Rogue Core — the whole game, ranked

**One game, one language group read as a sample. The ranked lists this page exists for.** Full read:
`findings/drg-rogue-core-english.md`. Plain-words lessons for Dominion: `DOMINION-TAKEAWAYS.md`.

**The controlled experiment of this study.** Same studio, same universe, same players as Deep Rock
Galactic. **97.1% positive against 70.3%.** Every variable except the game is held constant.

quality.**

---

## 1. The short version

**The same studio and the same fans gave the parent game 97.1% thumbs up and this one 70.3%.** Only
the game changed.

**Half of everything said is about how the game is built, and only 3.2% is about crashes or lag.**
Players are not saying it is broken; they are saying they disagree with it.

**The thing named most is the clock inside each run, and most people who name it still recommend the
game** — 69 of 112.

**One review in ten defends the game against its other reviews** (83 times), more than any single
complaint except the run clock and the upgrades that barely change anything (79).

**The score rose to 74.3% in June and fell to 50.0% by September,** after a July hotfix; what people
wrote about patches turned a month before the votes did.

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**The feature everyone argues about is not the feature driving the score, and the words move before
the score does.** The run clock is the most-named thing (112) and 69 of the 112 are on thumbs-up
reviews; that share did not move when the score fell 24 points. The score fell after a July hotfix,
and what players wrote about patches had already turned a month earlier.

### The findings, shortest first

#### 1. The headline rating hides a collapse

**June 74.3% up. September 50.0% up.** The aggregate is 70.3% because the launch month is 59% of the
sample. **The game got better, then got worse, and the single number reports neither.** Full table in
`drg-rogue-core-english.md` section 3.

#### 2. What people wrote moved a month before how they voted

`live-ops.patch-quality` ran 5.8 : 1 positive in June and 1 : 1 in July. **The thumb rate turned in
July.** The same lead showed up at corpus level: the bullet ratio at 300 reviews predicted the thumb
rate at 500. **The text is the leading indicator; the score is the lagging one.**

#### 3. The most-discussed feature is not disliked — it is divisive

The run timer is **112 bullets, the largest mode in the game**, and **69 of the 112 sit on reviews
that recommend it.** The split held inside two points across ten batches and **did not move when the
thumb rate fell 24 points.**

**For a designer this is the useful shape:** the feature everyone argues about is not the feature
driving the score.

#### 4. One review in ten is a defence, not a description

`marketing.reputation.judged-unfairly`, **83 bullets — the third-largest mode.** People are writing
to argue with the review page. **No other game in this corpus does this.**

#### 5. Half of all criticism is design; 3% is engineering

`game-design` holds **1,321 of 2,643 bullets (50.0%)**. `engineering` holds **85 (3.2%)**. **This
game is not broken. It is disagreed with.**

#### 6. The loudest complaint had a constituency nobody counted

The shared upgrade pool draws **62 complaint bullets**, the most of any system. Update 1 loosened it
and **two reviewers filed thumbs down over the fix**, calling the friction the point of the game.

#### 7. The culture-war complaint is 0.4% of what was said

**Nine bullets out of 2,643**, against 112 for the run timer. Loud per review, invisible in the
counts.

### What this closes

**`live-ops.abandonment.diverted-to-other-projects`.** Deep Rock Galactic players raised this mode
about *this exact game* — updates to the parent slowing while the studio built the spin-off. **It
appears twice here from the other side:**

> *"updates for DRG came to an almost complete stop for this, it doesn't feel like it was worth the
> wait."* (`227898874`, 100 helpful)
> *"they literally pulled drg out back and had it shot in order to make this game"* (`228545314`)

**The prediction that justified picking this game held.**
