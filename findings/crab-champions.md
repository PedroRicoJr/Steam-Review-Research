## 1. The short version

- **97.5% thumbs up, 18.7 complaints and 123.2 praise per 100 reviews; praise to complaint 6.6 : 1.**
  Between Deep Rock Galactic (7.4 : 1) and Risk of Rain 2 (5.8 : 1) on the same count (`cross-game.md`
  section 24).
- **Most reviews say almost nothing.** A thumbs up with nothing specific said: 915, **61.0 per 100,
  the highest in the corpus** (Risk of Rain 2 50.3). The mean review is 21.1 words.
- **Friends, coming back, and the price lead the praise.** Much better with friends 86 (rising from 4.1
  to 10.0 per 100), it keeps pulling you back 78, a fair price 69.
- **Movement is praised as in no other game but Warframe.** Quick, responsive movement is 39 (2.6 per
  100); every other game except Warframe is at 0.5 or less.
- **It is measured against Risk of Rain 2.** 43 reviews name it; all 9 times a reviewer says it beats the
  games like it, the game is Risk of Rain.
- **The complaints are many and thin.** No complaint reaches 1 per 100. The top named ones are too
  little content (9), missing modes (8), runs that run out (8), luck (7) and updates that made it
  worse (7).
- **182 of the 280 complaints (65.0%) come from reviews that recommend the game.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding: how it feels to move and to get strong is the product

**The named praise is about feel**: responsive movement (39), feeling far too strong (37), combat with
impact (22), music that fits (39). Players praise the build becoming absurd, and some enjoy the frame
rate dropping as it does (`crab-champions-english.md` section 4, Tech). **The complaints are the other
side of the same thing**: power that outgrows the challenge (6), a balance that assumes you restart for a
god build (4), runs that run out (8). **The one change that drew a cluster of complaints was the Island
Update (2025-11-02)**, which changed the islands and the map edge, not the feel; complaints per 100 rose
from 12.7 to 24.8 after it while thumbs up held at 97.8% (`crab-champions-english.md` section 3.3).
