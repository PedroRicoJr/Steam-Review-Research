## 1. The short version

- **97.5% thumbs up, 18.7 complaints and 123.2 praise per 100 reviews; praise to complaint 6.6 : 1.**
  Between Deep Rock Galactic (7.4 : 1) and Risk of Rain 2 (5.8 : 1) on the same count (`cross-game.md`
  section 24).
- **Most reviews say almost nothing.** `review.positive.unknown` is 915, **61.0 per 100, the highest in
  the corpus** (Risk of Rain 2 50.3). The mean review is 21.1 words.
- **Friends, coming back, and the price lead the praise.** `much-better-with-friends` 86 (rising from 4.1
  to 10.0 per 100), `keeps-pulling-you-back` 78, `fair` price 69.
- **Movement is praised as in no other game but Warframe.** `responsive` movement is 39 (2.6 per 100);
  every other game except Warframe is at 0.5 or less.
- **It is measured against Risk of Rain 2.** 43 reviews name it; all 9 `beats-its-rivals` bullets are
  against it.
- **The complaints are many and thin.** No complaint reaches 1 per 100. The top named ones are too
  little content (9), missing modes (8), runs that run out (8), luck (7) and updates that made it
  worse (7).
- **182 of the 280 complaint bullets (65.0%) come from reviews that recommend the game.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding: how it feels to move and to get strong is the product

**The named praise is about feel**: responsive movement (39), feeling far too strong (37), combat with
impact (22), music that fits (39). Players praise the build becoming absurd, and some enjoy the frame
rate dropping as it does (`crab-champions-english.md` section 5). **The complaints are the other side
of the same thing**: power that outgrows the challenge (6), a balance that assumes you restart for a god
build (4), runs that run out (8). **The one change that drew a cluster of complaints was the Island
Update (2025-11-02)**, which changed the islands and the map edge, not the feel; complaints per 100 rose
from 12.7 to 24.8 after it while thumbs up held at 97.8% (`crab-champions-english.md` section 4).

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- **Anything about other languages.** About 4,000 reviews; none pulled. 1 English-tagged review is in
  Norwegian.
- **Anything about a single month.** Most months are read at 20 to 54 reviews; the specific counts are
  small, and no complaint reaches 10.
- **Why most players like it.** 61 of every 100 reviews are a thumbs up with nothing specific said.
- **Whether reviewers' claims about the game are true.** The 40-player co-op (232914887) and what carries
  between runs (203287249) are the reviewers' words, not checked against the game.
- **Edits.** 89 reviews are counted on their created date.
