## 1. The short version

**It is judged as a game for laughing with friends.** Funny moments made by the players are the top
named praise (294 bullets, 19.8 per 100 reviews), and 246 more say it is much better with friends.

**The laughs come from voices the monsters can hear, loot that breaks, and silly robots.** 32 praise
the voices heard only up close, 20 the fragile loot, 21 the robots; 104 praise the scares as well.

**The top complaint was fixed.** For its first four months there was no way to play with strangers
(25 complaints); public games arrived on 2025-06-26 and the complaint stopped. Strangers then drew
praise (20) and complaints (8).

**Later players found it harder and more punishing.** Complaints that it is too hard and that a team
death loses everything both reached about 2.4 per 100 reviews in 2026, from none at launch.

**Very few complaints overall:** 18.6 per 100 reviews, second lowest of 22 games counted the same way.

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**In a co-op extraction game, the funniest thing is what players do to each other, and the game's
job is to give them the means.** R.E.P.O. gives voices only nearby players hear and monsters react to,
loot that breaks when handled badly, a cart that runs people over, and robots that look silly doing
all of it; players turn these into the stories that make up a quarter of all named praise (294 of 1,179 praise bullets that name something),
with very few complaints (18.6 per 100). What players did complain about is losing that time: no way
to find strangers until 2025-06-26 (25), losing the whole run on a team wipe (10), sitting dead with
nothing to do (5), and, after the monster update, dying in one hit with no warning or answer (too hard
13, rising to 2.4 per 100). Our reading for Dominion: build the shared mishaps on purpose, and protect
the group's time - matchmaking from day one, a checkpoint or retry between levels, something for the
dead to do, and a warning and an answer for every threat.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- **±2.52% on whole-sample shares**; periods ±4.4% to ±8.1%. Most complaint counts are under 25.
- **Reviews are very short**; 605 of 2,157 bullets are a bare thumbs up.
- **The launch month is read at 0.31% and weighted ×325**, but holds only 0.9% of reviews.
- **Not checked:** that the store cover was changed; a claim that enemies could once be held as long
  as you liked; hosting and server details; hacker reports; prices reviewers quote.
- **English only.** About 251,000 reviews in other languages; none pulled.
