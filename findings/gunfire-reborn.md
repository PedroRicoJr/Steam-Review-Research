## 1. The short version

- **93.9% thumbs up, 33.1 complaints and 130.0 praise per 100 reviews; praise to complaint 3.9 : 1.**
  Between Risk of Rain 2 (5.8 : 1) and Escape from Duckov (3.3 : 1) on the same count (`cross-game.md`
  section 22).
- **It is a friends game that also works alone.** `much-better-with-friends` 181 (9.6 per 100);
  `works-solo` 56 against `punishing-solo` 14.
- **It is explained as Borderlands plus Risk of Rain.** `explained-by-naming-other-games` 147; 114 of
  1,878 reviews name Borderlands (4 thumbs down), 57 name Risk of Rain (1 down).
- **The top complaint is the English translation** (`reads-badly` 36) — **1.9 per 100, ten times the
  next game of 300 or more reviews in the corpus.**
- **The thumb and the complaints move together over time**: 97.9% up and 20.9 complaints per 100 in
  Early Access; 88.8% and 46.3 in 2025–26. Translation, slow movement and too few players carry the rise.
- **387 of the 621 complaint bullets (62.3%) come from reviews that recommend the game.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding: a friends game whose friction is around the play, not in it

**The praise is about the run** — builds (110), coming back (106), impact (69), feeling overpowered (48).
**The complaints near the top are mostly about what surrounds the run**: the English on every upgrade
(36), the unlock pace before the run gets good (`slow-start` 22, `grindy` 18), finding strangers to
play with (23 reviews across the population tags), and trust issues late in its life — a clause in
the user agreement (91 helpful), AI-made art (5 reviews, 2026-05 to 2026-09) and a console version left
behind (6). **Inside the run, the one design complaint that keeps growing is movement** (`sluggish` 15,
`no-modern-moves` 9, 14 of the 22 reviews in 2025–26).
