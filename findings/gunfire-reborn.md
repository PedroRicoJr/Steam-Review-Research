## 1. The short version

**No tag names in this section.**

- **Players like it a lot, and most complaints come from fans.** 93.9% thumbs up, 33.1 complaints and
  130.0 praise per 100 reviews; praise to complaint 3.9 : 1. That is between Risk of Rain 2 (5.8 : 1) and
  Escape from Duckov (3.3 : 1) on the same count (`cross-game.md` section 22). **387 of the 621 complaint
  bullets (62.3%) come from reviews that recommend the game.**
- **It is a friends game that also works alone.** "Much better with friends" was said 181 times (9.6 per
  100); 56 say it works solo; those who say it is too hard alone: 14.
- **It is explained as Borderlands plus Risk of Rain.** Players named other games to explain it 147 times;
  114 of 1,878 reviews name Borderlands (4 thumbs down), 57 name Risk of Rain (1 down).
- **The top complaint is the English translation** (36 reviews) — **1.9 per 100, ten times the next game
  of 300 or more reviews in the corpus.**
- **The thumb and the complaints move together over time**: 97.9% up and 20.9 complaints per 100 in
  Early Access; 88.8% and 46.3 in 2025–26. Translation, slow movement and too few players carry the rise.

## 5. ⭐ The finding: a friends game whose friction is around the play, not in it

**The praise is about the run** — builds (110), coming back (106), impact (69), feeling overpowered (48).
**The complaints near the top are mostly about what surrounds the run**: the English on every upgrade
(36), the unlock pace before the run gets good (slow start 22, grind 18), finding strangers to play with
(23 reviews on too few players), and trust issues late in its life — a clause in the user agreement (91
helpful), AI-made art (5 reviews, 2026-05 to 2026-09) and a console version left behind (6). **Inside the
run, the one design complaint that keeps growing is movement** (slow movement 15, no modern moves 9, 14
of the 22 reviews in 2025–26).
