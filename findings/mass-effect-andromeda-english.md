⚠️ **Judged against three older games.** The reviews measure Andromeda against the Mass Effect trilogy
far more than against anything it does: 396 of 1,585 reviews mention the trilogy, Shepard's numbered
games or the Legendary Edition. 508 reviews are 15 words or fewer. **The notes per review are not even
across the read**: about 3.0 per review in batches 1-10, 2.3 in batches 11-28 (fewer notes on long reviews:
5-7 a long review early, 3-5 later), and 3.5 in batches 29-32 (`batch_quality.py`). The batches run in
date order, so per-100 rates in late 2025-2026 (batches 26-32) mix both densities; read the trend in the
thumbs-up column, which does not depend on it.

**How to read the words below.** A **bullet** is one thing one person said. A **tag** is where that
bullet was filed. Tags have three levels: a **division** (who at a studio owns it — game design, art,
engineering), a **subject** (the thing being talked about), and a **mode** (what actually happened).
`unknown` means the person raised a subject and never said what about it.

## 1. In plain words

**The trilogy decides how people feel about it.** The most common complaint is that it falls short of
the earlier Mass Effect games (213 notes); the second most common praise is that it was judged unfairly
and is better than its name (187). 55 say it lives up to the trilogy.

**People who stay praise the combat, the story and the worlds.** 127 notes praise the combat - jump jets,
dashes and powers on any class - and 75 the freedom to build a character; 126 say the story is worth
following, 119 like the characters, 106 the worlds to explore, 75 the looks.

**People who leave mostly blame the writing.** Of 461 thumbs down, the most common named reasons are
that it falls short of the trilogy (88), flat or annoying characters and dialogue (81), the EA app (51),
a thin story (45), bugs (32) and empty fetch quests (32). Choices that change nothing come up in 79 notes.

**The faces were a launch-era problem.** Stiff, dead faces fall from 16 per 100 reviews at the Steam
launch in 2020 to about 4 per 100 from 2023; 64 notes say the patches fixed what mattered.

**It is a game people buy on sale.** 110 notes say it is worth buying only on sale - the third most common
complaint - against 13 that call the price fair.

**Unfinished, and players wanted the rest.** 48 say the story threads are still unresolved, 23 that the
updates stopped, 21 that known bugs were never fixed; 55 ask for a sequel.

**The surprise: the co-op wave mode barely registers.** Only 56 of 1,585 reviews mention the multiplayer
or co-op at all; 19 single it out as a highlight, 9 say it still has players, and by 2024-2025 some say it
is dead.

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play.** Only 56 of 1,585 reviews mention it. The mode is a four-player wave mode
  like Mass Effect 3's: 19 single it out ("Surprised to discover Multiplayer still active. If you were a
  fan of ME3 MP, you'll won't find much novelty", 205495146, 18 found it helpful), 9 say it still has
  players, 3 that it is dead. 95276834 bought it only for the mode and lists its faults: "Communicating
  with server" on every menu action, peer-to-peer hosting so friends far apart lag, worse netcode than
  ME3 (dead enemies linger, players teleport back), no hard cover to reset on the host. By 2024-2025:
  "only 2 other people online in about 5 hours" (206804915); 178574192 (1,643 hours) says new players
  won't join veterans and veterans play only with friends. Strike teams in the single player earn
  multiplayer rewards without playing it (206804915), and a phone app once ran them (113911607).
- **Combat and feel.** The strongest praise (127): jump jets, dashes, biotics, any power on any class.
  "Amazing combat mechanics - melee and biotics make you feel like a freakin superhero!" (232254261).
  Cover is automatic: 10 say it lets you down, 6 that it works. Three powers on the bar are too
  few (19).
- **Movement and controls.** Movement responsive (22); the dodge and jump jets feel off to 7; the default
  layout awkward (7), with reload and weapon swap on one button (217283500). The Nomad is loved (19 of the 22 notes
  for a loved weapon or vehicle) and handles badly for 11.
- **Enemies, bosses and difficulty.** Too few enemy types and the same fauna on every planet (15);
  bullet sponges on high settings (9); too easy (9); enemies scale with the player, so levelling changes
  nothing (2, reviewers' accounts).
- **Progression, loot and randomness.** Deep builds and profiles (75) against shallow (12); one reviewer
  says open profiles make classes pointless (230497936). Crafting and research feel useless to some;
  rarity is fixed per gun type (221458305).
- **Runs, content and replay.** Not built on runs. Plenty to do (42) against empty errands (72),
  repetition (41), back-and-forth quests (39) and padding (30); the vault puzzles wear thin (13, a mode
  built here).
- **Money and price.** Buy on sale (110); too high (15) against fair (13); the Deluxe Edition's multiplayer
  packs are store text, barely mentioned.
- **Tech.** Bugs (80), breaks play (34), crashes (24), saves lost (20), long loads (10); well optimised
  (15) against poorly (13). Saving is blocked in missions and vaults (9).
- **The studio and the community.** Patches fixed the worst (64), then stopped (23); mods fill gaps (18)
  and extend it (24). Reviewers blame EA's deadline and BioWare's troubled development.
- **What players asked for.** A sequel or the missing DLC (55); the quarian ark; the power wheel and squad
  commands back; choices that carry weight; a way to skip travel scenes (48); no EA app.

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **Mobile third-person combat with powers is the part that ages best.** 127 praise it, including many
   who dislike the rest; jump jets and dashes are what they name. Dominion's movement and powers can
   carry a run. *Strong.*
2. **Let players build freely, but keep classes distinct.** 75 praise switching profiles and mixing
   powers; one warns that open profiles make classes pointless. Give each Dominion class something only
   it can do. *Medium.*
3. **Never put a launcher or a server check between the player and the game or its menus.** 70 object to
   the EA app, 25 could not start it, and one reviewer says the co-op mode waits on the server for every
   menu action.
   *Strong.*
4. **Listen-server co-op lags when the host is far away.** 95276834: peer-to-peer hosting, so friends far
   apart lag; dead enemies linger and players teleport. Region-aware hosting matters for Dominion.
   *Medium.*
5. **A co-op mode bolted onto a story game is easy to overlook.** 56 of 1,585 mention it. Dominion's
   co-op is the game itself, which avoids this. *Weak.*
6. **Keep the matchmaking pool from splitting veterans and newcomers.** 178574192: new players avoid
   veterans, veterans play only with friends, so lobbies stay empty. *Medium.*
7. **Don't pad with errands.** Empty errands (72), repetition (41), back-and-forth (39) and padding (30)
   are the content complaints; Dominion's runs should keep every objective short and tied to the run.
   *Strong.*
8. **Let players skip travel and transition scenes.** 48 complain. *Medium.*
9. **Ship the faces and animation right, or players remember it for years.** 98 notes, 16 per 100 at
   launch. *Medium.*
10. **Never lose a save.** 20 lost saves, often to cloud sync. *Strong.*

## 7. Confidence and limits

- 1,585 of 11,214 reviews, ±2.51%; periods ±4.8% to ±13.1%; 2020-06 is read at ×13.
- **Note density changed during the read** (about 3.0, then 2.3, then 3.5 notes per review by batch); per-100
  rates across periods carry that. This game is the test set of the third pace test (loop file).
- 120 reviews (7.6%) were edited later and are counted on the day first written.
- **Checked:** the store facts (BioWare, Electronic Arts, 2020-06-11, $39.99 on 2026-10-09, Online Co-op,
  the multiplayer packs in the Deluxe Edition text).
- **Not checked:** every claim about EA's launcher change, the last patch date, peer-to-peer hosting,
  server checks, the multiplayer's population, level scaling, the companion app, the cut DLC.
- Slurs and jabs are recorded only as "a crude jab" or "a crude remark"; remarks blaming hiring or
  cast politics are recorded as such without their wording.
- 10 reviews are in other languages.
