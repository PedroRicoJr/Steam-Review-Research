## 1. The short version

**Redfall's reviews are mostly about a purchase, a reputation, and a company — not about a game.**

- **The two most common things said are both about price**: buy it on sale (172) and it costs too
  much (144 on the 2026-09-03 page, 143 today). Together they are **8.3% of everything said**.
- **The largest single subject in the whole read is the game's reputation (356, 9.3%)** —
  people arguing about the game's standing rather than describing the game.
- **The most common piece of praise is "it is judged unfairly" (124)** — people saying the reviews are
  wrong. Nine people say the bad reputation is accurate. **A 14-to-1 argument.**
- **Talk about other players is 2.7% of everything said.** In Helldivers 2 it was 28.5% and the
  biggest area in the corpus. **This is a four-player co-op game whose reviewers almost never mention
  playing with anyone.**
- **3.76 observations per review — the densest in the corpus**, and only **7.2% of them say nothing
  specific** against 17.3% in Helldivers 2. **Redfall reviewers write long and they itemise.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding

### Two games under one score

| Window | Reviews read | % up | 95% interval |
|---|---|---|---|
| **May – Aug 2023** | 470 | **34.7%** | 30.4 – 39.0 |
| **Sept 2023 – Aug 2026** | 544 | **60.7%** | 56.6 – 64.8 |

**Nine consecutive rounds of reading, no interval overlap.** The turn is **October 2023, Game
Update 2** — not the first big patch in June, which moved nothing.

**What fell:** frame rate complaints 8.5% → 1.7% · enemy AI 17.0% → 8.5% · price 21.3% → 8.1% ·
shipped-broken 14.7% → 5.5%.

**What rose:** buy-on-sale 8.7% → **24.1%** · the studio closure 0.2% → **10.8%** · **crashes 1.7% →
5.5%** · cannot-connect 0.6% → 2.8%.

⚠️ **The game that exists now crashes more than the one that shipped.** Frame rate was fixed;
stability was not, and then the people who could fix it were let go.

**Steam shows one number for both games.** A 2026 buyer reads a 38.5% built almost entirely out of
May 2023.

### The commercial finding: it still sells and it does not start

**One in ten of every 2026 review read says the game will not launch.**

`engineering.servers.cannot-connect` holds 18 reviews, **13 of them after Microsoft closed the studio
in May 2024, seven with zero hours played, carrying 593 helpful votes between them.**

**The mechanism, as reviewers tell it:** the first launch puts a server-connection dialog on top of the
licence agreement. The agreement must be accepted to connect; the connection window is on top of it.
**The offline mode that bypasses it — shipped three weeks after the studio closed — is behind the same
screen.**

**The buyers became the support desk.** `players-teach-each-other-the-fix` (8) and
`one-setting-causes-the-crashes` (2) are both people publishing workarounds inside their reviews
because there is no one else to publish them.

⚠️ **The store keeps selling it.** `store-page-hides-a-dealbreaker` (8), refunds refused
(`wanted-to-but-could-not`, 34), and two reviewers asking outright for a delisting. **101 reviews in
this corpus have zero hours played and 89 of them are negative** — a permanent, self-renewing supply
of one-star reviews from people who never saw the game.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

1. ⚠️ **It is not Steam's 38.5%.** See section 7 of `redfall-english.md` (section 0 before the rebuild).
2. **English only.** 1,645 reviews in other languages were never pulled.
3. **It cannot separate a better game from a cheaper, better-warned audience.** After October 2023
   the patches landed *and* the price collapsed *and* every buyer had read the reviews first.
4. **Game Pass and hardware bundles are invisible here except through `came-free-with-hardware`
   (50).** Most people who played Redfall never paid for it and never reviewed it.
5. **175 reviews (17.3%) hold two dates and are filed under the first** (2026-09-03 count; the
   script's edit marker finds 102, 10.1%). An open question with Rico.
6. **Unchecked claims.** Server behaviour, refunds refused and delisting requests are what reviewers
   say. Patch dates and the studio closure date are from Steam's news feed.
7. **Thin months.** September 2023 is 16 reviews.
