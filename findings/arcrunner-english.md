## 1. In plain words

**What decides how players feel is whether the studio is still there.** In 2023, 74% of English
reviews were thumbs up; in 2024, 56%; in 2025, 45%; in 2026, none of the 2 reviews. Ten reviews
praised the studio for listening and patching fast, all in the first weeks; nine called the game
abandoned, from 2024 on.

**Players like how it looks and like playing it with friends.** 14 times a reviewer praised the look,
and 12 times a reviewer said it is much better with friends. 23 of the 121 thumbs-up reviews (19%)
give no reason at all.

**The top complaint is that you move too slowly.** 17 times it was said, 7 from thumbs-up reviews and
10 from thumbs-down — even after a patch that sped players up. Three recommenders say the slow pace
is the reason they bought it.

**The design choice everyone names is that finishing the game wipes your permanent upgrades.** 12
times it was said, 7 of them by people who still recommend the game. It is the one complaint that
both people who stayed and people who left make.

**Almost nobody can describe it without naming Risk of Rain 2.** 23 times a reviewer described the
game by naming another one, and 11 times a reviewer said another game does it better — 10 of those 11
are thumbs down. The most-helpful review of the game is three words of comparison.

**The other big complaints are about sameness.** 12 times a reviewer said many guns and skills are
useless, 12 times that the levels repeat, 10 times that key tools are locked at the start, and 9
times that there is too little in the game.

**The surprise: one thumbs down is not about the game at all.** One reviewer says the game is fun and
they would recommend it, but votes no because they think the studio has left it. The vote is a
protest against the studio.

## 3. The big stories

### 3.1 ⭐ Praised for listening in the launch month, called abandoned inside eighteen months

| | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reviews | **137** | 27 | 11 | 2 |
| % up | **74%** | 56% | 45% | 0% |
| `developer-communication.listens-and-acts` | **10** | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| `live-ops.abandonment.updates-stopped` | 3 ⚠️ | 3 | 3 | 0 |

> *"the devs really listen to the community, insane the speed they're putting patches out"*
> (`137512385`, May 2023)
> *"absolute GEM that the devs seemed to have abandoned"* (the same review, edited January 2024)

⚠️ **The three 2023 abandonment bullets are all edits made in 2024 or later**, flattened onto their
creation date — the multi-dated rule that is still open with Rico. Read them as 2024.

🔑 **Every game in this block so far has the same arc.** Zombie Girl: `steady-stream` ×3 in 2023,
*"the developer has run away"* in 2026. ZCREW: *"an example to All Early Access games"* in 2021,
*"another forsaken child of the game industry"* in 2026. ArcRunner: ten reviews praising
responsiveness in the first five weeks, nine calling it abandonware from 2024 on. **A small studio's
launch-window responsiveness is the most-praised thing about it and the least predictive.** Across
the block (`findings/cross-game.md` section 15), `listens-and-acts` is 23 bullets, every one in a
launch window, and `updates-stopped` 16, every one a year or more after; five games made the turn.

One reviewer makes the mechanism explicit: *"the game is fun, and I would give this a thumbs up... as
of right now I believe that this is abandonware"* (`189831000`). **The thumb is a protest vote against
the studio, not a verdict on the game** — `review.thumb-is-a-protest-vote`.

Checked against a primary source: nothing. The patch pace, the speed patch and the end of updates are
what reviewers say.

### 3.2 ⭐ Nobody can describe it without saying Risk of Rain 2 — and a few object

`explained-by-naming-other-games` is **23 bullets, tied for the largest mode in the game**, and 18 of
the reviews that name another game name Risk of Rain. `beaten-by-a-competitor` adds 11, **10 of them
thumbs down**.

> *"Risk of Cyberpunk Rain 2"* (`137389141`, 118 helpful — the most-upvoted review of the game)
> *"like playing Risk of Rain 2 and Gunfire Reborn at the same time, but without the fun"*
> (`137666125`)

**And a counter-current the other block games do not have:** reviewers who reject the comparison.
*"I don't know why there are so many comparisons to ROR2. This reminds me of Mass Effect
multiplayer"* (`137415655`, 77 helpful). *"it's apples and oranges... arcrunner is more like its
parents, doom and returnal"* (`139378615`). *"I haven't played Returnal, risk of rain, or any other
game that so many negative reviews seem to reference... maybe I'd simply enjoy one thing without
constantly being upset that it isn't something else"* (`137916317`).

🔑 **The comparison is doing the game's marketing and its damage at once.** The recommending
reviews use it as a description; the negative ones use it as a verdict.

### 3.3 ⭐ The meta-progression wipe is the design decision everyone names

`game-design.progression.unlock-pace.progress-does-not-carry-over` — **12 bullets, 7 of them on
thumbs-up reviews.** Finishing the campaign on normal resets every permanent upgrade.

> *"Had lots of fun.... Until the first clear on normal and all your meta progression is wiped and you
> start fresh again."* (`138323944`, thumbs up)
> *"None of the problems I mentioned until now are even remotely as bad as the fact that finishing
> the game resets all of your metaprogression."* (`138076943`, thumbs down)

**Recommenders name it as often as detractors do.** It is the one design choice in the game that
crosses the thumb line, and one reviewer says the studio patched it into a *different* reset rather
than removing it (`137459084`).

Two related modes sit beside it: `slow-start` (10 — core features like the secondary weapon and the
ultimate are locked behind the meta-currency) and **gap 320** — meta-progression makes the early
floors trivial and nothing lets you skip them. **This is the block's roguelite claim and it will
recur.**

### 3.4 ⭐ Slow is the word, and it splits the thumb

`game-design.game-feel.movement.sluggish` — **17 bullets, the largest complaint in the game, 7 up
and 10 down.**

> *"For a game with 'runner' in the title, you move remarkably slow, even after the patch to speed you
> up."* (`138076943`)
> *"Arc-Casual-Jogger... this is about the slowest 'Action' game I have in my library"* (`154220887`)
> *"risk of rain was to fast for me, if ur older with slower reflexes give this a shot"* (`137464145`,
> thumbs up)

**The same property is a fault to one buyer and the reason to buy for another.** The tree records
the property as a complaint because that is how it is written 10 times in 17; the three
recommenders who praise the pace sit on `pacing.a-game-you-can-unwind-to`. (The 2026-10-03 re-run
counts 2 bullets on that mode, not 3; the difference was not traced on this page.)

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **Make sure shots are not eaten by scenery in third person.**
   Evidence: 4 times in ArcRunner, 6 times across three third-person games.
   Dominion could: test the shot from the camera against the shot from the gun, so low ledges, rails
   and things behind the camera do not block a shot the crosshair says is clear; show a "blocked"
   marker on the crosshair when the gun cannot reach the aim point.
   Strength: medium (small counts, but in three games and a known third-person fault).
2. **Do not wipe permanent upgrades when players finish the game.**
   Evidence: 12 times it was said, 7 by players who still recommend it; one reviewer calls it worse
   than every other problem combined (`138076943`).
   Dominion could: keep earned upgrades after a "win"; if a reset is wanted, make it a choice the
   player opts into, with a clear reward.
   Strength: medium.
3. **Let players skip content they have outgrown.**
   Evidence: 2 times it was said, one game: early floors become a chore once upgrades stack up.
   Dominion could: let a team start later in a run, or scale early areas up, once they have proven
   they can clear them.
   Strength: weak.
4. **Do not lock core kit behind the between-run currency.**
   Evidence: 10 times it was said that the second weapon and the ultimate were locked at first.
   Dominion could: give every player their core tools from the first run; put side options, not core
   tools, behind unlocks.
   Strength: medium.
5. **Movement speed decides the feel of a shooter built on runs.**
   Evidence: 17 times it was said that it is too slow, the largest complaint, even after a speed
   patch; 3 recommenders liked the slow pace.
   Dominion could: tune sprint and dodge early with playtests; if a slower pace is part of the
   identity, say so on the store page.
   Strength: medium.
6. **Fast patches at launch win praise, but going quiet later costs more than the praise earned.**
   Evidence: 10 reviews praise listening (all 2023), 9 call it abandoned (2024 on); thumbs up fell
   from 74% to 45% by 2025. One thumbs down is a protest about the studio, not the game.
   Dominion could: plan updates at a pace that can last for years, not a burst at launch; tell
   players when updates will slow down rather than going silent.
   Strength: medium in this game; strong when read with the rest of the block (five games show it).
7. **Expect to be judged against the genre leader.**
   Evidence: naming other games is tied for the most common thing said (23 times); 11 say it is worse
   than another game, 10 of them thumbs down; the most-helpful review is "Risk of Cyberpunk Rain 2".
   Dominion could: decide early which game players will name, and make the difference clear in the
   first minutes and on the store page.
   Strength: medium.
8. **Put words next to icons.**
   Evidence: one reviewer could not remember what each gun and skill did from the picture.
   Dominion could: add short text to every gun, skill and upgrade icon, on hover or hold.
   Strength: weak.
