the studio, not a verdict on the game** — `review.thumb-is-a-protest-vote`.

## 2. Nobody can describe it without saying Risk of Rain 2 — and a few object

`explained-by-naming-other-games` is **23 bullets, tied for the largest mode in the game**, and 18 of
the reviews that name another game name Risk of Rain. `beaten-by-a-competitor` adds 11, **10 of them
thumbs down**.

> *"Risk of Cyberpunk Rain 2"* (`137389141`, 118 helpful — the most-upvoted review of the game)
> *"like playing Risk of Rain 2 and Gunfire Reborn at the same time, but without the fun"*
> (`137666125`)

**And a counter-current the other block games do not have:** reviewers who reject the comparison.
*"I don't know why there are so many comparisons to ROR2. This reminds me of Mass Effect
multiplayer"* (`137415655`, 77 helpful). *"it's apples and oranges... arcrunner is more like its
parents, doom and returnal"* (`139378615`). *"I haven't played Returnal, risk of rain, or any other
game that so many negative reviews seem to reference... maybe I'd simply enjoy one thing without
constantly being upset that it isn't something else"* (`137916317`).

🔑 **The comparison is doing the game's marketing and its damage at once.** The recommending
reviews use it as a description; the negative ones use it as a verdict.

## 3. The meta-progression wipe is the design decision everyone names

`game-design.progression.unlock-pace.progress-does-not-carry-over` — **12 bullets, 7 of them on
thumbs-up reviews.** Finishing the campaign on normal resets every permanent upgrade.

> *"Had lots of fun.... Until the first clear on normal and all your meta progression is wiped and you
> start fresh again."* (`138323944`, thumbs up)
> *"None of the problems I mentioned until now are even remotely as bad as the fact that finishing
> the game resets all of your metaprogression."* (`138076943`, thumbs down)

**Recommenders name it as often as detractors do.** It is the one design choice in the game that
crosses the thumb line, and one reviewer says the studio patched it into a *different* reset rather
than removing it (`137459084`).

Two related modes sit beside it: `slow-start` (10 — core features like the secondary weapon and the
ultimate are locked behind the meta-currency) and **gap 320** — meta-progression makes the early
floors trivial and nothing lets you skip them. **This is the block's roguelite claim and it will
recur.**

## 4. Slow is the word, and it splits the thumb

`game-design.game-feel.movement.sluggish` — **17 bullets, the largest complaint in the game, 7 up
and 10 down.**

> *"For a game with 'runner' in the title, you move remarkably slow, even after the patch to speed you
> up."* (`138076943`)
> *"Arc-Casual-Jogger... this is about the slowest 'Action' game I have in my library"* (`154220887`)
> *"risk of rain was to fast for me, if ur older with slower reflexes give this a shot"* (`137464145`,
> thumbs up)

**The same property is a fault to one buyer and the reason to buy for another.** The tree records
the property as a complaint because that is how it is written 10 times in 17; the three
recommenders who praise the pace sit on `pacing.a-game-you-can-unwind-to`.

## 5. What the tree gained — two modes, one gap, one question for Rico

**Zero modes unique to this game. Two built on cross-game evidence where ArcRunner tipped the count:**

| Mode | Sightings | Games |
|---|---|---|
| `game-feel.combat.the-scenery-swallows-your-shots` (−) | 6 | ArcRunner ×4, Helldivers 2, Immortal: Unchained |
| `review.used-a-cheat-to-get-through-it` (~) | 2 | ArcRunner, Immortal: Unchained — closes gap 316 |

The first is a **third-person camera fault**: shots stopped by fences, rails, lamp posts, ankle-high
ledges, and in one case geometry *behind* the camera. Nine of the corpus's ten games are now
third-person, and the tree had no name for it.

**Gap 320 opened:** meta-progression outlevels the early floors and nothing lets you start later. Two
sightings, one game. Build on the first sighting elsewhere in the block.

✅ **A new `accessibility` subject came out of this game.** `165667903`: *"It is hard to tell what each
gun has and each skill unless you memorize them by the picture... I have TBI and it's hard for me to
remember everything all the time."* The division had `hearing`, `motor`, `vision`, `phobia`,
`mental-health-portrayal` and nothing for memory. **Rico cleared `accessibility.memory-and-attention`
on 2026-09-10**; this bullet and one from Immortal: Unchained were re-homed onto
`.relies-on-remembering-with-no-text`.
