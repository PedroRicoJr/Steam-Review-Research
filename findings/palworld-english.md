## 1. In plain words

**Palworld is judged against Pokémon, and players say it wins.** 230 of the 1,325 reviews say the word
Pokémon. 140 notes explain the game by naming other games (230 reviews say Pokémon, 65 say ARK), and 77 say it
beats its rivals - 62 of those 77 name Pokémon, Nintendo or Game Freak, most often as the game Pokémon
should have been; most of the rest name ARK.

**The top named praise is that it keeps pulling you back.** 75 reviews say they can't stop playing -
"oh I'll just play an hour then hop off" and "8 hours have passed" (231158193), or a joke that they no longer sleep (231775473). The median review shows 41 hours played; 315 show 100 or more.

**Friends make it better, but it works alone.** 52 say it is much better with friends; 11 say it is
equally fine solo; 3 say some late content is too hard alone.

**The thumbs down are about lost saves, the grind and a world that goes quiet.** Only 85 of 1,325 are
thumbs down. Their most common named reasons: a lost or corrupted save or world (9), a grind that does
not respect your time (8), crashes (6), not being able to connect at launch (6), and shallow base
building (5).

**The lawsuit turned some thumbs into a vote.** Nintendo and The Pokémon Company sued Pocketpair on
2024-09-18 (Nintendo's own release). In this sample, 42 thumbs-up reviews are a vote against Nintendo -
none before the suit, then 6.8 per 100 reviews in the second half of 2024. The most-helpful review of
that kind (65 found it helpful) says the game "needs all the attention and money they can get"
(175578922).

**The 1.0 release lifted it.** After 1.0 (July to October 2026) the thumb rose to 96.0% and praise to 60.5
per 100 reviews, from 42-47 before. Nine reviews say the game grew into its promise, eight of them after
1.0.

**The surprise: two of the loudest complaints are about the Pals as workers.** 11 say Pals get stuck,
starve or path badly at the base, and 7 that feeding and food spoilage are chores. The creatures are the
reason people buy it and also the reason some people quit ("every time i log in my pals are dog piled on the pal box
starving to death", 157814412, 145 hours).

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play.** 52 say much better with friends; 11 that solo is just as good; 3 that some
  bosses and late content need a group. The store allows 4 players in online co-op and 32 on a dedicated
  server; one reviewer finds the cap of 4 on a private world too small now that crossplay brings more
  friends (220129810, reviewer's account). Hosting: whoever created the world must be logged in for
  others to play (231778624); two praise being able to host the server yourself and change any setting
  (201670417, 158336775). Official servers at launch: cheaters (5), cannot connect (6), lag (6), wrong
  region and ping (156739228). 2 want big shared servers or an online arena (234738263, 235869035); one
  praises the online PvP's base raids and sky dogfights (229890729).
- **Combat and feel.** Little said: 3 bullets on combat with no verdict, 3 feel overpowered, 1 impactful.
  Complaints: Pals are far stronger than you and do nothing once you die (223878139); "The combat and gunplay feels very janky"
  (156738630); combat "doesn't even pretend to function like 45% of the time" (234197065); 4 say bosses
  or attacks cannot be answered, including homing attacks through walls (234196689) and "police" that
  teleport in front of you until you die (227451451, 10 found it helpful). Two want a first-person view;
  one says the Pal over your shoulder blocks the screen and your shots hit terrain and your own Pal
  (231158504).
- **Movement and controls.** 3 call movement sluggish, 2 responsive; flying mounts carry you past the
  level design (234199787). Nothing else said.
- **Enemies, bosses and difficulty.** 5 call enemy AI poor; 3 too easy; 2 the player too fragile; 1
  too hard (needing 40 levels' advantage, 234196689); 4 well graded. The hard-mode towers after the
  final boss jump far above it (234199854). 15 praise the settings that let you tune the game.
- **Progression, loot and randomness.** 34 praise how the systems fit together; 19 call it grindy (8 of
  them thumbs down), most about the late game: "does not respect your time" (196660292, 30 found it
  helpful), breeding hundreds of one Pal into one four-star Pal, best weapons behind random schematics.
  13 have nothing left to chase; 12 like setting their own goals; 2 level too fast to use the gear; catch
  rates feel like luck (233549914).
- **Runs, content and replay.** Not built on runs. 75 keep coming back; 20 say plenty to do; 21 want
  more; 11 find the world empty; 6 repetitive; dungeons the same few rooms (234199787).
- **The base and the Pals as workers.** 23 love the building; 8 call it shallow or rigid (the Palbox area
  too small, random spots that refuse a piece for "overlap", 233553296); 11 say Pals get stuck, starve or
  path badly; 7 that hunger, sanity and food spoilage are chores; automation lacking (234199231); 12
  quality-of-life asks, 3 of them for a way to assign Pals to jobs.
- **Money and price.** 18 call the price fair ("An absolute steal for the price", 227446717, 156 found it
  helpful - the most in the sample); 3 buy on sale; 3 praise no microtransactions; 1 calls it a cash grab.
  Pocketpair said on 2024-09-14 the game "will remain buy-to-play and not f2p or GaaS" (Steam news).
- **Tech.** 17 buggy, 14 lost saves, 13 repeated crashes (including "Could not save" every 5-20 minutes
  after 1.0, 231159983), 4 unstable frame rate, 10 well optimised, 3 say it runs well on their platform (227446717 on a Steam Deck). One blames
  the engine (236577733).
- **The studio and the community.** 14 like the steady updates, 7 say patches fixed what mattered; 3 say
  the studio went silent after the bad launch; 3 that it worked on the wrong thing first (a Valentine's
  event, crossovers); one says the developers locked forum threads (222774158, 10 found it helpful,
  reviewer's account). 4 reviews teach the reader how to play.
- **What players asked for.** A way to assign Pals to jobs (3); better Pal pathing (11
  complaints); more endgame (13); a mini-map (1); inventory search, sorting and trading (2); cosmetic armour
  or a hidden helmet (2); first person (2); big shared servers or an online arena (2); more story
  (235363572; another has 500+ hours "and no idea what the plot is", 227446717);
  diagonal fences (1); a longer night (1).

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **Never lose a player's progress.** Lost saves, worlds and bases are the most common named reason for
   a thumbs down (9 of 85), led by a host's power cut that corrupted a shared world (169961983). Save
   often, keep backups, and make a host's crash end the session cleanly, not the save. *Strong.*
2. **Plan for the host leaving.** Here the world's creator must be online for friends to play
   (231778624). In a listen-server game, decide early what happens to a run and to the group's progress
   when the host quits or crashes. *Strong.*
3. **Public servers draw cheaters on day one.** The studio confirmed cheating on its official servers six
   days after launch; players say cheaters crashed servers and deleted bases. If Dominion ever has
   public lobbies, give hosts a player list and a kick, and keep each player's progress out of other
   players' reach. *Medium.*
4. **Let players host and tune their own game.** Hosting your own server and changing any setting is
   praised (2), and 15 praise the settings. A host-side settings screen (difficulty, loot, timers)
   is cheap goodwill. *Medium.*
5. **Companions must path and behave well.** 11 say Pals get stuck, starve or wander; one quit over it
   after 145 hours. Any drone or AI helper in Dominion needs pathing tested on every map. *Medium.*
6. **Keep the late game from turning into a spreadsheet.** 19 call it grindy, most about the late game;
   the most-helpful thumbs down says it "does not respect your time" (196660292). *Medium.*
7. **Upkeep is not fun.** Feeding and spoilage are chores for 7. Any upkeep Dominion adds between runs
   should be one click or automatic. *Medium.*
8. **Check designs against patents before launch.** A loved move (throwing a Pal into the fight) was
   removed under a lawsuit, and a thumbs down followed. *Weak* for design, *strong* for risk.
9. **A big finishing update can lift a game players already like.** After 1.0 praise rose from 42-47 to
   60.5 per 100. *Medium.*
10. **Humour and a strong hook sell.** 30 praise the humour; 75 can't stop playing; the joke premise is
    in the store page itself. *Weak* for Dominion's tone, which is not decided here.
