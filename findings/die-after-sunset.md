## 1. The short version

**What decides how players feel is when they played:** 71% thumbs up in early access (35 reviews), 40%
in the year of full release (10), 17% since (6). Players say full release made it worse and then the
studio left. **The broken promise is co-op** — 4 of the 5 "less than promised" comments. **The top
design complaint is slow, clumsy movement** (7, 5 of them from people who still recommend it), with a
dodge that does not fire reliably (3) and upgrades that barely matter (4). **It is the most negative
text in the block.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding

**Full release can be the moment a game loses its players.** Die After Sunset was liked in early
access — 71% thumbs up across 35 reviews, a studio called "extremely devoted" — and fell to 40% in the
year of 1.0 and 17% after. Reviewers say 1.0 broke the first boss, the promised co-op never came, and
the studio left. One reviewer who preferred it to Risk of Rain 2 in 2022 came back in 2024 to call it
a below-average buggy game. The page reads ArcRunner as the same studio's next game, with the same arc
a year later.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- That co-op was promised is the reviewers' claim; the roadmap is not in the study.
- The studio name differs between this page (Playstark) and the ArcRunner page; not settled.
