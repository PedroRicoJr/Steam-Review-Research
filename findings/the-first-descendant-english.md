## 1. In plain words

**Players judge this game against Warframe, and it usually loses.** 282 of 1,600 reviews (17.6%) name
Warframe, and 141 of those are thumbs down. "Another game does this better" is the most common
complaint, said on 112 reviews; "it beats the games like it" is said 44 times. Destiny is named in 186
reviews.

**How the game sells things, and how much luck it asks for, drive the complaints.** 294 reviews (18.4%)
complain or remark about the shop: it costs far too much (75 times), the grind seems built to sell
shortcuts (58 reviews), it feels like a cash grab (56), and bought colours are used up when you put them
on (16). 125 reviews talk about luck: the thing you need may never drop (66 reviews), the stated chances
feel rigged (34), and one random drop only leads to another random drop (20).

**Complaints outnumber praise, the only one of ten games counted this way where that happens.** There
are 122.2 complaints for every 83.6 bits of praise per 100 reviews.

**The most common specific remark is that the characters are made to look sexy.** It is on 149 reviews,
9.3 of every 100; the next game is Terminull Brigade at 3.3. Some like it, some are embarrassed, many
only joke. It grows from 6.6 per 100 reviews at launch to 16.8 in 2026.

**The grind is both the top praise and a top complaint.** 100 times players say it is too grindy; 71
times they say the grind feels earned, and 56 times that everything can be earned without paying.
Complaints that it is too grindy fall from 6.7 to 2.7 per 100 reviews across the periods.

**Who complains: mostly players who do not recommend it, and they played less.** 547 of the 1,955
complaints (28.0%) come from reviews that still recommend the game; the rest come from thumbs-down
reviews. The thumbs-down reviews show a median of 15.6 hours
played; the thumbs-up 39.2.

**It got better, and the later reviewers say so.** Complaints per 100 reviews fall 128.0 → 119.7 →
102.5 → 84.3 across the four periods, while thumbs up goes from 63.1% to 69.2%. Praise that an update
made it better rises from 1.5 to 5.9 per 100. Praise that the studio listens peaks at 6.5 per 100 in
the rest of 2024.

**The surprise: the most-read reviews are angry veterans.** The three reviews the most people found
helpful are all thumbs down, all from 2025-2026, from players with 117 to 3,012 hours (section 3.3).

### 3.1 ⭐ Warframe is the yardstick

**282 of 1,600 reviews (17.6%) name Warframe — 172 at launch, 47 in the rest of 2024, 42 in 2025, 21
in 2026 — and half of them (141) are thumbs down.** Destiny is named in 186. Comparison is the most
common thing a reviewer does here: `explained-by-naming-other-games` 124, `a-named-rival-does-it-better`
113, `derivative-of-an-older-game` 54, `beats-its-rivals` 44.

|---|---|---|

- **"Warframe and Destiny had a child" is the standard description**, used in both directions:
  *"Like the child of Warframe and Destiny"* (170901009, thumbs up); *"took the good parts of Warframe and

- **When it loses, the grind and the shop are why.** *"It's Warframe, but with more microtransactions
  and more micromanagement"* (213357044, 298 found it helpful). A Warframe veteran of 5,000 hours finds
  the farm never makes him feel stronger (226240847).
- **When it wins, the shooting and the time are why.** *"The shooter experience ... feels worst in WF and
  in my opinion best in TFD"* (178128680). A 500-hour Warframe player says it *"respects my time more"*
  and gave him endgame materials when he came back (229267499, `returning-players-get-a-catch-up`, built

  content, no broken PvP (203838929, 47 found it helpful).

  and from Arc Raiders; others went back to Warframe, Destiny or Anthem (`beaten-by-a-competitor`).

### 3.2 ⭐ The shop and the dice

**This is where the reviews live.** 294 reviews carry a monetisation bullet and 125 a randomness bullet.
234 reviews (14.6 per 100) carry a monetisation complaint — the highest in the corpus, five times
Warframe's 2.8 — and 116 (7.2) a randomness complaint, also the highest (`cross-game.md` section 25).

**The chain of chances.** Characters and weapons are crafted from parts; parts come from containers; a
container drops at one chance and opens to the part at another. Reviewers do the maths: *"a 25% chance
... then roll a separate reactor at 10%, about 2.5% each"* (176109236); *"52 runs for a material for 52
runs of a mission, still no code"* (176097031). The modes:

| What players say | Tag | Reviews |
|---|---|---|
| The thing you need may never drop | `the-thing-you-need-may-never-roll` | 66 |
| The stated chances feel rigged | `the-stated-chances-feel-rigged` | 34 |
| One random drop leads to another | `one-random-drop-leads-to-another` (built here) | 20 |
| Wants a token that guarantees the drop | `wants-a-token-that-guarantees-the-drop` (built here) | 5 |
| Failed tries do not raise the chance | `failed-tries-do-not-raise-the-chance` (built here) | 3 |
| The odds are shown (+) | `the-odds-are-shown` (built here) | 3 |
| Bad-luck protection exists (+) | `bad-luck-protection-exists` (built here) | 2 |

**The game later answered part of this.** The praise for shown odds and pity comes only in 2025-2026
(5 reviews), and 2026 reviewers say a run of updates removed the container chance and cut the grind
(232874117, 236591847). Randomness reviews fall from 8.3 to 4.9 per 100.

**The shop.** `the-shop-charges-far-too-much` 75, `frustration-is-built-to-sell-shortcuts` 58,
`feels-like-a-cash-grab` 56, `paying-only-shortens-the-grind` 21 (~), `pay-affects-play` 23. Specific
grievances built as modes here: colours used up when applied (16), a battle pass with poor rewards (7),
skins only for money (5), a bought character without its upgrades, items only in bundles, a skin bought
again for each character, prices not adjusted for a region (208632208, 325 found it helpful), regret over
money spent. **Purchases that went wrong** draw their own small cluster: currency bought and never
received, or a purchase that would not go through (`no-remedy-for-a-purchase-that-went-wrong` 4,
`the-store-would-not-take-their-money` 3).

**Pay to win is the rising word.** `pay-affects-play` goes from 1.0 to 2.7 per 100. The third most
helpful review in the sample (233515312, 545 found it helpful, 2026-08) says season 4's Transcendent
weapons, weekly reroll caps and sold drop boosts are *"an open push to pay for power"*.

**The finding, from the master page and `cross-game.md` section 25:** both this game and Warframe are
free; the gap is not price but the chain players describe — grind for a container, roll it for a part,
wait for a craft, and pay to skip any step. `frustration-is-built-to-sell-shortcuts` and
`the-shop-charges-far-too-much` are each under 0.5 per 100 in Warframe; Warframe's randomness complaints
are 1.3 per 100. Terminull Brigade is 13.2 on monetisation complaints and Helldivers 2 1.8; every other
English group of 300 or more reviews is at 1.4 or less. On randomness, DRG: Rogue Core is 6.6, Remnant 2
2.5, Escape from Duckov 2.0.

**Checked:** drop rates, prices and what a season added are the reviewers' words, not checked.

## 4. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Co-op and online play.** Works but strangers cause friction. `much-better-with-friends` 24; but a
  strong player clears everything (`a-strong-player-kills-everything-first`, 10), players idle
  (`players-idle-and-leave-the-work-to-others`, built here, 3), one wipe ends a party, and matchmaking
  can drop you into a fight that started before you loaded (`the-fight-starts-before-you-load-in`, built
  here, 2). Each player rolls their own drops (`each-player-rolls-their-own-drops`, built here, 1).
  Players colliding with each other is a complaint built here (`players-block-each-other`, 2). **Launch
  servers failed, then stopped being mentioned**: `cannot-connect` is 15, every one in the launch period;
  `engineering.servers.poor` is 13; server reviews go from 4.3 per 100 to 0.5. Also used only in this game, one or
  two each: `times-you-out-while-loading` 2, `cannot-choose-a-region` 2,
  `you-join-another-players-mission-by-accident` 1, `a-revived-player-gets-no-grace-period` 1,
  `a-reset-fills-groups-with-low-level-players` 1, `a-stronger-player-can-help-you-through` 2 (+),
  `every-activity-has-matchmaking` 1. No guilds or clans (`no-guilds-or-clans`, 4). **Nothing said** in
  the page's counts on hosting type, bots, friendly fire or crossplay.
- **Combat and feel.** **Characters are the draw; guns are second.** `impactful` combat 42,
  `each-role-plays-its-own-way` 27, `appealing-cast` 46; but abilities that clear the map make guns
  pointless (`the-skills-make-the-guns-pointless`, 4), and enemies that switch off abilities make the
  character pointless (`enemies-switch-off-your-abilities`, built here, 1). One gun type uses the
  scarcest ammo without hitting hard enough (`a-weapon-type-uses-the-scarcest-ammo`, 4). Balance: one
  character far stronger than the rest (`one-option-dominates` 16, rising 0.5 → 3.2 per 100), power
  creep (6). Used only in this game: `carrying-an-objective-stops-you-shooting` 1, `damage-depends-on-the-frame-rate`
  1, `abilities-cost-energy-and-a-cooldown` 1.
- **Movement and controls.** **Mixed.** `responsive` 19 (the grapple and double jump praised);
  `no-modern-moves` (no crouch or slide) and an unreliable grapple are the complaints. Used only in this game:
  `players-block-each-other` 2, `weightless` 1, `cannot-switch-shoulders` 1. **Nothing said** with a count
  on controller support or rebinding.
- **Enemies, bosses and difficulty.** `the-bosses-are-a-highlight` 15; `a-boss-rule-is-never-hinted` 4
  (an order or target you must guess); enemies get spongier each update (208640843, 289 found it
  helpful); enemies that switch off your abilities. Used only in this game: `a-timed-minigame-is-too-tight` 3,
  `a-revive-limit-restarts-the-whole-fight` 1. **Nothing said** with a count on difficulty settings or
  enemy variety.
- **Progression, loot and randomness.** **The grind is the game.** `grindy` 100, `grind-feels-earned`
  71, `everything-earnable` 56, `satisfying-progression` 18, `gated-behind-real-world-time` 25,
  `deep-and-varied` builds 34. The chain of chances is section 3.2: may never drop 66 reviews, chances

## 5. For Dominion — our reading

**These are suggestions drawn from what players said, not something the reviews said.** Dominion is a
third-person, four-player co-op sci-fi PvE extraction and arena shooter built on runs, hosted on one
player's machine (listen server), in Unreal Engine 5.

1. **Do not stack chance on chance, with money as the way out.**
   Evidence: shop complaints are 14.6 reviews per 100, the highest in the corpus; Warframe, also free, is
   2.8. Luck complaints are 7.2 per 100, also the highest. One random drop leads to another (20),
   frustration built to sell shortcuts (58 reviews), shop too expensive (75).
   Dominion could: keep each reward to one roll at most. Never put a random box inside a random box. If
   Dominion ever sells anything, sell nothing that skips a luck step.
   Strength: strong.
2. **Show the odds and end bad luck from day one.**
   Evidence: shown odds and bad-luck protection were praised only after the game added them (5 reviews,
   2025-2026); 5 reviews ask for a guaranteed-drop token; 3 say failed tries do not help; 66 reviews say
   the thing they need may never drop; 34 say the odds feel rigged. Luck reviews fell from 8.3 to 4.9 per
   100 once it changed.
   Dominion could: show drop chances in the run summary or codex; give a pity counter that guarantees the
   item after a set number of tries; let players trade duplicates for a token.
   Strength: medium (the praise is small; the complaints are large).
3. **Grind is fine if it feels earned and everything is earnable by play.**
   Evidence: too grindy 100 against grind feels earned 71 and everything earnable 56; real-world wait
   timers 25; grind complaints fell 6.7 → 2.7 per 100 once the grind was cut.
   Dominion could: make meta-progress between runs steady and visible; avoid real-time timers on crafting
   or unlocks.
   Strength: strong.
4. **Do not sell power. Pay to win is the rising complaint even in a free game.**
   Evidence: pay affects play 23, rising 1.0 → 2.7 per 100; the third most-helpful review (545) calls
   season 4 *"an open push to pay for power"*; "now more about cosmetics than play" 0.1 → 2.7.
   Dominion could: keep anything bought to looks only, and keep the game about play.
   Strength: strong.
5. **If Dominion sells looks, make them permanent and fair.**
   Evidence: colours used up when applied (16), skins only for money (5), battle pass rewards poor (7),
   a skin bought again for each character, items only in bundles, no prices for poorer regions (325
   helpful). Praise: shop only sells looks (12), fair business model (11), buying to support the studio
   (12), free items often (rising to 2.7 per 100).
   Dominion could: make bought colours and skins unlock for good and for every character; set regional
   prices; give some skins for play.
   Strength: medium.
6. **Unreal Engine 5 performance is judged hard at launch.**
   Evidence: poorly optimised 41, strong PC needed 16, long loads 18, crashes 19 and 16, shader compiling
   at launch; loading screens between small areas; detail popping in close; runs worse after updates (4).
   Performance reviews fell 8.8 → 4.9 per 100 once fixed.
   Dominion could: precompile shaders before the first match; set a frame budget for low PCs; watch
   pop-in and load screens; test that updates do not slow old machines.
   Strength: strong (same engine).
7. **The launch month sets the score for years.**
   Evidence: 62% of all English reviews were written in the first month. Complaints fell from 128 to 84
   per 100 later, yet Steam still shows 57%, Mixed. Cannot connect (15) was all at launch.
   Dominion could: fix the economy, performance and joining before launch, not after; run a public test
   first (11 reviews here came through a test before release).
   Strength: strong.
8. **Know your yardstick and win on one clear thing.**
   Evidence: 282 reviews (17.6%) name Warframe, half of them thumbs down; Destiny in 186. A named rival
   does it better 113; a copy of an older game 54; beats its rivals 44. When it won, it won on the
   shooting and on respecting time.
   Dominion could: expect to be compared to the co-op shooters near it, and make sure one thing is
   plainly better (feel, run length, fairness of drops).
   Strength: strong for the pattern; medium for which rival.
9. **Strangers in co-op need guard rails.**
   Evidence: one strong player kills everything first (10); players idle; one wipe ends a party; you can
   be matched into a fight already started; players bump into each other (2). Better with friends 24.
   Dominion could: give a late joiner a safe spawn and a moment of control before they can be hurt; make
   power gaps in a lobby smaller (scale or cap); let players walk through each other; reward taking part,
   not only kills.
   Strength: medium.
10. **Decide whether loot is shared or rolled per player, and say so.**
    Evidence: each player rolls their own drops, so one player's luck does nothing for the group (built
    here; count not given).
    Dominion could: make some drops shared or tradeable inside a team, so co-op helps everyone's luck.
    Strength: weak.
11. **Keep guns worth using next to abilities.**
    Evidence: abilities make the guns pointless (4); enemies that switch off abilities make the character
    pointless; one gun type uses the scarcest ammo (4).
    Dominion could: let abilities set up gun damage rather than replace it; avoid bosses that turn off
    the whole kit; keep ammo types fair.
    Strength: weak to medium.
12. **Buff the weak more than you nerf the loved, and do not promise "no nerfs".**
    Evidence: one character too strong (16, rising 0.5 → 3.2 per 100); the most-helpful review (592)
    is about a nerf right after buffs; took back a promise (4); power creep (6).
    Dominion could: balance by raising weak options; never promise you will not nerf.
    Strength: medium.
13. **Telegraph boss rules.**
    Evidence: a boss rule is never hinted (4); bosses a highlight (15).
    Dominion could: show the needed order or target with clear visual or voice cues.
    Strength: weak.
14. **Movement needs the modern moves.**
    Evidence: responsive movement 19 (grapple, double jump); complaints about no crouch or slide and an
    unreliable grapple.
    Dominion could: include a slide or dodge, and make any traversal tool reliable.
    Strength: medium (it agrees with Crab Champions).
15. **Protect accounts and purchases.**
    Evidence: locked out of own account 4 (linking wiped progress; a ticket closed); the second
    most-helpful review (554) is about a lost account; purchase that went wrong 4; store would not take
    money 3.
    Dominion could: never let linking a platform overwrite progress; back up saves; have a clear support
    path for lost progress.
    Strength: medium (few reviews, very high helpful votes).
16. **Choose the tone and keep looks inside it.**
    Evidence: fan service is 149 reviews (9.3 per 100) and rising to 16.8 per 100; skins break the mood;
    male characters weaker and less dressed up.
    Dominion could: decide the tone of its sci-fi look early, keep skins in that mood, and treat all
    characters with the same care.
    Strength: medium.
17. **A thin story is skipped; a light one can still win people later.**
    Evidence: thin or forgettable 47 vs worth following 29 (rising to 4.3 per 100 in 2026); a repeated
    word and names mocked.
    Dominion could: keep story short and avoid catchwords repeated too often.
    Strength: weak to medium.
18. **Listen early, keep saying so, and help returners catch up.**
    Evidence: listens and acts 45 (peak 6.5 per 100 in the first months, 1.1 in 2026); updates made it
    better rising to 5.9; returning player got catch-up rewards (229267499).
    Dominion could: post what changed and why; give returning players a catch-up.
    Strength: medium.
19. **Late reviews come from veterans, and they are read most.**
    Evidence: median hours rise from 20 to 50-70; the top three most-helpful reviews are thumbs down from
    players with 117 to 3,012 hours.
    Dominion could: watch long-time players' complaints (balance, power sales) as closely as new players'.
    Strength: medium.
20. **Missions farmed over and over get called repetitive.**
    Evidence: repetitive 67; too little content 14.
    Dominion could: lean on runs that change, so repeating does not feel the same.
    Strength: medium.
21. **Guilds or clans are wanted in a long-running game.**
    Evidence: no guilds or clans (4).
    Dominion could: consider a light group feature if it goes live-service.
    Strength: weak.

## 7. Confidence and limits

- **The sample is 3.1% of the population.** Whole-sample shares carry ±2.50%. The periods carry about
  ±3 to ±7.
- **The launch month dominates the population**: 31,555 of 51,208 English reviews (62%) are from
  2024-07, and 864 of the 1,600 kept reviews are from the launch period. Weighted shares lean on it.
- **Single months are thin.** From 2024-10 on, most months are read at 20 reviews (2026-10 at 6); only
  2024-07 (862), 2024-08 (139) and 2024-09 (65) are read at more.
- **199 reviews (12.4%) were edited after creation and are counted on the created date.** Many edits are
  verdicts on later seasons (a 2024 review edited in 2026), so a later change can sit in an early month.
- **Claims were not checked**: drop rates, lawsuits or fines against the publisher, account problems,
  prices, and what a season added are the reviewers' words. Season and nerf dates are as the reviewers
  give them; no Steam announcement was read for this page.
- **Counts in the ranked tables are bullets (times it was said)**; a few on the page are given as
  reviews (112 vs 113, 66 vs 67, 58 vs 59).
- **A tag can be one game's habit**: `a-named-rival-does-it-better` is partly how this game was tagged;
  other games file a similar idea differently.
- **Fan-service remarks and slurs were recorded as "a crude remark", "a jab" or "a joke" and not
  repeated. Personal and health details were left out.**
- **16 reviews were written in another language**, filed on the language tag and summarised from what
  they say. About 61,000 reviews in other languages exist on Steam; none were pulled.
