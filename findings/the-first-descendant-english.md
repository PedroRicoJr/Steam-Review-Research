# The First Descendant — English

**A sample: 1,604 of 51,208 English reviews read, ±2.50% on any whole-sample share.** The pull
(2026-10-01) found 51,208 English reviews across 29 months; Steam's own English count on 2026-10-02 was
51,209. Figures are as of 2026-10-02.

**A free third-person looter shooter for one player or up to four in online co-op, built on Unreal Engine
5, released on Steam on 2024-06-30** (Steam store page: *"a third-person looter shooter powered by Unreal
Engine 5 ... Up to 4 players"*). Developed by NEXON Games Co., Ltd. and published by NEXON (Steam store
page). 112,544 reviews in all languages on 2026-10-02, 57% positive, *Mixed*. **It is C28 on
`planning/third-person-shooter-list.md`, picked by the active loop as the next closest to Dominion
(round 615).** It shares Dominion's third-person camera, sci-fi setting, four-player online co-op, power
that builds over time and Unreal Engine 5. It differs in being free with a shop, built on grinding for
characters rather than runs, and having no extraction.

⚠️ **This is the most argued-over game in the corpus.** Reviews are long for this corpus (mean **60.1

The counts below are unweighted bullets unless a table says otherwise; the weighted shares are in

**How to read the words below.** A **bullet** is one thing one person said. A **tag** is where that
bullet was filed. Tags have three levels: a **division** (who at a studio owns it — game design, art,
engineering), a **subject** (the thing being talked about), and a **mode** (what actually happened).
`unknown` means the person raised a subject and never said what about it.

## 1. The short version

**It is judged against Warframe, and usually loses.** **282 of 1,600 reviews (17.6%) name Warframe**;
141 of them are thumbs down. `a-named-rival-does-it-better` is **112 reviews**, the top complaint;
`beats-its-rivals` is 44. Destiny is named in 186.

**The shop and the dice drive the complaints.** 294 reviews (18.4%) carry a monetisation complaint or
remark: the shop costs far too much (75), the grind is built to sell shortcuts (58), it feels like a cash
grab (56), colours are used up when applied (16, a mode built here). 125 reviews talk about chance:
the thing you need may never drop (66), the stated chances feel rigged (34), one random drop leads to
another (20, built here).

**Fan service is the top specific tag.** `the-cast-is-built-to-titillate` is **149 reviews, 9.3 per
100**; the next game is Terminull Brigade at 3.3. It rises from 6.6 per 100 at launch to 16.8 in 2026.

**The grind is the game, for better and worse.** `grindy` is 100 and `grind-feels-earned` 71;
`everything-earnable` is 56. Grind complaints fall from 6.7 to 2.7 per 100 across the periods.

**It got better, and the later reviewers say so.** Complaints per 100 fall **128.0 → 119.7 → 102.5 →
84.3** across the four periods while thumbs up goes 63.1% → 69.2%. `made-it-better` rises from 1.5 to
5.9 per 100. `listens-and-acts` peaks in the rest of 2024 at 6.5 per 100. But the three most-helpful
reviews are all thumbs down, all from 2025-2026 (section 5).

## 3. ⭐ Warframe is the yardstick

**282 of 1,600 reviews (17.6%) name Warframe — 172 at launch, 47 in the rest of 2024, 42 in 2025, 21
in 2026 — and half of them (141) are thumbs down.** Destiny is named in 186. Comparison is the most
common thing a reviewer does here: `explained-by-naming-other-games` 124, `a-named-rival-does-it-better`
113, `derivative-of-an-older-game` 54, `beats-its-rivals` 44.

- **"Warframe and Destiny had a child" is the standard description**, used in both directions:
  *"Like the child of Warframe and Destiny"* (170901009, thumbs up); *"took the good parts of Warframe and
  Destiny 2 and threw it out the window"* (171640774, thumbs down).
- **When it loses, the grind and the shop are why.** *"It's Warframe, but with more microtransactions
  and more micromanagement"* (213357044, 298 found it helpful). A Warframe veteran of 5,000 hours finds
  the farm never makes him feel stronger (226240847).
- **When it wins, the shooting and the time are why.** *"The shooter experience ... feels worst in WF and
  in my opinion best in TFD"* (178128680). A 500-hour Warframe player says it *"respects my time more"*
  and gave him endgame materials when he came back (229267499, `returning-players-get-a-catch-up`, built
  here). A 2,239-hour reviewer lists what it lacks from its rivals: no content islands, no paywalled
  content, no broken PvP (203838929, 47 found it helpful).
- **Some came because of a rival, some left for one.** Players arrived from Helldivers 2 after its nerfs
  and from Arc Raiders; others went back to Warframe, Destiny or Anthem (`beaten-by-a-competitor`).
- **For a later reader:** `a-named-rival-does-it-better` is almost entirely this game's tag (112 of the
  114 reviews on it in the corpus; the other 2 are EDF 5); other games file a similar idea under `beaten-by-a-competitor` (The Anacrusis 73,
  Redfall 41), so its size here is partly how it was tagged.

## 4. ⭐ The shop and the dice

**This is where the reviews live.** 294 reviews carry a monetisation bullet and 125 a randomness bullet.

**The chain of chances.** Characters and weapons are crafted from parts; parts come from containers; a
container drops at one chance and opens to the part at another. Reviewers do the maths: *"a 25% chance
... then roll a separate reactor at 10%, about 2.5% each"* (176109236); *"52 runs for a material for 52
runs of a mission, still no code"* (176097031). The modes:

| What players say | Tag | Reviews |
|---|---|---|
| The thing you need may never drop | `the-thing-you-need-may-never-roll` | 66 |
| The stated chances feel rigged | `the-stated-chances-feel-rigged` | 34 |
| One random drop leads to another | `one-random-drop-leads-to-another` (built here) | 20 |
| Wants a token that guarantees the drop | `wants-a-token-that-guarantees-the-drop` (built here) | 5 |
| Failed tries do not raise the chance | `failed-tries-do-not-raise-the-chance` (built here) | 3 |
| The odds are shown (+) | `the-odds-are-shown` (built here) | 3 |
| Bad-luck protection exists (+) | `bad-luck-protection-exists` (built here) | 2 |

**The game later answered part of this.** The praise for shown odds and pity comes only in 2025-2026
(5 reviews), and 2026 reviewers say a run of updates removed the container chance and cut the grind
(232874117, 236591847). Randomness reviews fall from 8.3 to 4.9 per 100.

**The shop.** `the-shop-charges-far-too-much` 75, `frustration-is-built-to-sell-shortcuts` 58,
`feels-like-a-cash-grab` 56, `paying-only-shortens-the-grind` 21 (~), `pay-affects-play` 23. Specific
grievances built as modes here: colours used up when applied (16), a battle pass with poor rewards (7),
skins only for money (5), a bought character without its upgrades, items only in bundles, a skin bought
again for each character, prices not adjusted for a region (208632208, 325 found it helpful), regret over
money spent. **Purchases that went wrong** draw their own small cluster: currency bought and never
received, or a purchase that would not go through (`no-remedy-for-a-purchase-that-went-wrong` 4,
`the-store-would-not-take-their-money` 3).

**Pay to win is the rising word.** `pay-affects-play` goes from 1.0 to 2.7 per 100. The third most
helpful review in the sample (233515312, 545 found it helpful, 2026-08) says season 4's Transcendent
weapons, weekly reroll caps and sold drop boosts are *"an open push to pay for power"*.

## 5. ⭐ A game that changed — and what its veterans say

**The trend is better.** Complaints per 100 fall from 128 to 84; `made-it-better` reaches 5.9 per 100 in
2026; `recovered-over-time` appears: *"after a streak of high-quality monthly updates, TFD is actually
finally a very good game"* (226202933, 43 found it helpful). `free-items-are-handed-out-often` rises from
0.1 to 2.7 per 100: free ultimate characters, login events, catch-up mail.

**The developer is praised for listening, most in the first months.** `listens-and-acts` is 45 reviews,
6.5 per 100 in the rest of 2024 and 1.1 in 2026. One review says season 1's content drops were moved
earlier after feedback (173145523).

## 6. What the sample says about the design itself

- **Unreal Engine 5 costs it on PC.** `poorly-optimised` 41, `demanding-hardware` 16, `long-load-times`
  18, shader compiling and crashes at launch. Performance reviews fall from 8.8 to 4.9 per 100. Two
  modes built here: `loading-screens-split-small-areas` and `detail-pops-in-close-up`.
- **Launch servers failed, then stopped being mentioned.** `cannot-connect` is 15, every one in the
  launch period; server reviews go from 4.3 per 100 to 0.5.
- **Co-op works but strangers cause friction.** `much-better-with-friends` 24; but a strong player
  clears everything (10), players idle (`players-idle-and-leave-the-work-to-others`, built here), one
  wipe ends a party, and matchmaking can drop you into a fight that started before you loaded
  (`the-fight-starts-before-you-load-in`, built here). Each player rolls their own drops
  (`each-player-rolls-their-own-drops`, built here).
- **Characters are the draw; guns are second.** `each-role-plays-its-own-way` 27, `appealing-cast` 46;
  but abilities that clear the map make guns pointless, and enemies that switch off abilities make the
  character pointless (`enemies-switch-off-your-abilities`, built here).
- **Movement is mixed.** `responsive` 19 (the grapple and double jump praised); `no-modern-moves` (no
  crouch or slide) and an unreliable grapple are the complaints. Players colliding with each other is a
  complaint built here (`players-block-each-other`, 2).
- **Fan service is the loudest single thing.** 149 reviews on `the-cast-is-built-to-titillate` (~): some
  praise it, some find it embarrassing, many only joke. 16.8 per 100 in 2026. Related complaints: skins
  that break the tone of a battle (`cosmetics-break-the-tone`), male characters weaker and less dressed up
  (`the-options-for-one-gender-are-poor`).
- **A story most skip.** `thin-or-forgettable` 47 against `worth-following` 29 (which rises to 4.3 per
  100 in 2026). The repeated word "Ironheart" and the names Greg and Jeremy are mocked.

## 8. Confidence and limits

- **The sample is 3.1% of the population.** Whole-sample shares carry ±2.50%. The periods carry about
  ±3 to ±7.
- **The launch month dominates the population**: 31,555 of 51,208 English reviews (62%) are from
  2024-07, and 864 of the 1,600 kept reviews are from the launch period. Weighted shares lean on it.
- **199 reviews (12.4%) were edited after creation and are counted on the created date.** Many edits are
  verdicts on later seasons (a 2024 review edited in 2026), so a later change can sit in an early month.
- **Claims were not checked**: drop rates, lawsuits or fines against the publisher, account problems,
  prices, and what a season added are the reviewers' words. Season and nerf dates are as the reviewers
  give them; no Steam announcement was read for this page.
- **Fan-service remarks and slurs were recorded as "a crude remark", "a jab" or "a joke" and not
  repeated. Personal and health details were left out.**
- **16 reviews were written in another language**, filed on the language tag and summarised from what
  they say.
