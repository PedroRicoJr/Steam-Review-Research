**How to read the words below.** A **bullet** is one thing one person said. A **tag** is where that
bullet was filed. Tags have three levels: a **division** (who at a studio owns it — game design,
art, engineering), a **subject** (the thing being talked about), and a **mode** (what actually
happened). `unknown` means the person raised a subject and never said what about it.

## 1. The short version

**Almost nobody dislikes this game, and the reviews are mostly not about the game.**

- **97.1% thumbs up** (2,072 of 2,133). 61 people out of 2,133 gave it a thumbs down.
- **Of 4,511 observations, 3,744 are positive and 515 are negative.** The positive outnumber the
  negative **7 to 1**.
- **The single most common thing a player writes is the community catchphrase** — 610 bullets, 14% of
  everything said. It is more common than every design observation the corpus contains.
- **`community` is 28.8% of all observations** and is the second-largest division after `game-design`
  at 30.9%. **For a co-op shooter, the people are half the product.**

**The 61 negative reviews are worth naming, because they agree with each other.** The top four
complaints are `production.content-variety.repetitive` (29), `solo-viability.punishing-solo` (27),
`playing-with-friends.needs-a-group` (22) and `content-amount.too-little` (20).

**Three of those four are the same complaint: you need people, and without them the game thins out.**
That is the exact opposite of the top positive mode, `much-better-with-friends` (215). **The game's
best feature and its main complaint are one property read from two sides.**

## 3. Month by month

### Read the volume, not the sample

**The sample is 20 reviews a month at the floor, so a month's sample size says nothing about that
month's real volume.** The numbers below are the **true** review counts on Steam.

| Year | Reviews on Steam | Share of all | Thumbs down | Bullets per review |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2018 | 4,221 | 2.0% | 4.1% | 2.51 |
| 2019 | 10,669 | 4.9% | 4.6% | 3.03 |
| 2020 | 28,489 | 13.2% | 1.9% | 2.49 |
| 2021 | 29,508 | 13.7% | 2.4% | 2.08 |
| 2022 | 37,241 | 17.3% | 2.5% | 1.96 |
| 2023 | **51,740** | **24.0%** | 1.7% | 1.72 |
| 2024 | 30,299 | 14.1% | 2.7% | 1.65 |
| 2025 | 15,811 | 7.3% | 3.4% | 1.94 |
| 2026 | 7,586 | 3.5% | 3.9% | 2.10 |

*(Thumbs-down rate and bullets per review are from the sample, not the population.)*

### November is the biggest month of the year, seven years out of eight

| Year | Biggest month | Reviews | Second biggest | Reviews |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2018 | **2018-11** | 989 | 2018-03 | 896 |
| 2019 | **2019-11** | 2,411 | 2019-06 | 1,770 |
| 2020 | **2020-11** | 6,802 | 2020-05 | 3,553 |
| 2021 | **2021-11** | 7,305 | 2021-03 | 3,222 |
| 2022 | **2022-11** | **10,316** | 2022-12 | 4,331 |
| 2023 | **2023-11** | 8,529 | 2023-03 | 6,014 |
| **2024** | **2024-06 — Season 05** | **4,365** | 2024-11 | 3,329 |
| 2025 | **2025-11** | 2,008 | 2025-02 | 1,563 |

**The Steam Autumn Sale and the Steam Awards nomination window both sit in November.** In 2022 that
month produced **2.4 times** the reviews of the next biggest month of the year, and the studio shipped
nothing in it.

**The one exception is the finding.** 2024 is the only year November did not win, and it lost to
**June 2024 — the month Season 05 "Drilling Deeper" shipped**, with four patches in eight days. **2026
so far follows the same shape:** its biggest month is **2026-01**, when Season 06 shipped.

**So content does move review volume — but only a full season does, and only enough to beat the sale
once in eight years.** Everything smaller — events, hotfixes, cosmetic DLC, the anniversary — leaves no
mark on the monthly count at all.

**In 2018 half of reviewers described the game. By 2023 three quarters of them wrote one line, and a
quarter of everything written was the catchphrase.** The peak review year is also the least
informative one.

**This matters for anyone reading Steam reviews as research:** a game with a strong community ritual
converts its reviews into a greeting. **The review count goes up and the information goes down.**

## 4. The patch notes, lined up

### The window is short, and that is a limit of the source

**Steam's news feed returns the most recent 100 items. For this game that reaches back only to
2024-03-14.** Everything before that — early access, the 1.0 release, seasons 1 to 4 — has **no patch
record in this corpus.** The alignment below covers **29 of the 103 months**.

### The one place a patch shows up in the tags

**`game-design.progression.unlock-pace.missed-content-comes-back` — 9 uses, and 4 of them land in the
eight months after the studio shipped season reactivation.**

The studio announced it on **2024-04-14** and it went live with Season 05 on **2024-06-13**. The mode's
uses: 2022-11, 2023-11, 2024-01, **2024-08, 2024-10, 2024-11, 2024-11**, 2026-06, 2026-08.

**Two of the nine predate the feature**, so the mode is not purely a response to it. **But 2024 holds
five of nine uses in a mode that averages one a year**, and four of those five fall after the feature
shipped. The most recent one names it outright:

> *"MASSIVE respect to the devs for allowing players to switch their battlepass progress to any of the
> previous seasons."* — `233551125`, 2026-08

**This is the only shipped feature in the whole corpus that produced a visible move in a tag count.**

### What the patch notes do not explain

- **The November peaks.** Neither November in the window shipped content — 2024-11 was a dev
  livestream, 2025-11 was an announcement.
- **The 2023 peak year.** 51,740 reviews, 24% of all time, and the patch record does not reach it.
- **The decline after 2023.** Reviews fall from 51,740 to 7,586 across three years while the studio
  keeps shipping seasons, events and patches on schedule.

**The honest reading: a full season moves review volume and a tag count. Nothing smaller does, and
the storefront calendar out-moves all of it in seven years out of eight.** That is a finding about how
Steam reviews work, not about whether the patches were good.

## 5. What this corpus cannot tell you

1. **Why review volume collapsed after 2023.** The sample says nothing about players who stopped
   playing, because they stopped writing too.
2. **Anything before 2024-03 about patches.** Steam capped the news feed at 100 items.
3. **What non-English players think.** Four other languages for this game are pulled and unread —
   `latam`, `spanish`, `russian`, `schinese`.
4. **Whether the 61 negative reviewers are representative.** 61 is a small number and they cluster on
   one theme; that is a pattern, not a population estimate.
5. **Accessibility.** 9 bullets. **The tree has no phobia branch**, and two observations that needed
   one were filed elsewhere. See gap 4 in `tag-tree-open-gaps.md`.
