## 1. The short version

- **64.5% thumbs up, 122.2 complaints and 83.6 praise per 100 reviews; praise to complaint 0.68 : 1.**
  The only one of the ten games on this count where complaints outnumber praise (`cross-game.md`
  section 25; ARC Raiders is next lowest at 1.53 : 1).
- **It is measured against Warframe.** 282 reviews (17.6%) name it; `a-named-rival-does-it-better` is the
  top complaint at 113.
- **The shop and chained drop chances lead the complaints.** 234 reviews (14.6 per 100) carry a
  monetisation complaint — the highest in the corpus, five times Warframe's 2.8 — and 116 (7.2) a
  randomness complaint, also the highest.
- **Fan service is the top specific tag.** `the-cast-is-built-to-titillate` (~) is 149, 9.3 per 100;
  the next game is Terminull Brigade at 3.3.
- **The grind is both the praise and the complaint.** `grindy` 100 against `grind-feels-earned` 71 and
  `everything-earnable` 56.
- **It improved, and later reviewers say so.** Complaints per 100 fall 128.0 → 84.3 across four periods;
  `made-it-better` rises to 5.9 per 100 in 2026. The three most-helpful reviews are still thumbs down.
- **547 of the 1,955 complaint bullets (28.0%) come from reviews that recommend the game.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding: the shop and the dice set the verdict

**The complaints are about how the game is sold, more than how it plays.** Monetisation complaints reach
14.6 reviews per 100 and randomness complaints 7.2, both the highest of any English group in the corpus;
Warframe, also free, is at 2.8 and 1.3. Players describe a chain: grind for a container, roll it for a
part, craft for hours, then pay to skip any step (`one-random-drop-leads-to-another`,
`frustration-is-built-to-sell-shortcuts`). **What the game did later is the other half of the finding**:
it began showing drop odds and added bad-luck protection, and the 2025-2026 reviews praise both
(5 reviews) while grind and price complaints fall by more than half
(`the-first-descendant-english.md` sections 4 and 5).

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- **Anything about other languages.** About 61,000 reviews; none pulled. 16 English-tagged reviews are
  in other languages.
- **Anything about a single month.** From 2024-10 on, most months are read at 20 reviews (2026-10 at 6);
  only 2024-07 (862), 2024-08 (139) and 2024-09 (65) are read at more.
- **Whether reviewers' claims are true.** Drop rates, fines and lawsuits against the publisher, prices,
  account losses and what each season changed are the reviewers' words, not checked.
- **Edits.** 199 reviews are counted on their created date; many are verdicts on later seasons.
