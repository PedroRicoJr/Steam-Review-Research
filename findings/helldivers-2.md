## 1. The short version

**The complaints are about decisions, not about design.**

- **Four of the top five complaints are choices somebody made**: a patch that made it worse, the
  owner, the countries it is not sold in, and the weakening of things players liked. **The fifth is
  crashes.**
- **The first complaint about how the game plays is seventh** — "it repeats itself", 22 times.
- **The most-praised thing about the studio and the most-criticised thing about the studio are both
  in the top lists.** "The studio listens and acts" is the second biggest praise (78). "The studio
  misreads what we want", "it fights its players" and "it ignores what we say" together are 51.
- **Talk about the community is 28.5% of everything said, and talk about the game's design only
  18.6%** — the lowest design share of the first three games in this corpus.
- **17.4% of what was said had nothing specific in it** — the highest of the first three games.

## 5. ⭐ The finding: the game got better and the relationship got worse

**Praise and complaint per 100 readers, by year:**

| Year | Reviews | Thumbs down | Bullets/review | Praise per 100 | Complaint per 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 1,093 | 13.9% | 1.72 | 110.2 | 48.0 |
| 2025 | 297 | **11.8%** | 1.73 | **114.1** | 46.1 |
| 2026 | 232 | **36.2%** | 2.17 | 89.7 | **101.7** |

*The 2026-10-03 re-run, which leaves out `review.*` bullets, gives praise 86.1, 94.6, 70.7 and complaints
46.0, 43.4, 97.8 per 100 for the same years (English page, section 2).*

**2026 is the break.** Thumbs down triples. Complaint per reader doubles and, for the first time,
**passes praise.** People also write more — 2.17 bullets each against 1.72 in the launch year.

**And it is not because the game broke.** `crashes-repeatedly` falls **32%** per reader from 2024 to
2026 — 2.5 bullets per 100 down to 1.7. What rises is everything about the company.

**The record of what players say about the studio, per 100 reviews read:**

| | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `developer-communication.listens-and-acts` (+) | 4.8 | **7.1** | **1.7** |
| The critical `developer-communication` modes (−) | 1.5 | 2.0 | **15.5** |
| Ratio, praise to criticism | **3.3 : 1** | 3.5 : 1 | **1 : 9.0** |

**2025 is the studio's best year and 2026 its worst — the ratio swings thirty-fold in twelve months.** Praise falls to a
third of its 2024 rate while criticism rises ten-fold. **The reversal is in the same subject, one
year apart.**

## 5. ⭐ The two open method questions this game raised

**Neither has been answered. Both are Rico's to settle, not mine.**

1. **`community.developer-communication` is defined as "what the studio says".** In this game the
   publisher is often the actor — the account requirement, the countries it stopped being sold in.
   A separate `publisher-communication` subject would be a change to how the tree works, not a new
   mode. **The sharpest case is review `225615617`, which carries two `listens-and-acts` bullets
   about two different actors.** Everything is currently filed under one subject.
2. **Reviews carrying several dated updates across years are flattened onto one date** — the date the
   review was created. Between 2 and 15 of these appear in every batch of 50. **The 2026 numbers
   above are affected**, because a review begun in 2024 and rewritten in 2026 counts as 2024.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

1. **What four of the five languages think.** 184,243 reviews on Steam, unread — 113 times what
   was read here.
2. **Whether 0.20% is enough.** This is the thinnest sample of the three games by a factor of five.
   A mode with 10 bullets is 10 people out of 819,840.
3. **Whether 2026 is the game or the sample.** 232 reviews stand for a whole year.
4. **What the reviewers actually wrote.** These are tags. Pulling the raw text behind the top
   entries is a separate pass — see plan §7, "The two-stage read".
5. **Whether reviewers' claims are true.** The country count, the account requirement and the
   doxxing story are what reviewers say; only the patch dates come from a primary source (Steam news).
