## 1. The short version

**A sequel that people accept as a sequel, play with friends, and cannot finish without a wiki.**

- **The two most-said specific things tie at 139 each**: *this is a worthy successor*
  (`successor-framing-accepted`, 10.1 per 100) and *much better with friends* (10.1 per 100). The
  first falls across the run (12.7 → 4.7 per 100); the second rises (7.3 → 12.4).
- **The number one complaint is that the best things are hidden behind a guide** — 61 bullets, 4.4 per
  100, **46 of them from people who recommend the game** — and it is the one complaint that rises
  without stopping: 2.6 per 100 in the launch quarter, 10.0 in 2026. The mode was built for this game
  in batch 5. Add the reroll that never gives you the item (24), the roll that decides which content
  you ever see (7), the unlocks that need a second player (3) and the third-party reroll tool (2), and
  **the content the game hides is seven bullets per 100 reviews — the biggest complaint cluster.**
- **The second complaint is the first game.** `falls-short-of-the-studios-earlier-games` is 53 bullets
  — 30 of them thumbs-down — against 139 who accept the sequel. **For every reviewer who says the
  first was better, 2.6 say this one is the better game.** The pattern *the first game had X* runs
  through both: armour set bonuses (seven reviews), a trait cap that let you max everything (nine,
  and the most-helpful review in the sample is two words long: *"uncap trait points"*, 174 votes).
- **The thumb fell six points and stayed there.** 86.5% in the launch quarter, 79.6% in 2025, 82.4%
  in 2026. Complaints per 100 rose from 67 to 91 over the same span while praise held at 104 to 126.
- **The engineering complaint is not crashes, it is a machine that cannot run it**: `demanding-hardware`
  (17), `only-runs-right-with-upscaling-on` (14, built in batch 2), `lowering-the-settings-does-not-help`
  (7, batch 8), `only-stable-with-the-cpu-slowed-down` (2, batch 20), nine reviews that never got past
  the launcher (8 thumbs-down, median 2 hours), and twelve lost saves.

and it is the last month over 1,400. From 2024-02 on no month reaches 800 and from 2025-02 on none
reaches 350. **Steam's 82.3% is the launch quarter's 86.5% dragged down by three years of a smaller,
crosser audience.**

**The volume fell to a floor and stayed there.** 2025-01 to 2026-08 holds 191 to 339 English reviews a
month, with no slope. A game that sells on sale for three years keeps producing 250 reviews a month —
and in this game, unlike Space Marine 2, the 2026 reviewer is not writing about the price.

## 4. ⭐ The wiki problem: one design choice, seven modes, a rising line

**The number one complaint was built for this game in batch 5, and by the end of the run the tree
needed six more modes to say the rest of it.**

|---|---|---|
| `level-design.the-best-things-are-hidden-behind-a-guide` | 61 | *"an archetype hidden behind a cloud of death that has a completely inconspicuous location"* (`189605023`); *"whole ass dungeons will be behind walls that you can walk past without ever realizing"* (`214769098`, said as praise) |

**Three things the sample can say about it.** First, **the people who say it recommend the game**: 46
of 61, median 49 hours, against the general 83.6%. Second, **it is defended as a feature by the
people who found the secrets**: *"the community maps secrets together"* (`144280701`), *"finding one by
chance, or finally solving something after hours of curiosity, the payoff is incredible"*
(`202701424`), *"welcome to souls-like stuff n00bs, git gud"* (`208065945`). Third, **the three

solutions"* (`209854285`), *"some classes are gated behind data that had to be datamined as an

digging. **The complaint is not that the secrets exist. It is that a 2026 player is handed the answer
key and told to go look.**

**No patch notes were pulled, so the page cannot say what the studio changed.** What it can say is
that the line rises in every period and never falls — the only complaint in the top ten that does.

## 5. What the sample says about the design itself

**The design praise is the builds.** `deep-and-varied` (98): *"pair an Engineer turret with Hunter
crit chains and suddenly you're John Wick in an interdimensional maze"* (`201175078`). Dual archetypes
are the thing the sequel is praised for having added (`changes-how-you-play`, `each-role-plays-its-own-way`),
and the firing range and loadout slots are named as quality of life (`quality-of-life-is-looked-after`,
`214769098`) after `no-way-to-save-a-loadout` (4) was asked for in 2023 and 2024 — **the sample holds
the ask and the thanks.**

**The design complaint underneath the wiki is the scaling.** `upgrading-makes-the-game-harder` (9,
built in batch 6, no other game has it): *"enemies scale with you. This means upgrading your weapon
often makes your enemies deal more damage and take less"* (`209854285`); *"you're better off not
upgrading them"* (`223833959`, said as a feature for trying weapons). Beside it: the trait cap (9 —
the most-helpful review in the sample), a new weapon starting the grind again (6), levelling up
changing nothing (`levelling-up-changes-nothing`, 3), and options that feel identical (the *tiny
increments* reviews). **A progression system that players describe as either invisible or harmful,
inside a game praised for its builds.** The two are not a contradiction: the builds are the
archetypes, mods and rings; the complaint is the +1s.

**The combat complaints are all about control taken away, and this game built five modes to say it.**
`one-hit-kills` (11): *"insta-kills are the worst and laziest kind of game design"* (`144291151`);
`stuns-take-control-away`; `the-boss-hides-behind-its-adds` (3); `attacks-land-beyond-their-visible-reach`
(3); `attacks-are-timed-to-bait-your-dodge` (2); `the-stamina-runs-out-too-fast` (2); `healing-is-too-slow-for-the-pace`
(3); `melee-has-no-parry-or-block` (2). **Nobody says the guns feel weak** — 63 say *impactful*, and
the sound of one pistol got its own bullet (*"like slamming a hammer on a solid wood door"*, `234976329`).

**The level design is praised as worlds and complained about as levels.** `world-worth-exploring` (92)
and `evocative-places` (20) against `the-spaces-are-scaled-too-big`, `hard-to-navigate` (13 — the map,
the minimap with no cardinal directions), `the-jumping-sections-do-not-belong-in-a-shooter` (3),
`slow-doors-and-lifts-pad-the-run` (4 — *"20 elevators in 2 hours"*), and `one-area-drags-the-rest-down`
(7 — the Labyrinth in three reviews, N'Erud in four). **Seven reviews name one area as the place the
game went wrong, and it is always one of two.**

**What the co-op design gets wrong is the guest.** `a-guest-can-decide-your-campaign` (3, built in
batch 11): *"if the other person does not talk to the NPC you have to Alt+F4… some random can
control your progress in a game you paid for"* (`222191203`). `a-dead-player-spectates-until-the-next-checkpoint`
(3, batch 27). `only-the-host-keeps-the-progress` (2). `the-guest-fights-at-a-disadvantage` (3, batch
3). **The friendly-fire tally for Rico**: six reviews say the friendly fire is the fun
(`friendly-fire-makes-stories`), one says its absence saves friendships (`201175078`) — and no mode
exists for approving the absence.

## 7. Confidence and limits

1. **This is a 3.6% sample, not a census.** ±3.27% on a whole-sample share; wider on any month; 32 of
   the 39 months are the 20-review floor and a single review moves 2026-09 by ten points.
2. **The launch quarter is a third of the sample and half the game.** 465 of 1,382 reviews are
   2023-07 to 09. Anything said about 2025 or 2026 rests on 240 and 170 reviews. The five-period
   table is the honest resolution.
3. **The launch week is under-sampled.** 2023-07 is 70 of 7,906 (0.89%) against 3.5% elsewhere. The
   grid split July into four weekly windows of 70; the first three end before the 2023-07-25 launch
   and returned nothing, and that is the whole gap between the planned 1,602 and the 1,382 read. The
   launch week's thumb (82.9%) rests on 70 reviews.
4. **79 reviews are dated by creation, not by their last edit.** The edits run both ways here too
   (*"tried again. Awful game"* and *"changed this to a recommend"*); the direction of the error is
   unknown.
5. **No patch notes were pulled.** The reviews name the three DLCs, the prism system, the boss rush
   mode, the loadout slots and the region filter as later additions; this page cannot confirm any of
   them from the studio's side, and cannot say whether the wiki problem was ever addressed.
6. **One language.** About 30,000 reviews in other languages are unpulled — and twelve of the 1,382
   "English" reviews were not in English.
7. **21% of bullets are `unknown`**, 396 of them a wordless thumbs up — between Space Marine 2's 19%
   and ARC Raiders' 31%. The direction audit found 0 disagreements and 0 unfitted observations across
   all 28 batches.
