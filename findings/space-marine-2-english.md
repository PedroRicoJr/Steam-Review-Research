## 1. The short version

**A game people love for what it is and complain about for how little of it there is.**

- **The single most-said specific thing is the game's own war cry.** `community.culture.shared-ritual`
  — *"For the Emperor"* — is 310 bullets, 21.9 per 100. One review in five says nothing but the slogan.
  Helldivers 2 had the same shape with *"for Democracy"*; this is the second game where a fiction's
  catchphrase is the review.
- **The number one complaint is that there is not enough game**: `production.content-amount.too-little`,
  84 bullets, 5.9 per 100 — **and 66 of the 84 come from thumbs-up reviews.** The median reviewer who
  says it has 17 hours. It is the complaint of someone who finished it and wanted more, not of someone
  who wanted out.
- **The praise is the power fantasy, and the tree had to learn to say it.** `makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong`
  (64) was built in batch 1 and is this game's biggest specific praise after the story (74) and the
  looks (64). Its inverse — `never-makes-you-feel-as-strong-as-the-fiction-says` (13) — came four batches
  later. **Five people say the game delivers the fiction for every one who says it does not.**
- **The thumb never moved.** 88.5% in the launch month, 87.8% in 2026 — the flattest line in the
  corpus. What changed is what people complain about (section 3): crashes went to zero, *buy it on
  sale* went up ten-fold, and the 2026 reviewer argues with the class lock and the parry, not the
  servers.
- **The most-helpful review in the sample is a thumbs-down with 632 votes about one faction** — the
  Chaos missions are *"a full difficulty tier above Tyranid missions, not by design but through
  clutter, misleading cues, flinching and stun-lock."* Section 4.

**The top complaint is a compliment with a number attached.** *Too little* is 7.1 per 100 in the
launch year, when the reviewer had finished a short campaign and the Operations mode had six
missions. **43 of the 81 reviews that say it have under 20 hours; 6 have over 100.** It falls to
3.0 in 2025 as Operations grew, and comes back to 5.1 in 2026 from the other direction — *"thirteen
missions after three years, PvP dead"* (`224995971`).

**Rows 4 and 14 are the launch and the April 2025 update, and both went to zero.** Crashes are 2.6
per 100 in 2024 and 0.0 in 2025. Disconnects are 0.5 in 2024, 2.3 in 2025 — the *Trygon* update
broke servers, added stutter and produced a false anti-cheat kick, fixed 2025-04-24 (`193373353`) —
and 0.0 in 2026. **This is a game whose engineering complaints were fixed, and the sample shows the
fixing.** `fixed-what-mattered` (25) is 2.5 per 100 in the launch month — ultrawide, then the
weapon and parry patches.

**Row 5 is the one complaint that rises without stopping**: 0.5 → 0.9 → 2.1 → 1.9 → 5.1 per 100
across the five periods. By 2026 one review in twenty says *buy it on sale* — 14 of the 22 from
people who recommend it. **The 2026 reviewer likes the game and thinks it is not worth full price
two years on.**

**Rows 12 and 13 are this game's own.** The class lock — *one Bulwark per squad, so if two of you want

small enemy can stun-lock. Both modes were built in this run and appear in no other game.

### ⭐ The power fantasy: 64 say it lands, 13 say it does not, and the 13 are the thumbs-down

**`makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong` is the game's biggest specific praise and it was built for
this game.** *"Every fibre calls out for death to heresy"* (`218331878`); *"peak testosterone gaming"*
(`221527851`); *"the power fantasy of being a Space Marine done right"* (`222216060`). 63 of the 64
bullets come from thumbs-up reviews.

**Its inverse is `never-makes-you-feel-as-strong-as-the-fiction-says`, 13 bullets, 12 of them
thumbs-down.** The complaint is always the same mechanism: a stun, a knock-down or a swarm takes
control away from a character the fiction says cannot be stopped. `stuns-take-control-away` (9)
and `the-parry-fails-when-you-need-it` (9) are the same feeling filed on the controls.

**The two populations barely overlap, and the split is the thumb.** The people who feel strong
recommend; the people who feel weak do not. **A game built on a power fantasy is reviewed on whether
the fantasy held in the moment the review was written** — and the curve of the praise (4.2 → 6.0 →
3.6) sits over the weapon and parry patches that reviewers name (`225612951`: a lifelong fan who
bounced at launch, and the weapon and parry fixes made it click).

### Read the volume, not the sample

**Two thirds of everything ever written about this game in English was written in its first four
months.** 84,460 of 124,849 reviews — 67.6% — arrived in 2024-09 to 2024-12, and 45.8% in the
launch month alone. **The sample's 88% thumb in September 2024 is the game's reputation, and it is
still 88% in 2026.** Steam's *Very Positive* is not the launch — it is the whole run.

**The volume never fell to nothing.** Every full month from 2025-01 on holds 1,300 to 3,100 English
reviews, with no downward slope: 2026-07 (2,122) is higher than 2025-05 (1,509). A game that sells
on sale for two years keeps producing reviews — which is why the 2026 complaint list is about the price.

## 4. ⭐ The Chaos problem: one faction, one review, 632 votes

difficulty tier above Tyranid missions, not by design but through clutter, misleading cues, flinching
and stun-lock — so players avoid them, halving the map pool to six."* The reviewer cites the studio's
own numbers — 35 billion Tyranid Warriors killed, over triple the Chaos Spawn count; the top three
missions all Tyranid; Chaos missions unranked for lack of plays — and closes: *"Saber has stayed
silent despite overwhelming feedback... not recommended, with a heavy heart."*

**The sample holds the same complaint across four modes and it was never one mode.**

| `difficulty-tuning.one-part-is-far-harder-than-the-rest` | 4 | the Chaos and Thousand Sons missions (two in 2026-01), and the campaign's final chapter (`222211857`, 2026-05: dropped to normal to finish) |

**The hated enemy is a Chaos Spawn and the reason is the stun.** Two reviews name the Chaos Spawn as
the enemy they will not fight and both give the same why — a grab that takes control away. The
Zoanthrope is the other named enemy, and its why is different: `a-whole-loadout-cannot-hit-it` (2),

sample made it.**

**No patch notes were pulled, so the page cannot say whether Saber answered.** What it can say is
that of the 169 reviews from 2026-02 on, one mentions Chaos at all. That is consistent with a fix
and with the players who cared having left; the tree records what the player felt (settled
2026-09-10), not which.

## 5. What the sample says about the design itself

**The design praise is the two-layer combat.** Impact (45): *"so satisfying and incredibly visceral"*
(`218905901`). The finishing moves: `the-finishing-moves-are-a-highlight` (3), *"the executions —
especially the newer Power Axe ones — make you feel like a real warrior"* (`206151338`, in a
thumbs-down). The modes: `good-selection` (51) — campaign, Operations, PvP — is the tenth most-said
thing, and `a-mode-falls-flat` (15) is almost always the PvP: *"PvP is buns, like Destiny 2's Crucible
without the random-roll guns"* (`234734108`).

**The design complaints are about the systems around the combat, not the combat.** The class lock
(13). The grind (15) — perk points, weapon mastery, *"only about 20% of players have maxed a gun"*
(`220131655`, said as praise). The bots (10 *useless*, 2 *helps*) — *"playing alone on higher levels
the bots assigned to you become dolts"* (`222211857`). The hub loop — `every-run-starts-with-dead-time`
(2), *"select a mission, see the captain, go to the armoury, go to the launch bay, and it repeats"*
(`222215927`) — and the in-mission lifts and walks (4), built in batch 25.

**The combat complaints that exist are all about control.** The parry window (9), the stuns (9),
the vignette that narrows the view (`narrow-view-is-a-handicap`, 9), the slow-motion on every
interaction (`206151338`: *"it slows your perception, not the game"*), the melee the design forces on
a player who wanted to shoot (`forces-the-melee-on-you`, 2). **Nobody says the guns feel weak — six
say *weightless*, forty-five say *impactful*.**

**What players ask for is content and looks, not systems.** The wish list: more Operations (the 84),
an iconic thing from the source — a Dreadnought to play, Necrons, T'au, Lightning Claws
(`an-iconic-thing-from-the-source-is-missing`, 10), a cape for the Bulwark (`183521531`, thumbs-down;
`226907394`, *"Got my cape 10/10"*), and a way to look like their own chapter (10 asks through
2025-02, 13 thanks from 2025-03 on).

## 7. Confidence and limits

1. **This is a 1.14% sample, not a census.** ±2.60% on a whole-sample share; wider on any month; 21
   of the 25 months are under 40 reviews and a single review moves 2026-09 by ten points.
2. **The launch month is the sample.** 591 of 1,418 reviews are 2024-09; 921 are 2024. Anything said
   about 2025 or 2026 rests on 301 and 196 reviews. The five-period table is the honest resolution.
3. **155 reviews are dated by creation, not by their last edit.** In this game the edits run both
   ways, so the direction of the error is unknown — unlike ARC Raiders, where it was one-way.
4. **No patch notes were pulled.** The reviews name the ultrawide patch (2024-10), the April 2025
   *Trygon* update and its 2025-04-24 fix, the weapon and parry changes, and the 2026 Siege mode;
   this page cannot confirm any of them from the studio's side.
5. **One language.** About 100,000 reviews in other languages are unpulled.
6. **19% of bullets are `unknown`**, 363 of them a wordless thumbs up — lower than ARC Raiders' 31%.
   The direction audit found 0 disagreements and 0 unfitted observations across all 29 batches.
