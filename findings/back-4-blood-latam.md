**There is no margin of error on this page.** Every number below is the whole population. Where a
count is 3, that is 3 people in the world who wrote this in this language, not an estimate.

**How to read the words below.** A **bullet** is one thing one person said. A **tag** is where that
bullet was filed. Tags have three levels: a **division** (who at a studio owns it), a **subject**
(the thing being talked about), and a **mode** (what actually happened). `unknown` means the person
raised a subject and never said what about it.

## 1. The short version

**This audience likes the game more, says less about it, and defends it harder.**

- **81.3% thumbs up** (579 of 712). 133 thumbs down.
- **Observations split 734 positive to 540 negative.** Positive ahead, unlike the English group.
- **1.87 observations per review**, and **20% of all bullets are `unknown`** — the reviewer raised a
  subject and never said what about it.
- **The single most common bullet is "good game" and nothing else** — `review.positive.unknown`, 209
  bullets, 16% of everything written.

**Median playtime is 10 hours.**

## 3. Where this audience differs from the English one

**Same game, same tree, same five years.** Rates are per 1,000 bullets so the two are comparable
despite very different sizes.

### Said much more often in Spanish

| Mode | LatAm | English | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| `art.fidelity.looks-great` | **39.8** | 15.8 | **2.5×** |
| `positioning.successor-claim-backfired` | **26.3** | 8.2 | **3.2×** |
| `sale-dependency.buy-on-sale-only` | **51.9** | 32.7 | 1.6× |
| `reputation.judged-unfairly` | **44.4** | 27.0 | 1.6× |
| `population.dead-game` | **22.6** | 11.2 | **2.0×** |
| `playing-with-friends.much-better-with-friends` | **50.4** | 28.6 | 1.8× |
| `review.positive.unknown` | **157.1** | 98.6 | 1.6× |

### Said much more often in English

| Mode | LatAm | English |
|---|---|---|
| `playing-with-friends.needs-a-group` | 1.5 | **10.3** |
| `randomness.luck-decides-the-outcome` | **0** | 5.0 |
| `randomness.randomness-keeps-it-fresh` | **0** | 6.2 |
| `build-and-customisation.does-not-belong-in-this-game` | **0** | 5.5 |
| `build-and-customisation.choices-cannot-be-undone` | **0** | 4.6 |
| `marketing.reputation.studio-lost-my-trust` | **0** | 4.6 |
| `publishing.price.fair` | 0.8 | 6.2 |
| `atmosphere.falls-flat` | **0** | 5.7 |

**Three things stand out.**

**1. Price is not a complaint here — it is a condition.** `buy-on-sale-only` runs 1.6× the English
rate, and **`price.fair` has one single bullet in 712 reviews.** Nobody in this group said the price
was right. **The recommendation is real and it is conditional on a discount, every time.** Three
reviewers named it directly as `regional-pricing.priced-for-another-country`.

**2. Nobody blames the studio.** `studio-lost-my-trust` has **20 bullets in English and zero here**,
while `judged-unfairly` runs 1.6× higher. **This audience argues the game is better than its
reputation and does not extend the complaint to the people who made it.**

**3. The card system's deep critiques are English-only.** `luck-decides-the-outcome`,
`does-not-belong-in-this-game`, `choices-cannot-be-undone` — **all zero here, 66 bullets in English.**
This group praises the cards (`changes-how-you-play` at 16.5 per 1,000, above the English 11.7) and
never argues about them.

**`dead-game` at 2.0× the English rate is the one that follows from the numbers themselves.** This
language has 712 reviews against English's 33,766. **A smaller cohort finds fewer teammates**, and
`much-better-with-friends` running 1.8× higher says the same thing from the other side.

## 4. Localization — eight bullets, and they disagree

**The whole localization record for this group:**

| Mode | | Count |
|---|---|---|
| `localization.voice-localization.dubbed-well` | **+** | 6 |
| `localization.language-barrier-in-multiplayer.cannot-communicate-with-teammates` | **−** | 2 |

**The Spanish dub is praised six times and never criticised.** The two complaints are not about the
game's translation at all — they are about **not sharing a language with the people in the lobby.**

**That is a design problem, not a translation problem**, and it sits next to the `dead-game` finding
above: a small language cohort gets matched with people it cannot talk to.

## 5. Year by year

**Every number here is the full population.**

| Year | Reviews | Thumbs down | Observations per review | One-observation reviews | Negative share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 91 | **7.7%** | 1.91 | 57.1% | 36.2% |
| 2022 | 73 | 30.1% | 1.90 | 58.9% | **49.6%** |
| 2023 | 102 | 23.5% | 1.96 | 51.0% | 47.5% |
| **2024** | **224** | 16.5% | 1.79 | 61.6% | 40.5% |
| 2025 | 161 | 18.6% | 1.80 | 57.1% | **35.9%** |
| 2026 | 61 | 21.3% | 2.08 | 42.6% | 37.0% |

**Two things to notice.**

**The launch was warm.** 2021 ran 7.7% thumbs down — the best year the game has in this language, and
2021-10 and 2021-11 together are 81 of the 91 reviews.

**2024 is the biggest year, by a wide margin.** 224 reviews — **2.5 times the launch year** — for a
game whose updates stopped in February 2023. The patch record for that window shows deep sales, the
copy protection removed in May 2024, and press coverage of a player revival. **The corpus records
that it happened; it cannot say which cause did it.**

**And the sentiment kept improving after the studio stopped.** Negative share falls from 49.6% in
2022 to 35.9% in 2025 — **its best year is its second-to-last, two years after support ended.**

## 6. What this corpus cannot tell you

1. **Anything with statistical width.** 712 reviews is the entire population, so there is no sampling
   error — but a mode with 3 uses is still 3 people, and no amount of certainty makes 3 people a
   trend.
2. **Why 2024 recovered.** Sales, the copy-protection removal and press coverage all land in the same
   window.
3. **Whether "LatAm Spanish" is one audience.** Steam's language bucket covers a continent. Nothing
   here separates Mexico from Argentina from Chile.
4. **What European Spanish thinks** — that is a separate group, 1,175 pulled and 50 read.
