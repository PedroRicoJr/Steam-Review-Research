**How to read the words below.** A **bullet** is one thing one person said. A **tag** is where that
bullet was filed. Tags have three levels: a **division** (who at a studio owns it — game design, art,
engineering), a **subject** (the thing being talked about), and a **mode** (what actually happened).
`unknown` means the person raised a subject and never said what about it.

---

## 1. The short version

**The reviews are an argument about a different game.**

- **69.2% thumbs up** (1,150 of 1,663). 513 people gave it a thumbs down.
- **Observations split 1,961 positive to 2,284 negative.** More negative than positive, in a corpus
  that is 69% thumbs up. **The thumb and the text disagree at scale.**
- **The second most common thing anyone writes is that this game is the successor to another game**
  — `positioning.successor-framing-accepted`, 241 bullets. **The fourth is that the comparison was
  unfair** — `invited-unfair-comparison`, 140.
- **`marketing` is 13.8% of all feedback.** Nothing about how the game plays comes close to how it
  was framed.

**Median playtime is 15 hours.** These are not people who bounced off it in an hour.

## 3. The successor problem, in numbers

**This is the finding that dominates the corpus.** Back 4 Blood was made by the studio behind an
earlier, much-loved co-op zombie shooter, and was sold on that lineage. **Every year of its life, the
reviews argue about it.**

| Mode | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `successor-framing-accepted` | **73** | 35 | 33 | 36 | 39 | 25 | **241** |
| `invited-unfair-comparison` | 17 | **31** | 24 | 28 | 21 | 19 | **140** |
| `successor-claim-backfired` | 4 | 3 | 6 | 5 | **11** | 7 | **36** |
| `reputation.judged-unfairly` | 11 | 21 | 17 | **28** | 26 | 15 | **118** |

**Read the shape, not the totals.** `successor-framing-accepted` peaks at launch and then settles —
that is people using the comparison as shorthand. **`invited-unfair-comparison` never falls below 17
in any year, five years running.** The comparison did not fade; it became the permanent frame.

**And `judged-unfairly` rises.** By 2024 more people are arguing *the game is better than its
reputation* than in any earlier year. **535 bullets — 12% of the entire corpus — are about a
comparison to a game that is not this one.**

## 4. The one patch that visibly killed a complaint

**`solo-viability.no-progression-solo` — 28 bullets in 2021, 4 in 2022, then gone.**

| Year | Bullets |
|---|---|
| 2021 | **28** |
| 2022 | 4 |
| 2023 | 0 |
| 2024 | 0 |
| 2025 | 2 |
| 2026 | 0 |

The patch record explains it exactly. **Three days before launch**, 2021-10-09: *"dev discussing ways
to address lack of progression in solo mode after player complaints."* The roadmap on 2021-11-08
promised an offline mode; **it shipped 2021-12-19.**

**A named complaint, a named fix, and the complaint stops.** This is the clearest cause-and-effect
pair anywhere in this project, and it is worth carrying: **the studio shipped the fix inside ten
weeks of launch and the reviews recorded it immediately.**

The neighbouring solo modes tell the rest: `unplayable-alone` runs 5 in 2021 and 1 after;
`works-solo` climbs from 2 in 2021 to 6 in 2024.

## 5. Month by month, and the patch record

**The patch record for this game is complete** — 116 items from 2021-05 to 2026-06, covering the
whole life of the game including launch. *(That is unusual. Steam's news feed caps at 100 items, so
older games lose their early record; this one fits.)*

### The volume

| Year | Reviews on Steam | Share | Sample thumbs down | Negative share of observations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2021** | **14,391** | **42.6%** | 29.9% | **57.7%** |
| 2022 | 7,549 | 22.4% | **36.0%** | 55.2% |
| 2023 | 3,798 | 11.2% | 33.1% | 53.8% |
| 2024 | 4,432 | 13.1% | **25.2%** | **46.7%** |
| 2025 | 2,657 | 7.9% | 25.8% | **44.4%** |
| 2026 | 939 | 2.8% | **36.1%** | 50.8% |

**2021-10, the launch month, is 7,631 reviews on its own — 22.6% of everything the game has ever
received.** The first three months are 42% of its whole review history.

**Then look at 2024.** Review volume goes **up** — 3,798 to 4,432 — for a game whose updates stopped
in February 2023. And the negative share drops nine points.

| Date | What happened | What the reviews did |
|---|---|---|

| 2022-08-30 · 2022-12-06 | Two more expansions | `patch-quality.fixed-what-mattered` peaks at 18 in 2022 |

**`live-ops.abandonment.updates-stopped` did not exist before 2023 and has 78 bullets since.**

| Year | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `updates-stopped` | 0 | 0 | **28** | 21 | 12 | 17 |

**The announcement was February 2023. The tag appears in 2023 and never goes away.**

### The price story decays, the sale story does not

| Year | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `price.too-high-for-what-it-is` | **42** | 16 | 16 | 14 | 8 | 10 |
| `price.fair` | 1 | 3 | 6 | 8 | 4 | 5 |
| `sale-dependency.buy-on-sale-only` | 29 | 19 | 20 | **35** | 28 | 12 |

**The "too expensive" complaint falls by 80% as the game discounts.** But `buy-on-sale-only` — *worth
it at a discount, not at full price* — **peaks in 2024, three years after launch.** The discount
solved the price objection and made it permanent: **the game is now recommended only as a cheap
purchase.**

## 6. What this corpus cannot tell you

1. **Why 2024 recovered.** The patch record shows sales, a Denuvo removal and press coverage in the
   same window. **The corpus records that sentiment improved; it cannot say which of those did it.**
2. **Whether the thumb or the text is right.** 69% thumbs up against a 52%-negative observation
   count. Both are real measurements of different things.
3. **Anything about the console versions**, or about players who never wrote a review.
4. **What non-English players think** — see the LatAm Spanish read for one other language. Brazilian,
   Russian and Simplified Chinese are pulled and unread.
