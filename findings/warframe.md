## 1. The short version

- **89.9% thumbs up, 50.9 complaints and 127.3 praise per 100 reviews; praise to complaint 2.5 : 1.**
  Between Deep Rock Galactic (7.4 : 1) and Helldivers 2 (1.95 : 1) on the same count.
- **The number one complaint is the grind** — `unlock-pace.grindy`, 264, 8.2 per 100 — and 84% of the
  reviews that make it are thumbs up. It is the only complaint that holds its rate for thirteen years
  while the reviews around it shrink by more than half.
- **The number one specific praise is the pull** — `keeps-pulling-you-back`, 221 — and the third
  complaint is the same pull read as a harm, `warns-that-it-takes-over-your-life`, 74, a mode no other
  game has.
- **Free-to-play is read as fair.** `price.fair` 198 and `everything-earnable` 133 are the fourth and
  sixth most common tags; monetisation praise outnumbers monetisation complaint 134 to 103, and the
  largest monetisation mode is the neutral one: paying only shortens the grind (73).
- **The thumb is flat for thirteen years** — 85.4% to 92.9% by year — while the mean review falls from
  67 words to 28.
- **983 of the 1,646 complaint bullets (59.7%) come from reviews that recommend the game.**

## 5. ⭐ The finding: a grind the players name, resent, and keep recommending

**The top complaint and the top praise are the same loop.** 264 say the grind is too much; 221 say
the game keeps pulling them back; 111 say the grind is long and worth it; 74 warn that it takes over
your life, and 60 of those 73 reviews are thumbs up. **The grind complaint does not predict the
thumb:** 223 of the 264 reviews that make it recommend the game, and the 41 that do not have a median
of 194 hours.

**Money is part of the same loop, not a separate complaint.** The largest monetisation mode is
`paying-only-shortens-the-grind` (73, neutral) — the same fact written as fair by one reviewer and as
*pay to progress* by the next. The shop complaints that are about power (`pay-affects-play`, 16) are a
twentieth of the grind line.

**The two ends of the loop are where the game loses people.** At the start: 267 bullets on learning
the game, peaking in 2016–18. At the end: `nothing-left-to-chase` (27) and `the-endgame-is-only-more-
unlocks` (5). The middle — the loop itself — is what is praised.

## 6. What this page cannot tell you

- **Anything about other languages.** About 381,000 reviews; none pulled.
- **Anything about a single month.** Every month is partly read; weights run to ×591.
- **Whether a mode fell because players changed or because reviews got shorter.** The mean review fell
  from 67 words to 28; the English page reads only the falls far larger than that.
- **The launch.** 5 reviews from the first month.
- **Anything about runs, extraction or arenas** — the questions Dominion most needs — because the game
  has none.
- **Edits.** 581 reviews are counted on their created date; the flattening rule is open with Rico.
