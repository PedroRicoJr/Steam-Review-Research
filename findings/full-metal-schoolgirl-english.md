## 1. ⭐ Twelve reviews decided this game's reputation, and they are all one sentence long

`marketing.promise-vs-reality.the-content-is-visibly-censored` — **12 bullets, 9 thumbs down.**

| The claim | Reviews | Helpful votes they carry | Share of every helpful vote on the game |
|---|---|---|---|
| The game visibly censors itself | **12** | **750** | **52%** |
| The price is too high | 22 | 271 | 19% |
| Everything else | 82 | 409 | 29% |

> *"Censored"* (`207472674`, 36 helpful) · *"Censored"* (`207375978`, 32 helpful) · *"The game is
> censored. Because of that, I cannot recommend the game."* (`207503610`, 97 helpful) · *"Positive
> review is hidden under black void."* (`207492760`, 50 helpful)

🔑 **One in ten reviews carries half of the helpful votes.** The seven most-upvoted English reviews
of this game are all this claim, and five of them are under ten words. **A buyer sorting by
"most helpful" sees nothing else.**

**And the other side exists, with the thumb reversed:** `the-mismatch-was-the-buyers-fault` is
**11 bullets, 11 thumbs up** — *"a game that is identical across all platforms and regions"*
(`207594888`), *"There is an achievement for looking up her skirt... It's not a H-game. It was never
advertised as one"* (`208277404`). Four more say the negative reviews are not about the game.

⚠️ **This is Zombie Girl's shape exactly.** There the argument was whether the store tags lied; here
it is whether the shadow is censorship. **In both games the two sides split the thumb almost
perfectly, and in both the argument is not about how the game plays.** Two block games in four have a
review pool that is mostly a fight about the fan service.

## 2. The price is the largest complaint, and recommenders write most of it

`publishing.price.too-high-for-what-it-is` — **22 bullets, the largest mode in the game, 14 of them
thumbs up.** `buy-on-sale-only` adds 10 more, 6 up.

> *"an easy recommendation... at like $20-30. 50 is complete nuts lol. Do not pay that."* (`207594888`)
> *"I got this game through a humble bundle... the value of the bundle was probably almost $100
> since these games are so overpriced."* (`219672669`)
> *"I paid $2.77, that was a 9 games bundle for $25"* (`215897327`)

🔑 **Seven reviewers say outright that they paid bundle prices — $2.77, $10, $25 for nine games — and
judge it worth that.** `publishing` is **11.1%** of this game's bullets, the highest share of any game
read; the co-op average is 7.1%. **The conversation about this game is a conversation about what it
should cost.**

## 3. The design complaint is the elevator key

`game-design.punishment-model.the-checkpoint-is-single-use` — **9 bullets, 5 up, 4 down. Built this
read.** The keys that skip floors are consumed on use, so a second death costs more than the first.

> *"smarter players will never use their higher level keys, because you need to build up your
> arsenal so you don't get smoked"* (`217406229`)
> *"If you only have one 85 key and die, your starting at the lobby."* (`208277404`)

It sits beside `demands-long-sessions` (8 — runs of two to seven hours) and
`cannot-save-and-come-back` (4 — *"desperately needs a quicksave between floors"*). **Three modes,
one structure:** a hundred floors, no mid-run save, and a checkpoint you spend. Every one of the
reviews that reached the top says some version of it.
