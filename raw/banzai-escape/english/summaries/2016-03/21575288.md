## What they said

- Says the options are bare - native resolution only, shadows, one master volume slider - and the help text is in broken English.
    → game-design.ui-ux.options-cover-what-you-need          (good)
- Says it is a third-person rail shooter and a mobile port, visible in the UI and menu navigation.
    → engineering.platform-support.built-for-another-platform (bad)
- Says later weapons are direct upgrades and the upgrade button only buys ammo reserve, and you cannot switch weapons at will.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.missing-expected-bindings (bad)
- Says there are no warning markers for incoming hits and you still get hit behind cover.
    → game-design.fairness.losses-feel-arbitrary             (bad)
- Says the gunplay is weak - no visual recoil, poor sound, janky animations that look like packaged Unity assets - and spread is a random cone.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.weightless                (bad)
- Says melee units instantly kill you for no reason.
    → game-design.enemy-design.no-counterplay                (bad)
- Says the short levels are designed to be replayed for money to buy the gun and ammo the next level needs, artificially extending the game.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.gated-behind-farming (bad)
- Says $6 is okay for what it is but he cannot recommend it, and points to Blue Estate and Typing of the Dead instead.
    → marketing.reputation.beaten-by-a-competitor            (bad)
