## What they said

- Says it feels like a mobile game ported to PC - the HUD and point-and-click instead of proper key bindings.
    → engineering.platform-support.built-for-another-platform (bad)
- Says it is simple but too easy - the AI reloads out of cover and keeps shooting the cover you left - and only explosives and running out of ammo can kill you.
    → game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour             (bad)
- Says you cannot swap between pistol and main weapon, so you may have to finish a boss with the pistol.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.missing-expected-bindings (bad)
- Says the laser section must be moved with the mouse, which is painful - a touch-screen design.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.unresponsive            (bad)
- Says the heavy rifle makes the game far too easy.
    → game-design.power-balance.one-option-dominates         (bad)
- Says the graphics are average for Unity and customisation is limited but worth mentioning.
    → art.fidelity.unknown                                   (~)
- Says the voice acting is bad but they made the effort.
    → audio.voice-performance.badly-acted                    (bad)
- Says the story throws you in with no intro and only makes sense at the end.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Says it is not bad nor good, two and a half hours, and not worth full price.
    → production.content-amount.too-little                   (bad)
