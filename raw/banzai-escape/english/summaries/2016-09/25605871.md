## What they said

- Says it is a port of the Android rail shooter and looks like it, yet has performance issues and, unusually, real graphics options.
    → engineering.platform-support.built-for-another-platform (bad)
- Says input lag of several seconds on reload and cover can be fatal, and the game is ninety per cent memorisation.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.unresponsive            (bad)
- Says there is no indication where or when you can swap cover - the target is sometimes off screen.
    → game-design.new-player-experience.poorly-explained     (bad)
- Says you can be killed behind cover while reloading, and once while doing nothing at all.
    → game-design.fairness.losses-feel-arbitrary             (bad)
- Says you will grind the same few levels for money to buy weapons and ammo capacity.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.gated-behind-farming (bad)
- Says the writing and voice acting are not good, though English voices deserve credit and the translation is serviceable.
