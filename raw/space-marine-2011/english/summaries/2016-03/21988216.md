## What they said

- Likes the setting, the cliché plot, the thunder hammer and the ground-shaking boots, but two design flaws ruin it (thumbs down, edited).
    → review.positive.unknown                                (good)
- Enemies stun you mid-attack but your normal attacks don't stun them, so big enemies take a minute of stunning.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.stuns-take-control-away (bad)
- The dodge roll has no invincibility frames, unlike God of War, Devil May Cry or Dark Souls, so dodging is pointless.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.no-modern-moves         (bad)
- Low health and shields plus rocket Orks from chapter 6 make it an unfair slog.
    → game-design.power-balance.rules-favour-the-enemy       (bad)
- You take heavy damage while standing still in execution animations, so it's safer to hang back and shoot.
    → game-design.power-balance.the-only-heal-is-charging-the-enemy (bad)
- Ork crowds soak up an astonishing number of bullets.
    → game-design.enemy-design.bullet-sponges                (bad)
- Small arenas and long-reaching enemy melee catch you on claustrophobic level design.
    → game-design.level-design.the-spaces-are-scaled-too-small (bad)
- Checkpoints sometimes put you behind two encounters each death.
    → game-design.punishment-model.harsh-restart             (bad)
- The first Ork boss is badly signposted, floods the arena and has a near one-hit kill.
    → game-design.enemy-design.bosses-are-a-chore            (bad)
- Graphics glitches on an Nvidia card needed a forced restart (reviewer's account).
    → engineering.bugs.buggy                                 (bad)
- Update: you can't know which weapon to take before a checkpoint, and some fights are built for one weapon.
    → game-design.fairness.losses-feel-arbitrary             (bad)
- Easy is much better on a first run.
