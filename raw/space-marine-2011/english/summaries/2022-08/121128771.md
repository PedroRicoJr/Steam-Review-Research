## What they said

- Looks terrific despite its age, apart from some low-res textures; nails the 40k vibe.
    → art.atmosphere.draws-you-in                            (good)
- Some of the chunkiest footstep sound ever: you feel like a giant hunk of metal; bolters sound like small cannons.
    → audio.sound-effects.sounds-great                       (good)
- Seamless melee-to-ranged combat, and the big toys arrive early.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful                 (good)
- By the end, enough variety and numbers that even berserkers must think before charging.
    → game-design.enemy-design.overwhelming-numbers          (bad)
- Squadmates pop the odd enemy and look after themselves; no babysitting.
    → game-design.ai-teammates.allied-soldiers-pull-their-weight (good)
- Corridor maps, mostly brown and grey, that only link combat arenas; jump-pack sections a small relief.
    → game-design.level-design.too-linear                    (bad)
- Too many cutscenes early on for no good reason.
    → narrative.story.cutscenes-are-badly-made               (bad)
- Anticlimactic final boss: a few waves and a long QTE.
    → game-design.enemy-design.the-final-boss-breaks-the-pattern (~)
- 8 hours on Normal; recommended even if completely new to Warhammer.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.works-for-people-new-to-the-source (good)
