## What they said

- 40k is Gears of War's great-grandfather; bloody, fast, in-your-face combat (1 found it helpful).
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful                 (good)
- Hard even on easy: three daemons can kill you in five seconds.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.satisfyingly-hard        (good)
- Ammo boxes are single-use and don't always refill you.
    → game-design.power-balance.resources-too-scarce         (bad)
- Shields regenerate; health returns only through executions, and big enemies take a lot to stun.
    → game-design.power-balance.fighting-is-how-you-heal     (good)
- No minimap and dull linear transitions, though fights are in large open areas.
    → game-design.level-design.too-linear                    (bad)
- Near-endless armour customisation; sparse modes.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.you-can-look-how-you-want (good)
- Latecomers get kicked and used as cannon fodder; level up in co-op first, where players are more forgiving.
    → community.culture.veterans-will-not-help-newcomers     (bad)
