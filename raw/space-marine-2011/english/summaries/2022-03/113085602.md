## What they said

- Well polished: you feel the weight of the armour and of each strike; easy to fall into a relaxed flow (113 hours).
    → game-design.level-design.well-built                    (good)
- Three classes, perks, weapons and maps that favour different classes give plenty of tactical options.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- The campaign is a full introduction to the multiplayer, though it gives little time as Assault or Devastator.
    → game-design.new-player-experience.easy-to-start        (good)
- Multiplayer lag: enemies seem to teleport when rolling; use a cable and close bandwidth hogs.
    → engineering.netcode.lag-and-desync                     (bad)
- Hardened veterans will crush you for maybe the first 70 hours; persist and use the guides.
    → game-design.power-balance.one-option-dominates         (bad)
- Seize Ground and Exterminatus are the most played modes, with players found constantly.
    → community.population.healthy                           (good)
