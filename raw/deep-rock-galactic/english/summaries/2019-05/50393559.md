## What they said

- Says it performs remarkably well and can be handled by pretty much any average gaming rig
    → engineering.performance.runs-well-on-modest-hardware (good)
- Says you will find very few to no bugs and he has never had a glitch
    → engineering.bugs.rare-and-minor                      (good)
- Says a friend's grappling hook disconnected during an internet spike and he fell to his death
    → engineering.netcode.lag-and-desync                   (bad)
- Says the controls are flawless: when you click it responds, when you press space it jumps
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.responsive-and-clear  (good)
- Says the game requires a lot of focus and teamwork to hold off hordes while doing an objective
    → game-design.co-op-design.demands-coordination        (good)
- Says there are multiple stages of difficulty and some must be unlocked by completing missions at the level below
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.well-graded            (good)
- Says the game is not built around the solo player and warns you to bring friends
    → game-design.solo-viability.punishing-solo            (bad)
- Says the quickplay option links you up with other players
    → engineering.matchmaking.finds-games-fast             (good)
- Says there are many biomes and a multitude of different enemy types
    → game-design.enemy-design.good-variety                (good)
- Says there are not many weapons to choose from, two main and two secondary per class
    → production.content-amount.too-little                 (bad)
- Says you get a decent amount of customisation of those weapons
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says class tools force a particular team make up, and a party that all take damage weapons will suffer
    → game-design.role-design.forces-a-fixed-team-composition (bad)
- Says you really feel like you are deep in an alien world
    → art.atmosphere.draws-you-in                          (good)
- Says the game has its own art style with a charm to its jagged geometry
    → art.visual-direction.looks-well-directed             (good)
- Says the art style keeps the install small
    → engineering.performance.small-install-size           (good)
- Says the music and the character quotes set the setting
    → audio.music.fits-the-game                            (good)
- Says the characters lack any individual voice because they all sound the same
    → audio.voice-performance.everyone-sounds-the-same     (bad)
- Says the sounds are directional so you know where an enemy is coming from
    → audio.sound-effects.cues-warn-you-in-time            (good)
- Says the caverns use premade tile sets but you rarely notice
    → production.content-variety.procedurally-varied       (good)
- Says he is still in awe entering a massive cavern or looking into a bottomless pit
    → art.environment-art.evocative-places                 (good)
- Says if the pod is about to leave you can just dig your way out or dig straight down
    → game-design.world-interaction.destruction-changes-play (good)
