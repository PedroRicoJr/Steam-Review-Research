## What they said

- Says it replicates a named classic closely while adding its own spin, instead of marketing on that experience and delivering something else.
    → marketing.promise-vs-reality.delivered-what-was-promised (good)
- Says it is a strong but incomplete beta with lots of promise and about seventy-five per cent delivery.
    → production.scope-mismatch.the-potential-is-still-there (good)
- Says it looks like an older engine's game in animation, play and feel despite being built on a modern one.
    → art.visual-direction.forgettable-look                  (bad)
- Says the basic gun balance is there across shotguns, submachine guns and rifles.
    → game-design.power-balance.well-tuned                   (good)
- Says the grenades are the most interesting weapons and are very powerful and dramatic when used well.
    → production.content-variety.varied-runs                 (good)
- Says the enemy roster maps onto the named classic's with a few tweaks, plus one that blinds and one that spawns more.
    → marketing.reputation.derivative-of-an-older-game       (bad)
- Says the level design and goals get more innovative after the first episode.
    → game-design.level-design.well-built                    (good)
- Says hidden terminals feed an upgrade system that adds chance and replay value.
    → game-design.randomness.randomness-keeps-it-fresh       (good)
- Says there is more robust support for custom maps than the named classic ever had.
    → community.user-created-content.mods-extend-the-game    (good)
- Says the game runs well on every machine he and his friends have.
    → engineering.performance.well-optimised                 (good)
- Says there is no gore modelling, so enemies gently fall over when shot, and calls that the biggest letdown in the game.
    → art.effects-and-gore.impacts-look-weak                 (bad)
- Says the guns feel very samey and give no visceral feedback without gore.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.weightless                (bad)
- Says every level in the named classic has its own character and here it all feels like more cruise ship.
    → game-design.level-design.repetitive-layouts            (bad)
- Says the grabbing enemy pulls from any distance through obstructions and there is no hanging mechanic, so a pull over a pit kills instantly.
    → game-design.enemy-design.ignores-physical-logic        (bad)
- Says the game is too easy: the big enemy is easy to dodge, allies revive anywhere, and running out of ammunition is impossible.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.too-easy                 (bad)
- Says the egg enemies are annoying, unintuitive to counter and largely unavoidable.
    → game-design.enemy-design.no-counterplay                (bad)
- Says the rare special weapons do not feel important or interesting.
    → game-design.game-feel.reward-moment.the-payout-lands-flat (bad)
- Says the upgrade choices are usually minor and boring.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.shallow-options (bad)
- Says the four characters blend together and sound like they know they are in a game and nothing matters, so he does not care which one he plays.
    → narrative.characters-writing.flat-or-annoying          (bad)
- Says the narrative touches baked into the named classic's maps, such as the graffiti, are not here.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.setting-feels-thin         (bad)
- Says there is basically no melee, only a push that does little or no damage.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.no-modern-moves         (bad)
- Passes on a tip that turning hints on shows where items and upgrades are through walls.
    → community.player-conduct.players-teach-each-other-the-fix (good)
