## What they said

- Says co-op campaigns are a rare treat - quadruple thumbs up, more of that.
    → game-design.modes.good-selection                       (good)
- Says the voice acting is great comedy - constant suspense over whether the conversations could get more manly.
    → narrative.characters-writing.funny-or-memorable        (good)
- Says the atmosphere is as thick as can be - fighting everywhere, soldiers dying in the distance, mega-cities in ruins.
    → art.atmosphere.draws-you-in                            (good)
- Says it feels spacious and lets him absorb the vibe of different planets, with extra points for borrowing from Elden Ring.
    → art.environment-art.evocative-places                   (good)
- Says the campaign is a bit too short for the price.
    → production.content-amount.too-little                   (bad)
- Says the Ops missions and rewards needed to be more roguelike for a motivating endgame loop.
    → game-design.replayability.runs-out-fast                (bad)
- Says to set Ops to private unless you want a random to join, pick the same class and prevent the mission from starting.
    → game-design.role-design.one-of-each-class-so-someone-loses-theirs (bad)
- Says on a disconnect he would rather return to the main menu than see the inside of the battle barge several times while it tries to reconnect.
    → engineering.servers.frequent-disconnects               (bad)
- Says the lore, audio logs and story dialogue were lost on him - when a thousand enemies rush him he cannot pay attention.
    → narrative.story.told-during-the-fighting-so-it-is-lost (bad)
- Says from zero to a hundred kills in ten seconds.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong (good)
- Says hard is actually challenging - he burned through stims so fast he got addicted.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.satisfyingly-hard        (good)
- Says the AI comrade is as stupid as he sounds.
    → game-design.ai-teammates.useless-in-combat             (bad)
- Says the AI comrade never fails to revive him with a caring bro caress.
    → game-design.ai-teammates.revives-reliably              (good)
- Says the jet-pack missions were fun, but the jet packs in Operations seem to have a cough.
    → game-design.role-design.the-same-class-is-weaker-in-one-mode-than-another (bad)
- Says the play style never feels agile, though it gets across that you are a metric ton of a man.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.unknown                 (~)
- Says once surrounded you forget the heavy weapon and melee; he stuck to one of the very similar mid-range rifles.
    → game-design.power-balance.options-feel-identical       (bad)
- Says he plays souls-likes on a gamepad but had to use mouse and keyboard for this one.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.you-have-to-switch-input-device-to-play-well (bad)
- Says a series of fun little puzzle games stands in for menus - he prefers a UI with a focus on usability.
    → game-design.ui-ux.hard-to-navigate                     (bad)
- Says the clunky customisation will not let you be as creative as with tabletop figures - next time include an armour paint shop for his made-up chapter.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.cannot-change-how-you-look (bad)
- Says nothing bad about performance on a Ryzen 3900X and RTX 4070.
    → engineering.performance.well-optimised                 (good)
