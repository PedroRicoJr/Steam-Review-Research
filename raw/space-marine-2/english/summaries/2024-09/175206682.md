## What they said

- Says the game is designed around co-op from the campaign to the PvE - you can play with bots but he has not heard it is as fun.
    → game-design.solo-viability.punishing-solo              (bad)
- Says the story is a bit cliche and predictable but the presentation is wildly impressive, and he enjoyed Space Marine 1's campaign and characters more.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Says the PvE characters are fixed - a Vanguard is limited to knife or chainsword, bolter or melta, and one pistol, with no change of special skill.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.most-classes-are-shut-out-of-a-weapon-type (bad)
- Says you have to grind up each character and weapon you care about, and the parts require oddly specific things like a boss that may not spawn - tedious in the Vermintide and Darktide way.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
- Says levelling makes a newcomer the odd man out - either you lower the difficulty for them or they do badly.
    → game-design.new-player-experience.late-joiner-outmatched (bad)
- Says three players maximum in campaign and PvE makes it less appealing long term.
    → game-design.co-op-design.group-is-too-small            (bad)
- Says the combat is alright, not Devil May Cry, with tons of blood and a variety of weapons.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful                 (good)
