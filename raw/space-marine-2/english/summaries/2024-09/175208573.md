## What they said

- Says he lost the four early-access days to the game not working on his average machine, and there was weird lag in single player that should never have existed.
    → engineering.performance.demanding-hardware             (bad)
- Says half the campaign is running through empty scenery between set-piece battles - he was incredibly bored.
    → game-design.pacing.nothing-happens-between-fights      (bad)
- Asks why picking up ammo, or using a lift that only goes up or down, means pressing and holding a key and waiting for the animation.
    → game-design.world-interaction.chores-instead-of-play   (bad)
- Says in the first game a death felt like his own fault against the Orks; here he regularly felt he died because the engine decided he must.
    → game-design.enemy-design.no-counterplay                (bad)
- Says the story starts intriguing and deteriorates into squad banter - a carbon copy of the hated Leandros with an emo edge, and it is all so forgettable.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Says whoever made it failed to understand that the Adeptus Astartes represent power - the bolter, the iconic weapon, feels like a spud gun, and the propaganda poster boys never feel like it - and says this is not about woke or anti-woke.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.never-makes-you-feel-as-strong-as-the-fiction-says (bad)
- Says you empty whole clips into weak swarms that then surround you and stun-lock you to oblivion.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.stuns-take-control-away (bad)
- Says the melee wants to be Soulslike but not too much, and is floppy and floating, with bullet-sponge enemies.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.weightless                (bad)
- Says the six co-op maps have boring level design, endlessly repetitive spawns, and an enemy that spawns in invulnerable pairs you must empty every bullet into.
    → game-design.enemy-design.enemies-arrive-in-the-same-places-every-run (bad)
- Says the multiplayer rewards are as uninspiring as a tin of salmon for a birthday - a pistol that does slightly more damage, a shoe with a different insole - and the perks so dull he lost interest after six hours.
    → game-design.progression.cosmetic-rewards.not-worth-chasing (bad)
- Says the graphics are pretty, the cast well voiced though he prefers the original Titus actor, and the music and environments what you expect from 40k.
    → art.fidelity.looks-great                               (good)
- Says the odd combat system suits neither mouse and keyboard nor a controller, and feels clunky and awkward.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.unknown                 (~)
