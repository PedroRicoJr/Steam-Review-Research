# Review 174828489 — space-marine-2 — english
Thumbs: down · 3h played · 1 found it helpful

## What they said

- Says the shooting feels good but the melee is light and floaty, as if attacks pass through enemies, with no stagger.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.weightless                (bad)
- Says you attack in the direction you are moving rather than facing, so stepping back to swing snaps you round to hit thin air with your back to the horde.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.attacks-go-where-you-move-not-where-you-face (bad)
- Says while you are swarmed, ranged enemies hit with 100% accuracy from any distance, so either the horde picks at you while you shoot them or they pick at you while you chop the horde.
    → game-design.enemy-design.ranged-enemies-hit-you-from-anywhere-while-you-are-swarmed (bad)
- Says the fight is the same loop of parry, quick-time gunshot and quick-time execution to regain armour - bosses too, fifteen times before the execution - and once you find the loop there is no skill.
    → game-design.progression.complexity.shallow             (bad)
- Says ripping a Tyranid in half looks cool the first few times and then is the same quick-time cutscene over and over.
    → game-design.world-interaction.a-button-prompt-stands-in-for-play (bad)
- Says the game is easy without ever making you feel powerful or like a Space Marine - a big clumsy oaf trudging between quick-time events.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.never-makes-you-feel-as-strong-as-the-fiction-says    (bad)
- Says the huge scenes with thousands of enemies can barely be seen because there is no FOV slider and the default is far too narrow - you mostly stare at your own back.
    → game-design.game-feel.camera.narrow-view-is-a-handicap (bad)
- Says no ultrawide support at a 2024 AAA release shows it was not ready.
    → game-design.ui-ux.does-not-support-my-screen-shape     (bad)
- Says the rat-like enemies look like a glitch and add nothing but clutter.
    → game-design.enemy-design.forgettable-specials          (bad)
- Says a seven-hour campaign and six half-hour operations, all on rails - find a sniper rifle and the next clearing is all snipers - with no strategy or different ways to play.
    → game-design.level-design.only-one-way-to-play-it       (bad)
- Says there are barely any guns and they all play the same, and the classes are essentially the same apart from which guns they can use.
    → game-design.power-balance.options-feel-identical       (bad)
- Says you cannot have two of the same class in a group, so if you want a class or a gun you had better hope nobody else does.
    → game-design.role-design.one-of-each-class-so-someone-loses-theirs (bad)
- Says it had a ton of potential and wasted it - 5/10.
    → production.scope-mismatch.wasted-its-potential         (bad)
