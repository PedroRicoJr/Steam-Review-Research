# Review 175208610 — space-marine-2 — english
Thumbs: down · 18h played · 0 found it helpful

## What they said

- Says it plays like an unrefined replica of Gears of War 3 without the cover mechanic, which will feel like a new genre only to those who never played Gears.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.feels-like-every-other-shooter (bad)
- Says the default FOV is narrow, perhaps 60 to 70 degrees, and cannot be changed.
    → game-design.game-feel.camera.narrow-view-is-a-handicap (bad)
- Says the control layout is poorly thought out and rebinding is limited - keys that share actions by default must be split when rebound.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.the-default-layout-is-awkward (bad)
- Says the short time to kill pushes you toward cover the game refuses to implement, so in PvP corner-peeking from range dominates and melee feels janky.
    → game-design.power-balance.whoever-shoots-first-wins    (bad)
- Says the PvP lag compensation is very poor and low latency is massively advantageous.
    → engineering.netcode.lag-and-desync                     (bad)
- Says there is no player collision, which feels cheap and breaks immersion.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.no-player-collision     (bad)
- Says compared with Gears 3 the guns, animations, melee and executions are not as punchy or weighty, and stagger is barely implemented - a melee hit in PvP does not stagger.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.weightless                (bad)
- Says the arsenal lacks diversity - 80% feels like a bolter variant and there are five melee weapons.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.shallow-options (bad)
- Says the parry system is great and gun-strikes are cool, but both are half-baked or absent in PvP.
    → game-design.modes.a-mode-falls-flat                    (bad)
- Says the campaign was fun, being immersed in the 40k lore.
    → narrative.story.worth-following                        (good)
- Says it is frustrating and feels like a lot of wasted potential.
    → production.scope-mismatch.wasted-its-potential         (bad)
