## What they said

- Says they do not make games like this any more - three full modes, a huge amount of free unlocks, extreme bang for your buck.
    → publishing.price.fair                                  (good)
- Says the campaign is about eleven hours, playable entirely in co-op with two friends, with a Left 4 Dead-style set of co-op mini-campaigns and a PvP mode tossed in free - underbaked and unbalanced but fun.
    → game-design.modes.good-selection                       (good)
- Says the story is messy but some character moments are great; the gameplay gets repetitive by the end and the plot is forgettable.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Says the art direction is fantastic - the first 40k game to capture the grand epic scale, battlefields to the horizon, ships covering the sky, where Darktide kept to smaller environments.
    → art.fidelity.looks-great                               (good)
- Says literally hundreds of enemies at once really sell the superhuman power fantasy of cleaving through hordes of weaker enemies.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong (good)
- Says a mid-range system ran it at a steady 60 fps on high, the lowest dip 50.
    → engineering.performance.runs-well-on-modest-hardware   (good)
- Says the only microtransaction is a cosmetic season pass whose profits go back into the free content.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.cosmetic-only         (good)
- Says it launches with a full suite of content, you pay once, and a long list of unlocks can only be earned by playing - no limited-time paid FOMO stream.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.money-does-not-touch-the-grind (good)
- Calls it the best representation of the 40k universe in a game yet.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.faithful-to-the-source-it-adapts (good)
