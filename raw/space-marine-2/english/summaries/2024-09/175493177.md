## What they said

- Says in an October 2025 update that after a year away his fully completed story-mode progress has been lost.
    → engineering.stability.progress-not-saved               (bad)
- Calls it one of his top games of 2024 - Campaign, Operations and Eternal War are all incredibly fun, and as a casual he was surprised to enjoy PvP.
    → game-design.modes.good-selection                       (good)
- Says class progression in Operations is fast enough to feel meaningful but slow enough to enjoy the lower-level play.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.satisfying-progression (good)
- Says the PvP maps are small enough that you spend most of the match engaging enemy players.
    → game-design.level-design.well-built                    (good)
- Says the jump pack works differently in each mode - the Operations version is less responsive, harder to activate and slower to recharge than in Eternal War, and should be updated to match.
    → game-design.role-design.the-same-class-is-weaker-in-one-mode-than-another (bad)
- Says menu navigation on a controller is inconsistent - some menus take the d-pad, some need the sticks - a little clunky.
    → game-design.ui-ux.hard-to-navigate                     (bad)
