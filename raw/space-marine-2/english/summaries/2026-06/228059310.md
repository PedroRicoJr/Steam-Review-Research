## What they said

- Says the game is trying to be multiple genres at once - a Tide-style horde shooter, a hero shooter and a daily grind game - while not knowing whether to focus on melee or ranged; a game for many people but not for anyone who wants one specific thing done well.
    → production.scope-mismatch.unknown                      (~)
- Says the customisation is absolutely great and the models look insane.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.you-can-look-how-you-want (good)
- Says you earn the models through insane grinds of tens of hours, or just buy DLCs that already hold the top models.
    → publishing.dlc-and-editions.unknown                    (~)
- Says the fighting mixes ranged and melee well and the hits feel crunchy.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful                 (good)
- Says something lacks - hitboxes a bit small, and basic Tyranids needing a couple of hits rather than dying at once.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.unknown                   (~)
- Says the bosses are insanely fun - the mission-specific ones always have a nice gimmick.
    → game-design.enemy-design.memorable-specials            (good)
- Says the classes feel good and all have a purpose - two heavies, two fast, a sniper, two generalists.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says what it lacks by far is music - Darktide, Vermintide 2, Rogue Trader and Deathwing have incredible music, and here it is either too quiet to hear or so boring the brain filters it out.
    → audio.music.forgettable-or-annoying                    (bad)
- Says it is a blast once you have a couple of hours - about two games, an hour and a half, then stop.
    → game-design.session-flexibility.unknown                (~)
- The Emperor Protects - 8/10.
