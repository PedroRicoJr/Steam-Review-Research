## What they said

- Calls it the best game he played this year - an astounding world and power-fantasy representation.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong (good)
- Says the parry and the last-moment dodge add a learning curve and depth the first game lacked, and make fights against special enemies more interesting.
    → game-design.progression.complexity.rewarding-once-learned (good)
- Says the campaign is a good follow-up with more spectacle, characters with more depth, and decent boss encounters - a direct upgrade of the first game.
    → marketing.positioning.successor-framing-accepted       (good)
- Says the campaign is too short - not in a not-worth-your-money way, but in an I-need-more way.
    → production.content-amount.ends-before-it-wears-out     (good)
- Says the narrative structure is too similar to the first game - xenos, then chaos was lurking.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Says the campaign cannot be replayed precisely as in the first game, where each mission had loadable checkpoints.
    → game-design.replayability.no-way-to-replay-one-chapter (bad)
- Says the boss roster is decent but small; Operations expands it and lets you refight campaign bosses, including the best boss in the game.
    → game-design.enemy-design.memorable-specials            (good)
- Says some classes feel extremely crutchy yet needed on higher difficulties, and some Operations are clearly not balanced for certain classes.
    → game-design.role-design.forces-a-fixed-team-composition (bad)
- Says perk and weapon balancing is lacking for some classes, making build paths very limited toward anything not meta.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.only-a-few-builds-are-viable (bad)
- Says Operations reduced the Assault class's power fantasy compared to the campaign and PvP - removing its verticality and its ability to shoot in the air.
    → game-design.role-design.the-same-class-is-weaker-in-one-mode-than-another (bad)
- Says replaying Operations feels fresh because random elements define every node of a mission.
    → production.content-variety.varied-runs                 (good)
- Says all six classes are playable and enjoyable, each with its own personality, fitting any team.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says PvP drinks from an older, slower, simpler style - Gears of War, the first Space Marine - not the faster-than-sound modern shooter.
    → marketing.reputation.explained-by-naming-other-games   (~)
- Says PvP has three modes and only three completely symmetrical maps, and the symmetry hurts team deathmatch.
    → production.content-amount.too-little                   (bad)
- Says some PvP weapons unlock extremely late, making it grindy if you just want to try them.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
- Says fixed spawns can get you spawn-killed before the spawn switches sides.
    → game-design.level-design.players-spawn-on-top-of-each-other (bad)
- Says Chaos Marines have no customisation other than colour.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.cannot-change-how-you-look (bad)
- Says every class has quite a few weapon options, allowing different playstyles within one class.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says a good comparison is Remnant: From the Ashes - a third-person shooter needing precision both shooting and defending against bigger-than-life threats.
    → marketing.reputation.explained-by-naming-other-games   (~)
