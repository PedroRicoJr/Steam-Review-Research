## What they said

- Says to play it only for the story and setting - the replayability was banked on Operations and PvP, which both suffer from overwhelmingly poor design.
    → game-design.modes.a-mode-falls-flat                    (bad)
- Says to play Darktide if you are in it for the gameplay.
    → marketing.reputation.beaten-by-a-competitor            (bad)
- Says the visuals and animations are beautiful, and the executions - especially the newer Power Axe ones and those against Chaos Spawns - make you feel like a real warrior.
    → art.animation.the-finishing-moves-are-a-highlight      (good)
- Says some executions could use work because of pacing and repetition.
    → art.animation.the-finishing-moves-repeat-until-they-are-stale (bad)
- Calls it cinemaslop - slow-motion on almost every interaction, parrying, dodging, gun-strikes, executions, even debris; it slows your perception not the game, so it hurts play, and he would flip to a disappointed yes without it.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.unknown                   (~)
- Says he would consider changing the review to a yes if the slow-motion were removed.
    → review.the-thumb-will-flip-when-one-thing-is-fixed     (~)
- Says the core mechanics are bad - gun-strikes and the parry window.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.the-parry-fails-when-you-need-it (bad)
- Says the developers hide the vital numbers for build-crafting - no true damage figures, only a make-believe weapon-data screen that oversells the stats.
    → game-design.ui-ux.hides-information                    (bad)
- Says most class and weapon perks are bad, niche or throwaway points, so builds are linear and lack variety.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.only-a-few-builds-are-viable (bad)
- Says most melee weapons are okay to subpar - the knife especially - while most ranged weapons are decent or strong.
    → game-design.power-balance.some-options-are-useless     (bad)
- Says fights with two or more enemies become a stun-lock fest after a single mistake - clips exist of players unable to act until they die.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.stuns-take-control-away (bad)
- Says the game was not built for more than one boss on the field, yet Siege puts two field bosses out so you slog through twice.
    → game-design.enemy-design.bosses-are-a-chore            (bad)
- Says trash swarms - Hormagaunts and especially Tzaangors - devolve into parry spam or ranged headshots because melee attacks are weak in a swarm.
    → game-design.enemy-design.all-fought-the-same-way       (bad)
- Says the difficulty scaling is absurd - enemies get 4x HP above Substantial and bosses 5x on Absolute while the player drops to half HP and a quarter armour, and enraged enemies heal to full with hyper armour.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.harder-only-changes-the-numbers (bad)
- Says enraged Devourer Warriors are the worst of it - those who know, know.
