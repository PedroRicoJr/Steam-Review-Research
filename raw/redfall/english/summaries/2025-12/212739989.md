- Says the premise of an open-world vampire shooter should have made it a genre standout.
    → production.scope-mismatch.wasted-its-potential         (bad)
- Says the four heroes have good conceptual variety and their powers complement each other in co-op.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says the town has a strong sense of identity and the environments set the stage well.
    → art.visual-direction.looks-well-directed               (good)
- Says the game achieves small moments of tension at its rare best.
    → art.atmosphere.draws-you-in                            (good)
- Says vampires must be weakened before a stake, UV blast or fire finishes them, and fights can feel energetic when the tools are coordinated.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful                 (good)
- Says playing with friends improves the pacing and makes basic encounters more dynamic.
    → game-design.co-op-design.rewards-teamwork              (good)
- Says large sections of the map are devoid of life and interactivity, and buildings are hollow set pieces.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.setting-feels-thin         (bad)
- Says enemies run forward or stand in place rather than stalking or flanking, which makes combat predictable.
    → game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour             (bad)
- Says elite vampire types are too rare to raise the stakes and the combat loop loses urgency as loot improves.
    → game-design.power-balance.progression-outgrows-the-challenge (bad)
- Says the story is conveyed through static images and exposition rather than the environmental storytelling the studio is known for.
    → narrative.story.cutscenes-are-badly-made               (bad)
- Says the villains are conceptually fascinating and underdeveloped, leaving threads unresolved.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Says the heroes rarely interact during missions, which is especially noticeable in co-op.
    → narrative.characters-writing.flat-or-annoying          (bad)
- Says performance was inconsistent at launch and beyond, with odd animations, texture pop-in and hit detection problems.
    → production.launch-state.shipped-broken                 (bad)

    → game-design.co-op-design.only-the-host-keeps-the-progress (bad)
- Says the game does not scale enemies to the group, so co-op alternates between trivially easy and chaotic.
    → game-design.co-op-design.more-players-makes-it-trivial (bad)
