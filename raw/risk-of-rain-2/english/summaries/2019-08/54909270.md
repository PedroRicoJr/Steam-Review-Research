## What they said

- Says they did not like the first Risk of Rain - minimal graphics and labyrinthine maps made getting through stages a chore - and this is the best transition to 3D they have seen.
    → marketing.positioning.successor-framing-accepted       (good)
- Says the maps do not change, but they are big enough that the random placement of chests and the teleporter is its own variation, and the teleporter's red particles make it easier to see.
    → game-design.randomness.randomness-keeps-it-fresh       (good)
- Says every single character and item has been fine-tuned for 3D - they could not believe how well a melee-only character, the Mercenary, works.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says still early access, but there is enough single-player content to satisfy new players for now, and the next update is around the corner.
    → production.early-access.good-value-while-unfinished    (good)
- Says they have not tried the multiplayer.
    → game-design.solo-viability.works-solo                  (good)
