## What they said

- Says in a 1.0 edit that before they only played with friends to pass the time; now the replay value from how enemies spawn and the random items from chests is huge.
    → game-design.randomness.randomness-keeps-it-fresh       (good)
- Says the Artifacts give far more replay value than they expected - Command lets you choose items from a chest, and there are 16 that each twist the game; they love combining Command, Sacrifice and Swarms.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.random-rule-changes-welcome (good)
- Says the characters' play styles appeal to almost everyone - Commando and Huntress ranged single-target, Mercenary and Loader melee, Artificer and Acrid area and damage over time, Captain and Engineer support.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says the best soundtrack they have ever heard in any game - Chris Christodoulou, who did Deadbolt and Risk of Rain 1; listen to it on Spotify even if you do not buy the game.
    → audio.music.fits-the-game                              (good)
- Says the art style is amazing.
    → art.visual-direction.looks-well-directed               (good)
- Says in the early access review that it already rocks - buy it even if it is not on sale.
    → production.early-access.good-value-while-unfinished    (good)
