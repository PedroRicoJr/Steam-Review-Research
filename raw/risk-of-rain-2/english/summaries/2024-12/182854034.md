## What they said

- Says fantastic, the pinnacle of the indie roguelike (was indie) - the art style, gameplay and lore all made with passion and care since 2016 by a team who wanted to make something timeless.
    → production.craftsmanship.made-with-care                (good)
- Says Hopoo gave the IP to Gearbox, so the game is not in the hands of its original makers, who have not poured the same love into it; the recent updates were mixed and it took a long time to fix things the original devs would never have done.
    → publishing.ownership.unknown                           (~)
- Says their biggest gripe is that there is no item sharing in multiplayer - unless the team thinks strategically about every pickup they will not survive the late game; many mods fix this, which makes many people never want to play vanilla.
    → game-design.co-op-design.teammates-can-take-your-things (bad)
- Says unlocking abilities ranges from simple to frustrating - the requirements are so hard that many players cheat them in; False Son's needs luck on three factors plus the final boss killed in the riskiest way, with one chance at all of it.
    → game-design.randomness.the-thing-you-need-may-never-roll (bad)
- Says locking characters from the previous game behind DLC is scummy, worse when a character like Chef arrives ruined and takes a long time to fix; locking overpowered characters behind DLC makes it pay-to-win.
    → publishing.dlc-and-editions.content-behind-a-second-purchase (bad)
- Says it is missing a lot of quality-of-life features - player stats, multiplayer drop-in, item descriptions before pickup - which makes it feel a bit outdated.
    → game-design.ui-ux.missing-quality-of-life              (bad)
- Says still amazing and worth the pickup - alone, wait for a sale; with friends, definitely worth full price; 7.5/10 single-player, 8.5/10 multiplayer.
    → community.playing-with-friends.much-better-with-friends (good)
- Says try mods such as Starstorm 2 - one of the best mods in gaming history; it expands the game a lot and stays vanilla-friendly.
    → community.user-created-content.mods-extend-the-game    (good)
