- Says one of the most fun gameplay loops in any game.
    → game-design.replayability.keeps-pulling-you-back       (good)
- Says the sense of progression feels amazing - struggling through three stages to unlock Huntress when Commando was the only starter was incredibly rewarding, and every character unlock feels like that.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.satisfying-progression (good)
- Says the range of play styles keeps it from getting boring - Loader speedruns, Railgunner one-shots, Huntress runs and teleports, Rex trades health for damage, Bandit grows stronger per kill.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says the alternate abilities are mostly locked behind achievements, and getting the better version feels good because you are rewarded for playing well.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grind-feels-earned (good)
- Says items can make or break a run - the free legendary on stage four might be a frost relic or a missile tripler - which is frustrating but not in a way that makes you quit; a run lost to bad luck makes them want to go back in.
    → game-design.randomness.randomness-keeps-it-fresh       (good)
