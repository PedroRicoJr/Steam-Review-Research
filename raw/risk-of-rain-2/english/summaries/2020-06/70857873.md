## What they said

- Says absolute mayhem and they adore it - they just sat through one two-and-a-quarter-hour round as Engineer with nine Bustling Fungi, lost only because an Overloading Worm one-shot the turrets.
    → game-design.replayability.keeps-pulling-you-back       (good)
- Says the difficulty ramping from easy to hysterical laughter is amazing; at late stages you suddenly realise what is happening and ask how you got here.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.satisfyingly-hard        (good)
- Says steer clear if you dislike RNG, because that is all this is - items, chests, enemies, bosses, teleporter position, even the secret buttons are placed at random.
    → game-design.randomness.unknown                         (~)
- Says if your PC is not great the game will become too much - even their high-end PC dropped to low frames in a chain of enemies exploding on death and killing more exploding enemies; the enemy count and particle effects overwhelm the machine.
    → engineering.performance.unstable-framerate             (bad)
- Says the explosions can give you a seizure.
    → art.effects-and-gore.effects-block-your-view           (bad)
- Says it goes from a basic mob looter-shooter to chaos where you scream for help - and it has co-op, so somebody can come.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.unknown                   (~)
- Says be careful when you start a game - there is no save function and you never know how long a run will take; do not think 'a quick round before work', you will miss your shift.
    → game-design.session-flexibility.cannot-save-and-come-back (bad)
