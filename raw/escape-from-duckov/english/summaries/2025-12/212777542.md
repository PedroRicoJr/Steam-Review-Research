## What they said

- Says not to let the silly presentation fool you: it is a genuinely good single-player extraction shooter.
    → marketing.expectation-management.looks-like-a-joke-plays-like-a-real-game (good)
- Says it is a proper single-player game, without the jank of single-player Tarkov mods, and you can pause.
    → game-design.session-flexibility.can-pause-anytime      (good)
- Says the game keeps save backups, so you can roll back after a bug.
    → engineering.stability.lost-progress-can-be-recovered   (good)
- Says a tutorial area introduces the mechanics one by one.
    → game-design.new-player-experience.teaches-you-as-you-go (good)

    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.well-graded              (good)
- Says it is difficult but far less punishing than Tarkov - a good balance.
    → game-design.punishment-model.stakes-worth-the-risk     (good)

    → marketing.reputation.explained-by-naming-other-games   (~)

    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.respects-your-time (good)

    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.satisfying-progression (good)
- Says talents and totems let you fit the build to your playstyle.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says the game tells you when an item is needed for the hideout or a quest.
    → game-design.ui-ux.clear-and-usable                     (good)
- Says it does not take itself too seriously; the quests can be funny.
    → narrative.tone.satire-lands                            (good)
- Praises absurd character customisation.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.the-character-creator-is-fun (good)

    → game-design.new-player-experience.guides-you-without-holding-your-hand (good)

    → publishing.price.fair                                  (good)
