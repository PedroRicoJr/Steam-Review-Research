## What they said

- Over thirty levels across four acts, with level design on par with the predecessor's
    → game-design.level-design.well-built                  (good)
- Event cards make each run a bit different, adding replay value
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.random-rule-changes-welcome (good)
- The versus mode is short and fun, and two matches fit in half an hour
    → game-design.session-flexibility.good-in-short-sittings (good)
- The versus mode has no ranking, so one team is often obviously better within thirty seconds
    → engineering.matchmaking.no-skill-matching            (bad)
- Leaving a lopsided match locks you out of matchmaking for five minutes, and longer if you repeat it
    → engineering.matchmaking.punished-for-leaving         (bad)
- Building your own deck lets you decide how you play: tank, medic, support, melee or guns
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.changes-how-you-play (good)
- The gunplay is amazing and addictive even for someone not usually into shooters
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful               (good)
- Appreciates having any progression at all, unlike the predecessor
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.satisfying-progression (good)
- There is no endless mode, which is the obvious mode for a horde game
    → game-design.modes.expected-mode-missing              (bad)
- Unlocking everything needs several dozen hours because you earn too few supplies a match
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy           (bad)
- The same objective, shooting nests, appears in about sixty percent of levels
    → production.content-variety.repetitive                (bad)
- Endless crashes, freezes and bugs stopped the game working for him and his friends
    → engineering.stability.crashes-repeatedly             (bad)
- After a failed join you must restart the search by hand instead of it searching again
    → game-design.ui-ux.missing-quality-of-life            (bad)
- A season pass went on sale without telling buyers what is in it
    → publishing.dlc-and-editions.sold-before-it-is-known  (bad)
- Says the price is a bit too high
    → publishing.price.too-high-for-what-it-is             (bad)
