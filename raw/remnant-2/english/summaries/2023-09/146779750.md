## What they said

- Says really fun with friends but far from a masterpiece.
    → community.playing-with-friends.much-better-with-friends (good)
- Says the worlds are unique and interesting to explore, each with its own mechanics, two biomes and its own look.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.world-worth-exploring      (good)
- Says you cannot get every event and secret in one run, so you reroll the adventure until you do.
    → game-design.randomness.the-thing-you-need-may-never-roll (bad)
- Says the procedural generation does not work - on paper it adds replayability, in practice it adds boredom; overworlds and dungeons are near-identical between rerolls.
    → production.content-variety.the-generation-adds-nothing (bad)
- Says the procedural generation just makes the content harder to reach to artificially increase playing time.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.padding-a-short-game (bad)
- Says the story is not very interesting and ends on a cliffhanger for future DLC; the lore of each world, Yaesha especially, is interesting.
    → narrative.story.the-ending-lets-it-down                (bad)
- Says the game lore-dumps a lot of information at once.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.unknown                    (~)
- Says lots of weapons and equipment - good for builds, but overwhelming with no UI to filter it; they ignored relic fragments entirely.
    → game-design.ui-ux.managing-the-inventory-is-a-chore    (bad)
- Says the archetypes are a great addition with very different play styles.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says levelling is too slow - another way to artificially increase playing time.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
- Says the items to unlock some archetypes are very hidden with no hint; one or two are impossible without a guide, which makes the secret pointless for most players.
    → game-design.level-design.the-best-things-are-hidden-behind-a-guide (bad)
- Says sometimes repetitive because of the rerolls, but not that boring in multiplayer.
    → production.content-variety.repetitive                  (bad)
- Says the NPCs usually lore-dump a lot of information on you.
    → narrative.story.the-game-talks-too-much                 (bad)
