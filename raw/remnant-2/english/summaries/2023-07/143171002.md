# Review 143171002 — remnant-2 — english
Thumbs: up · 40h played · 1 found it helpful

## What they said

- Says a more realised version of From the Ashes - secret finding and exploration, nearly every outing a fresh adventure.
    → marketing.positioning.successor-framing-accepted       (good)
- Says the worldbuilding is really strong - each world built from the ground up with its own lore, ideals and crisis; you feel you could go anywhere in this universe.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.world-worth-exploring      (good)
- Says your actions in the first game affect the world and how its people react to you.
    → narrative.story.unknown                                (~)
- Says it is a lot less linear than the original, with much more space to explore.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.world-worth-exploring      (good)
- Says certain enemies have almost identical attacks to the first game - glaringly recycled - and it can feel like going through the same motions.
    → production.content-variety.recycled-assets             (bad)
- Says the archetypes let you fashion yourself instead of being pigeonholed into armour set bonuses.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says melee is now fantastic for enemies in your face without invalidating firearms - flying and distant enemies wreck a pure melee build.
    → game-design.power-balance.well-tuned                   (good)
- Says it can be difficult but you can develop strategies that trivialise the difficulty.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.unknown                  (~)
- Says he wants more story variety per world - about two per mainline world, then only random events, of which there feel to be far fewer than the first game.
    → production.content-amount.too-little                   (bad)
- Says From the Ashes had a staggering number of unique encounters and bosses and it feels there is not as much here - a newer player should get the first game for now.
    → marketing.reputation.falls-short-of-the-studios-earlier-games (bad)
- Says wait for a sale if you are lukewarm.
    → publishing.sale-dependency.unknown                     (~)
