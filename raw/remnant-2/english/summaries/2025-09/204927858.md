## What they said

- Says 5/10 - fun, but abandoned far too quickly.
    → live-ops.abandonment.known-bugs-never-fixed            (bad)
- Says the first world is randomised from three, so you likely start somewhere different from your friends - which sucked them in more.
    → production.content-variety.procedurally-varied         (good)
- Says their starting world has a main-NPC bug that hard-locks the last portion of the world; it dates from launch and was never properly patched despite all the DLC; every recommended method failed and the only recourse is rerolling the whole campaign or a new character.
    → engineering.bugs.breaks-play                           (bad)
- Says the combat kept what made the first game's gunplay-plus-melee great and expanded it with classes and cross-classing that do not lock you into a playstyle; mods swap freely.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.changes-how-you-play (good)
- Says enemies are not terribly spongy and solo is a nice experience, though the bosses are designed for a buddy or two.
    → game-design.enemy-design.leaves-you-in-control         (good)
- Says the story is bland - they cannot remember any beats; the environments were the most interesting part.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Says they would have loved fewer cutscenes and dialogues that lock you in place - show, do not tell.
    → game-design.ui-ux.cannot-skip-what-the-game-plays-at-you (bad)
- Says you cannot edit your character after creation - even Destiny 2 and Elden Ring added that; they had to dig into the files.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.cannot-change-how-you-look (bad)
- Says bigger does not mean better - the maps are terribly empty; backtracking for loot made them wish for waypoints; minibosses or points of interest would have fixed it.
    → game-design.level-design.the-spaces-are-scaled-too-big (bad)
- Says they will not buy the studio's future titles if it makes a habit of leaving such issues unattended.
    → marketing.reputation.studio-lost-my-trust              (bad)
