## What they said

- Says his first souls-like - he does not like punishing games and this one is not too bad in co-op.
    → marketing.reputation.won-over-someone-who-avoids-the-genre (good)
- Says single-player is an entirely different story - he only farms resources solo and waits for his buddy for the meat of the game.
    → game-design.solo-viability.punishing-solo              (bad)
- Says the story is very rich and interesting, and he wishes for an offline reading mode for all the books to make it more coherent.
    → narrative.story.worth-following                        (good)
- Says you get a lot of stuff - rings, weapons, amulets, relic shards - and it needs a filter and a list of what things do instead of clicking each one in a grid.
    → game-design.ui-ux.managing-the-inventory-is-a-chore    (bad)
- Asks for loadouts - loaded by touching a stone, or via an official app.
    → game-design.ui-ux.no-way-to-save-a-loadout             (bad)
- Says with one class maxed it is hard to level a second, and it gets harder to find low-level materials to level lower guns you want to try - focusing one build and trying everything are not compatible past mid-game.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.a-new-weapon-starts-the-grind-again (bad)
- Says he has got stuck in walls and floors a few times, once needing a restart.
    → engineering.bugs.you-get-stuck-on-the-scenery          (bad)
- Says he is sometimes marked in combat when he is not, with enemies stuck in walls that cannot be engaged, so he cannot use the stones.
    → engineering.bugs.buggy                                 (bad)
- Says some keys are hard-coded - Surrender cannot be rebound, so as an ESDF player he surrenders when he tries to crawl to a revive spot.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.cannot-rebind           (bad)
- Says he loves collecting classes and combining two into a uniquely named archetype, the many unique weapons, and the unique look and feel of the worlds.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says watch where you are firing because the game has friendly fire - the Handler class mitigates up to 80% of it at level 10.
    → game-design.co-op-design.friendly-fire-unknown         (~)
- Notes you can group up to three players in co-op.
    → game-design.co-op-design.unknown                       (~)
