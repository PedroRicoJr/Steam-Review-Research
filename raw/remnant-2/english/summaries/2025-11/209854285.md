## What they said

- Says it thrives when it embraces the randomised campaign - a patchwork story of three worlds, each with three interconnected storylines, hundreds of encounters and loot; extremely satisfying to play several times.
    → narrative.story.your-choices-change-the-story          (good)
- Says some storylines have significantly stronger writing than others; confusing for first-timers.
    → narrative.story.unknown                                (~)
- Says varied gunplay with unique, satisfying guns and bows; skills, passives and mods are impactful and open many archetypes.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says rings and amulets could have used some creative love.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.unknown (~)
- Says the music is great, the world design impactful, the environments huge and beautiful.
    → art.environment-art.evocative-places                   (good)

    → game-design.level-design.the-best-things-are-hidden-behind-a-guide (bad)
- Says some randomised events are poorly done, locking you out of rewards unless you reroll the entire game; rerolling over and over for the right set of events.
    → game-design.randomness.the-roll-decides-which-content-you-see (bad)

    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.unknown                  (~)

    → game-design.power-balance.upgrading-makes-the-game-harder (bad)

    → audio.voice-performance.badly-acted                    (bad)

    → game-design.level-design.unknown                       (~)

    → engineering.bugs.buggy                                 (bad)
- Says menu navigation is slow, especially fast travel and checkpoints.
    → game-design.ui-ux.hard-to-navigate                     (bad)
