## What they said

- Says a step up from the previous game in many ways, with a few things left behind.
    → marketing.positioning.successor-framing-accepted       (good)
- Says far more variety in the places you fight - the first game was 90% flat; now you hop boat to boat in a harbour shootout.
    → game-design.level-design.well-built                    (good)
- Says a shame most of the first game's worlds are gone, save Yaesha and sort of Earth.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.unknown                    (~)
- Says four ring slots instead of two, and a new modifier slot per weapon that grows useful with upgrades.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says armour set bonuses were only partly carried into archetypes; each weight class blends together and what you wear comes down to looks - Leto's set was gimped.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.shallow-options (bad)
- Says the relic modifier slots give bonuses so small they never mattered - 1.34% more ammo.
    → game-design.power-balance.options-feel-identical       (bad)
- Says boss design took a step up - no two world bosses feel alike; the evil Rubik's cubes and the final boss were highlights.
    → game-design.enemy-design.memorable-specials            (good)
- Says the story is less interesting than the first, but seeing the impact of 'John Remnant' and the new lore is neat if you stop and look.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.world-worth-exploring      (good)
- Says the whole game plays co-op without the souls-like hassle - no waiting on summon signs, no fighting a boss once per person; just join.
    → engineering.matchmaking.easy-to-drop-in-and-out        (good)
