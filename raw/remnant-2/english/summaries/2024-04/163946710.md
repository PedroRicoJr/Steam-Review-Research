## What they said

- Says 300 hours in each Remnant; the first was the best co-op game they ever played, this one is the same but worse - none of its mistakes fixed and some good parts made worse.
    → marketing.reputation.falls-short-of-the-studios-earlier-games (bad)
- Says the 65 trait-point cap replaced the first game's unlimited earning, so only eight and a half traits of thirty are active and progress stops early - the worst change, it kills replayability.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.the-cap-stops-you-short (bad)
- Says on medium, normal monsters pose no threat and die in one or two shots, and are scarce - you walk and walk for a little action.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.too-easy                 (bad)
- Says enemies are scarce for some reason - long empty walks between fights.
    → game-design.enemy-design.too-few-on-screen             (bad)
- Says enemies are just moving objects of very limited types, against the first game's diverse, fun-to-fight roster.
    → game-design.enemy-design.variety-lacking               (bad)
- Says bosses can easily one-shot you and are more puzzle than battle, so they feel like a 'here we go again' chore.
    → game-design.enemy-design.one-hit-kills                 (bad)
- Says the bosses feel like a chore rather than a fight to look forward to.
    → game-design.enemy-design.bosses-are-a-chore            (bad)
- Says jumping puzzles are badly implemented - miss by a centimetre and start over, like a 1991 Nintendo game.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.unreliable              (bad)
- Says a lot of puzzles are unsolvable by logic - you google five screens of text for an insane sequence, or spend 5-10 hours for a garbage weapon; illusory walls have zero indication, unlike Dark Souls.
    → game-design.level-design.the-best-things-are-hidden-behind-a-guide (bad)
- Says quality of life is non-existent - insta-death falls with no revive, bad weapon management and comparison, a huge hub you run around, NPCs that take three seconds to turn to you.
    → game-design.ui-ux.missing-quality-of-life              (bad)
- Says weapon management and comparison are bad.
    → game-design.ui-ux.managing-the-inventory-is-a-chore    (bad)
- Says the maps are insanely large with weird composition - mostly empty space, occasional enemies who just are there, then a gimmick boss and a reroll.
    → game-design.level-design.the-spaces-are-scaled-too-big (bad)
- Says dozens of rings, amulets and relics with four slots to use them - most meh, and the quantity devalues them.
    → game-design.power-balance.some-options-are-useless     (bad)
- Says armour sets are now pure cosmetics that do nothing.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.shallow-options (bad)
