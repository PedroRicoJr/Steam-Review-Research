## What they said

- A top-down camera at an adjusted 60-degree view; fast shooting and survival for up to four (59 found it helpful).
    → game-design.game-feel.camera.camera-works-well         (good)
- Made from an Unreal Tournament 2004 mod by a team Valve hired, and never given the focus of Valve's bigger games (reviewer's account).
    → marketing.reputation.traces-the-studios-lineage        (~)
- Only one official campaign, Jacob's Rest, a handful of levels.
    → production.content-amount.too-little                   (bad)
- Four classes - officer, medic, tech with sentries, hacking and fast welding, special weapons - each important in its own way.
    → game-design.role-design.every-role-needed              (good)
- 40 pieces of equipment chosen by class, play style and need.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- The included SDK spawned a vibrant modding community with fantastic campaigns.
    → community.user-created-content.mods-extend-the-game    (good)
- Smooth multiplayer interface with built-in friend invites.
    → community.social-features.getting-your-friends-in-works (good)
- Fun alone, but it shines with four.
    → game-design.solo-viability.works-solo                  (good)
- Can be extremely punishing: difficulties, friendly fire, tight corridors, waves of aliens.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.satisfyingly-hard        (good)
- When you die, the game freezes, replays your death in slow motion and shows who killed you - calling out friendly fire.
    → game-design.co-op-design.the-death-replay-names-who-killed-you (good)
- Responsive firing.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful                 (good)
- Statistics tracked outside the game show your best classes, weapons, accuracy and friendly fire.
    → game-design.progression.achievements.plenty-to-hunt    (good)
