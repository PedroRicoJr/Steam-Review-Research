## What they said

- Decent, well-presented graphics for a top-down game (edited 2021-07-29).
    → art.fidelity.looks-great                               (good)
- Odd audio - gun sounds, a hissing radio-like mic - that somehow all works together.
    → audio.sound-effects.sounds-great                       (good)
- Super easy controls: pick up and master.
    → game-design.new-player-experience.easy-to-start        (good)
- Ranks to 12, then a prestige reset for a better badge, like Titanfall or Call of Duty - no real reason for it beyond the badge.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.little-reason-to-keep-going (bad)
- The campaign is very, very short.
    → production.content-amount.too-little                   (bad)
- Downloaded player creations add a lot more fun, though they are short too.
    → community.user-created-content.mods-extend-the-game    (good)
- Great difficulties to choose from.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.well-graded              (good)
- On Insane or the horde mode you run out of ammo before the first door.
    → game-design.power-balance.resources-too-scarce         (bad)
- Alone it is boring; it needs friends.
    → game-design.solo-viability.punishing-solo              (bad)
- Free; an easy few hours of fun.
    → publishing.price.fair                                  (good)
- A tiny download.
    → engineering.performance.small-install-size             (good)
