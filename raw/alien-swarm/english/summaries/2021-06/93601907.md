## What they said

- An extraordinary move by Valve to give it away; expected quality from Valve's reputation, and it's free (11 hours).
    → marketing.reputation.traces-the-studios-lineage        (~)
- Play online with friends, with strangers in public games, on a dedicated server, or offline with bots.
    → game-design.ai-teammates.enables-solo-play             (good)
- Best with friends; public games make it harder to find serious players.
    → community.playing-with-friends.much-better-with-friends (good)
- Has made some interesting gaming buddies through the public lobby.
    → community.player-conduct.strangers-became-friends      (good)
- Clear menus for classes, weapons, bios and story.
    → game-design.ui-ux.clear-and-usable                     (good)
- The story, told in log entries in the levels, is thin and not compelling, but skipping it costs nothing.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Anyone can draw a route on the lobby map for all to see - great for planning and for scribbling funny pictures.
    → community.social-features.players-can-draw-on-the-shared-map (good)
- Four classes and 8 characters with their own abilities; you need a balanced team.
    → game-design.role-design.every-role-needed              (good)
- Missions are nearly impossible without a tech, the only one who opens doors and objectives, through puzzle minigames under heavy pressure.
    → game-design.role-design.one-role-is-required-to-progress (~)
- If the tech dies, the level restarts.
    → game-design.punishment-model.harsh-restart             (bad)
- No respawns, so the team must stay alive; loves the challenge and the reason to survive.
    → game-design.punishment-model.stakes-worth-the-risk     (good)
- But once dead, you sit waiting until the team finishes or dies.
    → game-design.co-op-design.a-dead-player-spectates-until-the-next-checkpoint (bad)
- A big weapon list, unlocked by levelling to 27, with medals for each time you max out.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.satisfying-progression (good)
- 64 achievements, including a hard speedrun one they are still chasing.
    → game-design.progression.achievements.plenty-to-hunt    (good)
- Top-notch graphics, gorgeous even on normal settings.
    → art.fidelity.looks-great                               (good)
- Dynamic lighting draws you into the ominous industrial setting.
    → art.atmosphere.draws-you-in                            (good)
- Ammo is precious and reloading throws away the rest of the clip - for a habitual reloader, the biggest annoyance of all.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.reloading-throws-away-the-rest-of-the-magazine (~)
- Repetitive but never boring; high replay value from weapon and character mixes, achievements and who you play with.
    → production.content-variety.repetitive-and-still-fun    (~)
- Main criticism: no Mac version.
    → engineering.platform-support.not-supported-at-all      (bad)
- A fantastic must-have, and free.
    → publishing.price.fair                                  (good)
