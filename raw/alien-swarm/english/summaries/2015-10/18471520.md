# Review 18471520 — alien-swarm — english
Thumbs: up · 9h played · 8 found it helpful
Created 2015-10-14 · counted in 2015-10

## What they said

- A free top-down co-op shooter: a small campaign of 7 levels against cute aliens like Half-Life's headcrabs (8 found it helpful).
    → production.content-amount.too-little                   (bad)
- A remake of the Unreal Tournament 2004 mod of the same name, they heard.
    → marketing.reputation.traces-the-studios-lineage        (~)
- Strictly multiplayer: not designed to be beaten alone; practice with bots covers only the first two levels (reviewer's claim).
    → game-design.solo-viability.unplayable-alone            (bad)
- Public multiplayer is active enough to find at least one game.
    → community.population.thinner-than-hoped-but-playable   (~)
- Teamwork through loadouts: balance general and special roles, and don't shoot each other.
    → game-design.co-op-design.rewards-teamwork              (good)
- The short campaign keeps getting harder, a challenge even for a good team.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.satisfyingly-hard        (good)
- No bots filling empty slots, as in Left 4 Dead.
    → game-design.ai-teammates.no-bots-provided              (bad)
- With one friend, it's hard to balance firepower and specialists - you need a tech and a medic - even on easy.
    → game-design.co-op-design.small-groups-have-it-hard     (bad)
- No microtransactions.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.no-microtransactions-at-all (good)
- Modding support, but no Steam Workshop.
    → community.user-created-content.no-mod-support          (bad)
- Levelling unlocks new items to prolong it.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.satisfying-progression (good)
- The music could be better.
    → audio.music.forgettable-or-annoying                    (bad)
