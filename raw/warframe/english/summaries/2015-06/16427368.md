# Review 16427368 — warframe — english
Thumbs: up · 1194h played · 1 found it helpful
Created 2015-06-15 · counted in 2015-06

## What they said

- Says you will hardly find another online game that evolves as rapidly and grows as continuously; the devs love the game, not just the money.
    → live-ops.update-cadence.steady-stream                  (good)
- Says the classic space-ninja theme has evolved over the years into more of a mercenary theme.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.unknown                    (~)
- Says you are a Tenno woken from cryogenic sleep, answering to the Lotus, bringing balance rather than peace; your priorities re-align, by your choice or by the majority of Tenno.
    → narrative.story.your-choices-change-the-story          (good)
- Says some gear can feel grindy to acquire for new players; as a veteran the resources come naturally.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
- Says there are syndicates to run missions for, shared community goals that unlock lore, and optional PvP with capture the flag and deathmatch.
    → game-design.modes.good-selection                       (good)
- Says the devs regularly add story-driven events the whole community takes part in, which can fail, and sometimes split the players between paths.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.timed-events-give-a-reason-to-log-in (good)
- Says the cash shop is very fair: the prices look scary until you realise you never have to pay and can get it all at an acceptable pace.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.everything-earnable (good)
- Says the only things not buyable with in-game currency are some style items, and you can trade for premium currency to get those.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.currency-earnable-by-playing (good)
- Says the login bonus gives premium-currency discounts up to 75 percent and the Steam packs are half price at every big sale, so an impulse buyer who cannot hold back may struggle but a budget buyer does fine.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.unknown               (~)
- Says there is no pay to win, only 'pay to sleep' - a running gag you understand once you play.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.paying-only-shortens-the-grind (~)
- Says the devs have pulled cash shop items within days when the community strongly disagreed, always about style items going too far.
    → community.developer-communication.listens-and-acts     (good)
- Says the devs livestream every week, communicate changes and content directly, and change them if players dislike them; every two weeks they show what they are working on.
    → community.developer-communication.open-about-what-it-is-doing (good)
- Says they have redone entire game systems and polished the graphics multiple times; DX12 is announced and physically based rendering is arriving step by step.
    → live-ops.patch-quality.made-it-better                  (good)
- Says the one thing you cannot expect is anything to last: they change even the core, succeed eight times in ten, and try again when they fail; their vision sometimes clashes with players' opinions.
    → live-ops.patch-quality.the-game-keeps-changing-under-you (bad)
- Says they went on hiatus three or four times for a month, but the game and its content are free, so you pick it up again when new content lands.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.you-can-put-it-down-and-come-back (good)
- Says if you want fluent combat, this is yours.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful                 (good)
