## What they said

- Says they found it fun but cannot recommend it long term, which they realised after 400 hours and 90 dollars of microtransactions.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.players-buy-in-to-support-the-studio (good)
- Says fun solo or in multiplayer.
    → game-design.solo-viability.works-solo                  (good)
- Says updated regularly with hotfixes and content.
    → live-ops.update-cadence.steady-stream                  (good)
- Says platinum can be traded, so you need not spend a cent if you farm rare items and trade them, but that takes much patience when new.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.currency-earnable-by-playing (good)
- Says various mission types, random syndicate events that are tense when new, and each warframe plays differently.
    → game-design.modes.good-selection                       (good)
- Says everything can be had with in-game credits except some cosmetics.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.everything-earnable (good)
- Says the stealth aspect is substandard: outside Spy missions it is neither appreciated nor viable.
    → game-design.power-balance.some-options-are-useless     (bad)
- Says you are forced to buy inventory slots with platinum to experience more content.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.what-you-can-own-is-capped-unless-you-pay (bad)
- Says weapons and frames only reach full potential with 'potatoes', which cannot be obtained normally: buy them with platinum, or wait for a rare alert or a very rare login reward.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.gated-behind-a-different-mode (bad)
- Says the market makes no sense - a blueprint for a rare resource costs laughably more than farming it - and platinum is far too expensive even with discounts.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.the-shop-charges-far-too-much (bad)
- Advises never to buy platinum without a daily-login discount coupon, if you must buy currency at all.
    → community.player-conduct.the-review-teaches-you-how-to-play (good)
- Says there is no vote-kick or ban function.
    → community.social-features.no-way-to-remove-bad-players (bad)
- Says Archwing missions are boring and pointless.
    → game-design.modes.a-mode-falls-flat                    (bad)
- Says RNG rewards turn the game into a giant grindfest and a waste of time when you do not get what you want, which is 90 percent of the time.
    → game-design.randomness.the-thing-you-need-may-never-roll (bad)
- Says the gameplay gets grindy and repetitive, and at the end it is all numbers going up: no strategy when you one-shot everything with six- or seven-figure hits in Tower 4.
    → game-design.power-balance.progression-outgrows-the-challenge (bad)
- Says for lack of endgame every mission becomes a laborious linear chore, farming resources, credits or Prime parts to feed mastery rank.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.nothing-left-to-chase (bad)
- Says numerous game-breaking glitches despite the regular hotfixes.
    → engineering.bugs.buggy                                 (bad)

    → game-design.game-feel.movement.unreliable              (bad)

    → engineering.servers.high-latency                       (bad)

    → publishing.monetisation-practice.paying-only-shortens-the-grind (~)
