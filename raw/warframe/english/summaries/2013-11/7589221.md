## What they said

- Says they recommended it originally and now do not.
    → review.the-thumb-was-flipped-from-its-first-verdict    (~)
- Says the core gameplay is still fun: you progress warframes, weapons and companions on varied missions, with a real sense of power progression.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.satisfying-progression (good)
- Says there was no real story for a long time and the story since added makes no sense; the writing gets away with being amateur because it is ambiguous.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Says parkour animations, run-and-gun and decent melee, with flashy visuals reinforced by sound effects; the art and sound teams did a great job.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful                 (good)
- Says group play feels rewarding because nobody has to fight over loot.
    → game-design.co-op-design.loot-is-shared                (good)
- Says a sentinel or pet adds another layer, modded to protect, to deal damage, or both.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says mission variety exceeds most standards; some missions are objectively not fun, but there is plenty to do.
    → production.content-variety.varied-runs                 (good)
- Says the operator, added later, is another layer to keep up with and in places necessary to progress.
    → live-ops.patch-quality.the-updates-add-to-what-i-did-not-come-for (bad)
- Says so many warframes were added that they imbalanced the game and moved away from the whole space-ninjas thing; the Nidus Phryke and Zephyr Harrier skins do not match the game whatsoever; the studio is going in a totally different and weird direction.
    → live-ops.patch-quality.drifted-from-what-it-was        (bad)
- Says every open world adds resources to grind for its gear; the number of things needed is so high that the grind sits in their head even in a fun mission, and it does not respect their time.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
- Says they got so frustrated with the Necramech grind that they bought one with platinum.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.paying-only-shortens-the-grind (~)
- Says a Necramech is required for part of some Railjack missions, which another player told them mid-mission.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.gated-behind-a-different-mode (bad)
- Says the developers historically do not take critique well, especially about parts they are emotionally attached to, and their 'we'll fix it' hardly ever happens.
    → community.developer-communication.ignores-feedback     (bad)
