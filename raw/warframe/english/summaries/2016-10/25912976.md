## What they said

- Recommends it to players who enjoy teamwork.
    → game-design.co-op-design.rewards-teamwork              (good)
- Says optional quests and lore fragments spread through the game reveal a lot about the factions and characters.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.world-worth-exploring      (good)
- Says there is a learning and level curve, to about Mastery Rank 5.
    → game-design.progression.complexity.overwhelming-at-first (~)
- Says after the first planets the grind for components and experience sets in, and farming to use a weapon properly is boring.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)

    → game-design.power-balance.progression-outgrows-the-challenge (bad)
- Says there is PvP in the Conclave and a ball sport, Lunaro, for those not after pure PvE.
    → production.content-variety.more-to-do-than-fighting    (good)

    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says the community will help if you need a boost to finish a mission.

- Says the development team looks to the community for input on updates.
    → community.developer-communication.listens-and-acts     (good)
