# Review 11592642 — warframe — english
Thumbs: up · 166h played · 0 found it helpful
Created 2014-08-07 · counted in 2014-08

## What they said

- Says you are forced to use a lot of different equipment to raise mastery rank, though that is not bad, since you would try it anyway.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.unknown            (~)
- Says the gameplay is a bit repetitive - everything comes down to slaying lots of enemies in style - and asks whether that is really a con.
    → production.content-variety.unknown                     (~)
- Says items in the in-game market are pretty expensive.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.the-shop-charges-far-too-much (bad)
- Says the trade channel gets you better stuff cheaper, and the best equipment cannot be bought in the market at all but can be bought from other players, which is also how to get platinum without paying.
    → community.social-features.the-player-market-works      (good)
- Says the few things you have to pay for are inventory space for warframes and weapons; the prices are low and the basic space is enough for an occasional player.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.what-you-can-own-is-capped-unless-you-pay (bad)
- Says no pay-to-win: money gets things faster, not better.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.paying-only-shortens-the-grind (~)
- Says you can get everything by playing, apart from some decorations and inventory space.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.everything-earnable (good)
- Says you can paint warframes, companions and weapons, attach decorative armour and even choose animation sets.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.you-can-look-how-you-want (good)
- Says hundreds of mods customise the stats, and more weapons than they have seen in any game: machine guns to whips, bows, flamethrowers, hammers.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says the animations are excellent, with wall-walking, somersaults, sliding, stance mods, combos, dismembering and pinning enemies to walls.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.rewards-mastery         (good)
- Says despite the complex possibilities the controls are very accurate and easy to handle.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.responsive-and-clear    (good)
- Says clans are well handled: you build a large space station, the dojo, with a fully customisable interior, and there is support for clans of different sizes.
    → community.social-features.the-clan-hall-is-worth-building (good)
- Says the game is free.
    → publishing.price.fair                                  (good)
- Says not only one of the best free-to-play games they have seen, it holds its ground against full-price games with ease.
    → marketing.reputation.best-in-its-category              (good)
