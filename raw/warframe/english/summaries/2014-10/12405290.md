# Review 12405290 — warframe — english

## What they said

- Says an awesome, very original free-to-play title with a very enjoyable sci-fi style.
    → marketing.reputation.unlike-anything-else              (good)
- Says randomly generated levels.
    → production.content-variety.procedurally-varied         (good)
- Says a small guild of four to eight players is enough to reach and finish all the content, unlike most MMOs.
    → game-design.co-op-design.the-small-team-is-the-right-size (good)
- Says extremely responsive and enjoyable combat: shooting and melee are fast, precise and feel very good.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful                 (good)
- Says a good-looking game with a very original art style, the space ninjas and weapons most of all.
    → art.visual-direction.looks-well-directed               (good)
- Says the weapons, melee and explosions all sound great, and the enemies use their own alien language, which does a lot for the atmosphere.
    → audio.sound-effects.punchy                             (good)
- Says as a guild you can build your own base, a level in itself with fun fluff and special weapons to research, mostly a place to meet friends.
    → community.social-features.the-clan-hall-is-worth-building (good)
- Says somersaults, slides, wall-jumps and movement tricks almost make it a free-running game, and all of it works in combat.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.rewards-mastery         (good)
- Says it can be very challenging or very casual and relaxing; the difficulty is very scalable.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.well-graded              (good)
- Says loads of cool things worth getting, so you can spend a ton of time gathering, crafting weapons, warframes and items.
    → production.content-amount.plenty                       (good)
- Says all the warframes have unique styles with very powerful, essential skills, and the game allows a lot of customised builds.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says the free-to-play model is very fair: nothing needs buying, though it speeds you up immensely, and it is nice to support the team.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.paying-only-shortens-the-grind (~)
- Says the networking can be a problem because players host, and a host with a bad connection or a weak computer lags everyone.
    → engineering.servers.peer-to-peer-not-dedicated         (bad)
- Says a performance hog that will not run well on older computers, worse when hosting because the enemy AI taxes performance too.
    → engineering.performance.demanding-hardware             (bad)
- Says the maps are random but the tilesets are not, and they get old after a while.
    → game-design.level-design.repetitive-layouts            (bad)
- Says reactors and catalysts, essential to max out a frame or weapon, are hard to come by: you get lucky or wait a long time, unless you pay; the one questionable free-to-play decision.
    → game-design.randomness.the-thing-you-need-may-never-roll (bad)
- Says 9/10; the cons only start to matter after a couple of hundred hours, which for a free title is a good place to be.
    → review.positive.unknown                                (good)
