# Review 12306790 — warframe — english
Thumbs: up · 453h played · 1 found it helpful
Created 2014-09-30 · counted in 2014-09

## What they said

- Says one of the best free-to-play games they have played.
    → marketing.reputation.best-in-its-category              (good)
- Says pay to win? Hell no: most of everything is earned through boss fights, long defence or survival runs, or Void missions.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.everything-earnable (good)
- Says ranking up, through missions and levelling weapons, unlocks weapons of mass destruction that destroy everything with little effort.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.satisfying-progression (good)
- Says tons of warframes, starting with a choice of Excalibur for slicing, Volt for electricity and speed, or Loki for stealth.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says tons and tons of weapons - rifles, flamethrowers, laser-cube guns, bolt guns that pin enemies to walls, dual magnums, scythes, a boomerang blade - switched any time in the Arsenal.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says sentinels are droids that follow you, sucking up loot, blasting close enemies, or popping a shield while you revive; they take mods and level like weapons.
    → game-design.ai-teammates.helps-in-combat               (good)
- Says most boss fights are epic, each with its own way of being beaten.
    → game-design.enemy-design.good-variety                  (good)
- Says blueprints drop at random, so you fight a boss several times and keep getting the same part.
    → game-design.randomness.the-thing-you-need-may-never-roll (bad)
- Says after killing a boss an assassin like the Stalker hunts you mid-mission, your screen flickers, and killing him drops one of his weapons; they have fourteen Dread bows.
    → game-design.enemy-design.memorable-specials            (good)
- Says a dojo is where you design and build your own base with workshops that research Grineer, Corpus and Infested weapons not sold in the market; new players should join an existing clan, since building one costs a lot.
    → community.social-features.the-clan-hall-is-worth-building (good)
- Says the community is cool most of the time: high-ranked players help low-ranked ones with missions and give them mods and spare materials.
    → community.culture.welcoming-to-newcomers               (good)
- Says you trade in a dojo - keys, mods, blueprints - it is impossible to be scammed, and the wiki lists fair prices in case someone lies about them.
    → community.social-features.the-player-market-works      (good)
- Says the studio updates almost twice a month, and every time it is something big.
    → live-ops.update-cadence.steady-stream                  (good)
- Says events are awesome: a chance at all the blueprints for one warframe, or a sweet helmet.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.timed-events-give-a-reason-to-log-in (good)
