# Review 12111253 — warframe — english
Thumbs: up · 116h played · 0 found it helpful
Created 2014-09-15 · **Edited 2018-09-25** · counted in 2014-09

## What they said

- Says one of the best and most addicting free-to-play games they have played; a free-to-play worth paying for.
    → game-design.replayability.keeps-pulling-you-back       (good)
- Says the combat is incredible: fast, fluid, seamless switching from rifle to bullet-time pistols to a greatsword landing, a power fantasy through and through.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong (good)
- Says the parkour offers unparalleled movement and traversal - wall-running, gliding, dashing, sliding - and skilled players dash through levels barely touching the ground.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.rewards-mastery         (good)
- Says dozens of warframes, each with a theme and superpowers - DPS, tank, support - so something fits your style.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says the weapons are remarkably varied and each feels unique, not the same type with tweaked numbers; a plethora of unlocks.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says one of the friendliest online communities on Steam.
    → community.player-conduct.welcoming-community           (good)
- Says appealing, strong visuals.
    → art.fidelity.looks-great                               (good)
- Says some of the most insane grinding they have come across: dozens of hours to unlock one warframe unless you spend money, though the game earns it.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
- Says the timers are especially egregious: twelve hours for any weapon, and for a warframe three twelve-hour parts then three whole days to assemble.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.gated-behind-real-world-time (bad)
- Says platinum prices are reasonable and you can gain platinum by trading with other players, so it levels out even though they despise the timers.
    → community.social-features.the-player-market-works      (good)
- Says a generous and fair microtransaction system, a shining example of sustainable, healthy and ethical free-to-play monetisation.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.paying-only-shortens-the-grind (~)
- Says the story and quest design are the weakest part by far: tedious quests with uninteresting objectives, incoherent storytelling and mediocre voice acting.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Says mediocre voice acting.
    → audio.voice-performance.grating-or-repetitive          (bad)
- Says the mid-game reveal - that the warframes are puppets remotely controlled by teenagers - destroys the fun of being a space ninja, has a teenager butting in every mission, and removes any tension because the Tenno become unstoppable.
    → narrative.story.a-twist-took-away-the-fantasy          (bad)
- Says alternate modes such as Archwing are just not fun.
    → game-design.modes.a-mode-falls-flat                    (bad)
