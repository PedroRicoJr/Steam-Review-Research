## What they said

- Says they picked it back up months after quitting and are remembering why they loved it.
    → game-design.replayability.keeps-pulling-you-back       (good)
- Says solid controls.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.responsive-and-clear    (good)
- Says about 60 fps on a toaster of an Acer laptop with a few low settings.
    → engineering.performance.runs-well-on-modest-hardware   (good)
- Says tons of warframes and weapons to suit your favourite gameplay.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says an amazing free-to-play system: not a cent needed while still getting what you want.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.everything-earnable (good)
- Says a randomly generated tileset every mission, so it is not the same map every time, except defence.
    → production.content-variety.procedurally-varied         (good)
- Says a very helpful community.
    → community.player-conduct.welcoming-community           (good)
- Says a dev team that regularly keeps in contact with the community.
    → community.developer-communication.open-about-what-it-is-doing (good)
- Says tons of grinding: the game revolves on playing the same mission over and over for specific parts.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
- Says the random generator for loot drops is a cruel and unforgiving mistress.
    → game-design.randomness.the-thing-you-need-may-never-roll (bad)
- Says the waiting time on crafting can be long and gruelling; the gameplay is fast, the crafting is not.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.gated-behind-real-world-time (bad)
- Says gameplay gets very repetitive and stale over time: the end goal is always drop in, kill, reach objective A, reach objective B, drop out.
    → production.content-variety.repetitive                  (bad)
- Says the forums can be biased and players have been banned from the forums and the game for no good reason; the big-brother feel is frightening, so use the subreddit.
    → community.moderation.heavy-handed                      (bad)
- Says there will always be a weapon that outplays your favourite; of the many bows only two are worth your time.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.only-a-few-builds-are-viable (bad)
- Says the trade chat is notorious for scammers; keep a price site open so you are not ripped off.
    → community.player-conduct.unknown                       (~)
- Says complimentary bugs arrive with every update, a necessary evil, and the dev team is quick to fix them.
    → live-ops.patch-quality.unknown                         (~)
- Says under no circumstances buy platinum if you have a spending problem: a weapon and a decent warframe from the market is already over 20 dollars, and unless it is a cosmetic you cannot live without, it is not worth it.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.the-shop-charges-far-too-much (bad)
- Says a studio of about eighty pushes out a title with content to rival AAA developers, for free, and the turnout is infinitely better.
    → review.grades-it-against-the-studios-size              (~)
