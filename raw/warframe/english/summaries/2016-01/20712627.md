## What they said

- Says it is their most played game after two years; every update brings something, and players are always anticipating the next one to beat the last.
    → live-ops.update-cadence.steady-stream                  (good)
- Says a wide array of characters and play styles, none of them bad; every frame has a unique ability to bring to a fight, so anyone can play the way they like.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Explains that you start with one of three frames plus a spare slot, that further slots cost 20 platinum each, and that the starting 50 platinum buys two more.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.what-you-can-own-is-capped-unless-you-pay (bad)
- Says platinum gives no advantage; their method is the trade market, swapping rare items for platinum, so there is never a reason to buy it.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.currency-earnable-by-playing (good)
- Says people who call it pay to win saw a price tag and assumed premium-only content without looking for where the item drops; a new frame does not make the old one useless.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.cosmetic-only         (good)
- Says the early game is a problem: not much tutorial to show you what to do.
    → game-design.new-player-experience.poorly-explained     (bad)
- Says the studio is very active towards players' wishes and is working on a new system for new players.
    → community.developer-communication.listens-and-acts     (good)
- Says the Guides of the Lotus, players who dedicate themselves to helping newcomers with questions, missions or loot, are expanding through applications; they have applied.
    → community.culture.welcoming-to-newcomers               (good)
