## What they said

- Says there is a lot of mission running and material collecting for weapons, gear and frames, but you never have to chase things beyond your level bracket: cheap credit weapons and blueprints make strong early loadouts, and extractor drones mine resources for you.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.unknown            (~)
- Says the drops are inconsistent: the enemy carrying the part you need may not appear on its own faction's map, and if it does, it may not drop it, so you run missions many times.
    → game-design.randomness.the-thing-you-need-may-never-roll (bad)
- Says the new void relic system alleviated that, letting you tailor the drop chance of specific items.
    → live-ops.patch-quality.fixed-what-mattered             (good)
- Says any shop selling currency or boosters is read as pay-to-win, when it is really time spent equals free items, money spent equals more free time.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.paying-only-shortens-the-grind (~)
- Says nothing relevant to advancement and gameplay is unobtainable by playing, except event-only items, boosters and slots.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.everything-earnable (good)
- Says weapon, warframe and sentinel slots are relevant to gameplay because without them you limit your versatility.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.what-you-can-own-is-capped-unless-you-pay (bad)
- Says the trading system alleviates the grindy randomness: trade unwanted mods, keys and Prime parts for credits, items or platinum, so you never have to spend money if you are savvy.
    → community.social-features.the-player-market-works      (good)
- Says the 'bad community' is not the developers' fault; the ignore feature keeps someone who behaves badly out of your games, and vote-kick tends to get abused.
    → community.social-features.can-remove-bad-players       (good)
- Says a lot of content is better with a group, yields better drops and XP, and some of it needs a group, like certain defence missions or carrying four dragon keys to a Void vault.
    → game-design.solo-viability.some-rewards-need-a-second-player (bad)
- Says early on the tilesets were bland endless halls, but the art team has since increased the variety and look of each system, with open areas and less repetitive layouts.
    → live-ops.patch-quality.made-it-better                  (good)
- Says people rush through levels, leaving no time for new players to learn rooms or for anyone to find the secrets and loot.
    → community.player-conduct.players-want-different-things-from-a-run (~)
- Says the lore is there if you look: codex scans, boss dialogue, special missions and the level designs themselves tell a bigger story than an action game would normally have, loosely connected to Dark Sector.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.world-worth-exploring      (good)
- Says a very involved and very passionate team is behind it, and it is one of the more unique and high-quality free-to-play games.
    → production.craftsmanship.made-with-care                (good)
- Says it is enjoyable, especially with friends; bring some in so you have a tight group.
    → community.playing-with-friends.much-better-with-friends (good)
