## What they said

- Says it is a unique game with different weaponry and a cool universe to explore.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.world-worth-exploring      (good)
- Says the story is pretty common - saving the galaxy - but the Stalker is an interesting character who gets you wondering who he is.
    → narrative.characters-writing.funny-or-memorable        (good)
- Says the story deserves at most a five or six out of ten.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Says co-op difficulty is one player difficult, two challenging, three ok, four too easy.
    → game-design.co-op-design.more-players-makes-it-trivial (bad)
- Says bosses are challenging until you find their weakness, then too easy, except one on Saturn.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.too-easy                 (bad)
- Says the enemies are three creative races - an old empire surviving, a futuristic one, and mutants - plus one that is all three together.
    → game-design.enemy-design.good-variety                  (good)
- Says the graphics have too much blur.
    → art.fidelity.the-picture-is-blurry                     (bad)
- Says it is not easy to find a good squad or a server.
    → engineering.matchmaking.unknown                        (~)
- Says the enemies are not the smartest: you can sprint behind one without him noticing you.
    → game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour             (bad)
- Says the biggest problem is that it gets really repetitive, and quickly.
    → production.content-variety.repetitive                  (bad)
- Says the devs are still updating the game a lot, so they hope the problems get fixed.
    → live-ops.update-cadence.steady-stream                  (good)
