# Review 776269 — warframe — english
Thumbs: up · 218h played · 6 found it helpful

## What they said

- Says they hated Warframe for years - mindless combat, grindy, every weapon the same - until 2016, when the balance shifted from nerf-everything to everything-is-overpowered-in-its-own-way, and it is glorious.
    → game-design.power-balance.well-tuned                   (good)
- Says weapons are finally fun to level and collecting a vast armoury is a joy; mastery comes from levelling new weapons to 30, so you are always using tools you would never consider.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.satisfying-progression (good)
- Says missions are no longer pointless kill-five grinds but choose-your-own adventures, like Killing Floor or Payday with space ninjas; Lunaro, Pokemon Snap quests and pets besides.
    → production.content-variety.varied-runs                 (good)
- Says movement is not restricted to console-like loot hallways and reminds them of Tribes.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.rewards-mastery         (good)
- Says every three months new Prime parts are sold for 140 dollars, but the studio lets you craft them free with a little effort, and they have done so every time.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.everything-earnable (good)
- Says nothing requires a team; you can solo the entire game.
    → game-design.solo-viability.works-solo                  (good)
- Says full PhysX combat and gore with abilities identical to Jedi force powers makes fighting fun: throwing people off cliffs, slicing them in half.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful                 (good)
- Says warframes are like heroes with their own role and playstyle: Vauban plants traps, Ash's ultimate is Omnislash, Ember throws fireballs, healers heal through combat.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says everything is modular: combine elements into new ones, stack crit past 100 percent, strap damage onto a slow axe, stack range and nuke a room.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says a free-to-play game where you get premium currency just by playing: Prime parts from missions sell to other players for platinum; 100 platinum off one piece last night.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.currency-earnable-by-playing (good)
- Says daily login rewards include 75-percent-off currency coupons; 200 dollars of platinum for 50.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.unknown               (~)
- Says the developers price everything crazy high in platinum, but player-set prices are rock bottom; a healthy economy with people buying and selling at reasonable prices, and warframe.market makes it a breeze.
    → community.social-features.the-player-market-works      (good)
- Says boosters increase the amount gained, not drop numbers, so rare stays rare and there is no pay-to-win; crafting takes twelve hours and you can pay to rush, which is reasonable next to crate keys elsewhere.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.paying-only-shortens-the-grind (~)
- Says in 2016 you pretty much need the wiki, as nothing is explained in game.
    → game-design.progression.complexity.requires-outside-research (bad)
- Says the next update, expected summer 2016, will streamline the map and the new-player experience.
    → live-ops.update-cadence.awaiting-promised-content      (~)
- Says you eventually run into developers playing the game; they met them at PAX East, the coolest and friendliest people at the convention, and the reason they gave the game another try.
    → community.developer-communication.the-developers-play-with-us (good)
- Ends with the reviewer's own referral link.
