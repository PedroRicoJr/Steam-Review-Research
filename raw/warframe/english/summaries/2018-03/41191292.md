## What they said

- Says no gameplay mechanic is behind a paywall; everything needed can be got in game for free.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.everything-earnable (good)
- Says almost every area is accessible, with few invisible walls - if you can see it you can reach it.
    → game-design.level-design.no-invisible-walls            (good)
- Says the maps are generated from set tilesets, varied enough not to be boring.
    → production.content-variety.procedurally-varied         (good)
- Says the developers regularly take in player feedback and adjust the game.
    → community.developer-communication.listens-and-acts     (good)
- Says most of the huge variety of weapons are viable all the way to the endgame.
    → game-design.power-balance.no-build-is-required         (good)
- Says without platinum the build times feel long - 12 hours per part, 72 for a warframe, 24 for a weapon.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.gated-behind-real-world-time (bad)
- Says the waits can be mitigated through trading, since platinum is tradable.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.currency-earnable-by-playing (good)
- Says it is not new-player friendly: the tutorial covers basic controls only and never explains the mod system.
    → game-design.new-player-experience.poorly-explained     (bad)
- Says bugs happen often enough to notice, though not game-breaking.
    → engineering.bugs.buggy                                 (bad)
- Says the trading system is poorly put together; without third-party sites, trading would be dead.
    → community.social-features.the-player-market-works-badly (bad)
