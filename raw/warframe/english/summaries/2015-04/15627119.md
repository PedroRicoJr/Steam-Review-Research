## What they said

- Recommends it for the continuous support and nice developers, and because many people enjoy this sort of game.
    → live-ops.update-cadence.steady-stream                  (good)
- Says it is not a game for them: primarily a game about items, numbers and RNG, mindlessly grinding and watching numbers go up.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
- Says the cycle of grinding, building loadouts and customising your character, ship and clan dojo takes precedence over the gameplay itself.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.the-grind-crowds-out-the-game (bad)
- Says enemies one-shot you without good enough equipment, and once you have the mods you one-shot them; either you cannot succeed or you cannot have fun.
    → game-design.power-balance.gear-decides-the-pve-fight   (bad)
- Says in groups the veterans, playing the optimal way, melt the enemies or rush the objective before you get a chance to do much.
    → game-design.new-player-experience.late-joiner-outmatched (bad)
- Says every new addition is restricted by the same mentality: the open worlds are big pre-made maps for the same missions, their settlements sources of items you grind new currency for; weekly missions are more objectives to check off; events are the same mission run hundreds of times.
    → production.content-variety.repetitive                  (bad)
- Says the atmosphere suffers: bosses are unbeatable or drop like flies, and the elite assassin squad sent to hunt you dies by accident to the first shots of your shotgun.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.breaks-its-own-fiction     (bad)
- Says there is a lot of content and a lot to obtain that makes you feel powerful.
    → production.content-amount.plenty                       (good)
- Says the devs, though sometimes disconnected from their own game and from what the community wants, have shown time and again that they care more than the average AAA company.
    → community.developer-communication.listens-and-acts     (good)
- Advises not to spend money on platinum until the daily login gives a 75 percent discount, which may take a while if the game expects you to pay; returning players get one within days.
    → community.player-conduct.the-review-teaches-you-how-to-play (good)
