## What they said

- Says it is like the Mass Effect and Deus Ex games except multiplayer, with less epic questing, more gear management and faster action.
    → marketing.reputation.explained-by-naming-other-games   (~)
- Says there are loads of warframes, weapons and mods to customise them; the diversity is vast.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says there is no real endgame in the sense of something hard that still challenges the best players with the best gear; not much for demanding hardcore players yet.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.too-easy                 (bad)
- Says it is fun with friends or with the in-game community.
    → community.playing-with-friends.much-better-with-friends (good)
- Says in a nutshell it is an endless grind for gear and mods that end up forgotten in the inventory or sold to make room.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
- Says it is weird that there is no in-game wiki for drop locations; finding the information should not be the only challenging thing in the game.
    → game-design.progression.complexity.requires-outside-research (bad)
- Says there is a steady stream of content updates.
    → live-ops.update-cadence.steady-stream                  (good)
- Says the devs are really open and close to the players with their weekly streams and announcements.
    → community.developer-communication.open-about-what-it-is-doing (good)
- Says bugs are fixed swiftly.
    → live-ops.patch-quality.fixed-what-mattered             (good)
- Says the servers are up all the time.
    → engineering.servers.stable                             (good)
- Says they would have agreed with the Metacritic 68 at the start; now they give it 85 while waiting for endgame that uses their overpowered gear; if you were disappointed earlier, try it again.
    → marketing.reputation.recovered-over-time               (good)
