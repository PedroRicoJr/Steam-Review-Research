## What they said

- Says they wanted a sci-fi MMO with reflex combat and melee, not another fantasy world, and Warframe ticked every box; it knows its target audience.
    → review.says-they-are-the-target-audience               (~)
- Says they are eagerly awaiting the update that adds an NPC crew to the Railjack.
    → live-ops.update-cadence.awaiting-promised-content      (~)
- Says it is riddled with flaws, some of which only get worse over time, and so much content that the devs struggle to keep up with fixing it, so many players have lost patience and quit.
    → live-ops.patch-quality.unknown                         (~)
- Says the new-player experience has been retooled a few times so it is no longer as incredibly hostile as it was.
    → live-ops.patch-quality.fixed-what-mattered             (good)
- Says it can often get very grindy.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
- Says there are timegates on most things.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.gated-behind-real-world-time (bad)
- Says the timegates can be skipped with platinum, and it often feels like the devs are purposely nudging you to pay to avoid their shenanigans.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.paying-only-shortens-the-grind (~)
- Says power creep is a major problem: old things quickly lose relevance as new, more powerful gear is introduced.
    → game-design.power-balance.power-creep                  (bad)
- Says the gameplay is thrilling: leaping several feet, cleaving two soldiers in one slice, freezing a room, and none of it a cutscene.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong (good)
- Says each warframe's abilities make it play differently, and the basic mechanics let you gun, snipe, stealth, berserk or spam powers, as if you choose which kind of game you are playing.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says Warframe is their favourite story in any game ever made, with amazing twists and very rich, well-thought-out lore, and each faction has its own world-building.
    → narrative.story.worth-following                        (good)
- Says everywhere feels like a wild futuristic space adventure: Corpus ships glowing with power and data, ancient Orokin towers, even the Grineer junk-tech; they love exploring the areas.
    → art.environment-art.evocative-places                   (good)
- Says the most important reason to try it is that it is free - free to download, play, win and uninstall - and not total rubbish.
    → publishing.price.fair                                  (good)
