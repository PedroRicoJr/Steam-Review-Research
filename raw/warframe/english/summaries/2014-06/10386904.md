## What they said

- Says free and never-ending fun: 95 percent of items are obtainable, excluding event and founder exclusives and some appearance options.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.everything-earnable (good)
- Says this is not pay-to-win: platinum buys cosmetics like colours and capes and does not affect who is better.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.cosmetic-only         (good)
- Says platinum can also rush crafting wait times, which are reasonable and not much of a bother.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.paying-only-shortens-the-grind (~)
- Says the right mods or Prime parts sell in trade chat for platinum; they have not spent a penny and have five capes, palettes, lots of inventory space and potatoes.
    → community.social-features.the-player-market-works      (good)
- Says they probably will spend, because the developers are amazing.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.players-buy-in-to-support-the-studio (good)
- Says the sheer variety of customisation and weapons is insane: guns blazing or invisible assassinations, machine guns to kunai and katanas.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says the game has been severely criticised for being repetitive and people do not see the amount of options for fun.
    → marketing.reputation.judged-unfairly                   (good)
- Says the graphics are really top notch.
    → art.fidelity.looks-great                               (good)
- Says the recent update added kubrow companions beside the sentinels.
    → live-ops.update-cadence.steady-stream                  (good)
- Says the developers listen and read the forums: the warframe Nova and the Silva and Aegis weapon were created by players, with two more player designs in development.

    → community.developer-communication.open-about-what-it-is-doing (good)
