## What they said

- Says there are different styles: stealth without being noticed or stealth-killing everyone, a loud juggernaut blowing things up, or a healer keeping allies alive.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Says awesome graphics.

- Says quite a few controls, but they found them easy to get used to.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.responsive-and-clear    (good)
- Says there are moves to avoid and counter attacks: spinning with your swords, sliding round a corner and shooting, dropping onto a target.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.rewards-mastery         (good)
- Says quite a few weapons; you can have an arsenal, especially if you spend some cash.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
