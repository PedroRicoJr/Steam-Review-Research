## What they said

- Says the game is not worth forty dollars and not to buy it at full price.
    → publishing.price.too-high-for-what-it-is               (bad)
- Says he bought it at half price and was pleasantly surprised by how much fun he had.
    → publishing.sale-dependency.buy-on-sale-only            (bad)
- Says there are loads of weapons and attachments giving a variety of builds with the class perks.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says the gunplay is fun and the weapons feel lovely in sound, handling and impact.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful                 (good)
- Says the perks work as a grid puzzle where shapes and adjacency matter, and that maximising them is extremely satisfying.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.changes-how-you-play (good)
- Says the synths and the xenomorphs are separate factions, giving three-way fights.
    → game-design.enemy-design.good-variety                  (good)
- Says the game is all about grinding and that he replays the same missions to level his character, class and weapons.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
- Says the levelling only matters because the higher difficulties are where it becomes more fun.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.the-lower-settings-are-not-worth-playing (bad)
- Says all unlocks are random, so he can get weapons he dislikes and attachments for guns he never uses.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.you-cannot-choose-what-you-unlock (bad)
- Says every xenomorph type except the Spitter charges in a straight line, usually single file, and acts more like a dog than an apex predator.
    → game-design.enemy-design.they-come-one-at-a-time-instead-of-swarming (bad)
- Says his AI companions frequently refuse to fire, miss when they do, and throw grenades at their feet.
    → game-design.ai-teammates.useless-in-combat             (bad)
- Says almost every area is a tunnel with him at one end, and even at a crossroads the enemies use one tunnel at a time.
    → game-design.level-design.badly-laid-out                (bad)
- Says the maximum player count is oddly three.
    → game-design.co-op-design.group-is-too-small            (bad)
- Says a Prowler breathes so loudly it gives away its position minutes early, and that his own character calls it out even when he cannot see it.
    → art.atmosphere.your-own-character-gives-the-scare-away (bad)
- Says replaying the missions taught him there are only a few spots Prowlers can spawn in.
    → game-design.enemy-design.enemies-arrive-in-the-same-places-every-run (bad)
- Says the Drones break off and run for a vent the moment they are half dead, so they often die before attacking.
    → game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour             (bad)
- Says horde mode, the reason he bought the game, is locked behind the campaign.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.gated-behind-a-different-mode (bad)
- Says horde mode only beefs up the enemies and makes the lack of xenomorph variety more noticeable.
    → game-design.modes.a-mode-falls-flat                    (bad)
- Says the Doc's healing charge refills only from medkits and gives less than the medkit would, so the ability runs out halfway through a mission.
    → game-design.role-design.role-underpowered              (bad)
- Says the maximum mouse sensitivity is 2.5, too slow for him, and that the game also has mouse acceleration.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.aim-sensitivity-cannot-be-tuned (bad)
- Says everyone picks a challenge card and the game applies only one at random, which removes depth and replayability.
    → game-design.replayability.runs-out-fast                (bad)
- Says to get it on sale for quick casual fun with a friend, and to look elsewhere for a tactical shooter that makes you think.
    → marketing.positioning.sold-as-a-different-kind-of-game (bad)
