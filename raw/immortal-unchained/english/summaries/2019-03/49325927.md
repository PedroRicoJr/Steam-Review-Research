## What they said

- Says the lore must be pieced together from three types of collectible written as though you already know unmet characters and places, so without a wiki and hours of collecting most players will only have a small idea of what is going on.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Says most lore items sit in enemy-infested areas so you are rushed to read them rather than absorb them.
    → game-design.pacing.the-pace-leaves-no-time-to-explore  (bad)
- Says the three planets start small and expand after the first half, and each world is interesting for a short period before becoming boring because of the backtracking required.
    → game-design.level-design.repetitive-layouts            (bad)
- Calls the soundtrack rich and incredible, one of the highlights of the release, and says it is worth turning the sound effects down to hear it alone.
    → audio.music.fits-the-game                              (good)
- Says several key areas run at 22 to 26 frames per second at 1080p on an i7-6700k and GTX 1080, with stuttering and slow motion during the toughest fights.
    → engineering.performance.unstable-framerate             (bad)
- Says the studio chose to work on paid downloadable content instead of fixing known issues, and that DLC is coming in May instead of an optimisation update.
    → community.developer-communication.working-on-the-wrong-thing-first (bad)
