## What they said

- Says the game really does its best to make you hate it, as though somebody set out to make Dark Souls but nastier.
    → game-design.fairness.losses-feel-arbitrary             (bad)
- Says he waited until it was six dollars and it had been on his wishlist for years.
    → publishing.price.too-high-for-what-it-is               (bad)
- Says all the guns have really poor range and are not accurate unless you get into lock-on range, where they feel serviceable.
    → game-design.power-balance.rules-favour-the-enemy       (bad)
- Says using lock-on much will snap the camera to other opponents, ruin your view near an obstacle and constrain how you move relative to your target.
    → game-design.game-feel.camera.obscures-the-action       (bad)
- Says the game has well-hidden traps everywhere that hold you still while a melee enemy closes in, and sometimes random bottomless pits that kill you instantly.
    → game-design.world-interaction.hazards-punish-unfairly  (bad)
- Says enemies with guns are remarkably good at leading their shots to hit you no matter how fast you are, and have no ammunition worries while you do.
    → game-design.power-balance.rules-favour-the-enemy       (bad)
- Says that when you die you drop all your currency, which doubles as experience, and dying again before recovering it loses it forever.
    → game-design.punishment-model.dying-costs-you-money     (bad)
- Says if you get a sniper rifle early you can hang back and click on distant heads, and with so little ammo you had better like that kind of play.
    → game-design.power-balance.one-option-dominates         (bad)
- Says the plot is thinly disguised Scandinavian mythology and everything that is not you is high technology to the point of incomprehensibility, which he loves.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.world-worth-exploring      (good)
- Says the terrain is bizarrely entertaining and just nice to look at.
    → art.visual-direction.looks-well-directed               (good)
