## What they said

- Says to buy it only if you are a diehard fan of the formula, and names six other games in the genre worth picking up first.
    → marketing.reputation.beaten-by-a-competitor            (bad)
- Says the graphics are not technically bad but the game is just ugly - glowing lights over everything, intense bloom, then a filter to wash it out, giving a low contrast visual mess.
    → art.visual-direction.off-putting-look                  (bad)
- Says the Shadows setting specifically has to be kept on Low, because High or Medium took his capable PC from 144 frames to 40-50.
    → engineering.performance.one-setting-causes-the-slowdown (bad)
- Says building a character that makes sense is a challenge, because most weapons scale off one attribute but need points in other attributes that do nothing for your build.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.only-a-few-builds-are-viable (bad)
- Says the controls do not always feel responsive and his character sometimes would not heal or dodge despite mashing the button.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.unresponsive            (bad)
- Says locking on lets you circle to the weak spot but also causes missed shots, because no weapon is hitscan and you must lead a moving target.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.shots-go-where-they-want  (bad)
- Says a weak spot can take ten or twenty times the damage of a chest shot, so every tough encounter becomes an endless circle dance, which is not fun.
    → game-design.enemy-design.all-fought-the-same-way       (bad)
- Says the lore and story are passable, the character dialogue is well written and the English voice acting is good.
    → audio.voice-performance.well-acted                     (good)
- Says the ending has a twist you will not see coming and will either love or hate.
    → narrative.story.worth-following                        (good)
- Lists specific fixes for other players - shadows on low, turn off anti-aliasing in the config file, and two graphics driver settings.
    → game-design.ui-ux.settings-only-in-a-config-file       (bad)
- Says life is too short to waste on games this mediocre, though it is playable and beatable and only crashed three or four times.
    → review.calls-it-average-rather-than-good-or-bad        (~)
