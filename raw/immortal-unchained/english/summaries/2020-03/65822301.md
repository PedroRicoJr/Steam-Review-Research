## What they said

- Says the game is almost good in a lot of ways but constantly shoots itself in the foot.
    → review.calls-it-average-rather-than-good-or-bad        (~)
- Says it looks like a third-person shooter but is not really one, because playing it like one goes badly and it requires its janky lock-on system to beat certain enemies.
    → marketing.positioning.sold-as-a-different-kind-of-game (bad)
- Says the game is full of bugs with enemies shooting through walls, lots of ways to get stuck and strange collision problems.
    → engineering.bugs.buggy                                 (bad)
- Says the weapons look like they came from a free real-world gun asset pack with glowing bits added, and nothing feels good to shoot.
    → production.craftsmanship.looks-assembled-from-bought-parts (bad)
- Says melee is broken and awful - one basic attack and enemies do not even react to it - and warns not to be fooled by the character creation screen into thinking melee is viable.
    → game-design.power-balance.some-options-are-useless     (bad)
- Says it has maybe the worst stat and weapon requirement system he has seen, because requirements are far too high and with one respec per playthrough you only ever try a fraction of the guns.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.only-a-few-builds-are-viable (bad)
- Says every enemy has its weak point on its back and rotates on a dime to face you even during attacks, and that a wall or tight hallway leaves you no option at all.
    → game-design.enemy-design.ignores-physical-logic        (bad)
- Says the level design is often quite good with lots of secrets to find and the unique bosses are cool, if weirdly easy.
    → game-design.level-design.well-built                    (good)
- Says the enemies' shots have infinite range and no leash, and that his weapons had two to three times less range at launch than they do now.
    → game-design.power-balance.rules-favour-the-enemy       (bad)
- Adds that almost all enemies are invisible until you get close to where they are supposed to be, so you cannot make an educated plan for exploring an area.
    → game-design.enemy-design.unfair-spawns                 (bad)
- Says there are far too many enemies that teleport constantly, and calls it one of the most obnoxious things he has seen in a game.
    → game-design.enemy-design.no-counterplay                (bad)
