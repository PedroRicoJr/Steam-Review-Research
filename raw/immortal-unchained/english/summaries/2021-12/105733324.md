- Says there are long stretches of enemies where without lucky ammunition drops you run out well before a checkpoint.
    → game-design.power-balance.resources-too-scarce         (bad)
- Says the enemy statistics are overtuned and there are a lot of non-viable weapon choices from the start.
    → game-design.power-balance.some-options-are-useless     (bad)
- Says he farmed his stats to an even spread just to try as many starting weapons as he could.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.gated-behind-farming (bad)
- Says the encounter design is sloggy and zone-based rather than active like other games in the genre.
    → game-design.pacing.no-let-up                           (bad)
