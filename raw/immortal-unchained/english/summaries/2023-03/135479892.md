## What they said

- Recommends it on sale with low expectations instead of a named far larger game, saying they share flaws such as reused bosses but this one has better pacing, controls and length at a lower price.
    → marketing.reputation.beats-its-rivals                  (good)
- Says the story is linear but the second half can be done in any order, with decent build variety though sniper rifles and grenade launchers vastly outperform other types.
    → game-design.power-balance.one-option-dominates         (bad)
- Says the game saves on exit and you can pause and continue without walking back to the last checkpoint.
    → game-design.session-flexibility.you-choose-when-to-stop (good)
- Says the locations are diverse but too large, filled with traps in the first half and teleporting enemies in the second.
    → game-design.level-design.the-spaces-are-scaled-too-big (bad)
- Says opponents often rise from the floor, appear out of the air or drop from the sky, so it is not possible to see and attack them first.
    → game-design.enemy-design.unfair-spawns                 (bad)
- Says the four main bosses are unique while the optional ones are reskinned regular enemies with five to ten extra mobs.
    → game-design.enemy-design.forgettable-specials          (bad)
- Says melee attacks feel weightless and unsatisfying for both the player and the enemies.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.weightless                (bad)
- Says backtracking is inconvenient because fast travel arrives only in the second half, unlocking each point costs currency, and you cannot see whether everything has been collected.
    → game-design.ui-ux.does-not-show-what-is-left-to-earn   (bad)
- Says there are many locked chests and doors and it is hard to remember which needs which key.
    → accessibility.memory-and-attention.relies-on-remembering-with-no-text (bad)
- Calls the soundtrack irritating.
    → audio.music.forgettable-or-annoying                    (bad)
- Says the teleporting snipers make a distinct noise when they spawn.
    → audio.sound-effects.cues-warn-you-in-time              (good)
- Says some non-interactive level architecture produces sounds when the player is close.
    → audio.sound-effects.sounds-out-of-place                (bad)
- Says the controls are comfortable and mostly rebindable with five-button mice supported, but shooting and aiming are hard-bound to the mouse buttons.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.missing-expected-bindings (bad)
- Says he hit one soft-lock in the last area where the path did not open, fixed by reloading at a checkpoint.
    → engineering.bugs.breaks-play                           (bad)
- Says the ending was relatively original and unsatisfying.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
