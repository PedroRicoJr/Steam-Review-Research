## What they said

- Says the theme is unique and the story is presented in a way that is easier to follow than Dark Souls.
    → narrative.story.worth-following                        (good)
- Says the weapon upgrade system is cool and it is nice that you can break down useless weapons for parts.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Calls the music and audio decent.
    → audio.music.fits-the-game                              (good)
- Says it has some of the worst, least intuitive level design he has seen in a souls-like, and is one of the few games where he needed a walkthrough video to know where to go.
    → game-design.level-design.badly-laid-out                (bad)
- Says the art style, graphics and theme leave you disoriented because everything looks the same and there is not enough detail to build reference points for navigation.
    → game-design.readability.geometry-unclear               (bad)
- Says the lack of ammo is annoying early on for some classes.
    → game-design.power-balance.resources-too-scarce         (bad)
- Says a sniper round does no damage at all to a mob roughly twenty metres away.
    → game-design.power-balance.rules-favour-the-enemy       (bad)
- Says the AI is extremely poor and there is a sweet spot where you can stand and shoot an enemy without it ever noticing you.
    → game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour             (bad)
- Says the transitions between dodging, shooting, melee and sprinting lack fluidity and feel abrupt.
    → art.animation.stiff-or-clunky                          (bad)
- Says not to pick the sniper class because of very limited ammo and extremely long reloads.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.only-a-few-builds-are-viable (bad)
- Says reloading takes three seconds and sprinting or dodging at ninety-nine percent through it makes you start again.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.sluggish-weapon-handling  (bad)
- Says a charged secondary attack is cancelled completely if you release a fraction early, instead of firing weaker for less resource.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.abilities-are-awkward-to-trigger (bad)
- Says it carries a AAA price tag when the animations, fluidity and presentation feel very indie, and is worth half its price.
    → publishing.price.too-high-for-what-it-is               (bad)
