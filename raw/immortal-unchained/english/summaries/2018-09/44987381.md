## What they said

- Three worlds that interconnect and double back on each other, with hidden chests and shortcuts - he calls it metroidvania and says doors turn out to have been doors all along.
    → game-design.level-design.well-built                    (good)
- Opening a chest always feels good because you know you have the collectible needed to open it.
    → game-design.game-feel.reward-moment.the-payout-lands-well (good)
- Single-shot high-damage weapons are king; five legendary assault rifles are all worse than a green sniper rifle even fully upgraded.
    → game-design.power-balance.one-option-dominates         (bad)
- Acid weapons are useless while pure physical weapons beat nearly every enemy in the game.
    → game-design.power-balance.some-options-are-useless     (bad)
- About ten enemies in the whole game plus reskins of those.
    → game-design.enemy-design.variety-lacking               (bad)
- Basic enemies hit harder than most bosses; the bosses are downright easy compared to some of the areas.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.badly-scaled             (bad)
- Enemies teleport in or crawl out of the ground with no warning, so an area cannot be planned until it has been run repeatedly.
    → game-design.enemy-design.unfair-spawns                 (bad)
- Asks for a per-world checklist of collectibles and locked chests - after two full playthroughs he cannot tell whether the things he has not found even exist.
    → game-design.ui-ux.does-not-show-what-is-left-to-earn   (bad)
- Says he got at least fifty dollars of enjoyment out of it and will get more.
    → publishing.price.fair                                  (good)
