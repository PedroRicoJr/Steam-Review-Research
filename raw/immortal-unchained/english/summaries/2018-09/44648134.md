## What they said

- Calls the science-fiction setting and lore interesting and original.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.world-worth-exploring      (good)
- Says the variety of class choices - long-range snipers, hybrids, melee-only warriors - means restarting with a different build is an entirely different play-style.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.changes-how-you-play (good)
- Says the different builds give it good replay value.
    → game-design.replayability.keeps-pulling-you-back       (good)
- Says weapons carry unique pre-installed attachments granting special abilities, and that weapon upgrading from scrapped gear is a very interesting part of the game.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says many attachments and statistics are not described and you can only guess what they do.
    → game-design.ui-ux.hides-information                    (bad)
- Calls the general lack of polish noticeable enough to give a sense of unfinished work.
    → production.craftsmanship.needs-more-work               (bad)
- Says the AI will get stuck in objects or fail to path to you, and other times sense you from incredible distances and come from the other side of the map.
    → game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour             (bad)
- Says some enemies perform homing attacks, rotating in unnatural and almost glitchy ways mid-animation to line up a hit, and that dodging is unreliable compared to similar titles.
    → game-design.enemy-design.ignores-physical-logic        (bad)
- Says several boss fights are badly designed, lackluster or boring, which is the opposite of what a souls-like needs.
    → game-design.enemy-design.bosses-are-a-chore            (bad)
- Says some boss fights create artificial challenge with homing undodgeable ranged attacks and extra mobs spawning at ridiculous rates, and that this disappointed him greatly.
    → game-design.fairness.losses-feel-arbitrary             (bad)
- Says the balance clearly favours ranged over melee because most enemies are melee.
    → game-design.power-balance.one-option-dominates         (bad)
- Says killing tough enemies and finding hidden loot around the levels still gives real satisfaction.
    → game-design.game-feel.reward-moment.the-payout-lands-well (good)
- Says models are good quality but textures and environments are inconsistent, with a marked slow fade-in of shaders and vegetation as you walk.
    → art.fidelity.rough-in-places                           (bad)
- Calls the sound effects and music average quality.
    → audio.music.forgettable-or-annoying                    (bad)
- Says the game is optimised quite well with no major issues.
    → engineering.performance.well-optimised                 (good)
- Reports several minor graphical bugs and problems with enemy charging animations on stairs.
    → engineering.bugs.rare-and-minor                        (good)
- Says at a fifty euro tag he cannot recommend it, and to wait for a sale and a few patches unless you need another souls-like right now.
    → publishing.price.too-high-for-what-it-is               (bad)
- Says the devs state the game lasts twenty to thirty hours depending on play style.
    → production.content-amount.plenty                       (good)
- Closes with a link to his own Steam curator archive.
    → review.promotes-the-reviewers-own-curator-page         (~)
