## What they said

- Opens by asking the developers to make a New Game Plus mode, and says he could easily spend another hundred to two hundred hours if they did.
    → game-design.modes.expected-mode-missing                (bad)
- Calls the boss fights amazing.
    → game-design.enemy-design.memorable-specials            (good)
- Warns that the prologue level looks very similar throughout and can turn people off, while later levels get much better.
    → game-design.level-design.repetitive-layouts            (bad)
- Each weapon class has its own playstyle, so a new combination of guns feels fresh.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.changes-how-you-play (good)
- Weak points are not all in the same place - some are in the back, some in the front - and you work out which limb to shoot off to bring an enemy down fast.
    → game-design.enemy-design.good-variety                  (good)
- Says there are many secret passages, hidden areas and shortcuts back to the obelisk, and that the checkpoints are far fairer than any Dark Souls game.
    → game-design.level-design.well-built                    (good)
- Says he never felt the game was unfair if you really open your eyes.
    → game-design.fairness.losses-feel-earned                (good)
- Says you start with a class and its stats but are not bound to its weapons - a marksman can build into shotguns effectively later.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.every-class-can-use-every-weapon (good)
- Says the game was made by a relatively small development group and asks the reader to cut them some slack.
