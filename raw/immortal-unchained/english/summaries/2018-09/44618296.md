## What they said

- Says he wanted desperately to like it - a conceptually interesting mix of shooter, Souls and dungeon crawler.
    → marketing.reputation.explained-by-naming-other-games   (~)
- Says the controls feel sluggish and you never get an intuitive feel for the shooting.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.sluggish-weapon-handling  (bad)
- Says the crosshair design is terrible - even as a marksman with a sniper rifle it is not clear where the aiming point and hit box are.
    → game-design.readability.cannot-spot-what-you-need      (bad)
- Says shooting off limbs is a waste of scarce bullets because the arm and its gun respawn one to two seconds later, killing a tactic that could have been a real mechanic.
    → game-design.enemy-design.ignores-physical-logic        (bad)
- Says melee is basic button-mashing with no weight or oomph, and comes across as an afterthought.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.weightless                (bad)
- Says the AI is tepid and supernaturally gifted at spotting you at once, and that he watched a mob retrace a semicircle ten times to reach him past one block of debris.
    → game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour             (bad)
- Says the boss arena is small, which breaks the souls rule that boss areas should be big enough to run around and learn the fight.
    → game-design.level-design.the-spaces-are-scaled-too-big (bad)
- Says the first level is linear narrow corridors where it is hard to distinguish directions and every other room looks the same.
    → game-design.level-design.repetitive-layouts            (bad)
- Says the colour and art is bleak and drab and lacks colour and life, making it dreary to play.
    → art.visual-direction.off-putting-look                  (bad)
- Says he uninstalled it under an hour in, thinking it unlikely he would ever come back.
    → game-design.replayability.runs-out-fast                (bad)
