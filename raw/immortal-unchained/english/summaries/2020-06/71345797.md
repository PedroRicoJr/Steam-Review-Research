## What they said

- Says he has a deep love-hate relationship with the game - invigorating on one hand, with infuriating mechanics, areas and enemies on the other - and that this is exactly what makes him revisit it.
    → game-design.replayability.keeps-pulling-you-back       (good)
- Says there is a massive array of guns, a reasonable selection of melee weapons, and ciphers that add extra variety, with attachments that reward inspecting weapons carefully.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says combat is very intense with enemies dealing incredible damage, which did not surprise him coming from Dark Souls 3 and The Surge.
    → game-design.enemy-design.pressure-feels-good           (good)
- Says he is fond of the worldbuilding and lore told through artifacts and epitaphs, and that it kept him interested to the end even when confusing.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.world-worth-exploring      (good)
- Says the game suffers a striking lack of content, with the base game spread over only three worlds, so the ending can sneak up on you.
    → production.content-amount.too-little                   (bad)
- Says the optional backtracking and boss fights give rewards that are not very rewarding, and arrive when you are already about to finish the game.
    → game-design.game-feel.reward-moment.the-payout-lands-flat (bad)
- Says it is nearly impossible to find and use all the Protocols without an outside walkthrough or guide.
    → game-design.ui-ux.hides-information                    (bad)
- Says he thinks the Protocol system exists to give the game more content without creating new areas or enemies, and is disappointed by that.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
