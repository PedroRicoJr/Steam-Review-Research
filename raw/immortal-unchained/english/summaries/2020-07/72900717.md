## What they said

- Says the game only became fun on the third new game cycle, once his gear and stats could deal with the enemies at a reasonable rate.
    → game-design.power-balance.challenge-outgrows-the-player (bad)
- Says it is an overly ambitious undertaking from a small indie studio.
    → review.grades-it-against-the-studios-size              (~)
- Says amazing bugs are still present after two years and a downloadable content pack.
    → engineering.bugs.buggy                                 (bad)
- Calls the AI a parody, with enemies stuck on scenery one moment and spotting him from miles away the next.
    → game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour             (bad)
- Says the hitboxes are terrible, especially on charging enemies that explode into a run, and on most melee attacks.
    → game-design.enemy-design.ignores-physical-logic        (bad)
- Says a lot of enemies have no tells for their ranged attacks and some attacks are hitscan, making them very hard to avoid.
    → game-design.readability.threats-unclear                (bad)
- Says enemies are chain triggered together from incredible distances.
    → game-design.enemy-design.always-knows-where-you-are    (bad)
- Says enemies are placed behind shields or on high ground backed by melee enemies that spawn when you approach, and sometimes spawn silently behind you mid-fight.
    → game-design.enemy-design.unfair-spawns                 (bad)
- Says ranged attacks cost no stamina but melee and dodging do, while many enemy attacks stun or knock you down at a full stamina bar, which usually kills you instantly.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.stuns-take-control-away (bad)
- Says the level designs are good but not made for ranged combat, with many cramped spaces the game keeps filling with enemies you are meant to shoot in the back.
    → game-design.level-design.the-spaces-are-scaled-too-small (bad)
- Says the guns have little range with a significant damage drop off, which gimps gun combat further.
    → game-design.power-balance.rules-favour-the-enemy       (bad)
- Says the level design, visuals, shortcuts and the connectedness of the zones are the game's strengths.
    → game-design.level-design.more-than-one-way-in          (good)
- Says the lore is great and served well through story, pickups and enemy drops.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.world-worth-exploring      (good)
- Says upgrading armour by finding armour shrines is a great idea.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says the downloadable content makes things worse, with broken enemies that come in droves and the most unfair boss fight in the game.
    → publishing.dlc-and-editions.dlc-not-worth-it           (bad)
- Says there are ankle-high elevations his character cannot traverse.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.no-modern-moves         (bad)
- Says enemies get knocked out of bounds and keep tracking and firing at him without dying.
    → engineering.bugs.buggy                                 (bad)
