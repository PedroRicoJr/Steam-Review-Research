## What they said

- Says the game did not sell well on release and that its extreme difficulty and occasional flaws made critics give it harsher reviews than justified.
    → marketing.reputation.judged-unfairly                   (good)
- Says some NPC animations are ropey, with jaws flapping about when they speak.
    → art.animation.the-faces-do-not-move                    (bad)
- Describes three distinct worlds - a frozen surface over a mechanical necropolis, dense jungle over lava, and the ruins of a sprawling city.
    → game-design.level-design.well-built                    (good)
- Says weapons come in five types with different attachments, five rarities and five damage elements, and enemies are resistant or vulnerable to different ones you can read off their colour or their world.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says combat takes a minute to get used to but is extremely satisfying once you have the hang of it - head shot to weaken, close in, lock on, dodge the melee, blast the weak spot.
    → game-design.progression.complexity.rewarding-once-learned (good)
- Says the game universe is massive and varied, on a par with Dark Souls in size, filled with secrets, shortcuts and locked chests worth returning for.
    → game-design.level-design.more-than-one-way-in          (good)
- Says the optional bosses are a lot harder than the main ones, which he mostly beat on the first attempt.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.badly-scaled             (bad)
- Says he wrote the review in so much detail because the game does not tell you how to play it beyond the very basics.
    → game-design.ui-ux.hides-information                    (bad)
