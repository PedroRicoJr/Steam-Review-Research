## What they said

- Says the atmosphere is awesome, set through the world and level design, the enemy design and the soundtrack.
    → art.atmosphere.draws-you-in                            (good)
- Says he loved the variety of weapons and secondary attacks, though he never found a use for pistols and could not equip the launchers on his build.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says the story is more coherent than Dark Souls or Elden Ring and singles out the ending.
    → narrative.story.worth-following                        (good)
- Says the grenades and ammo packs are reusable and plentiful so running out is not a problem.
    → game-design.power-balance.well-tuned                   (good)
- Says the armour shrines give free permanent upgrades instead of making you manage equipment weight, and that the upgrades are good.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.changes-how-you-play (good)
- Says you can stagger enemies and destroy their limbs so they cannot hold a gun or must crawl towards you.
    → game-design.enemy-design.leaves-you-in-control         (good)
- Says the stat requirements for the better weapons are high enough that levelling into one or two stats makes another weapon type inaccessible, with only one respec core.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.choices-cannot-be-undone (bad)
- Says the game crashed repeatedly in one area until he verified the game files through Steam.
    → engineering.stability.crashes-on-specific-event        (bad)
- Says that having to spend currency to unlock fast travel at each shrine is annoying, especially as you are about halfway through the game before you unlock the feature.
    → game-design.ui-ux.fast-travel-comes-late-and-costs     (bad)
- Says that after the second boss you appear to hit a dead end with no dialogue about where to go, when the game wants you to backtrack the whole level you just cleared.
    → game-design.ui-ux.hides-information                    (bad)
- Says the distances between save shrines are sometimes very long and made him paranoid about dying and losing progress.
    → game-design.punishment-model.harsh-restart             (bad)
- Says he wishes melee were more useful, since most enemies are ranged and melee is only good for breaking portable shields or getting a quick hit on a teleporting enemy.
    → game-design.power-balance.some-options-are-useless     (bad)
