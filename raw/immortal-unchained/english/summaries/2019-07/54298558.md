## What they said

- Says the game launched at fifty dollars, and a year later an ultimate version sells for forty while launch buyers only get a discount on the DLC, so early supporters pay about sixty in total.
    → publishing.dlc-and-editions.no-upgrade-path-between-editions (bad)
- Says he asked the studio publicly whether launch buyers would be upgraded and was told only that they get a discount, which he calls pretty lame.
    → community.developer-communication.talks-but-never-about-the-problem (bad)
- Says he tried both controller and mouse and keyboard and preferred the controller, because headshots are not instant kills so the extra precision does not pay.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.you-have-to-switch-input-device-to-play-well (bad)
- Says he likes the weapon variety and the levelling system.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)
- Says the environment really pulled him in.
    → art.atmosphere.draws-you-in                            (good)
- Says the difficulty at times really annoyed him but it was so satisfying when he got past it.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.satisfyingly-hard        (good)

    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.cannot-change-how-you-look (bad)
