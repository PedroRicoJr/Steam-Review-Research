## What they said

- Says the RPG and level design elements, the mysterious lore-heavy storytelling and the inventory system are all lifted straight from Dark Souls, with combat focused on gunplay.
    → marketing.reputation.derivative-of-an-older-game       (bad)
- Says you will die a lot in the first few hours and the deaths will feel cheap, but they are not - every enemy follows a strict pattern you can learn and then exploit mercilessly.
    → game-design.fairness.losses-feel-earned                (good)
- Says the game runs fine so long as you turn the shadows down, which does not hurt the visuals much given the foggy gloomy style.
    → engineering.performance.one-setting-causes-the-slowdown (bad)
- Says the setting is interesting and dark, with solid sound design, solid voice acting, decent music and a distinctive art direction.
    → art.visual-direction.looks-well-directed               (good)
- Says the controls are responsive, especially on mouse and keyboard, and you can shoot enemy limbs to stagger or disarm them.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.responsive-and-clear    (good)
- Says the game may not instantly click and may seem janky, but persevere and it becomes a rewarding experience.
    → game-design.progression.complexity.rewarding-once-learned (good)
