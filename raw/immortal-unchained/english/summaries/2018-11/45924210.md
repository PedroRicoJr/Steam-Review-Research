## What they said

- Says action RPGs and third-person shooters are his two favourite genres so this seemed a match made in heaven, but a couple of design decisions meant it was not for him.
    → review.calls-it-average-rather-than-good-or-bad        (~)
- Says he was not doing much damage even with headshots until he realised every enemy has a weak point on its back.
    → game-design.readability.cannot-spot-what-you-need      (bad)
- Says there is only one way to expose most weak points - bait an attack, dodge, get a few shots away - so he ended up playing a less polished Dark Souls and doing minimal shooting.
    → marketing.positioning.sold-as-a-different-kind-of-game (bad)
- Says if enemies had varied weak spots, some best tackled at range and some up close, it would alleviate the problem.
    → game-design.enemy-design.all-fought-the-same-way       (bad)
- Says he felt disincentivised from free aiming because the most effective way to kill is to forgo the range benefit of having a gun.
    → game-design.power-balance.the-tool-everyone-carries-has-no-job (bad)
- Says aiming does not feel right on a controller - there is almost momentum to it, so the cursor takes time to decelerate when you release the stick.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.you-have-to-switch-input-device-to-play-well (bad)
- Says his character's animations were very jittery when turning in small increments while everything else moved smoothly.
    → art.animation.stiff-or-clunky                          (bad)
