- The combat may be the studio's best, taking the best of Elden Ring and Sekiro.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.impactful                 (good)
- Each class is distinct in how it controls space and recovers from mistakes; the archer is finally done well.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Most class abilities make you briefly untouchable, adding timing to defence.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.abilities-double-as-dodges (good)
- The night bosses are among the studio's best fights; the final one is a masterpiece.
    → game-design.enemy-design.the-bosses-are-a-highlight    (good)
- Relics that change core abilities add some lasting power between runs.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.deep-and-varied (good)

    → game-design.randomness.not-random-enough               (bad)

    → game-design.randomness.guaranteed-best-rewards-narrow-the-builds (bad)

    → game-design.enemy-design.variety-lacking               (bad)
- Shielded enemies turn up too often for such a fast game.
    → game-design.enemy-design.the-common-enemies-slow-down-a-fast-game (bad)

    → engineering.matchmaking.no-penalty-for-leaving         (bad)
