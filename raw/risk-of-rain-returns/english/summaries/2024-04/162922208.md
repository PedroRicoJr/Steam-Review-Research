## What they said

- A good remake, but not as great as it could have been: every good change comes with a questionable one.
    → marketing.positioning.the-remake-changes-too-little    (bad)
- All the spritework is lovingly redone and looks much better.
    → art.fidelity.looks-great                               (good)
- The default camera is zoomed in, but the game is balanced around the old zoom, so you turn it off or suffer.
    → game-design.game-feel.camera.narrow-view-is-a-handicap (bad)
- No more clearing every enemy to leave a stage.
    → game-design.pacing.you-can-move-on-without-clearing-every-enemy (good)
- To compensate, enemies scale much faster, so after the third area everything takes pointlessly long to kill.
    → game-design.enemy-design.bullet-sponges                (bad)
- The damage dealt and taken settings should not be needed at all.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.unknown                  (~)
- The new characters are fun and mostly well designed.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Artificer clearly was not designed for this game.
    → game-design.role-design.role-underpowered              (bad)
- Alternate skills are a nice addition, though not all are useful.
    → game-design.power-balance.some-options-are-useless     (bad)
- The Providence Trials range from fun to frustrating, and one unlock needs a top score on 25 of them.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.an-unlock-challenge-is-a-chore (bad)
- The trials clearly had little playtesting.
    → production.craftsmanship.barely-playtested             (bad)
- The controls are fine with the Risk of Rain 2 option, after the original's very poor keyboard controls.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.responsive-and-clear    (good)
