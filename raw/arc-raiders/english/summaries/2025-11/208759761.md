    → art.atmosphere.draws-you-in                            (good)
- Says missions start to feel the same - hop in, loot, fight the same bots, extract.
    → production.content-variety.repetitive                  (bad)
- Says the AI swings between standing still to be deleted and instantly locking on and melting you.
    → game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour             (bad)

    → community.player-conduct.a-third-player-swoops-on-your-fight (bad)
