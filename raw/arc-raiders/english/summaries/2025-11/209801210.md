## What they said

- Says he reached level 75 and 100% completion at 125 hours - achievement difficulty about 2 of 5, the max level the grindy one, three achievements bugged on the first attempt.
    → game-design.progression.achievements.do-not-track-what-you-actually-did (bad)
- Says in solos most people were surprisingly cooperative for the quests that need help.
    → community.player-conduct.strangers-team-up-instead-of-fighting (good)
- Says there is much to do - loot, a free battle pass, weekly trials, cosmetic quests that sadly give no XP, and PvP.
    → production.content-amount.plenty                       (good)
- Says the developers listen to the community and make changes, and the community is fresh and growing.
    → community.developer-communication.listens-and-acts     (good)
- Says the world is beautiful, dangerous and unpredictable, the tension constant, the loop addicting, and the fake friendlies who shoot you in the back are part of the charm.
    → community.player-conduct.not-knowing-who-to-trust-is-the-thrill (good)
- Says the first thirty hours went on learning crafting, loadouts, materials and weapon scaling - the game throws a lot at you and gets smoother once the systems click.
    → game-design.progression.complexity.rewarding-once-learned (good)
- Says the skill trees need tuning and you cannot respec yet, so watch an overview before spending points - the studio is working on it.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.choices-cannot-be-undone (bad)
- Says once everything is done the loop feels slow with nothing to go for, forcing PvP, though more content is coming.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.nothing-left-to-chase (bad)
- Teaches the grind - dismantle spare items, upgrade low-tier weapons, keep level-one bags early, practise PvP with free loadouts, help kill big enemies and loot everything.
    → community.player-conduct.the-review-teaches-you-how-to-play (good)
