## What they said

- Says the behaviour-based matchmaking is an okay system until you refuse to turn the other cheek - shoot back at a player, even one who just murdered someone, and the algorithm decides you like PvP and throws you back in the bucket of crabs.
    → engineering.matchmaking.shooting-back-once-sorts-you-with-the-killers (bad)
- Says you cannot talk to other players from a distance - they must be close enough to kill you to hear you, and the first one shot dies.
    → community.social-features.unknown                      (~)
- Says the free loadout puts nothing but time on the line, so camping extractions and ambushing looters is the most efficient way to gain value - calm and stocked against someone shaken and loaded.
    → game-design.punishment-model.dying-costs-nothing       (bad)
- Says the extraction points are built to promote camping - calling the lift blasts an audio cue, the terminal holds you defenceless for several seconds and cancels on damage, and hiding spots surround it.
    → game-design.world-interaction.interacting-freezes-you-and-that-is-when-you-die (bad)
- Says you cannot move fast without holstering your weapon, so getting around the map makes you a fish in a barrel.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.unknown                 (~)
- Says he has been killed during Matriarch events by players shooting anyone looting the bots, even those who helped kill them, and a free loadout loses nothing for the attempt while he can lose 100K of gear chasing a 13K item.
    → community.player-conduct.a-third-player-swoops-on-your-fight (bad)
- Says the game makes you a worse person - it punishes trust, punishes justice, punishes peaceful gathering and rewards predatory rat behaviour.
    → game-design.co-op-design.working-together-buys-you-nothing (bad)
- Says the studio reused the assets of a good game to make a Tarkov clone because the CEO wanted Fortnite money.
    → marketing.positioning.sold-as-a-different-kind-of-game (bad)
