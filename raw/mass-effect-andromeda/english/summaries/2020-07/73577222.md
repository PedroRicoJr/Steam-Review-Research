## What they said

- Shows the flaws of a rushed release forced out by EA after an earlier version was thrown away (reviewer's account).
    → production.launch-state.rushed-out-by-the-owner        (bad)
- Points to the multiplayer, with microtransactions and a year more of support, as the tell.
    → publishing.monetisation-practice.mtx-in-premium-game   (bad)
- Crafting is lacklustre though about a third of the talents feed it.
    → game-design.progression.complexity.shallow             (bad)
- Single-player storylines cut off abruptly or are never mentioned again.
    → narrative.story.still-unfinished-years-on              (bad)
- Characters stir neither love nor hate.
    → narrative.characters-writing.flat-or-annoying          (bad)
- The dialogue is rendered meaningless, with no Paragon or Renegade, so the player has no say in how things turn out.
    → narrative.story.your-choices-change-nothing            (bad)
- Likes the return to a skill system where builds matter, but not the limit of three active skills.
    → game-design.role-design.too-few-abilities-per-character (bad)
- Likes mid-air battles and freer movement.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.responsive              (good)
- The worlds are gorgeous and make them want to explore more than the game asks.
    → narrative.world-and-setting.world-worth-exploring      (good)
- Likes the return of the rover, even without weapons.
    → game-design.progression.build-and-customisation.a-named-weapon-or-vehicle-is-loved (good)
- Got it for $14.99; would not be positive at $60 and probably not at $29.99.
    → publishing.sale-dependency.buy-on-sale-only            (bad)
- The mandatory Origin overlay alone would drop their score to 5/10 or lower.
    → engineering.access.unwanted-third-party-software       (bad)
