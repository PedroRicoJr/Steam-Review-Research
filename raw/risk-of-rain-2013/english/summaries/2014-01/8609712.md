## What they said

- Addictive even after most objectives are done (177 hours, edited 2015-10-14).
    → game-design.replayability.keeps-pulling-you-back       (good)
- Co-op is a blast, and the classes must work together, which changes everyone's strategy.
    → game-design.co-op-design.rewards-teamwork              (good)
- Each class calls for its own skills and strategies.
    → game-design.role-design.each-role-plays-its-own-way    (good)
- Single player is surprisingly addictive too.
    → game-design.solo-viability.works-solo                  (good)
- A great run ends with you an 'angry demigod'.
    → game-design.game-feel.combat.makes-you-feel-superhumanly-strong (good)
- Long runs dropped frames even on high-end hardware; after the move to a newer engine version it runs well even on weak machines (reviewer's account).
    → live-ops.patch-quality.fixed-what-mattered             (good)
- At first the game would not fill a 1920x1080 screen without editing a settings file; an update added proper display options (reviewer's account).
    → game-design.ui-ux.does-not-support-my-screen-shape     (bad)
- Some in-game achievements are near impossible without a co-op partner.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.gated-behind-a-different-mode (bad)
- Online needs port forwarding, with no server browser, matchmaking or Steam joining - a pain to get working.
    → community.social-features.joining-needs-the-hosts-ip-address (bad)
- No in-game voice chat.
    → community.social-features.cannot-communicate           (bad)
- Online play desyncs and needs a restart at times, though stability improved later.
    → engineering.netcode.lag-and-desync                     (bad)
- Controller support in menus was patchy and unplugging froze the game; an update fixed both (reviewer's account).
    → live-ops.patch-quality.fixed-what-mattered             (good)
- Much more playable with a gamepad.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.one-input-device-is-the-better-way (~)
