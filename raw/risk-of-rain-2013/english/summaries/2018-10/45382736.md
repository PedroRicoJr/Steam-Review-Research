    → game-design.ui-ux.missing-quality-of-life              (bad)

    → game-design.ui-ux.hides-information                    (bad)
- Rough around the edges - 'no frills'.

    → game-design.power-balance.progression-outgrows-the-challenge (bad)

    → game-design.randomness.luck-decides-the-outcome        (bad)

    → game-design.game-feel.combat.unknown                   (~)
