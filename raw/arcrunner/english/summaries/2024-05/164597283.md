## What they said

- Says the aesthetics are gorgeous vaporwave neon, though it is cyber without the punk - no social criticism.
    → art.visual-direction.looks-well-directed               (good)
- Says it ran perfectly on two rigs with never a frame dip.
    → engineering.performance.well-optimised                 (good)
- Says the controls are intuitive and responsive once rebound for a non-QWERTY keyboard.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.responsive-and-clear    (good)
- Says meta-progression is very slow - eight runs in he had not cleared the first zone - and easy mode cuts the nanite gain, so it is barely easier.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.slow-start         (bad)
- States that he used a memory editor to give himself nanites and unlock a third of the meta-progression, after which bosses became a joke.
    → review.used-a-cheat-to-get-through-it                  (~)
- Says the ninja's stealth removes all enemy AI, even the parts you need, so auto-missiles never fire.
    → game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour             (bad)
- Says the hacker needs a different way to cast because time does not slow while selecting a hack.
    → game-design.game-feel.controls.abilities-are-awkward-to-trigger (bad)
- Says unlocking a late-found weapon needs 250 kills with it across runs, which stretches a standard roguelike mechanic too far.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.grindy             (bad)
- Says levels are corridors with a few cover spots and many exploding destructibles, and enemies spawn too close to use the environment.
    → game-design.world-interaction.hazards-punish-unfairly  (bad)
- Says the run structure is long and repetitive, and being able to save to the menu is what stops you wishing for death to do the laundry.
    → game-design.session-flexibility.you-choose-when-to-stop (good)
