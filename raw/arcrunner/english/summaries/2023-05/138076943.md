## What they said

- Says it is fun for a while with fundamental design failings, and offers very limited variety for a roguelite - three classes, one slow to unlock.
    → production.content-amount.too-little                   (bad)
- Says many weapons are redundant or bad, a handful are so overpowered the rest need not exist, and heavy weapons are underwhelming with no upgrades.
    → game-design.power-balance.one-option-dominates         (bad)
- Says weapons are tied to regions reached in order, and unlocking a weapon to start with makes the first areas trivial time-wasters with no floor skip, boring even with a friend.
    → game-design.pacing.the-early-stages-of-every-run-are-dull (bad)
- Says dropped gear has no power progression, so once you have your build further drops can be ignored, and end-of-floor upgrades are mostly bland stat increases.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.every-unlock-is-a-sideways-swap (~)
- Says the ninja is absolutely overpowered solo because the AI breaks down completely against an invisible player, and asks whether the studio play-tested it.
    → game-design.enemy-design.poor-ai-behaviour             (bad)
- Says difficulty scaling is only enemy damage and health, and hard mode's challenges go from five multi-kills to seven - laughable.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.harder-only-changes-the-numbers (bad)
- Says it took a post-release patch to make movement merely slightly terrible, and for a game called runner you move remarkably slowly.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.sluggish                (bad)
- Says area-attack circles sometimes fail to draw on certain surfaces or lie, hitting him when he is clearly outside.
    → game-design.readability.threats-unclear                (bad)
- Calls it Red Barrel City for the number of things that explode when lightly damaged.
    → game-design.world-interaction.hazards-punish-unfairly  (bad)
- Says the music fits and has nowhere near enough variety, and as a sound designer he recognises many of the stock samples.
    → audio.music.forgettable-or-annoying                    (bad)
- Says the visual style is nice and many enemies blend into the background.
    → game-design.readability.cannot-spot-what-you-need      (bad)
- Says the story is basic and boring, barely enough context.
    → narrative.story.thin-or-forgettable                    (bad)
- Says the worst problem is that finishing the game resets all meta-progression, which happened again after hard despite a patch note he thought changed it, and to wait for a 75% sale.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.progress-does-not-carry-over (bad)
