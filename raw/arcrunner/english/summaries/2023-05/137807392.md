## What they said

- Says the guns are fun, the world looks nice, the enemies are fair, the biomes are varied and most elites are interesting and intuitive to fight.
    → game-design.enemy-design.memorable-specials            (good)
- Says it plays more like a beta than a release - movement is clunky especially for melee builds.
    → game-design.game-feel.movement.sluggish                (bad)
- Says the UI needs clarity - no damage numbers, enemies hard to see, augment effects unclear.
    → game-design.readability.cannot-spot-what-you-need      (bad)
- Says it needs more content to broaden builds and make runs differ, and has the potential to be one of the best action roguelike shooters.
    → production.content-amount.too-little                   (bad)
- Says in a first edit that the developers update and receive feedback well, and in a second that they abandoned it when they had ample opportunity, so it is only worth it on sale.
    → live-ops.abandonment.updates-stopped                   (bad)
