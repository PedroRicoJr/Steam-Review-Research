## What they said

- Says runs are not much different from one another and he has seen nearly every weapon drop.
    → game-design.randomness.not-random-enough               (bad)
- Says weapons do not get better as you progress - the last area's SMG has the starting one's stats - so loot is boring once a gun is modded.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.every-unlock-is-a-sideways-swap (~)
- Says the moment-to-moment play is the saving grace and the enemy intensity ramps in a straight line.
    → game-design.difficulty-tuning.satisfyingly-hard        (good)
- Says the ninja's right click is an instant de-aggro that carries runs, there is no point playing the soldier, and the hacker takes more effort for more reward.
    → game-design.power-balance.one-option-dominates         (bad)
- Says the soundtrack lacks variety and one song will grate if you are stuck on the first level.
    → audio.music.forgettable-or-annoying                    (bad)
- Says core features - secondary weapon, heavy weapon, ultimate, mod slots - are all locked behind nanite upgrades mixed in with the rest, and the game went from mediocre to pretty good once he bought them.
    → game-design.progression.unlock-pace.slow-start         (bad)
- Says that because he can always build the same thing, it becomes a grind rather than a series of surprises, unlike roguelites that make him adapt.
    → game-design.replayability.runs-out-fast                (bad)
